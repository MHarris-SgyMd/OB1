## Changelog

test-live's long and rest cells, and extract's [41], wait out fewer timers their assertions don't need: [38r]'s one-shot runs at a 300 ms capture and poll, its mid-switch case at a 6 s poll, [16]'s pause, outage wait and hung-judge timeout cut, [9]'s heartbeat run scaled to two-thirds, and fixed sleeps in [10g] and [41] polled for. **What changed**, all in `db/test-live.ts`, by the ticket's items:
- **[38r]** (long), ~21 s locally, beside main; each part below timed alone, which together come to more:
  - **Its eight one-shot runs** (`oneSleep`, seven asserted and one a drain) run at `ONE_SHOT`, SMD-3733's 300 ms capture and poll, moved into `sleepScaffold` for [38] and [38r] alike. About 13 s alone, ~1.6 s a call, within the ~1–2 s each SMD-3733's note on the ticket found in [38]; it counted five calls, not eight.
  - **The mid-switch case** (item 1) polls every 6 s, not 15. The ticket said a pass's end doesn't wake the scheduler; it does (`passEnded`, since SMD-1794 PR 2). The 15 s went on the period's start, which waits a whole poll out after the previous case's delete, a live write. The follower meets the switch at its read before the next batch, once the call in hand ends ~1.5 s in, so a 6 s poll keeps it first, as the case's comment asks. A scheduler poll that came first would change nothing either: it finds the pool claimed, and a follower's exit 2 as the pool drains still reads as the switch (`endReembed`). 16.9 → 7.8 s.
  - **Its two 3 s captures** (item 6, the ticket's `quiet: 3`) take the scaffold's 1 s. They were the quiet's gap for the old note, which the capture no longer gates since PR 1c-4b: the period's end and the next idle poll's whole read take it up either way. Only the first cost time: 5.3 → 3.3 s.

- **[9]** (long, item 8): the heartbeat run is two-thirds of itself: 400 ms rows and a 4 s lease, not 600 ms and 6 s, the 1 s heartbeat kept. Every margin in rows holds (fifteen outlast the lease, the twelve claims again past the deadline), and a 2 s process pause still misses only two of the three beats the lease covers. 9.4 → 6.3 s.

The run's end already waits for the count: none was left at the poll, and the case passes with no wait at all. So the sleep waited for nothing; the poll guards a run that stops waiting. 3.5 s.
