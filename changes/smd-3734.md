**What changed**, all in `db/test-live.ts`, by the ticket's items:

Every margin in rows holds (fifteen outlast the lease, the twelve claims again past the deadline), and a 2 s process pause still misses only two of the three beats the lease covers. 9.4 → 6.3 s.
