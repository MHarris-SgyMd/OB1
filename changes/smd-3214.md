## Changelog

The sleep scheduler, board-sync and the two claim workers come back after a host, Docker daemon or podman machine restart: each is `unless-stopped`, run by `db/supervise.ts`, which holds a refusal and tries again on a clock of minutes instead of exiting, and preflight says a held service is held, why and when it tries again; a follower refused at its start names the refusal on its heartbeat by kind, with its remedy (SMD-3214).

## FORK

The followers come back after a reboot, and a refusal holds instead of looping (SMD-3214)

**What changed.**
- **`db/supervise.ts`** (new) is the command of `sleep`, `extract`, `consolidate` and `board-sync`. It runs the follower's own command line (`--run`, as `sh -c` runs it, exec'd).
  - A follower that ends on its own (a refusal exits 2; a crash exits 1) runs again after 1, 2, 4, 8, then every 15 minutes. The count starts again after a run of 15 minutes or more.
  - The container never exits, so the runtime has nothing to restart in a loop.
  - It ends the follower's heartbeat with `restart: {in_s, code, tries}`. The row keeps the follower's own last word (`refused`, the scheduler's `slept`, `held` and `budget`), drops its waits, and stays ended, so the sleep scheduler and preflight never count a held follower as running.
  - A follower that wrote no row gets one: the claim workers' under the job their configuration names (`extractionKey`, `consolidatePassKey`).
  - Its handlers are installed first, and each SIGTERM or SIGINT goes on to the follower. Once stopped, or while waiting, it exits 0.
- **The guards:** compose's `sh -c` key guard and digits-only guards move into it (`--require-key`, `--digits`). Either refusal holds until the service is recreated, the follower never started, the row `refused` (identity or usage) with `in_s: null`.
- **`deploy/compose.yaml`:**
  - The four services are `restart: unless-stopped`, their command the supervisor.
  - Every comment that gave the `on-failure:3` reason is rewritten.
  - `jev` stays `on-failure:3`.
- **`server-portable/brain-info.ts`:**
  - `parseHeartbeats` reads `restart` on an ended row, and `refused` on every worker's row, not the scheduler's alone.
  - `restartWords` and `heartbeatState` say "held: refused (model), 3 end(s) in a row — its supervisor runs it again in 4 min".
  - Once the try is more than 15 min past with nothing stamped, they say so.
- **`server-portable/preflight.ts`:** the `workers` and `sleep` rows give a held row's fix (`heldFix`):
  - the cause: the kind's remedy, or "its log says which";
  - the next try;
  - the recreate a fix in `deploy/.env` needs.
  - Never "start it again". Long past its try, the supervisor is taken for gone, and the start and the retire are named.
- **`db/config.mjs`:** `startRefusalRemedy`'s `identity` and `usage` cover the guards' two refusals.
- **`db/connect.ts`:** `databaseUrlIfAny`, which returns a URL without exiting for want of one.
- **Check 32:** `RESTART_POLICIES` holds the four as `unless-stopped`. `supervisorGapsIn` holds that a service running a db/ follower runs it through the supervisor, its `--worker` the follower's, and its `--run` an `exec bun db/<follower>`. Seven probes cover the rule.
- **`.github/workflows/fork-checks.yml`:**
  - The workers and sleep steps run each refusal detached, as the service runs.
  - Each case must log its line, still be running, and exit 0 within 15 s of a stop. The sleep step reads `heartbeat:sleep`'s refusal and next try.
  - The two jq config asserts hold the supervisor's command and `unless-stopped`.
- **Docs:**
  - `deploy/README.md`: "After a reboot", board-sync, the workers' stopping and refusals, and the sleep service's section.
  - `db/README.md`: the heartbeat's `restart`.
  - `deploy/canary.sh`'s no-key comment.

**Why.** On 2026-10-22 a podman update restarted the dogfood Mac's podman machine. Every `unless-stopped` container came back. Both brains' `sleep` (exits 143 and 130) and stable's `board-sync` (130) did not, until another session started them by hand.

The machine's `podman-restart.service` starts `always` and `unless-stopped` containers alone, as Docker's daemon does. Its `podman stop --all` is a deliberate stop, which `on-failure` never restarts.

`on-failure:3` was chosen because a refusal exits 2 and podman restarts at once, with no backoff, never resetting the count: an unconditional restart made board-sync's refusal a crash loop. The supervisor keeps that refusal from looping, and lets the policy be `unless-stopped`.

**PR 2: the refusal named on the row.**
- **`db/extract-entities.ts`, `db/consolidate.ts`:**
  - `run()` hears the start's refusal through `onStart`. A follower of its own (`--follow`, not the scheduler's check nor a pass it drives) ends `heartbeat:<worker>:<job>` with `refused` before returning 2.
  - It goes through `db/pass-stamp.ts`'s new `endOnRefusal`, bounded at 10 s. The supervisor keeps that word, so the held row says why.
  - A refusal mid-pass is named too: the provider answering 4xx to a real call (a revoked key, spent credit) ends the follower's own row `provider`. Extract's case is held by test-live [10]; consolidate's identical line is held by a cold read alone.
  - **One cost:** two followers of one job share one row. A second follower refused at its start, beside a live one, now ends that shared row until the live one's next poll stamps it, about 15 s. Until then the sleep scheduler's followers-outside check would not see it. An ordinary end always did the same.
- **`db/sync-linear.ts`:** its refusals go through one `refuse(kind, line)`. Under `--loop` (not `--dry-run` or `--audit`), it ends `heartbeat:board-sync` with the kind, then exits 2. The kinds are:
  - `linear-key`: no `LINEAR_API_KEY`;
  - `board`: `BoardConfigError`;
  - `usage`: the interval, or the findings cap;
  - `egress`: the endpoint refused wholesale.
- **`START_REFUSALS` and `SLEEP_REFUSALS`** gain `linear-key` and `board`.
- **`startRefusalRemedy`** gets remedies for those two, and its `usage` covers board-sync's settings.
- **`connect.ts`'s `databaseUrlIfAny`** takes the `--url` flag, and test-connect's `--url` rule accepts it beside `databaseUrl`.
- **The reader:**

  - Preflight's `workers` row gives an ended row's kind and remedy before "start it again".
