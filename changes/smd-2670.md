## Changelog

Board-sync no longer moves a capture-only key's `supersedes` pointer onto another row when it re-chains a ticket's rows. A key's paste of a ticket's header that pointed at the key's own thought could leave a board-sync or write-key row superseding that thought for good, after another key took it; the pointer now stays where the key's capture wrote it, where it lapses, or is dropped and the pass says so (SMD-2670).

## FORK

Board-sync's chain never carries a capture-only key's pointer to another row (SMD-2670)

**What changed** (`db/sync-linear.ts`, no migration).
- **The read.** Every ticket row board-sync reads carries `capture_pointer`: its `supersedes` is the pointer a capture-only key's capture wrote, under the capture scope, and no update has written it since. That is 082's lapse predicate for the row's own pointer, as 097's `ob1_lapse_walk` spells it.
- **The chain.** `desiredPointers` still moves a pointer onto a thought outside the group to the chain's tail, but not a `capture_pointer` one.
  - When its own row is the tail, it stays there, ahead of any other row's outside pointer, which is then the one named as DROPPED.
  - Otherwise that row's change names it as not carried, and the pass log says it was DROPPED.
- **The undo.** When the database refuses a pointer mid-chain, `chainRows` writes every pointer back except a `capture_pointer` one. Board-sync writing it back would make it board-sync's pointer, which the lapse never clears. Its row is written back cleared, even when that row's own set landed before the refusal, and the refusal names it.

**Why.** 082 (SMD-2638) lapses a capture-only key's pointer once another party takes the thought it points at, but only on the row whose capture wrote it. When the key's header paste S joined a ticket's group pointing at the key's own thought T, board-sync moved S's pointer to the chain's tail with its own `update_thought`. The tail was a write key's row, or board-sync's own row when the ticket's text had moved. After a write key took T, T still read as superseded by that row, ranked ×0.25. Neither live brain had such a paste or such a pointer on 2026-10-22, so no repair step is needed.

**Tests.**
- `sync-linear.ts --self-check`:
  - the paste in the chain's middle, at its head, at its tail, and before or after another row's outside pointer;
  - a pointer no capture-only key wrote still moving;
  - the undo writing the key's row back cleared, whether or not its set landed;
  - the pass log.
- `test-live` [23b], on the real store and lapse:
  - the ticket's repro, with the paste in the chain's middle and at its head (the text moved). Both fail on the old code. After a write key takes T, nothing supersedes it.
  - a regression guard for the tail, where the pointer stays and the take lapses it.
