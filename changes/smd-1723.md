## Changelog

A thought can now be forgotten, not just deleted (SMD-1723, migration 139). `forget_thought(id, actor)` removes the thought and what in the database holds its text or id, in one transaction, and names what it can't reach. That covers the extraction's entity links and bare orphaned entities, the snapshot's vectors, other thoughts' `derived_from`, relation facets and expired citations, the page sections built on it, the priority statements and open items read from it, its deliverable's events, and the query log's references. Every event of it in the audit log is redacted in place, keeping who did what and when. A thought already deleted can be forgotten too, from what the log and the stores still hold. A ledger records each forgetting with the evidence, and the same text is refused on capture unless reinstated. A thought another cites is refused `CITED`, as a delete is. The REST and MCP operation follows in PR 2.

## Record

forget_thought — a thought removed from every store in the database that holds its text, its events redacted in place, and the forgetting recorded (SMD-1723)

**Why.** `delete_thought` removes the row, but the brain keeps what it said.
- **The log:** every event carries the text: a capture's content, each edit's before and after, and the tombstone's previous content and metadata.
- **Everywhere else:** the entities, the snapshot's vectors, other thoughts' `derived_from`, the facets, the page sections, the priority statements, the open items, the deliverables and the query log keep the text or the id.
- **No removal path:** `thought_audit` refuses every DELETE. Nothing could answer "is this gone" with evidence.

**What changed.** The maintainer's choices (2026-11-13):
- three PRs;
- a cited thought refused;
- the fingerprint kept, so a forgotten text is refused on its way back;
- the page sections built on it deleted.

Migration 139:
- **`forget_thought(id, actor)`,** in one transaction:
  - the supersession lock, the capture's fingerprint lock for every text it has had, then the row — so a capture of one of its texts waits for the forget and is refused after (`OB005`);
  - the fingerprints read again under the row lock, so an edit that committed meanwhile gives its text to the ledger too;
  - a thought deleted before is forgotten from what remains: its snapshot vectors and every store below (`gone: true`);
  - every bare orphan entity (no mention, edge, priority statement or merge record) deleted, on every call: which orphans were the thought's can't be told once their mentions are gone;
  - a thought with an active citation refused `CITED`; the operator detaches first, deleting it with `delete_thought(id, actor, true)`, then forgets it, its detached citations deleted;
  - every fingerprint the text had, read from the log first, less any a standing twin holds;
  - `rebuild_derived(id, 'forget', true, fingerprints)`;
  - relation facets targeting it and expired citations of it deleted;
  - its id stripped from other thoughts' `derived_from`;
  - page sections built on it (as evidence or pending evidence) deleted through `delete_page_section`, the page re-rendered; every page whose own log names it listed;
  - the query log's `result_ids` (each score with its id) and `result_items` (the other items kept) cleared of it, and its action rows on it deleted;
  - the row deleted through `delete_thought`;
  - then, the row gone, priority statements on it, on its open items, or taking a due time from either, deleted and re-projected; those items, their events and close proposals deleted, as are proposals whose evidence it is (the reason an accepted one copied onto another item's status event blanked first); its deliverable's events deleted;
  - every event of it redacted;
  - a ledger row written.
- **What it returns:**
  - each store's count;
  - `rebuild_derived`'s report;
  - `irreproducible` (thoughts derived from it) and `pages` (pages re-rendered, or whose own log names it), for a person to review;
  - `twins`;
  - `outside`, the copies beyond the brain.
- **The log's `redact` amendment,** its third beside `backfill` and `payload`:
  - **What it changes:** an event's diff becomes `ob1_audit_redacted_diff(diff, action)`. Every text is the marker `[forgotten]` and every metadata object `{"forgotten": true}`; ids, fingerprints and times are kept. The `source` it copied from the metadata is blanked. `redacted_at` is stamped, once, and every other column stays byte-equal.
  - **When:** only under the forget's own setting, and only once the thought is deleted.
  - **Why `payload` can't undo it:** a capture always gains the marker as its content, and the `payload` amendment never changes a content once set.
- **`thought_forgotten`,** the ledger: append-only, one row per forgetting (when, by whom, the fingerprints, the evidence).
  - A capture or edit to a forgotten text is refused `OB005` unless `ob1.reinstate_forgotten` is on, or a standing thought already holds that text (reinstated, or a twin).
  - `db/ingest-records.ts` counts a record whose text was forgotten as `forgotten` and goes on; `db/sync-linear.ts` lists a forgotten ticket under `forgotten` in its pass report, not as an error.
  - Each role writing thoughts reads it: the capture group gains `SELECT`, and 139 grants it to each existing role.
- **The append-only guards of 116, 117 and 134** admit the forget's deletes of the rows that hold that thought's text, once it is deleted, and nothing else: the forget's setting never names a living thought.
- **A write racing a forget** (the maintainer, review pass 2: simplify): another store's write that lands beside a running forget — a statement restated, an item updated, a close proposal, a page section, a derived thought — may stay. A second `forget_thought` of the same id sweeps it, the items an earlier forget took included (the ledger's `item_ids`), and answers `FORGOTTEN` when there is nothing left. PR 2's preflight check names a forgotten id still held. Serialising every writer against a forget is SMD-4299.

**The limit, named.**
- **Prose the forget can't reach:** prose that absorbed the text, in a derived thought, a page's earlier render in its own log, or a section once built on it and since rewritten (revisions record no evidence; the maintainer: listed, not deleted), can't be removed mechanically. Those thoughts and pages are listed for review.
- **Another thought's own history:** a thought that once held the same text and was edited away keeps it in its log.
- **Edge cases not reached:** a twin whose key a raw write left stale is not read as a twin; a raw INSERT takes no fingerprint lock, so one racing the forget is not held.
- **Kept on purpose:**
  - an entity a surviving thought still mentions keeps its name and aliases;
  - other thoughts' events keep the id they named. An id is not the text.
- **Outside the brain:** sleep dumps, Linear comments already posted, digest files and extraction dumps are named, not reached.
