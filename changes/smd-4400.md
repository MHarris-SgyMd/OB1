## Changelog

A test of re-embed's stop on one SIGINT no longer fails about one run in fourteen when the suite is run by hand: it depended on which thought the pass claimed first (SMD-4400). No behaviour changes.

## Record

test-live [9]'s "one SIGINT stops reembed's CLI" row named its first claim: run with the background slots on, it failed whenever the pool's tie put a long thought first, which one stop gives back (SMD-4400)

**Problem.** The row spawns `reembed.ts` with one worker, waits for a row in hand, sends one SIGINT and expects that row to finish: one more succeeded, exit 130. It failed in two of eleven local runs of the long shard during SMD-4352, each time with exit 130 and all 42 claims pending. The message gave the exit code and the claims, so it could not say whether a row had been in hand at all.

**What it was.** Two things together: the row in hand was a long thought, and the run had the background slots on.

- test-live [9]'s pool is 42 thoughts. Thirty-nine are one embedding call. Three are 12,389 characters, embedded in windows, several calls at once.
- With the background slots on, re-embed's first stop gives back a thought whose call is waiting for a slot (SMD-3379 PR 2b-1). A long thought's calls past the share wait behind its own first calls, so the rule gives it back: nothing written, the claim pending again. The run's summary says so, "1 set back in the queue, the embedding call in hand aborted by the pass's caller, or by its stop as it waited for a slot".
- A pool under a fresh key is made in one statement. Its dates tie, and the first claim is whichever the tie gives.
- `db/ci-parity.sh` and CI's suite jobs set `OB1_BACKGROUND_SLOTS=off`. There no call waits, and every thought finishes. The failing runs were made by hand and by a mutant harness, with the variable unset and the slots at their defaults.

So the row could not fail in CI or parity. It failed by hand when a long thought came first. Whether one stop should give back a thought that waits only on its own calls is SMD-4444.

**How it was found.**

- **Not load.** Three long shards at once all passed.
- **Not the wait giving up.** The row's wait for a row in hand has a 5 s bound. Over 270 runs of the case alone, the spawned CLI had a row in hand 102 to 278 ms after it started.
- **Six runs back to back, the wait now saying what it saw.** The third failed, with a row in hand and three calls at the stub where the others had one (caught: run-it).
- **Each thought in turn put first in the pool.** The three long ones failed and the other 39 passed, the same three twice.
- **The slots, last.** A row written for the long thought passed alone and failed in parity, twice. The first reading blamed load and lengthened its calls; the second run failed the same way with every call made at once, which is what `OB1_BACKGROUND_SLOTS=off` does (caught: run-it).

**Change.** In `db/test-live.ts` [9]; no engine changes.

- **`inHand` returns what it saw.** Whether a claim was held and a call had reached the stub, and how long it waited. It returned nothing, and gave up after 5 s without a word.
- **The two rows that expect the thought in hand to finish name it.** The in-process stop by a caller's signal, and the CLI's one SIGINT. Each makes its pool first and dates a one-call thought ahead of it (`firstInPool`). They pass with the slots on or off.
- **The CLI's two signal rows say more when they fail.** That no row was in hand, the calls at the stub at the signal and after, and the CLI's last stderr lines.
- **A row for the long thought, with a share of its own.** A long thought first, `OB1_BACKGROUND_SLOTS=embed=2` in the CLI's environment, 5 s calls, one SIGINT: two calls at the stub, exit 130, nothing written or failed, no call after the signal, and the summary's set-back line. It holds what the engine does today and cites SMD-4444, whose decision changes it.

**Verify.** Two mutants, each run against the long shard (caught: mutant).

| Mutant | Rows it fails |
|---|---|
| The first stop no longer gives back a waiting thought (`db/reembed.ts`) | The long thought's row, alone |
| A first SIGINT made a hard stop (`db/lease.ts`), the ticket's | The one-SIGINT row and the long thought's, and [9]'s `--follow` SIGINT row, where the suite stopped |

The long shard is at 387 assertions, one more than main.

**Not done from the ticket's Verify.** "The long shard passes 30 runs in a row, three at a time" was not run: it is about four hours, and the cause is decided by the first claim and the slots, not by chance under load. The rows that depended on the first claim now set it.

**Not looked at.** Extraction's and consolidation's signal rows wait the same way (`inHand` at their own lines). Whether a windowed thought of theirs can be first and be given back was not checked.
