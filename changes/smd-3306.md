## Changelog

Migration 111 records a thought's own subject: a `subject` facet, one standing per thought, keyed `ticket:`, `pr:`, `version:` or `phrase:`, with its lineage at the text's fingerprint. `record_thought_subject` writes it, refusing a text that moved since it was read. The thoughts about one subject can then be read as one work thread, in capture order (SMD-3306).

## FORK

A thought's own subject recorded — the `subject` facet, its key and its lineage, one standing per thought, so the thoughts about one subject read as one work thread (SMD-3306)

**Why.** The consolidation pass decides "evolves" pair by pair: H2O's verdict, then, since decide version 4, a 27B link step that caps a pair linked only through a third subject (SMD-3203). Three rounds of asking that step another way did not meet their bars (SMD-3299). SMD-3116 defines evolves as a later segment of the same work. That is a property of each thought's own subject, not of the pair. Asked of one thought alone, the 27B names the ticket blind raters agree on for 241 to 242 of the 245 canary thoughts whose own subject is a ticket, under the test's versions 2 and 3, where the raters agree with each other on 98.1% (SMD-3299, comment ac4f9321). A work thread is one subject's thoughts in time order. This change records the subject; the pass that finds it, the threads and the consolidation pass's use of them follow.

**What changed: migration 111.**
- **The `subject` facet kind.** `thought_facets_validate` is 102's body, with the kind check admitting `subject` and a new branch. The payload is:
  - `key`: `ticket:<ID>`, `pr:<number>`, `version:v<n>.<n>[…]` or `phrase:<lower-case words>`. `subject_key_valid` is the one rule for the validator and the writer.
  - `by`: `metadata`, `first_line` or `model`.
  - `judge_key`: the pass that found it.
  - `origin: derived`, the validator's word.

  The facet is written standing and only ever closed, as a relation is (084). One standing subject per thought is a partial unique index, and a second index probes the key.
- **`record_thought_subject(thought, key, by, judge_key, agent, fingerprint, recipe)`,** the pass's one write.
  - It is refused, answering `stale`, when the thought's fingerprint is not the one the pass read.
  - The same key, way and pass is kept, its lineage moved.
  - Anything else replaces it, the old facet closed as history.
  - A NULL key closes the standing subject.
  - A missing thought answers `gone`.
  - It takes an advisory lock per thought, and the thought FOR KEY SHARE first, as 084 does.
- **Lineage.** `subject` joins the artifact kinds: 084's CHECK and `ob1_record_derivation`, on 084's body. The facet's row names the thought at the fingerprint read, in the same transaction (`ob1:subject-lineage-with-its-artifact`). An AFTER DELETE trigger drops the row with its facet.
- **Preflight's lineage check** fails a standing subject without its row, naming it, with the remedy: close it and record it again. It counts no subject as stale: the subject pass reads the fingerprint itself until `rebuild_derived` gains an arm.
- **It refuses by name** without 016, 042, 061, 084 or 102, reading 102 by its door's sentinel. A re-apply is a no-op. No grant moves.
