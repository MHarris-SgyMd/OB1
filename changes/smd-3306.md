## Changelog

Migration 111 records a thought's own subject: a `subject` facet, one standing per thought, keyed `ticket:`, `pr:`, `version:` or `phrase:`, with its lineage at the text's fingerprint. `record_thought_subject` writes it, refusing a text that moved since it was read. The thoughts about one subject can then be read as one work thread, in capture order (SMD-3306). The extraction pass now records each thought's own subject when a rule finds it: the ticket the thought is filed under, else a session summary's header ticket when the header lists that one alone and one of the session's prompts names it. `extract-entities.ts --subjects` finds the rest: it asks the judge model, with a question of the subject's own, about a thought no rule keys, and takes up the thoughts extracted before migration 111, edited since, or keyed under an older rule or question; `--subjects rules` asks no model. The judge model reasons before it answers unless `OB1_SUBJECT_REASONING` is off. Migration 113 gives the sweep a pool: new and edited thoughts join it, a run takes only what it holds, a thought the model named nothing for is not asked again until its text or its ticket changes, `--retry-failed` asks again those that failed, and a run under another pool's key, `--subjects rules` among them, needs `--switch-key` (SMD-3306, its second to fifth PRs). `--subjects` stops, exit 3, once more than a fifth of the judge model's last 48 answers, kept across runs, gave no subject, not JSON or naming nothing, and puts the thoughts it was answered nothing for back in its pool; `--judge-checked` runs it with that alarm off, for a judge checked and found right (SMD-3306 PR 5b). A trip holds a scheduler's following runs of each judge model whose answers gave no subject until a `--judge-checked` run of that model lifts the hold; one that a trip naming its model lands under, after it began and before its lift, ends, exit 3, nothing claimed, and a provider's transient error puts the thought back where it stood, asked again after the pause (SMD-3306 PR 6a). A scheduler's run of `--subjects` reports its start and each refusal by its kind, its check reading the pool's name as the run would; its start waits out a database not answering and probes its judge model, waiting for one not answering; past its pauses it waits out the provider's outage as a claim pass does, a thought the provider errs on again right after answering a probe failed as its own, for `--retry-failed`; it says those waits through its row, and runs its pooling exempt from its `statement_timeout` while watched (SMD-3306 PR 6b-1).
