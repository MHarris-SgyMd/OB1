## Changelog

Migration 111 records a thought's own subject: a `subject` facet, one standing per thought, keyed `ticket:`, `pr:`, `version:` or `phrase:`, with its lineage at the text's fingerprint. `record_thought_subject` writes it, refusing a text that moved since it was read. The thoughts about one subject can then be read as one work thread, in capture order (SMD-3306).

The extraction pass now records each thought's own subject when a rule finds it: the ticket the thought is filed under, else a session summary's header ticket when the header lists that one alone and one of the session's prompts names it. `extract-entities.ts --subjects` finds the rest: it asks the judge model, with a question of the subject's own, about a thought no rule keys, and takes up the thoughts extracted before migration 111, edited since, or keyed under an older rule or question; `--subjects rules` asks no model. The judge model reasons before it answers unless `OB1_SUBJECT_REASONING` is off. Migration 113 gives the sweep a pool: new and edited thoughts join it, a run takes only what it holds, a thought the model named nothing for is not asked again until its text or its ticket changes, `--retry-failed` asks again those that failed, and a run under another pool's key, `--subjects rules` among them, needs `--switch-key` (SMD-3306, its second to fifth PRs).

### PR 4: the subject question's own reasoning

**What changed.** `OB1_SUBJECT_REASONING` (`embed.ts`'s `subjectReasoning`; declared in `root.ts`, forwarded in compose's api environment, documented in `deploy/.env.example`) is the judge model's reasoning for the subject question alone, read as `OB1_METADATA_REASONING` is but on when unset, and `askOwnSubject` sends it in place of extraction's. The model's pass key names it: `subject:<model>@p2r` with its reasoning on, `@p2` with it off; an effort asked for is keyed as on (`none` is off), though only the provider's default was measured, and the recipe names the setting (`reasoning`: on, off or the effort). `subjectCurrent`, and the sweep's SQL with it, takes a model's subject found with its reasoning off while it is on; turning it off takes none found with it on. The sweep's default limit a call follows the setting, 900 s with reasoning on and 300 s off, and `extract-entities.ts` without `--timeout` now leaves it to the sweep; its first line names the reasoning and the limit. A call past the limit names `--timeout`, and a provider's refusal names `OB1_SUBJECT_REASONING` and the effort, when one was sent. No migration; the question's text is unchanged.

**Why.** Verify item 1 met its bar for "the rules, then the 27B with its reasoning on": 0.945 against 0.941 on a third fresh draw (Linear 06d09434, results 18d6fd63). Round 2 had missed by 0.012 (0.918 against 0.930, e58bd13a), and a post-hoc test on its 55 session summaries put the 27B at 0.764 with its reasoning on against 0.673 off. On round 3's draw the stored subjects, found with it off, would have missed (0.933); the difference alone was 8 thoughts better and 4 worse (sign test p 0.39). `OB1_METADATA_REASONING` would turn it on for every extraction too. It costs about 8x a call: a median of 71 s against 9 s, on those 55. Of 200 calls with reasoning on, two took over 300 s (the slowest 369 s); without it, the slowest of 160 took 133 s.

**Upgrading.** On by default: an upgraded brain's next `--subjects` run asks again about every subject a model found before, now with its reasoning on, and about every thought the model has not answered yet. On the canary that is its 712 model subjects and a few others, at about 70 s each measured alone: about 14 hours, more while sleep's passes share the model; a run stopped is taken up by the next. A judge model with no reasoning mode answers as before, its subjects asked again once for nothing. `OB1_SUBJECT_REASONING=off` asks as before and keeps them, where `OB1_METADATA_REASONING` was unset; a brain that set it asked with it, and keeps asking so by setting the new knob to the same value.
