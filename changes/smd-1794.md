## Changelog

`db/extract-entities.ts`'s hard stop (a second Ctrl-C, or the second call of the stop an in-process caller is handed) now aborts the model call in hand and sends no further window or retry, as `db/consolidate.ts`'s hard stop already aborted its judge's call. Before, run() waited for the call to answer — up to `--timeout` (300 s) per window, every remaining window of a long thought included — writing nothing for it (SMD-1794).

## FORK

Extract's hard stop aborts the model call in hand — the first cut of the sleep scheduler, which must hand the model back to a live caller at once (SMD-1794)

**Why first.** SMD-1794's scheduler runs extraction and consolidation while the brain is quiet and yields on the first live call. A yield is the pass's hard stop. Consolidate's aborted the judge's call (SMD-2304 PR 3); extract's returned the leases but let the call in hand run to its answer, and the window loop go on to the thought's last window. On stable a thought takes p50 37 s, p90 121 s and p99 629 s (SMD-2424's claim durations), so a yield could leave a live capture queued behind the local model for ten minutes.

**What changed.**
- **`server-portable/entities.ts`:** `extractEntities` takes an optional `stop` signal after `windowing`. It joins each call's deadline (`AbortSignal.any`), so it aborts the request and a streamed answer's read; and no window or retry is sent once it has aborted (`throwIfAborted` before each call, so an uncalled window is not counted in `callsMade`).
- **`db/extract-entities.ts`:** an `onHardStop` controller, aborted by the hard stop and passed to `extractEntities`. A worker whose call the hard stop aborted returns at once, abandoning the thought as before (nothing written or released for it). SMD-2401 removed the controller when nothing read it.
- **Not aborted:** a `--decide` decider call in hand (SMD-2321's separate service) is waited for.

**The idle signal, measured (the ticket's step 1).** On the stable brain, 2026-09-23 to 10-07 (14 days, read-only), live events are every `query_log` row and every `thought_audit` row whose `actor_kind` is not `ingested` (board-sync's). That rule from the schema gives the same answer as a hand-kept list of background actors (board-sync, reembed, ingest-records):
- 3,379 events, bursty: the median gap is 0.3 s, the p90 19 s, the p99 2 h.

| quiet before sleep | sleeps in 14 days | share of time asleep | median sleep | p10 sleep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 s | 303 | 98% | 12.6 min | 0.9 min |
| 300 s | 222 | 93% | 16.6 min | 2.4 min |
| 900 s | 143 | 84% | 25.5 min | 3.2 min |

Counting board-sync's writes as live halves the median sleep at 300 s (6.5 min, 337 sleeps).

**Tests.**
- `test-local-provider` (+3): a stop already aborted sends no window and counts no call; a stop during the first of a long thought's windows returns in ~200 ms against a 3 s answer, with one window sent; a stop during the runaway's penalised retry aborts it (202 ms against a 3 s answer). Mutants: the stop dropped from the fetch signal fails the second (3002 ms); the checks before each call dropped fail the first (1 counted); the retry not given the stop fails the third (3003 ms).
- `test-live` [10] (+1): the hard stop against a 5 s answer returns at once (4 ms) and no call follows it. Mutant: the signal not passed to `extractEntities` fails it (4979 ms).

**Measured after.** test-live 1131/1131, test-local-provider 196/196, tsc in db and server-portable, check-fork-consistency.

**Not here.** The scheduler itself (`db/sleep.ts`: the idle signal, the fixed pass order, the yield, its heartbeat), its compose profile and preflight row, the budgets, and the re-derive pass are the next cuts of SMD-1794.
