## Changelog

`db/sleep.ts` runs entity extraction and consolidation while the brain is quiet and hands the model back on the first live call (SMD-1794). With `--follow` it sleeps whenever no read has been logged and no write audited, sync writes aside, for `--quiet` seconds (300). It then runs the extraction follower alone until its pool drains, and the consolidation follower beside it after. A live read or write hard-stops both: every lease is returned and the model call in hand is aborted. Its heartbeat, `heartbeat:sleep`, is read by preflight's `workers` row.

`db/extract-entities.ts`'s hard stop now aborts the model call in hand and sends no further window or retry, as `db/consolidate.ts`'s did. It is the second Ctrl-C, or the second call of the stop an in-process caller is handed. Before, run() waited for the call to answer — up to `--timeout` (300 s) per window, every remaining window of a long thought included — writing nothing for it.

## FORK

The sleep scheduler, `db/sleep.ts`: extraction and consolidation while the brain is quiet, yielding on the first live call — "dolphin sleep", its first cuts (SMD-1794)

**PR 1 — extract's hard stop aborts the model call in hand.**
- **Why first.** A yield is the pass's hard stop. Consolidate's aborted the judge's call (SMD-2304 PR 3). Extract's returned the leases but let the call in hand answer and the window loop go on to the thought's last window. On stable a thought takes p50 37 s, p90 121 s and p99 629 s (SMD-2424's claim durations).
- **`server-portable/entities.ts`:**
  - `extractEntities` takes an optional `stop` signal after `windowing`.
  - The signal joins each call's deadline (`AbortSignal.any`), so a stop aborts the request and a streamed answer's read.
  - No window or retry is sent once it has aborted. `throwIfAborted` runs before each call, so an uncalled window is not counted in `callsMade`.
- **`db/extract-entities.ts`:** an `onHardStop` controller, which the hard stop aborts and passes to `extractEntities`. SMD-2401 had removed it when nothing read it. A `--decide` decider call in hand is still waited for.

**The idle signal, measured (the ticket's step 1).** Stable brain, 2026-09-23 to 10-07, read-only:
- **A live event** is every `query_log` row, plus every `thought_audit` row whose `actor_kind` is not `ingested` (board-sync's kind). This rule from the schema gives the same numbers as a hand-kept list of background actors.
- **Volume:** 3,379 events, bursty — the median gap is 0.3 s, the p90 19 s, the p99 2 h.

| quiet before sleep | sleeps in 14 days | share of time asleep | median sleep | p10 sleep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 s | 303 | 98% | 12.6 min | 0.9 min |
| 300 s | 222 | 93% | 16.6 min | 2.4 min |
| 900 s | 143 | 84% | 25.5 min | 3.2 min |

Counting board-sync's writes as live halves the median sleep at 300 s (6.5 min, 337 sleeps). 300 s is the default.

**PR 2 — `db/sleep.ts`.** An engine (`run(opts)`, SMD-2304's shape, in test-engines' census) and its CLI: `--follow`, one sleep, `--dry-run`, `--quiet`, `--poll`, `--workers`.
- **Awake** — reads the logs for the newest live event, on the database's clock.
  - It reads again when the brain could first fall asleep: the event's age plus `--quiet`, at most a minute apart.
  - The read is bounded below, so a brain whose newest audit rows are all sync writes is not scanned back to its last live one.
  - Only the owner can read `query_log`; a role that cannot is told once, and then only writes wake the brain.
- **Asleep** — the extraction follower runs alone until its pool is drained (nothing pending, in flight or not yet pooled), then the consolidation follower joins it.
  - This holds the rule SMD-2424 left to an operator in `deploy/README.md` ("Start `extract` alone on a backlog").
  - Followers, not one-shot runs, so SMD-2599's outage waits apply. A one-shot pass would fail the thought in hand during an outage.
  - A pass that ends by itself (a refusal, exit 2) ends the sleep with its code.
  - One sleep (no `--follow`) stops both followers once both pools drain, and exits 0.
- **Waking** — every `--poll` seconds (5) while asleep:
  - a live event since the sleep began hard-stops both passes through their own `signal` and `PassStop`;
  - the read reaches back up to 30 s, within the quiet just read: an audit row is dated at its transaction's start, so a capture begun before the sleep and committed after it is dated before it;
  - the scheduler's own first stop is the passes' first, and a second is their hard stop.
- **The passes write no audit row** (extraction writes the entity tables, consolidation proposals), so the scheduler's own work cannot wake it. test-live holds that.
- **Heartbeat** — `heartbeat:sleep`, `--follow` only.
  - The engines gained a `stamper` option, so their followers stamp through the scheduler's row and a wake ends no follower row for preflight to warn about.
  - `pass-stamp.ts` gained `alive()`: a re-stamp with no pass counted. It is the beat while awake and a pass's word mid-sleep.
  - `STAMPING_WORKERS`, `brain-info.ts`'s reader (no job, as board-sync) and `restartCommand` (from a checkout until a compose service) know `sleep`.
- **Followers outside it** (the `workers` profile) do not yield. The start and `--dry-run` name any whose heartbeat is fresh.
