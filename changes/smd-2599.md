## Changelog

A `--follow` run of `db/extract-entities.ts` or `db/consolidate.ts` now outlasts its database or its provider going away. It says so once, checks again after 5 s, doubling to 5 min, and resumes when the database or the model answers. A thought it holds goes back to the pool rather than being recorded failed. Before this fix, a Postgres restart made it exit 1, a provider outage failed every thought it claimed, and a model being pulled made it exit 2 (SMD-2599).

## FORK

A `--follow` worker outlasts a database or provider outage — it waits and resumes, where it exited or failed what it held (SMD-2599)

**What changed — the database (PR 1).**
- **`worker-bootstrap.ts`** has three new pieces, shared by both workers:
  - `databaseUnavailable(e)` says whether an error means the database is not answering. These are Bun's `ERR_POSTGRES_CONNECTION_*`, idle and lifetime timeouts, and the server's SQLSTATE class 08, 57P01–57P03 and 53300.
  - `outageWait` is the schedule: 5 s, doubling, at most 5 min.
  - `waitOut` sleeps, then checks, until the check answers or a stop wakes it. A check that fails for another reason is thrown.
- **Each worker's `followedPass()`** catches a database-unavailable error from a pass. It says so once, waits until a `SELECT 1` answers, and polls on. Bun's pool reconnects by itself (measured on Bun 1.4), so there is no reconnect code.
- **A worker hit mid-thought** records nothing for the thought.
- **Leases returned when the database is back.** A worker's leases that could not be returned as it ended are returned at the start of the next pass, not after `--ttl` (900 s).
- **Any other database error** (a syntax error, a missing function, a password refused) still ends the run.

**What changed — the provider (PR 2).**
- **What is an outage**, under `--follow` only:
  - a transient error (429, 5xx, a lost connection) still failing after the 5/15/45 s pauses;
  - `modelMissing`: a 404 whose body names the model (Ollama's `model "x" not found`, OpenAI's and vLLM's `does not exist` or `model_not_found`). A bare 404 is a wrong base URL and stays fatal;
  - a timeout after which a probe gets no answer either. That probe has the call's whole `--timeout`, since a local model busy with another worker's call may need it, and a stop ends it. A timeout during another worker's outage joins it unprobed. Consolidate checks at the pair, by throwing `ProviderDown`.
- **What happens.** The thought in hand goes back to the pool unrecorded, and the other workers stop after theirs. The follower then probes on `waitOut`'s schedule until the model answers. A follower's `--limit` counts the thoughts it finishes, so a returned thought is not counted twice.
- **`probeChat`** is a one-token chat call to the pass's model, with a fixed prompt and nothing of the brain's in it. It is not preflight's GET `/models`: Ollama answers that while its chat endpoint answers 503 or the model is still being pulled.
- **`ProviderOutage`** keeps the rule from the ticket. A thought that draws a provider error again within 15 min of a probe answering is its own (a document that crashes the server), so it is recorded failed, "provider error again right after the provider answered a probe".
- **Where the line falls.** A follower probes once before it writes anything, the worker key's registration included:
  - a model the provider does not serve (extract's `OB1_EXTRACT_ESCALATE_MODEL` too, asked at start only), a 401/402/403 or a bare 404 is a refusal at start, exit 2;
  - an unreachable provider is waited for;
  - once the model has answered, its going missing (Ollama pulling it again) is an outage;
  - any other 4xx stays fatal.
- **A run without `--follow`** keeps every exit and failure it had: the pauses, then the thought recorded failed and the worker stopped.

**Why.** SMD-2424's review (run-it, scratch stack `ob1-2424r1`) found three events a follower did not survive:
- A `podman restart` of Postgres crashed a follower in 4 of 9 tries. With Postgres stopped for 30 s, both followers spent `restart: on-failure:3` in about 1 s and stayed exited. The cause: a database error outside a worker's own try (`counts()`, consolidate's `enqueue_thoughts`) rejected `run()`.
- A 4-minute chat 503 turned three captures into three rows failed for good. A failed extraction has no entities, so 029/079 never pair it.
- Ollama's 404 while a model was pulled exited extract 2 four times within 2 s.

**Measured after.** Against a real Postgres container (not the relay), both followers stayed up through five `podman restart`s and a 60 s stop, and exited 0 on SIGINT. Of the 15 thoughts captured, 15 were extracted and 13 judged, with none failed. The same CLI followers (two workers each) then met a stub provider that answered 503 for 5 minutes and then 404 for the model for 2 minutes, with two captures a minute arriving. Both stayed up, and all 13 thoughts were extracted and judged once the provider was back, with no failed row. The resume came at the next probe on the 5-minute cap, about 4.5 minutes after the provider returned. Consolidate met no outage itself here, since nothing was extracted during it; [16] holds its provider path.

**Not taken.**
- **A per-thought attempt count** in place of the "again right after a probe" rule. `attempt_count` is the lease's, and is given back when a lease is returned.
- **Probing the escalation model during an outage.** It would load the larger model on every probe; it is asked at start only.
- **One shared follower-outage helper** for both workers' copies. That is a pure move, left to its own PR.

**Not here.**
- A follower that starts while the database is down still exits; compose's `depends_on` covers it.
- SMD-2261's heartbeat should say "waiting for the database" or "waiting for the provider", so preflight can tell a waiting follower from a dead one.
