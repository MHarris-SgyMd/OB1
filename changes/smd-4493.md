## Changelog

What ends a listen stream is measured for a client that is gone, in `docs/mcp-listen-streams.md` and `deploy/README.md`, and decided for a client, a key and a grant that is gone, in the first: a vanished and a frozen client accepted, and three things to build (SMD-4493). The first of the three begins with a measurement of the real client, which is in `docs/mcp-client-refresh.md`. For that first one the REST core gains a route, `POST /v1/callers/standing`, by which the MCP server will ask whether the callers of its streams still stand, and `whoami` gives a caller's `digest`; no server holds a stream yet, and none is ended.

**Why.** A listen stream keeps its place under the server's bound of 256 and a caller's of 32 until its connection ends. SMD-4458's third review found a frozen client holding its places for 680 s and a revoked key its 32, and SMD-4459's a relay that hid a client's leaving, its caller at its bound in 22 s. The maintainer set this ticket to come before SMD-4347 turns streams on, and decided on the measurements below, 2026-11-23: a vanished client and a frozen one are accepted; a revoked caller's streams are ended; an operator's order ends one caller's streams; and the streams of a grant the operator revokes, or that is ended for a refresh token used twice, are ended. The last three are this ticket's second, third and fourth PRs. SMD-4347's PR 2b waits on this ticket's first three PRs. The second begins with a gate, what the real client does when the server ends one stream: a client that did not open its stream again would be left without one by each of the three things to build. The result is below, and what the maintainer settled on it, 2026-11-24, is on the design page. With PR 2b's route the change is a `minor` one: a new REST route is, by the maintainer's word of 2026-11-26, which `MAINTAINING.md` now has. They kept, the same day, the drop of the REST core's remembered answer for a digest read as revoked.

- In PR 2b: a row of the route under a real registry that cannot be read (there the forwarder's own lookup answers first, `BUSY`; a stand-in shows `STORE_UNAVAILABLE`); the cap's own leaving of the pooled connection as it found it; and a deadline on a forwarder's body that never ends.
