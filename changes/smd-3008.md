## Changelog

A capture made during a sleep — a sync write, which does not wake the brain — is no longer stuck behind a thought whose window outlasts every sleep: the sleep scheduler dates the stopped thoughts ahead of the sleep's own captures, and restarts extraction when one lands while such a thought is in hand, so the capture is extracted and consolidation joins past the stalled thought in the same sleep. While consolidation waits for a judge that does not answer, extraction takes the newer work instead of nothing, the stalled thought still waiting for consolidation (SMD-3008).

## FORK

A capture made during a sleep goes ahead of the stalled thought — dated after it, it waited out the window in hand, and with one worker held the join back for the sleep — and a judge that does not answer no longer leaves extraction idle (SMD-3008)

**What changed** (PR 1).
- **`db/sleep.ts`, the stopped thoughts dated forward:** as each sleep begins they are dated 30 days ahead (`FORWARD_DAYS`), not now, still the least stalled first. A capture made during the sleep is enqueued at its own `now()` by 016's trigger, so it goes ahead of them. `claim_thoughts` orders by `enqueued_at` and reads no clock, so the date is an order, not a delay. A wake's tail move dates them now again, and the next sleep forward.
- **A restart for work behind a stalled thought in hand:**
  - Before consolidation joins, two polls in a row that read extraction with only stalled thoughts in flight and newer work behind them (a row pending that is not stalled, or a thought not yet in the pool) stop extraction hard. The stalled thought(s) are moved behind that work and extraction starts again.
  - With the forward date this is the backstop: for a capture that lands between the stalled thought's claim and the next poll, a thought not yet in the pool, or a stalled thought reclaimed from a lapsed lease with an old date.
  - At most once a minute (`RESTART_EVERY_MS`), and not again once the move fails, since such a thought would be claimed first again.
  - The stop counts on the thought as any hard stop does: a stall only past the floor.
  - The pool read gains `stalledPending`. The join's set-aside and the restart share one helper.
- **Known limits:**
  - Once consolidation has joined, a stalled thought in hand keeps its turn, and a capture made then waits for the next sleep, where it goes first.
  - A thought still gaining windows, or a stall or two short of the join, is not stopped for a capture, which waits for its window.

**What changed** (PR 2).
- **`db/sleep.ts`, extraction let out while the judge waits:**
  - Set aside past a stalled thought, extraction got nothing until consolidation's pool drained, which it cannot while consolidation waits for its provider.
  - While consolidation waits, extraction is let out for the newer work (a pending row that is not stalled) at most once a minute (the restart's bound), and set aside again once it has only stalled thoughts in hand or nothing left to do.
  - The stalled thoughts still wait for consolidation, so the judge, on the same chat endpoint, does not queue behind their window when it answers. Claimed after the newer work by its place, a stalled thought is set aside at the next poll, its call cut within a poll.
  - If the judge answers and consolidation drains while extraction is out, extraction stays out, as after any drain.
  - The wait read is either the start's (`startWait`) or a mid-pass one. The poll reads the claims only, skipping the scan for thoughts not yet in the pool.
- **`db/pass-stamp.ts` and `db/consolidate.ts`:** `PassStamper` gains `providerWait`, a pass's word that it waits mid-pass for a provider that stopped answering, past its workers' pauses. consolidate.ts says it around `waitForProvider`, and the scheduler keeps it off the row, since the mid-pass `waiting` word is SMD-2905's.
- **The cases this covers:** the judge model failing while the extraction model serves (both are on the one chat endpoint), such as a larger judge that will not load, or a gateway routing by model.
- **A judge answering 5xx** is waited for only past the pauses (5, 15 and 45 s), a minute and more. A start wait or a missing model is waited for at once.

**Why.**
- SMD-2694 moved the stopped thoughts behind new captures at each sleep's start, but dated them then. A capture made later in the sleep queued behind them, and nothing wakes the brain for a sync write.
- With one worker the pool then read {the stalled thought in hand, the capture pending}: not all stalled, so consolidation did not join past it either, and both waited out the window or the wake.
- Measured on the stable brain, read-only, over the 14 days to 2026-10-15 (sleeps reconstructed from its logs at a 300 s quiet): 99 of 149 sleeps had a sync write inside them that enqueues extraction (a capture, or an edit of the text), about 4.8 per such sleep.

**Held.**
- test-live [38b], a planted stalled thought (an 8 s window), a pair to judge, and two notes a sync writes during one sleep with no wake:
  - Both are written as the pair's first call begins. The first is extracted ahead of the stalled thought.
  - The second is dated just behind it, so it is pending beside the thought in hand at every poll: it restarts extraction. The call in hand is cut short, the note is extracted next, the stalled thought is taken again, and consolidation joins past it in the same sleep, the thought still pending, polling at its pace.
  - The restart's stop is counted, with no stall.
- Measured on main b4b5b1c3: test-live 1332 (1329 before), test-cli 432, test-connect 861.
- Mutants, killed:
  - the forward date dropped (the first note went behind the stalled thought);
  - the restart dropped (the 8 s window ran out, and the join never came that sleep);
  - the restart's move dropped (the stalled thought, dated ahead of the second note, was taken first again);
  - the set-aside's re-armed wait dropped (the poll spun from the restart to the join);
  - newer work read from the pool's unpooled count alone;
  - the in-hand clause dropped (held by the older case of a capture in every awake period);
  - the move's order reversed (held by the older order case).
- Survived:
  - the second poll before a restart (one poll restarts as well here; it is there for a second worker caught between its claims);
  - no restart once the move fails (nothing here makes the move fail);
  - the once-a-minute bound (no third note lands here);
  - the unpooled term (every note here is pooled by the trigger);
  - the restart kept out once consolidation joins (a --follow sleep reads no pool then; only a one-sleep run reaches it);
  - pending rows counted as newer work whether stalled or not. This one is equivalent: with every row in hand stalled, the only state where the two counts differ (every pending row stalled, nothing unpooled) is the join's, which is taken first.

**Held** (PR 2).
- test-live [38b], a planted stalled thought, a pair to judge, and the judge on its own model: once with its probe answering 500 from the start (consolidation waiting at its start), once with its judgements answering "not found" from the first (a missing model, which a follower waits out mid-pass at once).
  - Each time a note a sync writes while the judge is down is extracted before the judge answers, and extraction is set aside again.
  - The stalled thought, claimed after the note, has its call cut within a poll, and none begins again until the judge answers.
  - Consolidation then judges, drains, and extraction resumes.
- Measured on main b4b5b1c3 with PR 1: test-live 1336.
- Mutants, each killed:
  - the let-out dropped;
  - the set-aside on reaching the stalled thought dropped;
  - the start's wait not read (the start case);
  - consolidate.ts's `providerWait` not said (the mid-pass case).
