## Changelog

**Breaking:** stable's proxy routes no tier. Its `/canary/mcp` and `/working/mcp` routers, deprecated in the release before, and their services are gone, and every path under either prefix is its bodiless 404 until the legacy route closes; a client still given either URL moves to the tier itself, a canary's own port or the three-brain stack's route. `db/bench-live.ts` takes its endpoint and key from the canary's connector (SMD-3101).

## FORK

Stable routes no tier: its canary and working routes removed at v2.0.0, a release after their deprecation (SMD-3101)

**What changed.** SMD-2874 PR 3:
- `deploy/compose.yaml`: the route table loses the `canary` and `working` routers, their services, `tier-absent` and SMD-3057's `tier-deprecated`; `tier-off` (priority 25, `not-served`) answers every path under `/canary` and `/working` with the bodiless 404, kept until SMD-2532 removes the legacy route. The table's comment, the backends' mesh-name note, the proxy's networks and DNS notes follow.
- `scripts/check-fork-consistency.ts`: `ROUTE_TIER_OFF` is the tier-off router alone, which check 28's `PROXY_ROUTE_TABLE` takes, with no `tier-absent` or `tier-deprecated` and a `server` service alone; check 27's `TIER_ROUTE_TABLE` keeps `ROUTE_TIER_PATHS` (the tier routers and tier-off) for `compose.tiers.yaml`, whose tiers are its own and whose future is SMD-2878's.
- CI: "The tier routes reach a tier on the mesh…" becomes "Stable routes no tier, and nothing under a tier's prefix reaches stable". A keyed `tools/list`, as a client sends it, to each tier-off path, `/canary/mcp` and `/working/mcp` among them, and a GET of `/canary/mcp/health`, each get the bodiless 404 with no `Deprecation` and no deprecation `Link`. A stand-in under each tier's old mesh name, and under `server` as a tier's container once was, is reached by none of them, and stable's `/mcp/health` still answers `ok` while `server` names more than one container on the mesh. One container polls both stand-ins, each try bounded, and names each one's last failure, with their logs, if they never both answer. The Host, status-passthrough and deprecation checks, which held the routes to a tier, go.
- `db/bench-live.ts`: its default was stable's `/canary/mcp` with stable's key, which the canary refuses since SMD-2874 PR 2; it now reads the url and the key from one `~/.claude.json` entry, `open-brain-canary` by default: its `x-brain-key`, else the `?key=` on its url, as `deploy/canary.sh` reads a connector's. `--mcp` names another endpoint and needs `--key-from` beside it, as another brain refuses the canary's key; the self-check holds the entry's reading.
- `deploy/README.md`'s route table, its OAuth smoke paragraph (a tier at `/canary/mcp` is the three-brain stack's now) and its n8n row, `docs/operator-surface-tiers.md`'s row 77, `db/README.md`, the ADRs' as-built notes and the session-capture hook's README (its tier warning is for a stack before v2.0.0) follow.

**Why.** Since SMD-2874 a canary is a brain of its own on its own port, with keys of its own, so stable's routes serve nothing `deploy/canary.sh` stands up. CHANGELOG 1.6.0 shipped them, and FORK.md's MAJOR rule lets a URL a configured client uses go only after a release has said so on every answer: SMD-3057 did (the maintainer's choice, 2026-10-16), and this removes them at v2.0.0, beside SMD-2532's closing of the legacy route. It merges only after the release carrying SMD-3057 is cut, and no sooner than two weeks after it, as that release's changelog says. It has a ticket and a fragment of its own because that cut numbers and deletes `changes/smd-2874.md`, PRs 1–2's record.
