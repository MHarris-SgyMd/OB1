## Changelog

Preflight warns when the HNSW indexes outgrow `shared_buffers` (SMD-1499), naming the size and `ALTER SYSTEM SET shared_buffers`; at ten million rows, `shared_buffers` sized to the indexes gave about three times the throughput at ten connections. A second row, `filter bitmap memory`, reports what a filter's bitmap needs against `work_mem` as information only, until `match_thoughts`' plan mode is settled.

## FORK

The server sized for the table — preflight compares the HNSW indexes with shared_buffers and reports the filter bitmaps against work_mem, and at ten million rows a sized shared_buffers triples ten-connection throughput (SMD-1499)

**What changed.**
- **`db/config.mjs`:** `memorySizing`, the rule as a pure function of the numbers preflight reads; `memoryRows`, the two rows' wording from its result; `BITMAP_BYTES_PER_PAGE` (64) and `BITMAP_PAGE_SHARE_GATED` (1 − 1/e).
  - *Resident:* the valid HNSW indexes against `shared_buffers`, the size to set rounded up to 64 MB.
  - *Bitmap:* PostgreSQL 16's `tidbitmap.c` sizes an exact page entry at 48 bytes, and `tbm_calculate_entries` divides `work_mem` by that plus two pointers. Past it a bitmap goes lossy and rechecks whole pages.
    - On a heap of 8,192 pages or more, 037's gate (074) sends a filter to the HNSW walk when its 8-page sample, drawn again every call, holds 8 hits on 3 pages and puts it at ten times `v_exact`. On a heap of 10 × `v_exact` pages or more a filter at about one match a page takes the GIN route on about half its calls; on a smaller gated heap, or at a larger match count, the third test raises the boundary. A filter at one match a page touches 1 − 1/e of the pages, so that is a floor. Below the gate every filter takes the GIN route.
    - On `match_thoughts`' custom plans the GIN-routed bitmap is the routing count's, under `LIMIT v_exact + 1`, so a lossy one rechecks pages only until it has its rows: little. The bitmap that costs when lossy is a generic-plan GIN walk's, up to the whole heap.
- **`server-portable/preflight.ts`:**
  - `vector index memory`, a warning since a managed platform may not let the operator change the setting. It counts valid HNSW indexes by access method (a valid staging index built before 039 adopts it is counted too). Its remedy: `ALTER SYSTEM SET shared_buffers` and a restart of postgres, where the host has the memory, and how to take the line out of `postgresql.auto.conf` if postgres then will not start.
  - `filter bitmap memory`, **information only**: an ok row with no remedy (a skip where `thoughts` doesn't resolve; on a failed read both rows warn with the error and the reads they need). Under 037's gate it gives the whole heap's bitmap against `work_mem`; above it, the gate-boundary filter's (a floor) and, for a generic-plan walk, the whole heap's, each with its verdict. It says a lossy routing count costs little, and names SMD-1464: `match_thoughts`' default walk is static SQL that plpgsql can move to a cached generic plan after five executions on a connection, and a larger `work_mem` moved those plans both ways (below).
  - A `thoughts` that doesn't resolve is a skip; each row prints once on every path; both are in `DIRECT_CHECKS` for the PostgREST path.
- **`db/with-postgres.sh`:** `OB1_PG_ARGS`, `-c name=value` pairs only, each checked.
- **Docs:** `deploy/.env.example` (the rule, the statement, the way back), `deploy/README.md`, `server-portable/README.md`. The older walk-memory notes in `.env.example` and `compose.yaml` now count both pools (server and api, twenty at the default) and the measured use.

**Why.** SMD-1018 measured the 10M bench at the container's defaults (128 MB `shared_buffers`, 4 MB `work_mem`), and SMD-1500 found ten connections capped at about 4× one connection's throughput with the database on half its CPUs (SMD-2750). Nothing derived a server size from the table, and preflight said nothing. On the stable brain today (1,764 thoughts, 6.5 MB of HNSW, 373 heap pages) both rows are ok.

Read together:
- **`shared_buffers` is the lever.** The page cache held the indexes in every run. Moving them into `shared_buffers` took ten connections from about 5× one connection's throughput to 7–8×, ran the database CPU-bound (7.1 of 8), and nearly tripled the broad path's QPS. So SMD-1500's ceiling was waits inside the server, not the client or the port forward (SMD-2750). The page cache alone gave about a third of the throughput.
- **The exact branch's cost per row** at 0.1% (10,231 rows) went from 21 µs (warm defaults) to 11–14 µs, towards the ticket's 6–8 µs at 1M but not to it.
- **`work_mem` at 31MB moved generic plans both ways.**
  - In section C the 2,000-row walk's chunk side took the HNSW index (33 ms with `shared_buffers` alone → 17.4 s), and section D's forced generic walk followed (16 ms → 1.4 s). The 50% generic cell went 29.9 → 14.9 s, noisy, and the bench prints no lossy counts.
  - `match_thoughts`' default walk is static plpgsql SQL; only the `min_trust` walk runs through `EXECUTE` (074). It did not take a generic plan here: the 2,000-row tier stayed at 47 ms in section B in the cold-defaults and `work_mem` runs, and section F's 50% slot at a 29 ms p50 under ten connections. That is plpgsql's cost choice, not a guarantee, which is why the bitmap row recommends nothing until SMD-1464.
- **What a larger `work_mem` costs is memory.** A direct run on this corpus, ten connections on 50% filters for 20 s each: 51 MiB of growth at 4MB, 260 MiB at 31MB ×8, 257 MiB at 31MB ×1.
  - The 50% walk visits about 80 tuples, and 014's tuple cap (about 21 MB a node here) binds before 31MB × 1. So the growth is from elsewhere in the plan, and these runs say nothing about the multiplier on walks that run long.
  - The bench's own broad-10 runs grew 4–5 MiB at 4MB; only each run's deltas are compared.
- **The band tier's latency does not follow `work_mem`.** A direct run of 30 queries: 507 ms at 4MB, 522 ms at 31MB ×8 and 527 ms at 31MB ×1, every answer identical.

**Not taken.**
- The ticket's rule was `shared_buffers` ≥ the indexes **plus the hot heap**; the warning compares the indexes alone, and the remedy says "more for the hot heap if there is room".
- The ticket's `work_mem` check (a 50% filter's bitmap) became an information row, the maintainer's call after review pass 2: on custom plans the GIN-routed bitmap is a capped routing count, and the costly one is a generic-plan walk's (SMD-1464).
- "On a sized server it says nothing": preflight prints ok rows.
- "The 11.6 s generic plan loses its lossy blocks": lossy counts were not measured, and today's 50% generic cell is 15–58 s across runs.
- "test-live covers the check with the default and a SET-lowered pool": test-preflight covers the `work_mem` row both ways. The `shared_buffers` warning runs end to end nowhere, since a pool cannot be lowered for one database; test-schema holds its arithmetic and its wording (`memoryRows`).
- The bench ran at `work_mem` 31MB, not the ticket's 64MB. Managed platforms: the remedy names the parameter group, no more.
- Compose knobs for the two settings: `postgres -c` outranks `ALTER SYSTEM` and `postgresql.conf`, so a default there would reset an operator's tuning on upgrade.
- Lowering `hnsw.scan_mem_multiplier` alongside a larger `work_mem`: on this corpus it changed neither memory nor latency, and these walks never reached it.
- A container memory limit read by preflight: the cgroup is readable only by a superuser.
