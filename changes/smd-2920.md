## Changelog

The `ob-graph` and `work-operating-model-activation` recipes retire, with the `work-operating-model` skill and the `bring-your-own-context` recipe, which call or deploy that server. Neither server was used on a dogfood brain. The brain's extracted graph replaces ob-graph's hand-curated one, and a plugin port is the path back for the operating-model workflow (SMD-2920).

## FORK

Two standalone MCP servers retire: `recipes/ob-graph` and `recipes/work-operating-model-activation`, the latter with the skill and recipe that depend on it (SMD-2920)

**What changed.**
- **Deleted:** `recipes/ob-graph/`, `recipes/work-operating-model-activation/`, `skills/work-operating-model/` and `recipes/bring-your-own-context/`. Also `recipes/_shared/` (its `auth.ts` and `sse.ts` copies), which only those two servers imported.
- **Grants and schemas:** the `recipes` grant group loses `graph_nodes`, `graph_edges` and the five `operating_model_*` tables (`db/config.mjs`, the `db/README.md` table). `CONTRIB_SCHEMA_FILES` loses the two schema files. test-schema's grant cell now counts 46 objects, 31 tables and 13 recipe tables, and its graph-function assertion goes. Its four-verb DELETE probe moves to `life_engine_habits`.
- **The vendored-server suites:**
  - `extensions/test-auth.ts` drops both servers' rows. Its mid-upload case runs at family-calendar's route, and its `RPC_READS` loses the two graph traversals.
  - `extensions/test-tools.ts` drives forty-five tools on seven servers.
  - The copy lists and `extensions/package.json`'s `sync-auth` and `sync-sse` no longer name `recipes/_shared`. The `auth.ts` and `sse.ts` copies stay byte for byte.
- **The checker:** check 24's `smoke-graph-rpcs.mjs` entry goes, with the recipe.
- **Records:**
  - `docs/vendored-disposition.md`: the Servers table gains the three decisions (two retired, meal-planning's `shared-server.ts` not ported); the recipes' rows and the rollup show the retirements.
  - `docs/mcp-sdk-v2-migration.md`: stage 2 is done.
  - The README tables, the GPT context doc and the prose that compared other things to ob-graph (entity-extraction, entity-wiki, lint-sweep) drop them.

**Why** (decided 2026-10-14). Under SMD-2308 a contribution that adds operations to the brain is a plugin, not a server, and these two fell outside SMD-2311's and SMD-2690's ports.
- **ob-graph** was a second graph, curated by hand, beside the brain's extracted one. It was written outside the event log, so it couldn't be replayed or rebuilt. A relationship you state is a thought, and extraction makes the edge; its traversal reads are SMD-2922's.
- **The skill and Bring Your Own Context** depend on the operating-model server: the skill calls four of its tools, and BYOC's step 4 deploys it. So the three retire together.

None of their tables existed on stable, and no thought came from them. This also removes 2 of the 26 files that import `compat/supabase-sql` (SMD-3216).
