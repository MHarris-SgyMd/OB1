## Changelog

A renewal that revives a lapsed work claim now says so: `renew_claim`, and `claim_work` with a token, answer when the claim had lapsed and name any claim made on its work meanwhile. The session hook marks such a renewal for the agent, and AGENTS.md's rule drops its own check. A plain release's refusal now says to ask the user before claiming again, and the own-key words and `list_claims`' description no longer send an agent to a lost-token path it doesn't need (SMD-3654).

## FORK

Claim replies that lead an agent right — a revived lapsed claim names what was claimed meanwhile, a release asks before a re-claim (SMD-3654)

**What changed.**
- **The lapse, reported.** `renew_claim`, and `claim_work` with a token, read the claim's lease by its token before 115's writer runs. 115 answers with the new lease, so the old one is read first (`claimLease`).
  - If the renewal revived a lapsed claim, the value carries `lapsed: { at, claimedSince }`. The claims are those made on overlapping work after the lapse, read by 115's own piece key (`claimsMadeSince`): the same piece, or either the whole ticket.
  - Each claim made since carries its id, work, holder, `ownKey`, times and `state`, never its label or reason. Those are another key's words.
  - `claimedSince` is null when it couldn't be read; the renewal stands either way.
  - The words add: "It had lapsed at T; no other claim was made on its work meanwhile." Or: "…and since then another claim was made on its work: … Another agent may have built part of the work: stop, and tell the user before carrying on." Or: "…the claims made on its work since could not be read: check list_claims with include_ended."
  - A live claim's renewal is unchanged. No migration: two reads beside 115's writers, the token a bind parameter matched by its hash, the server role's existing SELECT.
- **A plain release** refusing a renewal says "ask the user before claiming it again: a release may be an operator's, made on purpose", where it said "claim it again".
- **The own-key words** name this session's own claim on overlapping work, its id in the worktree's `.work-claims/` (work under it, or release it and claim the piece), before another session and a lost token.
- **The descriptions:** `list_claims` says a session finds its own claims by its worktree's token files, and that `holder` lists every session of a key. `renew_claim` says the reply reports a lapse. The OpenAPI document and the rest-client are regenerated.
- **The session hook** (recipe 1.7.1) marks a renewal that reports claims made while the claim had lapsed, or couldn't read them, with the same `<id>.blocked.json` as a covered claim. That covers its own renewal, and the agent's own when the claims hook reads it. It records the renewal, and the log says "renewed — but it had lapsed …". The lapse check it makes before reviving a claim it knows lapsed stays, for brains from before this change.
- **The rule** (AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md): a renewal reply naming claims made while the claim had lapsed is handled as `WORK_CLAIMED`. Listing the claims before renewing stays only for brains from before this change.

**Why.** SMD-3602's review passes found that 115 revives a lapsed claim nothing covers without a word, so an agent renewing after a long absence could carry on over work another agent built while the claim was free. The client could only patch around it: a marker for one window, and a check the agent had to remember. Three reply texts also contradicted the rule they serve. The maintainer kept SMD-3602 client-only and raised this to Medium.

**Held.**
- `test-rest-sql.ts` `[10]`, against Postgres:
  - a live renewal carries no lapse;
  - a lapsed one nothing touched reports none;
  - one renewed after another key claimed and released the whole ticket names that claim, with holder, state and no reason;
  - neither a claim on another piece nor one made before the lapse is counted;
  - `claim_work` with the token reports the same;
  - a released claim's renewal says to ask the user.
- `test-server.ts` pins the three lapse sentences and the changed refusals.
- `test-session-capture.mjs` `[13]` marks a revival with claims since (or unread), its own or the agent's, and leaves one with none unmarked.
- Ten mutants of the server change through `test-rest-sql`, each killed:
  - no lapse reported;
  - the lease not read first;
  - a live renewal reported as a lapse;
  - every claim overlapping;
  - claims from before the lapse counted;
  - `claim_work` silent;
  - the words silent;
  - the old release words;
  - the old own-key words;
  - a since-claim's reason leaking.
- Four mutants of the hook's marking through `test-session-capture.mjs`, each killed: its own renewal ignoring a reported lapse, an unread list not marked, the agent's reported lapse not marked, and a lapse with nothing since marked.

**Not taken.**
- **A migration changing 115's writer to report the old lease.** Two reads in the core say the same, and leave 115, which every brain runs, untouched.
- **Refusing to revive a lapsed claim when claims were made meanwhile.** The renewal still stands: the claim is the agent's, and the reply tells it to stop. A refusal would leave a claim held by nobody, while unmerged work may still exist.
