## Changelog

The slot broker reads a model server's loaded models (`ps` in its registry, Ollama's `/api/ps`): a background call for a model that is not loaded waits its turn, and the server's other background calls pause while it loads, so no load waits behind a model that background keeps busy (SMD-3379 PR 3c-1). `db/bench-brains.ts` (new) measures two brains on one model server: it loads a backlog of generated notes into each, reads what each one's sleep held of the slots from its heartbeat, and deletes the notes after; `db/bench-live.ts` takes `--key-file`, for a brain no connector names, and neither harness's refusal says a path, a file's text or a parser's words (SMD-3379 PR 3b-3b). Measured on the canary and a scratch brain: one backlogged brain takes the slot broker's whole background room, and model residency, not slots, stalled live calls.

## Record

### PR 3c-1: a model that is not loaded waits its turn

**What changed.** The maintainer's cut of PR 3c (2026-11-18): 3c-1 the rule, 3c-2 its measurement on this Mac. A server's registry entry takes `ps`, where the server lists the models it has loaded (Ollama's `/api/ps`), for a `per_model` server alone, and `stay_s` with it. `deploy/federated/broker.ts` reads the list every 3 s, and every second while a load is under way, a model's name read as the list writes it (lower-cased, `:latest` where no tag is said). The rule is background's alone; a live call is granted at once, as before:

- **A listed model's ask** goes by the room and the turn, as before.
- **An ask for a model not listed** is taken at once where none is listed. Else it waits out `stay_s` (60 unset) from the end of the server's last background load, the list naming its model, then goes once the listed models have had no background call for 5 s and no live call for 120 s, or once it has waited its own `max_wait_s` (60 s unset) and no live call runs there at that moment.
- **The drain.** Then the server's other models are granted no background, the ask is granted once none of theirs runs, and they stay held until the list names its model, or its calls end and the list is next read. One load at a time a server.
- **A live call to a model not listed** holds the other models' background the same way from its grant, and comes before a background load. It does not begin the stay.
- **Two bounds of 120 s.** A drain not done by then is given up, the others going on, and none begun for 120 s more: no load is granted beside background still running. A load holds the others that long; past it the model is left out of the rule for 120 s, then held to again while it lasts, turn about, and no drain is begun while it is left out.
- **A model whose calls end with the list never naming it** is judged by the list at each call's grant and at the read after its end, where no other load was under way at the grant. A model gone and none come but ones asked for by name: it put a model out and did not load, so its next load waits 60 s more each time from the first, ten at most, none of its asks is granted meanwhile, such a load begins no stay, and what it put out is taken for due. Else, three reads running: it is left out of the rule until it is listed, or until a call of its puts a model out (a name listed another way, a model unknown).
- **A live call running 120 s and more** holds back no load: a brain gives its call up at `OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT`, 120 s unset.

The list unread, or unreadable for 10 s (no answer in 2 s, no 200, a redirect, over 1 MiB, another shape), is no rule, each change said once in the broker's words. A call's end sets no pace for the list's reads: 250 ms apart at least. An unnamed model is taken for loaded. The hello's servers say `residency`; `federated.ts`'s summary names the servers read. The broker's compose service names the host as the stack's api does (`host.docker.internal:host-gateway`), for the list under Docker Engine on Linux. **Version contract.** Two new optional registry keys, and a rule only where `ps` is set: a minor.

**Why.** PR 3b-3b's measurement, below: a load of qwen3.8:27b that Ollama had accepted waited 24 minutes for qwen2.5:7b, which the backlogged brain's extraction kept busy within the broker's room, and requests for the evicted embedder queued behind it, five of each brain's timed live calls timing out at 120 s. Ollama puts a model out for memory once its calls have ended, and loads one model at a time; the broker counted slots per model and knew nothing of which models a server held. Decided by the maintainer (2026-11-18): the drain, which the design's outline did not have; the ask's own `max_wait_s` as its bound; and, after the first review pass, the stay as a registry setting, since at the asker's bound alone one brain's small `max_wait_s` cut the other brain's turn (74 calls to 23 in a reviewer's simulation, 47 to 53 with both at one value).

**Held.** `test-broker.ts` [15], 177 rows over real WebSockets against a stand-in list the rows move, on a clock they move: the wait to `max_wait_s` and no sooner, the drain with room to spare, a live call through it, the hold until the list names the model, the stay from the list's naming a load (a slow one, a quick one whose call ended first, one left out at its bound), by the registry's value, for an idle model too, and unmoved by a live call's load or one never listed, no drain begun under a live call younger than 120 s, the 5 s and the 120 s, a drain given up and tried again, a load's hold, its turn about and no drain in the other half, a live call's load and its coming before background's, a read under way as calls end, a wanted model's brain gone or its model listed meanwhile, the list failing (a status, a redirect not followed, another shape, over 1 MiB, silent 2 s), a name never listed with no model gone (apart and overlapping, background's calls and live ones), one that puts a model out (its back-off and its most, its own waiting asks held, what it put out asked for next, a leave-out undone), a call with no word (another load under way at its grant) and two ended before one read, the reads' pace, an unnamed model, kept grants and the lease, a server of 70 pools, 300 names at once, a silent socket's ask, what is said of each brain, and a server with no `ps`. `broker.ts --self-check`, 6 rows more: the keys, a list's reading, a model's name; `federated.ts --self-check`, 1 more: the host's name in the broker's compose service. ### PR 3b-3b: the two-brain measurement and its harness

**What changed.** PR 3b-3b of the ticket, whose earlier PRs are change 495. The maintainer's cut of PR 3b-3 (2026-11-14) left 3b-3b the two-brain measurement, its arms decided 2026-11-16: the canary and a scratch brain (`ob1-3379`, the canary's model settings, sleep its one pass) on this Mac's Ollama (`OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=3`, `OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS=2`) and its h2o decide tier, both keyed to an `ob1-federated` broker (Ollama `slots: 3, reserve: 1`; h2o `slots: 1`, `per_model: false`), each brain's fixed share `chat=1,embed=1`, the two-brain share `.env.example` names, and stable left running and only read; three arms of 20 minutes (both brains backlogged under fixed shares, the same with the broker, one backlogged with the broker), each with the same ~150 generated notes in every backlogged brain (200 in the arm that ran) and `bench-live.ts` timing live calls on both from minute 12.

`db/bench-brains.ts` (new) is the harness. `load` captures an arm's notes (the same text in every brain, source `smd3379-measure`, ids kept). `sample` reads every brain's `brain_info` and `worker_status` and Ollama's loaded models every 15 s. `clean` reads each listed or found thought back and deletes it only where its whole text is the generator's own. `summary` reads from each brain's sleep heartbeat tallies (PR 2b-2a) the sleep's background calls, their mean number in flight and its slot waits, in all and by pass, over two windows of an arm, a tally that falls counted as the sleep begun again. `load` and `clean` run only on a brain that says its tier is canary or working. `bench-live.ts` takes `--key-file` beside `--mcp`, for a brain no connector names. A refusal in either harness names a flag or a brain and a failure's code, never a path typed, a file's text or a parser's words. The runs' samples, live calls and Ollama's scheduler lines are kept on the maintainer's machine; the canary's 386 notes and every probe were deleted after, and the scratch brain and the broker torn down.

**Why.** The ticket's Verify on two brains: with both backlogged, neither brain's live captures wait for a slot and their background work splits the background slots; with one backlogged, it uses its share alone under fixed shares, or every free slot under the broker. Nothing measured what a brain's background work held of a shared model server. **Version contract.** Maintainer tooling and a record, nothing a brain reads: a patch.
