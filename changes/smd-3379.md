## Changelog

A `migrate.ts` run now takes a session advisory lock, `ob1:migrate`, before it writes anything, and holds it until it returns: a pending file, a plugin's file or repair, 096's re-run, or `--reapply`. A second run meanwhile is refused by name, exit 2, writing nothing, and the refusal names the backend that holds the lock, with the `pg_terminate_backend` that ends a run that was killed. Before, two runs at once raced on the ledger's rows and the catalog's, and one failed at once on a duplicate key, sometimes as a stack dump. `--dry-run`, `--grant` and a run with nothing to write take no lock (SMD-3379).

The claim workers — `extract-entities.ts`, `consolidate.ts` and `reembed.ts` — take an `admit` gate in-process: awaited before each claim, at the start of each pass, between a long thought's windows and between a consolidation's pairs, so a caller can pause a pass without stopping it, and a stop reaches a waiting worker at once; a run without one behaves as before (SMD-3379 PR 1b-1). The claim workers' `onPass` now hands the caller `abortInHand(olderThanMs)` beside the pass's stop: it aborts each worker's model call in hand at least that old, puts the thought at the back of the queue — extraction keeping its answered windows and counting the stop — and the worker goes on with its next claim (SMD-3379 PR 1b-2). A worker mid-thought now also reads its claim again after a closure that opened again during its call or a provider pause, where a gate says so (`closedSince`); `lease.ts`' `liveGate()` is the gate the scheduler closes (SMD-3379 PR 1c-3a).

The sleep scheduler runs its passes in work periods: a period begins once a pass has work and no live write has landed for `OB1_SLEEP_CAPTURE` seconds (`--capture`, 20), and runs until its pools drain. The idle scheduler reads its passes' claims each poll, and its work whole at its start and every `OB1_SLEEP_RESCAN` seconds (`--rescan`, 3600, new), so a backlog no claim shows — re-embed's, the orphans a raw delete of a window or a replay leaves — begins a period too; a vector arriving — the vector mender's, a re-embed's — brings that read forward, within a minute, since consolidation judges only a thought with a vector (SMD-3379 PR 1d). In a period a live write pauses the passes rather than stopping them: they wait between thoughts and go on once `OB1_SLEEP_CAPTURE` seconds pass with no live write, a model call in hand longer than `OB1_SLEEP_ABORT_AFTER` seconds (`--abort-after`, 60, new) aborted meanwhile and its thought set back. A statement past its timeout holds its pass until a restart, the other passes going on, where it stopped the sleep. A one-shot run with no work exits 0 at once. Its log says `working:`, `paused:`, `resumed:` and `the pools drained — the period ends`; its row writes `paused`, and `holds`, the passes held out of its work, which preflight and `brain_info` read, preflight's `sleep` row saying working, paused or idle (SMD-3379 PR 1c-2 to 1c-4b). It waits out a migration in progress at its start, and ends with exit 75 when it sees one while it runs, its supervisor running it again at once (SMD-3379 PR 1c-1). Its live reads keep the database's microseconds. **Upgrading:** `OB1_SLEEP_QUIET` and `OB1_SLEEP_BUDGET` (`--quiet`, `--budget`) are removed: the CLI refuses either with 2, naming what replaced it, and the coordinator no longer reads them, so delete them from `deploy/.env`. A period begins on work rather than after a quiet, and runs until its pools drain rather than to a clock, so a backlog drains in one period, paused by each live write (SMD-3379 PR 1c-4b).

### PR 1d: a vector come brings the whole read forward

**What changed.**
- `db/sleep.ts`: idle, a vector come since the last whole read — a `thought_audit` row whose diff says `embedding_present` true, whoever wrote it (the vector mender, a re-embed, an edit) — makes the next poll read the work whole, where it waited for the rescan: consolidation judges only a thought with a vector (029's `consolidation_pool`), and its arrival queues no claim. `vectorMark` takes the mark before each whole read and before a period's end read (the database's now() less the 30 s slack, and the rows already visible after it); `vectorCome` is one range read of `thought_audit_created_at_idx` against it, each poll that no whole read has found work yet, consolidation may begin a period and `VECTOR_FLOOR_S` (60) has passed since the mark. A vector read once is not read again, and a burst — the mender after an outage, a re-embed by hand — reads whole once a minute at most. The rescan alone still re-arms a set-down re-embed.
- `db/README.md`, `deploy/README.md`, `deploy/.env.example`, the header: the trigger; and "a delete's orphans" corrected — a thought's delete leaves none (below).
- **Tests:** test-live [38r]: two thoughts planted extracted and vectorless, out of the claims and the pools, with re-embed out of sleep; the mender's write gives each a vector. P1's, with no entity, brings a whole read forward that finds nothing; P2's, mended just after, waits out the floor (4 s) from that read and begins a period that judges it; P3, with no entity, is mended during that period, whose end read takes the mark, so past the floor after it no whole read.

**Why.** Draft 5's PR 1d. A vectorless capture — the provider down, SMD-2888 — is extracted in a period while consolidation passes it by; the mender gives it its vector within a minute of the provider's return, and its judgement waited up to an hour for the rescan.

**Version contract.** Additive: a whole read sooner, no setting, flag, log line or row field. A minor, under the note's major.

**Held.** Four mutants, each killed by its own row in [38r]: no trigger; no floor; no dedupe (the seen ids dropped); and the end read taking no mark, which survived until P3, mended during a period, joined the case. Not killed by a case: the mark taken after the whole read rather than before (a vector committed during the read, a race), and the `wholeWork` and `mayBegin("consolidate")` skips, which save reads and change no outcome. The first run of the case found its own setup wrong: a vectorless row is re-embed's backlog, so a whole read began a period for P2 before its vector came; the case keeps re-embed out of sleep.

**Measured after.** `./db/ci-parity.sh`: every row passes; all suites in 555 s. The long cell runs 294 s beside sleep's 307 s, rest's 314 s and extract's 282 s on this machine, the four side by side; the case's own waits add about 20 s to [38r] (not timed alone).

**Not taken.** **The delete trigger.** Draft 5 had a thought's `delete` audit row start the sweep. Measured on the canary (read-only, 2026-11-04): 525 deletes in its audit, 0 lineage orphans of the swept kinds. `thoughts_drop_derivations` (061) drops a deleted thought's lineage rows with it, and the census counts orphans of a thought that stands, so a delete gives the sweep nothing; what it sweeps — a raw delete of a thought's windows or mentions, a replay — writes no audit row to trigger on. **Triggering on the mender's rows alone** (draft 5's words): a vector arriving by any writer gives consolidation the same work, and the diff names it. **Re-embed's backlog**, which a vectorless row is part of: still read at the rescan, as before; the mender, which gives the vector, triggers the read. **A vector come while the gate is closed** is read as any is: the whole read's find holds until the period, as 1c-4b's does.
