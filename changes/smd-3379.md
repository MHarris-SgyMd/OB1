## Changelog

The slot broker reads a model server's loaded models (`ps` in its registry, Ollama's `/api/ps`): a background call for a model that is not loaded waits its turn, and the server's other background calls pause while it loads, so no load waits behind a model that background keeps busy (SMD-3379 PR 3c-1). Measured on a real Ollama (PR 3c-2): beside one brain's backlog on two models that cannot share memory, another brain's live capture took 8.5 s at the median with `ps` set and 53 s without, and 4.1 s with the backlogged brain's `OB1_SLEEP_MAX_WAIT` at 600.

## Record

Model residency at the slot broker — a background call for a model that is not loaded waits its turn, and the server's other background drains for its load, measured on a real Ollama: PRs 3c-1 and 3c-2 (SMD-3379)

**What changed.** PR 3c-1 of the ticket, whose earlier PRs are changes 495 and 544. The maintainer's cut of PR 3c (2026-11-18): 3c-1 the rule, 3c-2 its measurement on this Mac. A server's registry entry takes `ps`, where the server lists the models it has loaded (Ollama's `/api/ps`), for a `per_model` server alone, and `stay_s` with it. `deploy/federated/broker.ts` reads the list every 3 s, and every second while a load is under way, a model's name read as the list writes it (lower-cased, `:latest` where no tag is said). The rule is background's alone; a live call is granted at once, as before:

- **A listed model's ask** goes by the room and the turn, as before.
- **An ask for a model not listed** is taken at once where none is listed. Else it waits out `stay_s` (60 unset) from the end of the server's last background load, the list naming its model, then goes once the listed models have had no background call for 5 s and no live call for 120 s, or once it has waited its own `max_wait_s` (60 s unset) and no live call runs there at that moment.
- **The drain.** Then the server's other models are granted no background, the ask is granted once none of theirs runs, and they stay held until the list names its model, or its calls end and the list is next read. One load at a time a server.
- **A live call to a model not listed** holds the other models' background the same way from its grant, and comes before a background load. It does not begin the stay.
- **Two bounds of 120 s.** A drain not done by then is given up, the others going on, and none begun for 120 s more: no load is granted beside background still running. A load holds the others that long; past it the model is left out of the rule for 120 s, then held to again while it lasts, turn about, and no load for another model is begun while it is left out with its calls running on, nor any of its own asks granted. It is the same load still, judged when its calls end, and its time left out ends with it.
- **A load whose calls all end with the list never naming its model** is judged once, by the list as the load began and at the first read begun after its last call's end, where no other load was under way as it began, one left out at its bound or over and not yet judged included, and where no call to the model runs on under another spelling. Once one of its calls has ended, or it has been left out at its bound, no more of its model's asks are granted into it, and none until it is judged. A model gone that a brain had used within 120 s of the load's beginning, and none come but ones asked for by name: the load put it out and did not load, so the model's next load waits 60 s more for each such load running, ten at most, said with the models gone (once a minute a server, with the count since), none of its background asks is granted meanwhile, such a load begins no stay, and what it put out is taken for due for 120 s. Else nothing is held against it and that count begins again.
- **A model the list never names stays under the rule** (the maintainer, 2026-11-19: a misconfiguration, a name the server lists another way or a model it does not know). Each call to it is a load that waits its turn, and from its third the log says so, with what the server lists that no brain asks for.
- **A live call running 120 s and more** holds back no load: a brain gives its call up at `OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT`, 120 s unset.

The list unread, or unreadable for 10 s (no answer in 2 s, no 200, a redirect, over 1 MiB, another shape), is no rule, said in the broker's words as it begins and each hour it lasts, and what was in hand of a load then is dropped with it. A call's end sets no pace for the list's reads: 250 ms apart at least. An unnamed model is taken for loaded. A drain that has running background to wait for is said as it begins, one given up names the oldest call it waited on and its brain, and a model a brain asks for in another spelling than the list's is written both ways in every line that names it. The hello's servers say `residency`; `federated.ts`'s summary and the broker's start line say where each list is to be read, `check` and `up` say a `ps` that looks wrong and is taken all the same (the broker's own loopback, a host of none of the server's bases, one of its bases, a path that is not `/api/ps`), and `logs` shows each line's time. The broker's compose service names the host as the stack's api does (`host.docker.internal:host-gateway`), for the list under Docker Engine on Linux. **Version contract.** Two new optional registry keys, and a rule only where `ps` is set: a minor.

**Why.** PR 3b-3b's measurement (change 544): a load of qwen3.8:27b that Ollama had accepted waited 24 minutes for qwen2.5:7b, which the backlogged brain's extraction kept busy within the broker's room, and requests for the evicted embedder queued behind it, five of each brain's timed live calls timing out at 120 s. ### PR 3c-2: the rule measured on this Mac's Ollama

**What changed.** A record, and the two passages of `deploy/README.md` it settles: what Ollama's list says while a model loads, now probed, and the rule's costs, now with figures beside them. No code changed. **Version contract.** Documents alone: no bump of its own.

**Probed first** (2026-11-20; Ollama 0.33.3, `OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=3`, `OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS=2`, context 32768, 64 GiB). A reader of `/api/ps` ran beside every run below, every 250 ms, and every 15 ms from each scheduler log line that begins a load or an eviction until its `loaded runners`: 276,625 reads, none failed. Over the 187 loads it followed (qwen2.5:7b 53, 2.5 s at the median; qwen3-embedding:4b 85, 3.3 s; qwen3.8:27b 49, 8.7 s, 27 s at most) the list first named the model 13 ms after `loaded runners` reached the reader at the median, 1 ms at least and 0.67 s at most, and in no load before `llama-server started`: the list names a model only once it has loaded, as PR 3c-1 took from the log. Of the 254,258 reads made during loads and evictions the median took 0.5 ms and 162 over 100 ms; the slowest, 0.67 s, were the reads across which the 22 GiB model left the list, so a read waits while a model is put out, inside the broker's 2 s. A model put out leaves the list before the next begins to load: the list is empty through a load of qwen3.8:27b. Compared on one clock: the reader's ran 28 ms an hour behind the log's stamps, which first read as eight models listed early.

**The order Ollama puts models out in**, which the simulations took as idle first. Its source (`sched.go`, `findRunnerToUnload`) takes an idle model before a busy one, among idle ones the shortest keep-alive and then the lowest model path, not the least recently used, and with none idle waits for the first in that order to end its calls. On this Mac the order never chose. qwen3.8:27b (22.4 GiB predicted, 3 to 26 GiB free at the arms' evictions) puts every other model out, both gone within 0.2 s where idle; either of the others puts it out, waiting up to 99 s for its running call where it had one. Not probed with models that leave a choice.

**The arm's shape is not change 544's.** Since that arm the sleep finishes extraction before its judge model's passes begin in a period, so a fresh backlog asks for one model at a time: a 20-note rehearsal ran qwen2.5:7b alone until extraction drained. Each arm therefore loads 30 notes, waits until the subject sweep has recorded a thought (qwen3.8:27b in hand, 30 thoughts ahead of it), then loads 150 more, so extraction has work beside the sweep and one brain's background asks for both models. The brains are the measurement's own, on main f22ab8e8 with the canary's model settings and 544's `OB1_SLEEP_WORKERS=3` and shares `chat=1,embed=1`: `scratch`, sleep its one pass, made fresh for each arm, and `scratch2`, no pass, which takes live calls alone. Both were keyed to a throwaway `ob1-federated` broker (Ollama `slots: 3, reserve: 1`; h2o one slot), its registry naming `ps` or not. The canary and stable were read alone: no background call, capture or search of theirs fell in the three arms' 20 minutes. Live calls: one `capture_thought` and one `search_thoughts` on `scratch2` every 30 s, sent whether or not the last had answered and each timed to its answer, and from minute 12 `bench-live.ts` on both brains at once with 544's counts.

**What the rule did.** With `ps`, the broker's log repeats one cycle eight times: an ask for qwen3.8:27b reaches 60 s, the server's other background waits for extraction's running calls where any run (six of the eight), and the ask is granted 61 to 90 s after it was made (76 s at the median). Within 30 s the next live call asks for a model no longer loaded, and Ollama answers it once the sweep's call in hand ends: the wait the 15 captures over 30 s had, the README's first cost. Extraction then runs on the models that call loaded, for the stay. No drain was given up, no load left out, no model backed off, and the list was never unreadable. Without `ps` both models' asks were granted at once, Ollama loaded 43 times, the sweep's model was listed for two thirds of the arm, and an extraction call took 90 s, against 18 and 34 s in the arms with `ps`. With the wait at 600 s the sweep's model loaded twice, 607 and 611 s after its ask (no 120 s passed without a live call), and the one capture over 30 s came 49 s after the second.

**How far it goes.** Each arm ran once, in the table's order from its second column, the baseline between the third and fourth; a first baseline was spoiled by its own probes, which `scratch`'s sleep extracted and took the judge model for three times, one capture 82 s. Other sessions' test runs held the machine's load average at 22 as the arm without `ps` began, 11 for the arm with it, 25 for the baseline and 16 for the last: the fastest captures came at the highest load, and `bench-live.ts`'s direct metadata calls took 4.2 to 5.0 s at the median in every arm, so the arms are not parted by the machine. A fresh brain's consolidation judged no pair here, so the judge model's work is the subject sweep alone. In the rehearsal a drain began 160 ms after one sweep call's end, the next call already granted, and extraction's turn waited that call out too: 141 s from its ask, not 66.

**Kept** on the maintainer's machine: each arm's samples, timed calls, list reads, and the broker's, the sleep's and Ollama's scheduler lines, with the scripts. The scratch brains and the broker were torn down by name and their keys deleted.

**Not taken.** **The canary as the backlogged brain**, 544's: on brains of its own nothing moved under an arm and no session was asked to hold. **Arms B and A**, both brains backlogged: still after SMD-4382. **A second run of each arm, or another order.** **The open-loop pinger and the list reader as repository tools**: scripts beside the evidence. **A per-model `slots`**: Ollama runs qwen3.8:27b one call at a time whatever the registry says (544), which SMD-4432's reading of the server's memory sits beside.
