## Changelog

A `migrate.ts` run now takes a session advisory lock, `ob1:migrate`, before it writes anything, and holds it until it returns: a pending file, a plugin's file or repair, 096's re-run, or `--reapply`. A second run meanwhile is refused by name, exit 2, writing nothing, and the refusal names the backend that holds the lock, with the `pg_terminate_backend` that ends a run that was killed. Before, two runs at once raced on the ledger's rows and the catalog's, and one failed at once on a duplicate key, sometimes as a stack dump. `--dry-run`, `--grant` and a run with nothing to write take no lock (SMD-3379).

The claim workers — `extract-entities.ts`, `consolidate.ts` and `reembed.ts` — take an `admit` gate in-process: awaited before each claim, at the start of each pass, between a long thought's windows and between a consolidation's pairs, so a caller can pause a pass without stopping it, and a stop reaches a waiting worker at once; a run without one behaves as before (SMD-3379 PR 1b-1). The claim workers' `onPass` now hands the caller `abortInHand(olderThanMs)` beside the pass's stop: it aborts each worker's model call in hand at least that old, puts the thought at the back of the queue — extraction keeping its answered windows and counting the stop — and the worker goes on with its next claim (SMD-3379 PR 1b-2). A worker mid-thought now also reads its claim again after a closure that opened again during its call or a provider pause, where a gate says so (`closedSince`); `lease.ts`' `liveGate()` is the gate the scheduler closes (SMD-3379 PR 1c-3a).

The sleep scheduler runs its passes in work periods: a period begins once a pass has work and no live write has landed for `OB1_SLEEP_CAPTURE` seconds (`--capture`, 20), and runs until its pools drain. The idle scheduler reads its passes' claims each poll, and its work whole at its start and every `OB1_SLEEP_RESCAN` seconds (`--rescan`, 3600, new), so a backlog no claim shows — re-embed's, a delete's orphans — begins a period too. In a period a live write pauses the passes rather than stopping them: they wait between thoughts and go on once `OB1_SLEEP_CAPTURE` seconds pass with no live write, a model call in hand longer than `OB1_SLEEP_ABORT_AFTER` seconds (`--abort-after`, 60, new) aborted meanwhile and its thought set back. A statement past its timeout holds its pass until a restart, the other passes going on, where it stopped the sleep. A one-shot run with no work exits 0 at once. Its log says `working:`, `paused:`, `resumed:` and `the pools drained — the period ends`; its row writes `paused`, and `holds`, the passes held out of its work, which preflight and `brain_info` read, preflight's `sleep` row saying working, paused or idle (SMD-3379 PR 1c-2 to 1c-4b). It waits out a migration in progress at its start, and ends with exit 75 when it sees one while it runs, its supervisor running it again at once (SMD-3379 PR 1c-1). Its live reads keep the database's microseconds. **Upgrading:** `OB1_SLEEP_QUIET` and `OB1_SLEEP_BUDGET` (`--quiet`, `--budget`) are removed: the CLI refuses either with 2, naming what replaced it, and the coordinator no longer reads them, so delete them from `deploy/.env`. A period begins on work rather than after a quiet, and runs until its pools drain rather than to a clock, so a backlog drains in one period, paused by each live write (SMD-3379 PR 1c-4b).

### PR 1c-4b: the work period replaces the quiet and the clock

**What changed.**
- `db/sleep.ts`: idle, the scheduler waits for work (`awaitWork`), not for a quiet. A period begins once a pass has work and no live write has landed for `--capture` seconds. The work is read whole at the start — the baseline: each pass's pool, re-embed's backlog among them, and the sweep's orphans — and every `--rescan` seconds (`OB1_SLEEP_RESCAN`, 3600, 60–86,400); between, each poll reads one EXISTS a claim pass. What a whole read found holds until the next, through a closed gate. A pass held begins none (`mayBegin`): a statement's hold, re-embed set aside, or set down until the next period's start or rescan. A period ends once its pools drain (`the pools drained — the period ends`): each poll past consolidation's join reads the claims, and with none the pools whole. A one-shot run with no work exits 0 (`idle: no work — nothing to do`). A live write during the sweep ends the period, the next reading its work whole.
- N9: a statement past its `statement_timeout` holds its pass until a restart — `<pass> held until a restart`, `holds` `{ pass, why: "statement", until: null }`, `budget.stops` the passes held, the row failed — and the other passes go on. Re-embed's too.

**Not taken.** **Ending a drained period while live writes keep its gate closed.** A closed poll reads no pool (1c-3b): writes landing less than the capture apart hold a drained period open, its passes idle, until a gap. **A timeout landing as extraction restarts** (`setAside` then `start`): held and running for one poll. **One empty period per rescan beside another process's live re-embed lease**, and the consolidation work a thought extracted during a period's soft stop leaves, which waits for the next period or the rescan. **Both passes held at a period's start**: the path has no case. **Claim reads for re-embed while idle.** Re-embed's backlog is a scan of every thought; it is read at the rescan, or goes with a period other work begins. **Detecting another process's `--switch-key` before the rescan.** Its captures queue under the new key, which no claim read here sees; the rescan finds them outside the pool, and the start refuses.
