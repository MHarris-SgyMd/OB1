## Changelog

`OB1_BACKGROUND_SLOTS` (new, off unless set) holds background model calls — the sleep passes', board-sync's, the vector mender's, the provider reprobe's and a detached job's, among them — to a share of each model server's slots, across every process of the brain, by Postgres advisory locks, so a live call finds one free: types with optional per-model overrides, e.g. `chat=1,embed=2,decide=1,chat:qwen3.8:27b=1`, a type left out unlimited, the decide tier shared per server alone; a live call never takes a slot. A semaphore whose database does not answer lets the call run without one, said once. Consolidation's per-pair `--timeout` no longer counts a wait for a slot (SMD-3379 PR 2b-1).

## Record

**What changed.**
- `server-portable/slots.ts` (new): `installBackgroundSlots`, the model-call seam's hook, set once per process at its entry point, off unless `OB1_BACKGROUND_SLOTS` is set. A background call counts against its share — per normalised model server and model, the decide tier per server alone — and waits for one of its N slots: the two-key advisory lock `(hashtext(key), i)`, taken with `pg_try_advisory_lock`, polled every 250 to 500 ms, held for the call, let go after it; a process that dies lets its slots go with its session. In a process one of a share's calls polls at a time, the rest queued there in order, each polling and then holding its slot on a connection of its own (`application_name` `ob1-slots`): a connection per slot held and one per poller. A connection that lets its slot go cleanly is kept 2 s for the process's next call, at most four, then closed: none when idle. A poller holds the share's lock `(hashtext(key), -1)` shared, and a call about to make its first try that finds it held elsewhere sits out a poll, so one process's backlog does not keep the slots from the others; a slot a process lets go while no other waits goes to its own poller at once, woken from its nap or, between its try and its nap, trying again — the poller's own waiting lock told from another process's by its backend. Every connect and statement is bounded by 5 s, and a stop ends a wait at once; a session the database ends after it has answered (a restart, a terminate) is asked again on a fresh one, and the semaphore's sessions carry TCP keepalives, so one whose client went away unseen ends within about a minute. A live call, and a type left out, take none. A value that does not parse is said and the hook left off. The semaphore fails open: its database not answering, the call runs without a slot, said once, the database not asked again for 30 s (the maintainer, 2026-11-05). A slot's connection lost while held — ended or stalled, told apart from a pooler by the backend that took the lock — and an unlock reaching another backend (a pooler) are each said once.
- Set in `sleep.ts`, `extract-entities.ts`, `consolidate.ts`, `reembed.ts`, `sync-linear.ts` and `weekly-digest.ts` after `--url` is read, and in the REST core (`api.ts`, through `core/`) before its mender and reprobe start, its drain taking it down. A wait ends on the call's stop, or the process's: the end of the REST core's drain ends every wait and takes the hook down, a call already running keeping its slot to its end, and a background call after it running without one.
- `db/lease.ts`' `inHandCalls` gains `abortWaiting`, which each claim engine calls at its first stop: a call in hand waiting for a slot (`slotWait`, the hook's wait alone), or starting to after it, is aborted with `StoppedWaiting` and its thought set back, so a stop does not wait out other processes' calls past the coordinator's grace; a call that gets its slot at once, or is running, finishes. Extraction treats it as its first stop, no stop counted; its hybrid decider's call now carries the stop's and the hard stop's signal, a stopped one not read as a decider outage. A slot wait that ends inside `idleWhile` leaves the entry idle, whichever began first: the decider's call, queued for its slot before `idleWhile` marked it, is not one N12 aborts once it runs (review pass 7).

a FIFO across processes needs a table or blocking locks. **A local poller is woken only where no other process waits** (review pass 6): with one waiting, a slot this process lets go goes to whichever poller asks first, a poll's gap at most — fairness kept over throughput there. **A stopping thought's running calls** (a fan-out's, sharing its one abort) are aborted with the one that waits: the thought is set back anyway. **A call between its turn and its first wait at a stop** (review pass 7): connecting, or its alone check or first try, it has not told its wait, so `abortWaiting` passes it by;
