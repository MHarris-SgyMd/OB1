## Changelog

The slot broker reads a model server's loaded models (`ps` in its registry, Ollama's `/api/ps`): a background call for a model that is not loaded waits its turn, and the server's other background calls pause while it loads, so no load waits behind a model that background keeps busy (SMD-3379 PR 3c-1). Measured on a real Ollama (PR 3c-2): beside one brain's backlog asking for its extraction model and its judge model at once, another brain's live capture took 8.8 s at the median with `ps` set and 53 s without, and 4.1 s with the backlogged brain's `OB1_SLEEP_MAX_WAIT` at 600 (one run of each, on one Mac). With two brains backlogged at once (PR 3c-3), each asking for one model: each brain's background held one of the model server's slots under fixed shares and on the broker alike, a capture's metadata call made by a probe beside them took 3.2 s at the median (1.9 s with no background at work), and captures timed as the backlogs wound down took 2.8 to 7.0 s (2.7 s at the median with none). Each asking for two models, in the 12 minutes before any capture: under fixed shares the judge model and the embedder were not loaded and 46 of 48 searches were answered without their embedding; on the broker with `ps` all three models took turns and all 48 were answered with theirs, 18.9 s at the median, with extraction at 27 thoughts against 72 (one Mac; each arm once, the one-model pair twice; on the build before a sleep's judge-model passes yielded to extraction). The two-model arms were run again on a build with that yield (PR 3c-4), the broker arm twice, each run under other conditions than PR 3c-3's: under fixed shares the judge model again stayed unloaded for the 12 minutes and the embedder for all but their first seconds; on the broker a search took 51.2 and 22.4 s at the median, and 12 and 4 of 48 were answered without their embedding, against 18.9 s and none on the earlier build. The yield's own part in that was not told apart from a busier machine, other brains at work and how each run's notes had loaded. Run once more on builds one pull request apart and a model server nothing else called (PR 3c-5; fourteen arms, ten on a calm machine): on the broker, in four calm arms a build, extraction ended 38 to 44 thoughts in the 12 minutes with the yield against 24 to 29 before it, and the judge model's sweep 6 calls against 8; a search was answered at 4.9 to 12.0 s at the median against 9.3 to 18.1, and none of the probe's calls failed on either build. Under fixed shares one side went without in every run: with the yield the judge model stayed unloaded for the 12 minutes and extraction ran, in three runs of three; before it the judge model held the server in two of three, where 22 of 24 metadata calls timed out and extraction ended 4 thoughts; the embedder went unloaded either way. Whether the yield decides the side was not established. PR 3c-4's slow first notes and slower broker figures did not come back on either build under these conditions.

## Record

Model residency at the slot broker — a background call for a model that is not loaded waits its turn, and the server's other background drains for its load, measured on a real Ollama: PRs 3c-1 to 3c-5 (SMD-3379)

**What changed.** PR 3c-1 of the ticket, whose earlier PRs are changes 495 and 544. The maintainer's cut of PR 3c (2026-11-18): 3c-1 the rule, 3c-2 its measurement on this Mac. A server's registry entry takes `ps`, where the server lists the models it has loaded (Ollama's `/api/ps`), for a `per_model` server alone, and `stay_s` with it. `deploy/federated/broker.ts` reads the list every 3 s, and every second while a load is under way, a model's name read as the list writes it (lower-cased, `:latest` where no tag is said). The rule is background's alone; a live call is granted at once, as before:

- **A listed model's ask** goes by the room and the turn, as before.
- **An ask for a model not listed** is taken at once where none is listed. Else it waits out `stay_s` (60 unset) from the end of the server's last background load, the list naming its model, then goes once the listed models have had no background call for 5 s and no live call for 120 s, or once it has waited its own `max_wait_s` (60 s unset) and no live call runs there at that moment.
- **The drain.** Then the server's other models are granted no background, the ask is granted once none of theirs runs, and they stay held until the list names its model, or its calls end and the list is next read. One load at a time a server.
- **A live call to a model not listed** holds the other models' background the same way from its grant, and comes before a background load. It does not begin the stay.
- **Two bounds of 120 s.** A drain not done by then is given up, the others going on, and none begun for 120 s more: no load is granted beside background still running. A load holds the others that long; past it the model is left out of the rule for 120 s, then held to again while it lasts, turn about, and no load for another model is begun while it is left out with its calls running on, nor any of its own asks granted. It is the same load still, judged when its calls end, and its time left out ends with it.
- **A load whose calls all end with the list never naming its model** is judged once, by the list as the load began and at the first read begun after its last call's end, where no other load was under way as it began, one left out at its bound or over and not yet judged included, and where no call to the model runs on under another spelling. Once one of its calls has ended, or it has been left out at its bound, no more of its model's asks are granted into it, and none until it is judged. A model gone that a brain had used within 120 s of the load's beginning, and none come but ones asked for by name: the load put it out and did not load, so the model's next load waits 60 s more for each such load running, ten at most, said with the models gone (once a minute a server, with the count since), none of its background asks is granted meanwhile, such a load begins no stay, and what it put out is taken for due for 120 s. Else nothing is held against it and that count begins again.
- **A model the list never names stays under the rule** (the maintainer, 2026-11-19: a misconfiguration, a name the server lists another way or a model it does not know). Each call to it is a load that waits its turn, and from its third the log says so, with what the server lists that no brain asks for.
- **A live call running 120 s and more** holds back no load: a brain gives its call up at `OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT`, 120 s unset.

### PR 3c-2: the rule measured on this Mac's Ollama

**What changed.** A record, and the passages of `deploy/README.md` it settles: what Ollama's list says while a model loads, now probed; the rule's costs, now with figures beside them; and what a raised `OB1_SLEEP_MAX_WAIT` does under the rule. A reader of `/api/ps` ran beside every run below, every 250 ms, and every 15 ms from each scheduler log line that begins a load or an eviction until its `loaded runners`: 276,625 reads, none failed.
