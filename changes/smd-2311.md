## Changelog

The learning path's family calendar is a plugin, `family-calendar`: its six tools are operations of the brain itself behind its own keys, its tables in a schema of its own, and the standalone server and its key list are gone; yearly dates now come round on their next anniversary (SMD-2311).

## FORK

The family calendar becomes a plugin — the first of the six extensions to leave its standalone server (SMD-2311)

**What changed.** SMD-2311 ports the six curated extensions onto the plugin model, in four PRs (decided 2026-11-02). This is PR 1: family-calendar, and the suite the other ports join.
- **`plugins/family-calendar/`.** The six tools of `extensions/family-calendar/index.ts` are six operations under the same names:
  - `family_calendar_add_family_member`, `_add_activity` and `_add_important_date` need write;
  - `_get_week_schedule`, `_search_activities` and `_get_upcoming_dates` need read.

  Each is a REST route under `/v1/plugins/family-calendar/` and an MCP tool on the brain's own connector, closed-world, and an add marked as destroying nothing. Inputs are checked before the database: a date is `YYYY-MM-DD` from the year 0001, a time `HH:MM`, a day a lower-case day name, and text holds no NUL (which Postgres will not store). Refusals:
  - an activity with neither a day nor a start date, which no week would list: `422 NO_WHEN`;
  - a member that is not there: `404 NO_SUCH_MEMBER`;
  - an end before a start: `422 BAD_DATES`.
 Answers fold the member into `family_member`, `{ id, name, relationship }` or null.
- **Its tables.** `migrations/001_family_calendar.sql` makes `family_members`, `activities` and `important_dates` in `plugin_family_calendar`, as the plugin's role.
  - Two views, `activity_rows` and `important_date_rows`, build each answer as one jsonb column, so an operation selects named columns (check 41).
  - The activities table holds the rules the operation answers too (an end on or after the start; a day or a start date), as named checks.
  - Nothing is adopted from `public` (decided 2026-10-26). The plugin's README gives the copy for a brain that held the old tables, which neither dogfood brain did: one transaction, re-runnable after a refused row, `user_id` kept in the column the plugin ignores.
- **`user_id`** stays in each table, nullable, and nothing writes or reads it (decided 2026-11-02, Work 4). `DEFAULT_USER_ID` goes with the server.
- **Two behaviours fixed in the port:**
  - A yearly date counts on its next anniversary. The old `get_upcoming_dates` compared the stored date, so a birthday stored with its birth year was never listed. Each date now carries `next_date`, `days_until`, and `remind`: whether it falls within its `reminder_days_before`.
  - The week is seven days. The old query ran eight (`start_date <= week_start + 7`), and listed every weekly activity whatever its own dates. Now a weekly one is listed only where its day in that week falls within its dates.

  Beyond the old server, `search_activities` also matches the member's name (as its description promised), escapes `%` and `_`, and takes a `limit`.
- **Retired.** `extensions/family-calendar/index.ts`, `schema.sql` and `.env.example` are removed. The README there is now Extension 3's "turn on the plugin", and its metadata needs no Bun or primitives. Also:
  - `extensions/test-auth.ts` loses the server's row; its mid-upload case runs at job-hunt's route, which takes the `/` health probe.
  - `extensions/test-tools.ts` loses its section: thirty-nine tools on six servers.
  - `ROLE_GRANTS.extensions` loses the three tables, with `db/README.md`'s row and `CONTRIB_SCHEMA_FILES`' entry. `test-schema` [50] and `test-live` [18] count five extension schemas and fifteen extension tables. `--groups extensions` still grants the other five extensions' tables, until PR 4 empties it.
  - Three primitives' lists of the extensions that use them drop it.
- **`server-portable/test-plugin-ports.ts`**, new, in CI after the plugin tables step and in `ci-parity.sh`. It runs every ported plugin against Postgres through both servers:
  - the migrator makes its tables and views in its own schema, each its role's, and nothing in `public`;
  - a read key's tool list is its reads alone and a capture key's its captures alone, a read and nothing else annotated read-only;
  - an operation a key's scope does not reach is `403` naming its scope over REST, and no such tool over MCP, for a read key and a capture key both;
  - every read's MCP structured content is its REST body;
  - its drift guard fails a tool no section drives.

  Each later PR adds its plugins to `PORTED` and a section.
- **`server-portable/test-plugins.ts`** reads the known plugins in its unknown-name message from the registry, not a pinned `example`.

**Why.** Each extension was a standalone MCP server with a key list of its own, reaching Postgres through `compat/supabase-sql` outside the REST core's keys and audit (SMD-2308, SMD-3216). As a plugin, it runs behind the brain's keys and scopes. Its SQL runs as a role holding nothing but its own schema (SMD-2756), and the operator has one connector, not one per extension.

**Version.** `minor`. The brain gains six operations while the plugin is enabled, and none otherwise. The standalone server goes, and an operator who ran it turns the plugin on instead. Its tool names gain the plugin's prefix, and no dogfood client held the old ones (decided 2026-10-26). No core route, setting or table changes.

**Held.**
- **`test-plugin-ports.ts`** (82 assertions, Postgres 16). The family calendar's section:
  - members, the name trimmed, `user_id` left null, and an impossible date, one in the year 0000 and a NUL in a body or a query refused;
  - six activities around one week. The week lists the weekly one and the two dated ones that reach it. It does not list the one ended before, the one starting the day after the seventh, or the weekly one whose dates ended. They are ordered by start time, with members folded in, and a summer week lists the weekly one again. A Monday class starting on that week's Wednesday is left out and listed the week after; a Monday club ending the Sunday before the week's Monday is left out of a week starting on a Tuesday and listed in its last week;
  - search by title in any case, by the member's name, by type, filters together, all seven latest start first and the two with none newest first, the limit, and `%` and `_` as themselves;
  - `NO_SUCH_MEMBER` over both doors, `NO_WHEN`, `BAD_DATES`, a capitalised day, a time of 25:00 and a week in the year 0000 refused, none of them leaving an activity or a date; the table itself refuses an activity with no day and no start date;
  - its hints: an add destroys nothing, every operation closed-world;
  - upcoming dates against the database's today. A yearly birthday stored eight years back is listed on its anniversary ten days on, with `remind` false. Today's date is zero days off, reminded. The past one and the one beyond are left out, and the window widens. With `days_ahead` 0 a yearly date whose anniversary is today is listed. Unless given, the window takes the thirtieth day and not the thirty-first. A yearly 29 February comes round on the date a JavaScript reckoning from the database's today expects.
- **Mutants**, each run against the suite and each failing it:
  - the week's end a day late;
  - yearly dates on their stored day;
  - no ILIKE escaping;
  - no member check;
  - no member-name search;
  - weekly activities ignoring their end;
  - `remind` strictly inside;
  - `add_family_member` declared read;
  - weekly activities by overlap alone;
  - year 0000 let through;
  - no `NO_WHEN`;
  - today's anniversary skipped;
  - a default of 31 days;
  - NUL let through;
  - the table's day-or-date rule dropped;
  - the no-start-date tiebreak oldest first;
  - an add with no hints.
- **The README's copy,** run against the old `schema.sql` seeded with a NULL `created_at`, a capitalised day, a time with seconds, a NULL `recurring_yearly`, two users and an activity with no day or date: refused whole, nothing copied; the row fixed, the block run again copies every row, each `user_id` kept, the day lower-cased, the time answered to the minute.
- **Unchanged suites:** `extensions/test-auth.ts` (508), `extensions/test-tools.ts` (147), `db/test-schema.ts` (3127), `server-portable/test-plugins.ts` (284). The checker and the typechecks pass.
