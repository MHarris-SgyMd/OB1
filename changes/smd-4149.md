## Changelog

A note's open items now reach its priority (SMD-4149, migration 125): a thought that states no level takes the highest level on the open items opened from it, and with no due time of its own their soonest. So search's `priority_weight` and `min_priority` see a bill due tomorrow that only its open item records. Each result's priority names the item (`fromItem`). The agenda's order is unchanged.

## Record

`effective_priority` reads a note's open items, each named as a `from_items` term, so search ranks a note by the level and due time its open items carry (SMD-4149)

**Why.** Migration 122 scored a thought from statements on the thought itself, or from the ticket it is about. It never read the open items opened from it (117). On SMD-3662's seeded set every personal note is planted as an open item: the bill due tomorrow, the gutters two weeks overdue, the commitment due Friday, the passport at a stated 4. Read as thoughts, all of them scored unknown 2 with no due time. SMD-3668's `priority_weight`, which scores search hits as thoughts, could not see what the agenda sees for the same notes, and reached top-1 on at most 4 of 7 prompts at any weight.

**What changed.** The maintainer decided the rule on 2026-11-09, before the build and the run:
- **Level:** a level stated on the thought wins. Otherwise it takes the highest level counted on its open items, with no hop discount, since the item is the note's own to-do. Otherwise it inherits from its ticket, as 122 does. Otherwise it is unknown. Of items at one level, the one due soonest is named, then the lower id.
- **Due time:** the thought's own (its statement's, or its `due_ref`'s) wins. Otherwise it takes the soonest due time among its open items. An item at level 0 passes no due time, and a thought reading level 0 from its items takes no urgency.
- **Scope:** only a thought that is no ticket's row. A ticket's row reads as its ticket, as before.
- **What counts:** only an item whose status is `open`. Each gives its own counted level and its own due time, never what it inherits. An open item still inherits only the level stated on its note, so nothing is counted twice and no read goes round in a cycle.
- **Migration 125** replaces `effective_priority` with the same signature and columns and adds two helpers: `ob1_priority_due` (122's due-time rule, lifted out) and `ob1_thought_items` (a thought's open items, each with its counted level and due time). The level term is `from_items`, naming the item, its level, tier and maker. An urgency or overdue term from an item has `due_from: from_items` and names the item.
- **The server:** a `from_items` level counts as a known priority for the label and for `min_priority`; a due time alone from an item doesn't, as an unknown with urgency doesn't. Each result's `priority` carries `fromItem` (the item's id and level) beside `inheritedFrom`, and the words say `… from open item <id>`. A server before this change refuses a row carrying the new term, so the server and the schema upgrade together, as the compose stack does.
- **The eval:** the seeded run prints what each note reads as a thought and fails unless it matches `plannedNoteReads` (SMD-4149's first Verify, held at every run).

**Held.**
- test-schema [112]: one row per rule. The bill +2, the gutters overdue +3 and the passport 4, each naming its item. A stated Low beats its item's High. An item's Medium beats a Critical ticket's inherited 4. Of three items the highest, at one level the sooner. The note's own due time wins. A level-0 item passes no due time, and a level 0 from items takes no urgency. Done, dropped and proposed items give nothing. An inferred level counts only when asked. An item's due time comes by `due_ref` and from its statement. A sibling item inherits nothing from the note's level read from another item. A ticket's row reads nothing from its items. The shape, and a re-apply.
- test-upgrade [20bp]: onto a populated brain, refused by name without 122's helpers; once applied, a note goes from unknown 2 to its item's High +2, with the agenda's answer and every stored row unmoved; a re-apply is a no-op.
- test-server [36] and [36b]: a level from items is known to the floor; a due time alone isn't; the value, the line and the title name the item; a None from an item reads as stated.
