## Changelog

Unlinking a thought (`update_thought` with `{"supersedes": null}`) now waits for a supersession accept in progress, as re-pointing one already did (SMD-3045). Before, an unlink could commit while an accept was running, and the accept then cleared a capture-only key's pointer above a link that was no longer under the accepted thought.

## FORK

An unlink takes the review lock: `update_thought` takes `ob1:supersession-review` whenever its envelope names `supersedes`, a clear included (SMD-3045)

**What changed** (migration 098).
- **`update_thought`** is 088's body under 088's signature with one change. The supersession advisory lock is taken whenever `p_provenance` names `supersedes` (`v_set_supersedes`): a thought's uuid, as before, or a JSON null that clears it, now too. It is taken before the fingerprint lock and the row, 033's order, whatever the row's pointer is: that is read under the row lock, after this one, and an unlocked read to skip the lock would reopen the race. A new sentinel `ob1:pointer-write-takes-review-lock` sits at the gate, and the function's comment says it. Nothing else moves: the same answers, refusals and writes, and the grants kept.

**Why.** `review_supersession_proposal` holds the review lock for the whole accept. Its lapse (097's `ob1_lapse_walk`, then the clears) reads the tree above the accepted row and then clears the capture-only pointers it found. A re-point onto a thought and `delete_thought` wait on that lock, but an unlink took none. SMD-3035's review pass 2 raced it on a real Postgres with a sleep between the walk and the clears. B's unlink of X (a write key's link X→S) returned during the sleep, and the accept then cleared the hook's pointer Y→X, although X was no longer under S. The same pass found a 40P01 with the same cause: one transaction unlinked a row (row lock, no review lock) and then re-pointed it (waiting on the review lock), while the accept's clear waited on that row.

**Lock order.**
- An unlink now takes supersession lock → (fingerprint lock) → row, as a re-point and `delete_thought` (036) do, so the 40P01 above has no crossing left.
- Outside the order, as for a re-point before and now for an unlink too: a transaction of several calls that holds a row (a content edit, say) and then writes a pointer waits on the lock while an accept holding it waits on that row. Postgres breaks that with 40P01 where an unlink used to pass. No shipped caller makes such a transaction (each store call is its own); taking the lock first (it is re-entrant) or writing the pointer first keeps one in the order.

**Not here.**
- 097's NOT HERE says that under an accept "only an unlink (which takes none, 088) moves one". Since 098 nothing does. 097 is not edited, because migrations are append-only (`migrate.ts` checks each file's sha256), so db/README's 097 paragraph says what now holds.
- A taker that holds no review lock (a write key's re-capture, a text move) still has the window between the walk's read and its clears: SMD-3046.

**Tests.**
- test-schema [88]:
  - the shape: one `update_thought`, 098 its last definer, the gate and its sentinel, the comment naming 098 beside 088, the order supersession lock < fingerprint lock < row;
  - 098's DDL equals 088's but for the gate and the comment's one sentence;
  - inside a transaction, the review lock is held after a re-point, an unlink and an unlink of an unlinked row, and not after a patch, a `derived_from` alone or a change of text;
  - re-apply a no-op; 088 re-applied, an unlink holds no lock; restored, it holds one again.
- test-live [6j], two connections, each order fixed by holding one side's transaction open:
  - the unlink first: the accept waits on the review lock, then accepts and leaves the hook's pointer standing;
  - the accept first: the unlink waits, then unlinks after the lapse cleared the pointer;
  - neither deadlocks.
- test-upgrade [20ar]: refused without 073; onto a populated brain at 097 an unlink holds no review lock, after 098 it holds one; an edit answers as 088's did; no row or audit row moved; a re-apply is a no-op.
- The ticket's live Verify, on a throwaway Postgres: SMD-3035 pass 2's race table (26 rows, the delete races included), with a 1.5 s sleep injected after the lapse's walk on a firing that has pointers to clear, run once under 088's gate (the control) and once under 098's.
  - The unlink during an accept: under 088 it returned at 420 ms, inside the window, and the accept still cleared the hook's pointer above it. Under 098 it waits for the accept's commit (1,536 ms), and the result is the order the two committed in.
  - The unlink first, held open: under 088 the accept ran through it and cleared the pointer. Under 098 the accept waits for the unlink's commit, and the hook's pointer stands.
  - SMD-3035's 40P01 (an unlink, then a re-point, in one transaction): a deadlock under 088, none under 098.
  - The class this joins, measured: a content edit then a re-point in one transaction deadlocks against an accept under both, and a content edit then an unlink now does too (under 088 it passed). Every other row comes out the same under both.
- The last-definer asserts that named 088 for `update_thought` name 098, test-schema's `CARRIES` carries 073 for 098, and [79] restores the shipped body after its re-apply of 088. Preflight's remedies name 098 as `update_thought`'s last definer.
