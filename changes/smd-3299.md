## FORK

The mediated-pair link step asked five other ways, the last two under a version 3 of its test: version 4's cap measured on the canary, nothing better found offline, and version 4 kept (SMD-3299)

**Why.** Decide version 4 caps a pair when its link step answers H or F (SMD-3203). PR 3 re-judged the canary under `@p4d4`, and two blind raters labelled 80 sampled pairs.
- The cap's precision was 0.686 against a bar of 0.7, and its miss rate 0.297 against 0.3 (SMD-3203, comment 2ffbe929).
- 12 of the 15 wrong caps were F links on pairs where one thought works on the other's own deliverable.
- 7 of the 10 misses were N links on pairs whose thoughts both name the same third subject.

The maintainer chose to refine the link step. Version 4 keeps running on the canary, and stable stays on v1.9.0.

**What changed: `evals/eval-mediated-pairs.ts`, rounds 3 to 5.** Every candidate asks the writer:
- **Round 3:**
  - `control`, version 4's own question asked again (`linkMessages`);
  - `walk`, one call whose answer walks the test in order: each thought's own subject and deliverable, the names both share and whose each is, then D, H and F as yes or no, the link being the first yes (`walkLink`);
  - `names`, the walk plus up to ten names both texts contain (`sharedNames`). Those are ticket ids, PR numbers, files, endpoints, versions, migrations and code identifiers; inside a URL only a ticket id or a PR number counts. Each name is shown where it first appears, read as the pass's prompt cuts each side, inside a `<shared_names>` block whose tags are escaped as a thought's are.
- **Round 4,** `narrow`: three narrower questions.
  - Each thought alone gives its own subject and deliverables, once per thought, cached beside `--out` (`ownMessages`, `parseOwn`).
  - A plain match finds whether either thought names the other's (`ownNames`, `hitsOf`).
  - One question follows: on a hit, D or H (`dhMessages`); with none, F or N (`fnMessages`). Their facts sit inside an escaped `<facts>` block.
  - Each question quotes only the test's own lines (`testSpan`, `testLine`).
- **Round 5,** the test's version 3. The maintainer's wording says where D ends for the other thought's deliverable.
  - `testV3` builds it from version 2 with three edits (`V3_EDITS`, `V3_RULE`), each held to the ADR's words. The pass keeps version 2.
  - `link3` asks version 4's question around version 3's text.
  - `narrow3` asks round 4's questions over version 3's lines.
- **Scoring on the cap alone** (`scoreLinks`, `--links`): κ on mediated or not, the cap's precision and its miss rate.
  - An unreadable answer caps, and an error counts against.
  - `--strata` weights a stratified draw by its groups' populations, as PR 3 estimated.
  - `--set 1|2` applies that set's bars (`linkBarMisses`).
  - A pair's time counts the own answers it asked.
- **`docs/subject-anchored-claims.md`:** PR 3's result, rounds 3 to 5, version 3 (proposed), the decision, and the path's next step.
- **`evals/README.md`:** rounds 3 to 5.

**Held.** `--self-check`, which CI's "Mediated-pair rules" step runs, holds:
- **the walk's reading:** its link is the first yes in the test's order, and both of its guards answer F;
- **the names:**
  - every kind of name and nothing else, and a URL's ticket id and PR number alone;
  - no name past the prompt's cut, and at most ten;
  - excerpts cut at whole code points;
  - no delimiter a thought can close;
- **the narrow questions:**
  - their rules are the test's own lines, with D and H, and F and N, kept apart;
  - an own answer's names count only if its text holds them, and a hit is exact;
  - every guard caps;
- **version 3:** each edit is the ADR's words, the composition is version 2 plus the edits, and a missing edit refuses;
- **the scoring:** the cap's numbers by hand, the weighted estimate across strata, and the bars and their edges.

Mutants: round 3's 17 of 18 fail on the check's own message, and the 18th (names on one side only) by a crash. Round 4's 11 of 11 fail, and version 3's 5 of 5.

**Measured.** Pre-registered on SMD-3203 before any pair was asked: rounds 3, 4 and 5 are comments be3a4672, b3eed1b6 and 2fb7b75e, with results in 2a0e090a, a8fd376a and c8033c53.

- **Set 2** is PR 3's 77, weighted by version 4's strata. It is the analysis's source.
- The control is deterministic: asked again, it gave the canary's recorded link on all 77 of set 2's pairs and the same link on all 80 of set 1's.

- **Post hoc, against the control:**
  - round 3's two asks trade wrong caps for misses;
  - round 4's hit finds the link, but its D-or-H question calls most direct pairs "only names it";
  - link3 adds no wrong cap but fixes almost none of version 4's N misses.
- **Asked of one thought alone, the 27B names the ticket blind raters agree on** for 98.8% of the thoughts whose own subject is a ticket. The raters agree with each other on 98.1% (post hoc, 275 thoughts).

**Not taken.** The 27B's reasoning mode on version 4's question: its thinking tokens would multiply the step's time on every pair.

**Decided** (the maintainer, 2026-10-25): stop, and keep version 4. Evolves moves to work threads built from each thought's own subject (SMD-3306).
