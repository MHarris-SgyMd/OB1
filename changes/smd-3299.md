## Changelog

`evals/eval-mediated-pairs.ts` measures two other ways of asking the mediated-pair test's link step, the step decide version 4 caps a pair on, and records that version 4's cap missed its precision bar on the canary (0.686 against 0.7). Neither candidate qualifies, so decide mode is unchanged; next, the step as narrower questions (SMD-3299).

## FORK

The mediated-pair link step asked another way: version 4's cap measured on the canary, and two link prompts measured offline (SMD-3299)

**Why.** Decide version 4 caps a pair when its link step answers H or F (SMD-3203). PR 3 re-judged the canary under `@p4d4`, and two blind raters labelled 80 sampled pairs. The cap's precision was 0.686 against a bar of 0.7, and its miss rate 0.297 against 0.3 (SMD-3203, comment 2ffbe929). 12 of the 15 wrong caps were F links on pairs where one thought works on the other's own deliverable. 7 of the 10 misses were N links on pairs whose thoughts both name the same third subject. The maintainer chose to refine the link step: version 4 keeps running on the canary, and stable stays on v1.9.0.

**What changed.**
- **`evals/eval-mediated-pairs.ts`, round 3.** It asks the writer three ways, one call a pair:
  - `control`: version 4's own question (`linkMessages`), asked again.
  - `walk`: the test, the pair, then an answer that walks the test in order. It gives each thought's own subject and deliverable, the names both share and whose each is, then D, H and F as yes or no. The link is read from the answer (`walkLink`): the first yes, or N when all three are no. If a rung before the first yes is unreadable, the answer reads as none, which the pass caps.
  - `names`: the walk, plus up to ten names both texts contain (`sharedNames`). The names are ticket ids, PR numbers, files, endpoints, versions, migrations and code identifiers. Inside a URL only a ticket id or a PR number counts. Each name is shown with about 80 characters either side of where it first appears in each thought. Both thoughts are read as the pass's prompt cuts them. The list sits inside a `<shared_names>` block, its tags escaped as a thought's are, and its head's injection guard answers F.
- **Scoring on the cap alone** (`scoreLinks`, with `--links`). It reports κ on mediated or not, the cap's precision and its miss rate.
  - `--strata` takes a stratified draw's key and weights each pair by its group's population over the group's gold pairs, as PR 3 estimated.
  - `--set 1|2` applies that set's bars (`linkBarMisses`). Set 1's include κ at least the control's.
- **`docs/subject-anchored-claims.md`:** PR 3's result, round 3's, and the path's next step.
- **`evals/README.md`:** round 3.

**Held.** `--self-check`, which CI's "Mediated-pair rules" step runs, holds:
- the walk's link as the first yes in the test's order, and both heads' guards answering F;
- every kind of name and nothing else; inside a URL, a ticket id or a PR number alone; no name past the prompt's cut; at most ten names; excerpts cut on whole code points; no thought able to close the names' delimiter;
- the cap's precision, miss and κ, worked by hand: an unreadable answer caps, and an error counts against;
- the weighted estimate across strata;
- the bars and their edges.

Mutants: 17 of 18 fail on the check's own message. The 18th, names on one side only, fails the self-check by a crash.

**Measured** (SMD-3203, comments be3a4672 and 2a0e090a). The candidates, prompts by hash and bars were fixed before any pair was asked. Set 1 is item 3's 79 agreed pairs, held out from the error analysis. Set 2 is PR 3's 77, weighted by version 4's strata; it is the analysis's source.

| Ask | Set 1: κ | precision | miss | Set 2: precision | miss | Median per pair (sets 1, 2) | The bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| control | 0.666 | 0.826 | 0.152 | 0.686 | 0.297 | 11.6 s, 11.4 s | the floor |
| walk | 0.523 | 0.712 | 0.050 | 0.720 | 0.000 | 18.7 s, 17.6 s | misses set 1 |
| names | 0.582 | 0.755 | 0.115 | 0.697 | 0.063 | 18.0 s, 18.4 s | misses both |

- **Neither qualifies,** so decide version 5 is not built.
- **The link step is deterministic.** Asked again, the control gives the canary's recorded link on all 77 of set 2's pairs.
- **Post hoc, against the control,** both candidates trade wrong caps for misses. They turn N into F, finding real third subjects, but call D less often. On set 1 the walk fixed none of the control's 8 wrong caps and made 9 new ones.
- **The walk's own answers on the wrong caps it kept** often name the shared file as one thought's deliverable, and still call it a third subject.

**Not taken.** The 27B's reasoning mode on version 4's question: its thinking tokens would multiply the step's time on every pair.

**Next.** The maintainer chose narrower questions as round 4 (SMD-3299):
1. Each thought is asked alone for its own subject and deliverables.
2. A deterministic check finds whether either thought names the other's.
3. On a hit, one question: D, if the thought works on what it names, or H, if it only names it.
4. With no hit, F or N.
