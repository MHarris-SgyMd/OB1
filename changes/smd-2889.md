## Changelog

A thought saved without a vector — captured while the model provider was down, refused by the egress gate, or imported bare — now gets its vector by itself: the servers embed such thoughts once the provider answers and the gate allows the call, 8 a tick (`OB1_VECTOR_MEND_INTERVAL`, every 60 seconds unless set; `off` for none). Before, only re-capturing the same text or a hand-run `db/reembed.ts` gave one a vector, and a session summary saved during an outage stayed out of semantic search. The cost lands as the rows do: a backlog after an outage, or an import written bare for a later `db/reembed.ts`, is embedded on the configured provider, a hosted one's bill included, unless the knob is off. `brain_info` and a keyed `/health` say how many thoughts wait for a vector, and preflight says the knob (SMD-2889).

## FORK

The servers give a thought saved without a vector its vector, by themselves — the vector mender (SMD-2889)

**What changed.**
- **`core/mend.ts` (new), the vector mender:**
  - **`mendOnce`:** one tick, under the store's leader lock (`store.mendLock`: `pg_try_advisory_lock(hashtext('ob1:vector-mender'))` on a reserved connection; another holder makes the tick a skip). It reads up to 8 thoughts with `embedding IS NULL` in id order from a cursor (`store.listVectorless`, both stores, with the door of each row's capture event), embeds each by the capture's path (`ctx.embedder.embedCapture`: windows, blurbs and the head-window fallback), and writes it through `update_thought` with the text and the stamp it read, actor `vector-mender` through the server's door.
    - 088's presence-flip arm makes that one `update` event, and its same-text arm keeps the row's stamp, so who wrote the text and its trust stand. The event moves `updated_at`.
    - **An outage** (`providerDown`) stops the tick, one call spent, and the cursor passes the row: during an outage each tick asks once, with the next row, and a text the provider fails alone (a 500 or a timeout for that input) holds back nothing. Once the sweep has mended a row, an outage on a row is that row's failure.
    - **A failure** (a 4xx, the wrong width, a write the database refuses) holds the row for 1, 2, 4 … 32 sweeps, doubling. No other row waits on it.
    - **A write refused as `STALE_READ`, `NOT_FOUND` or `DUPLICATE_CONTENT`** (the row was edited, deleted or given a vector since the read) is left for the next sweep, which reads it afresh.
    - **A short page** ends the sweep, and the next tick starts over.
  - **The egress gate asks what the row's writer asked.**
    - **A row a key captured through a server** (its capture's `origin` is `open-brain` or `open-brain-api`) is asked as the capture was asked: the key that wrote the text (the DB-stamped `actor_name`) and the text. It is also asked as a pass asks of a row, with its metadata too, and the text is sent only when both answers allow. So a claimed `source:` opens nothing (SMD-1941), and a `source:`/`type:`/`topic:` deny term keeps the row home.
    - **Any other row** (an import, board-sync's, a raw write) is asked as `db/reembed.ts` asks: the row's metadata and text, with no actor. Its `actor_name` is a label the writing tool chose, not a key the server proved.
    - Under `OB1_CHUNK_CONTEXT`, a chat endpoint refused for the row leaves the blurbs off, and the windows go in bare, as a capture's do.
  - **`startVectorMender`:** the loop. The first tick comes 5 s after start, then one every interval, never two at once, on an unref'd timer clamped below `setTimeout`'s ceiling.
    - A tick that throws (the database away) doubles the wait, up to 32 times; a tick that runs resets it.
    - It logs when it mends (saying how many got the head window's vector or bare windows), when the provider stops answering, at a sweep's end when what it left (refused; failed or held) differs from the last sweep's, and a throwing tick when its words change. Never once per idle tick.
    - `stop()` starts no further row and writes nothing a cut call answered.
- **`index.ts` and `api.ts`:** both start the mender at boot under `OB1_VECTOR_MEND_INTERVAL` (`mendIntervalFrom`: whole seconds 1 to 86400, `0` or `off` for none; a value that does not read is the default, said). Both stop it in the drain's every-path hook, before the pool closes.
  - It is not a tracked call, so a stop never waits on a re-embed.
  - The REST core runs one so that the mender outlives the MCP endpoint moving to `mcp-server/`, which holds no store (SMD-2287).
  - The knob is declared in `root.ts`'s `Env` and forwarded by `deploy/compose.yaml` and `compose.tiers.yaml`, with an entry in `.env.example`. Preflight says it when set (an interval, off, or a value read as the default, as a warning), and its egress rows say what becomes of a refused capture under it.
- **`db/sleep.ts`:** the quiet check reads past `vector-mender`'s audit events, as it reads past ingested ones: a mend is background work and wakes no sleep.
- **`brain-info.ts`:** a guarded read, `vectorless` (`SELECT count(*) … WHERE embedding IS NULL`, with stats), so `brain_info`'s new `Vectors` row and a keyed `/health`'s `database.vectorless` say how many wait.
- **The replies (`render.ts`) and preflight:** each no-vector note says the server embeds the thought by itself once the provider answers, or once the egress policy allows the call, and that a changed policy takes effect when the server restarts. That covers the capture's provider-down note (the clause SMD-2888 promised), its egress-refused note in both forms, and the edit's no-vector note. A key that cannot read is still told no endpoint.
- **Docs:**
  - server-portable/README.md: "The vector mender" paragraph, plus the knob in the optional list;
  - SETUP.md: the gate paragraph;
  - docs/01-getting-started.md: both "no vector" remedies;

**Why.** Since SMD-2888 a capture made during an outage lands without its vector, and egress-refused captures always have. Nothing gave such a row its vector but a re-capture or a hand-run `db/reembed.ts`, whose default pool takes these rows already but which no service runs. The session hook no longer re-sends an outage capture (a capture-only key cannot see a delete), so its summaries stayed out of semantic search until this landed. That is why this was to merge before the next release cut.

**Where it runs, and why.** On the servers, on a timer, under a leader lock. A server is the one process every brain runs, the dogfood's included, and it already holds the capture's embedder, the egress policy and the store. Both servers run one, so that it survives SMD-2287 putting `mcp-server/` in front of `/mcp`, and the lock keeps two processes or replicas from embedding a row twice (measured in pass 1: two lock-free servers embedded 60 of 60 rows twice).

**Not taken.**
- **A sleep-scheduler step:** absent on a brain without `--profile sleep`, and the scheduler hard-codes its two passes in four places.
- **`reembed.ts --vectorless`:** its pool already takes these rows. What was missing was something that runs it.
- **The claim table:** `embedding IS NULL` is the state the mender resumes from, and its holds live in the process. A claim row would need the worker grant group, which the server's documented role lacks, and a failed claim is terminal until `--retry-failed`, a hand step.
- **A partial index on `embedding IS NULL`:** it would need a migration. The batch read is a sequential scan, measured at 0.9 ms over stable's 2,023 thoughts; a follow-up holds it for scale.

**Held.**
- **The egress policy is read at start,** so a row the gate refused is mended after the restart that allows it. The replies say so.
- **The replies say the server embeds the row by itself even where `OB1_VECTOR_MEND_INTERVAL=off`, and on Cloudflare Workers, where no mender runs:** the process that answers cannot tell whether one runs. Preflight's egress rows do follow the knob.
- **On long content one window's failed call fails the whole embedding,** as on a capture, so such a row is held and tried again.
- **A row held after failures waits up to 32 sweeps,** and the holds are the process's: a restart tries every row again.
- **An import and the orchestration runner's `reembed.ts`:** a row both reach is written once but may be embedded twice.
