## Changelog

`bun db/backfill-metadata.ts` runs thought-enrichment's type and sensitivity backfills in place of `recipes/thought-enrichment`'s `backfill-type.mjs` and `backfill-sensitivity.mjs`: it writes `metadata.type` (the capture's type, an alias's words in `type_raw`) and `metadata.sensitivity_tier` through `update_thought`, and the `type` and `sensitivity_tier` columns too where `schemas/enhanced-thoughts` added them, as those scripts did. `db/weekly-digest.ts` now also holds back what the sensitivity patterns (`db/sensitivity.ts`: the recipe's, widened, and the session-capture hook's secret table) rate over each row's whole text and the type, topics and tags it sends, an untagged capture included, and compares the key and the column in any case and white space; on a brain without that column it runs, by the key and the patterns, where it refused; a column the role may not read refuses the run. **Upgrading:** keep `DATABASE_URL` (not `SUPABASE_URL`) in the environment or a `.env` of the directory you run from, then call `bun <checkout>/db/backfill-metadata.ts` (a dry run) and add `--apply` to write, where you ran either script; a role of its own needs `bun db/migrate.ts --grant <role>`; `--dry-run` is refused (a run without `--apply` is one) and `--batch-size` is `--batch` (SMD-3318).
`bun db/lint-sweep.ts` runs lint-sweep's Tier 3 again: a model's reading of the newest thoughts for contradictions, stale facts, superseded decisions, orphans and missing links, through the brain's own chat endpoint and model, each thought sent only as the egress policy lets it leave and only past weekly-digest's sensitivity hold-back; `--tier all`, the default, includes it, `--max-llm-calls 0` turns it off, and under the default deny with no allow term or local endpoint it is skipped before any read. `providerCall` takes several subjects, and dials only when the gate lets every one leave. **Upgrading:** Tier 3 reads its endpoint, model and egress policy from the job's own environment, not the stack's: a job that ran the recipe's Tier 3 sets `OB1_LLM_BASE_URL` (or `OB1_CHAT_BASE_URL`) at an address the host reaches, its key, a model it serves (`OB1_METADATA_MODEL` or `--model`, which replaces `--llm-model`), and releases the tier with `OB1_EGRESS_ALLOW=actor:<the worker key's name>` (with `OB1_WORKER_KEY` and `MCP_ACCESS_KEYS`), a source, type, topic or marker its thoughts carry, or a local model (`OB1_LLM_LOCAL=1`, `OB1_CHAT_LOCAL=1` for a chat endpoint of its own); `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is still read, as `OB1_LLM_API_KEY`'s fallback, so remove it unless the endpoint is OpenRouter (SMD-3318).

PR 2b: lint-sweep's Tier 3 reads sampled thoughts through the brain's chat endpoint, each thought put to the egress gate (SMD-3318)

**What changed.** `db/lint-sweep.ts` runs Tier 3 again, which the recipe sent to OpenRouter with its own key and no egress handling: the newest dated thoughts of the last `--days` (365) whose text, trimmed, is 20 characters or more, read in the sweep's snapshot through one cursor in `created_at` order until as many as the calls can send are to be sent (`--sample-size`, 100 and at least 10, or `--max-llm-calls` × 20 if less), the window ends or 10,000 are read, then sent 20 a call (`--max-llm-calls` 5; 0 turns it off) after the transaction ends, so no lock is held across a model's answer, each batch read again under the same filters and judged again just before its call; a read that fails fails Tier 3 alone, under a savepoint, the report written with the other tiers. Each goes as a ref (`t1`…), its date, type, first 400 characters and five topics and tags (each cut to 100 characters), with today's date; the model names contradictions, stale facts, superseded decisions, orphans and missing links, each ref mapped back to its thought's id, a finding naming none of its batch dropped and counted, the model's words made safe as a preview is and cut to 300 characters. An answer that is no JSON object, or no text, loses its batch's findings, counted (the recipe stopped the run); a call that fails after the transient retries stops the tier, and the report is written with what came before (exit 1), its reason made safe. A thought counts as sent once its call is dialled. `--model` names the model (the brain's `OB1_METADATA_MODEL` by default); `--llm-model` is refused naming it. The hold-back moved from `db/weekly-digest.ts` into `db/sensitivity.ts` (`sensitivityColumn`, `tierExcluded`, `heldByPatterns`), its statements unchanged, and both read it. `providerCall` takes several subjects (`server-portable/embed.ts`), gating each, an empty list refused; the gate has a `lint` kind. **Decided 2026-11-17 (maintainer):** the gate judges each thought on its own units, as consolidation's judge does: `OB1_WORKER_KEY`'s name as the actor, its source, type and topics, and as the marker what the prompt carries of it and, apart, its whole text (both must pass, after review pass 1); a refused one is left out, and a deny term (`topic:health`) under allow holds its thoughts back. Not one made-up subject per batch, as weekly-digest's synthesis has: the gate could not see the thoughts inside it. And Tier 3 holds back what weekly-digest does: a `restricted` thought, or a `personal` one unless `--include-personal`, by its key or the enhanced-thoughts column, or rated so by the patterns over what is sent and its whole text. A policy that would refuse every thought skips the tier before any read; the worker key is resolved only when a thought could leave, and only once the connection is known to have reached the URL's database.
