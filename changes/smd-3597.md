## Changelog

Every brain can now record who is working on what. An agent claims a ticket, or one piece of it (`PR 1b-2`), under a lease, and a second agent's claim on the same work is refused with an answer naming the holder, its session and since when. A whole ticket and any of its pieces count as the same work; two different pieces don't. A claim lapses unless renewed, and an operator key can release a stuck one with a reason. This release adds the database side: the `work_claims` table and the `claim_work`, `renew_work_claim`, `release_work_claim` and `list_work_claims` functions, with a new `claims` grant group. No client calls them yet: the REST and MCP operations and the label on search results come in later releases. If your server connects as a `--grant` role, re-run `migrate.ts --grant` after upgrading so the role gets the `claims` group (SMD-3597, migration 113).

## FORK

Work claims in the core: who holds a ticket, or one piece of it, under a lease (SMD-3597)

**Why.** On 2026-10-29 a session recommended SMD-3379 PR 1b-2 while another session had its worktree open: the ticket had been In Progress since its first PR, the branch was unpushed, and nothing an agent could read said the piece was taken. The maintainer decided every brain should be able to track claimed work, so this is a core migration, not a plugin. It is the first child of SMD-3596.

**What changed** (migration 113).
- **`work_claims`**, one row per claim, kept after it ends.
  - The work: `issue`, a ticket key in the form the readers find (`stated-status.ts` KEY_RE), upper-cased and trimmed; `piece`, optional, its spaces collapsed, compared case-folded through the generated `piece_key` (`''` for the whole ticket).
  - The holder: `holder_name`, `holder_agent_id` and `holder_kind`, from the call's actor envelope (`p_actor`, set as `ob1.actor`, 029's shape) through 046's registry lookup, never from an argument. A call whose envelope names nobody raises. Sentinel `ob1:work-claim-holder-from-the-envelope`.
  - `session`, the client's own name for the agent holding it, required; `label`, free text for people.
  - The lease (`claimed_at`, `renewed_at`, `expires_at`) and the end (`released_at`, `released_by` / `_agent_id` / `_kind`, `release_reason`). Nine CHECKs hold the shapes.
  - `work_claims_one_unreleased`, a partial unique index: one unreleased row per ticket and piece. Sentinel `ob1:work-claim-one-per-work`.
- **Four calls**, each returning jsonb `{outcome, code, message, claim, held_by}`:
  - `claim_work(p_issue, p_session, p_piece, p_label, p_lease_seconds, p_actor)`: `claimed`; `renewed` for the holder's own unreleased claim on the same work; or `refused` with `WORK_CLAIMED`, a message naming the first holder, and every live claim in the way.
  - `renew_work_claim(p_claim, p_session, p_lease_seconds, p_actor)`: `renewed`, or refused with `CLAIM_NOT_FOUND`, `CLAIM_NOT_YOURS`, `CLAIM_RELEASED` or `WORK_CLAIMED`.
  - `release_work_claim(p_claim, p_session, p_reason, p_force, p_actor)`: `released`, `already_released`, or refused with `CLAIM_NOT_FOUND` or `CLAIM_NOT_YOURS`.
  - `list_work_claims(p_issues, p_holder, p_include_ended, p_limit)`: the live claims, or every claim with its state (live, lapsed, released).
  - Nine helpers (`ob1_work_claim_*`): the key, piece, session and lease checks, the envelope, the lock, the holder test, the claims in the way, and a claim as jsonb.
- **The `claims` grant group** (`db/config.mjs`): SELECT, INSERT and UPDATE on `work_claims`, no DELETE. `--grant` issues it.
- **Docs:** db/README.md (the migration's paragraph, the map, the count, the grants paragraph).

**Decided.**
- **A claim is the caller's own only when the key and the session both match.** The ticket called the client's field "trusted for nothing", but the dogfood's Claude Code sessions share one brain key: by the key alone, the 2026-10-29 sessions read as one holder and the second claim is granted. A session names nobody outside its key, so it can't make a claim look like another key's. A call naming an agent id is compared by the id; a call by name alone matches only a claim made by name alone.
- **A refusal is an answer, not an exception**, so a caller branches on it without losing its transaction. A malformed argument (not a ticket key, a blank session, a lease out of range, no holder) is the caller's bug and raises by name.
- **Lapse needs no reaper.** A claim past `expires_at` counts as free everywhere; the next claim on the same work closes it as `lapsed; taken over`, naming the taker. A lapsed claim nobody covers can still be renewed.
- **Default lease four hours**, 60 seconds to a week per call. An agent's session waits on a person for hours, and a worker's fifteen minutes (015) would lapse under it.
- **One advisory lock per ticket, READ COMMITTED only.** A whole-ticket claim and a piece claim wait for each other, and each reads under a snapshot taken after the lock; above READ COMMITTED the read would predate the lock, so the calls refuse it by name (068's rule). The unique index holds the exact-work half for a write made without the lock.
- **Not the event log.** Claims change no thought, and `thought_audit` is the thoughts' log; the table keeps its own history. SMD-2608 took the same line for a reject.
- **An operator's forced release reads the kind 046's way**: the agent id's, else the name's. A raw caller who can set `ob1.actor` can already write the table directly, so the gate is for the server's callers, whose envelope the key decides.

**Not taken.**
- **The server.** Preflight checking the `claims` group, and the REST and MCP operations, are SMD-3598; the `claimed` label on results is SMD-3599; board-sync's release when a ticket closes is SMD-3600.
- **Pruning.** An ended claim is a small row, and history is the point; nothing deletes them.

**Upgrade.** MINOR: a new table, an index, thirteen functions and a grant group. Nothing existing changes. A server connecting as a `--grant` role needs `migrate.ts --grant` again before SMD-3598's operations work, and nothing fails meanwhile.

**Verify.** test-schema [101]: the shape and sentinels; a claim's key, holder and default lease; the same key in another session refused by an answer naming the holder; the holder's own claim renewed in place; pieces against pieces and against the whole, either order; no envelope, bad keys, a blank session and leases out of range raised with nothing written; lapse and takeover aged by construction; renew of a lapsed claim, free or covered; renew and release refused for another key, another session, an unknown id, an unforced release and an agent's force, and an operator's force with no reason raised; a forced release recorded; a name-only claim not the same holder as an id-carrying call; REPEATABLE READ refused; the index refusing a raw duplicate; the list's filters and limit; the grant group, with and without; a re-apply. test-live [46]: two claims at once on real Postgres, held until the second waits on the ticket's advisory lock — the whole against a piece refused, the same piece refused by the answer and never by the index's error, two pieces both claimed. test-upgrade [20be]: refused by name without 061, nothing created; onto a populated brain at 112, no thought or audit row moved, a claim taken and another holder refused, the grant group's statement, a re-apply.
