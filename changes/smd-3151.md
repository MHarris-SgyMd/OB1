## Changelog

Decide mode's quote check now reads quote and thought without their markdown, so the writer's quote of a thought's visible words is found across `**bold**`, a code span or a link, and the writer is asked only when the decider's P(outdates) clears the pass's floor, where its proposal would be recorded (SMD-3151). The pass key moves to `@p4d3`, so a brain running decide mode judges its pool again.

## FORK

The consolidation quote check reads quote and text without their markdown, decide mode asks its writer only at the pass's floor, and decide mode's version moves to 3 (SMD-3151)

**Why.**
- **The quote check missed what the writer quotes.** `evidenceIn` decides whether a model's quote is in a thought. It folded case, whitespace, curly quotes, dashes and the ellipsis, then looked for the quote as whole words in the raw text — markdown and all. Decide mode's writer (qwen3.8:27b) quotes the words it reads, without the markup, and most thoughts are markdown: Linear tickets, session summaries, change records. On SMD-3116's writer test (56 pairs, blind labels) the writer named a side on 18 of the 22 settling pairs, and `stands` refused 11 of them; 10 of the 12 quotes it missed there are in the text word for word once markdown is read away.
- **Reading more quotes alone would have cost relations.** Under the floor (`--min-confidence`, 0.5 by default and under the sleep scheduler) a standing proposal records nothing and closes the pair's relation. Every relation the canary held for a pair whose quotes had failed sat under it: P(outdates) 0.35 to 0.49, all 67. With quotes read without markdown about three in four of them stand, so about 50 relations would have closed for no proposal (review pass 3).

**What changed.**
- **`withoutMarkdown`** (`server-portable/consolidate.ts`, new). The quote arrives on one line, so a link's text, a strike and a code span may cross one line break in the text, never a blank line.
  - An inline link or image, `[text](target)`, keeps its text and loses its target. A bare URL or an autolink stays as text.
  - Struck text goes with its `~~`, read as withdrawn, so a stale claim the current thought strikes through is not in it. GFM's rule applies: the markers flank the words and never sit inside a code span.
  - Asterisks, backticks and any bracket or `~~` left over go. An escape loses its backslash, and an opening bracket after a letter or digit reads as a space, so `argv[2]` keeps `argv` a word.
  - A heading's `#`s and a `>` run go where they open a word, not only a line. The hashes need their space, so `#433` stays, and so does `>=`; a comparison's `>` goes from both sides alike.
  - An underscore run at a word's edge is emphasis and goes; one inside a word (`snake_case`, `a__b`) stays.

  Every run a rule may take is bounded or, a single character repeated, tried from its start alone, so each costs a linear scan.
- **`evidenceIn`** reads the quote and both texts through it: each text is cut to the characters the model was sent, then folded, then folded again by `plainText`. The whole-word rule, the three-character floor and "not in the other side" are as before, over the folded text. Every caller reads it so: `stands` (decide mode's quote check), the decide note's `evidence_found` and `stale_found`, version 2's `quoteIn`, the judge's `evidence_found` and the evals' replays.
- **The writer gate** (`decidePair`): the writer is asked only when H2O's top verdict is outdates and its P(outdates), read as the pass records a proposal's confidence (`outdatesConfidence`, rounded to two places), clears the pass's floor, which `db/consolidate.ts` now passes in. Under it the pair takes H2O's best verdict that proposes nothing, as a quote that fails does: the relation that verdict gives at the floor, else its edge is closed. A relation written so carries the floor in its note. The pass counts such a pair under confidence and as a proposing verdict (its verdicts line counts the relation word it takes, as for a failed quote), settles a stale proposal on it as an outdates under the floor, and the run's summary gives the writer's calls by what came back (asked, stood, declined, quotes failed, unparsed) beside the pairs not asked. Fewer writer calls too: of the canary's 157 relations on pairs H2O called outdates, whose writer declined or whose quotes failed, 156 sat under the floor.
- **The writer's evidence quote** is kept in the decide note beside the stale one, so a recorded proposal can be checked again under another quote rule. Verify reads the canary's `@p4d3` proposals by it.
- **`DECIDE_VERSION` 3**, the pass's: version 1's writer, its quotes read without markdown, asked only at the floor. The docblock's rule is that the version moves when "the rules between" the decider and the writer change, and both decide which judgements become proposals. Version 2, the shelved record rule, stays the eval's (`--record-rule`, `--v2-quotes`). The key is `consolidate:<decider>+<writer>@p4d3`.

**Measured.**
- **What was measured is the quote check, not the pass.** The check finds more of the writer's quotes, and refused none the old one found:
  - on SMD-3116's 56 recorded writer answers, standing quotes on settling pairs went from 7 of 22 to 15 of 22, with precision from 7/11 to 15/22;
  - on a fresh blind sample of 60 canary pairs (bars fixed on the ticket before it was drawn), they went from 26 of 51 to 45 of 51, with precision from 26/30 to 45/53;
  - of 126 standing proposals asked again (the sample's 30 and 96 more), all still stand.
- **None of the gained pairs can become a proposal.** The gained ones were relations whose P(outdates) sat under the floor: 0.35 to 0.49 on all 67 the sample drew from. They are the pairs the gate now leaves at H2O's relation (above).
- **The pairs this change can turn into proposals were never measured.** These are pairs whose top verdict is outdates at the floor, with no proposal, and quotes the old check refused. Under version 1 they stored nothing, so they left no row to sample.
- **Verify is on the canary, after it runs `@p4d3`.** Its bars are fixed on SMD-3151 before the upgrade.
  - **The window:** a snapshot before the first `@p4d3` claim, and a read once every snapshotted thought still eligible is judged.
  - **What is labelled:** the new proposals the old check would have refused, read from the writer's note, which now keeps both quotes, against an arm of other `@p4d3` proposals.
  - **The gate is checked on the canary's 157 relations on outdates-topped pairs:** each keeps a relation, or the one at 0.4969, which rounds to the floor, may become a proposal.

**Not here.**
- **The judge path's proposals.** `proposalVerdict` never read `evidence_found`, so the 7B pass proposes what it did. Its recorded flag now reads quotes without markdown under its unchanged key `@p4`, and nothing reads that flag to decide anything.
- **Version 2's reading.** `quoteIn` folds markdown, but `onlyQuoted` reads the raw text, so a refutation that quotes its claim across markup no longer stands under `--v2-quotes`. Version 2 is shelved, and the eval's version 2 numbers predate the fold.
- **The writer's own quote errors.** A stale phrase the current thought repeats: 1 of the 11 in the writer test, 3 of the 6 still refused in the fresh sample. Evidence quoted from the other thought: the other 3 of those 6.
- **Preflight still reads a key as this server's by its decider and writer at any version** (Operators below). That is SMD-2655's gap for prompt versions, with the decide-version case added there.
- **Not folded:**
  - HTML entities and tables;
  - reference-style links, and links whose target holds a space or parentheses, whose text holds a bracket, or whose title is single-quoted;
  - an image inside a link, whose outer target stays;
  - single-tilde strikes;
  - `-`, `+` and `1.` list markers;
  - a fence's info string.
- **Rare quotes still missed:** one that copies half a link with its target, and one that starts inside a code span holding `~~`.

**Operators.**
- **A brain in decide mode starts a fresh pass under `@p4d3`** once it runs this tree. The sleep scheduler starts it at its first sleep. The pass judges every pooled thought again: up to `--k` tier calls each, and a writer call for each pair H2O calls outdates at the floor that has no proposal yet. The canary's `@p4d1` pass took about 6 hours of sleep at about 3.8 s a pair, and its 27B pushed stable's models out of a shared Ollama while it ran; expect the same order. `bun db/consolidate.ts --dry-run` names the pool first.
- **What it does to relations and proposals:**
  - A pair judged again has its relation rewritten under the new key.
  - A pair the pass no longer reaches keeps its `@p4d1` relation, which `--status` counts as judged under another key.
  - A proposed pair is not judged again (the candidate rule, 029; a stale one is, 063), so proposals stand as they are.
  - A thought whose neighbour was proposed or superseded reaches a further one. So some `@p4d3` proposals are pairs version 1 never judged, not the quote check's doing.
- **A `@p4d1` pass need not finish.** `@p4d3` judges every pooled thought again, and nothing takes what `@p4d1` left pending, claimed or failed. Preflight reads a key as this server's by its decider and writer, not its version, so:
  - it shows those claims as the scheduler's;
  - it offers retry_failed for failed ones;
  - after a dead worker, or with no scheduler, it offers `bun consolidate.ts`, which runs `@p4d3`.

  Clear them once upgraded: `DELETE FROM thought_work_claims WHERE work_type = 'consolidate:<decider>+<writer>@p4d1' AND status <> 'succeeded'`. The canary's `@p4d1` pass was finished when this was written: 1,900 claims, all succeeded.
- **A brain in judge mode keeps its key and its proposals.**
