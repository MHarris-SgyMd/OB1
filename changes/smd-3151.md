## Changelog

Decide mode's quote check now reads quote and thought without their markdown, so the writer's quote of a thought's visible words is found across `**bold**`, a code span or a link (SMD-3151). The pass key moves to `@p4d3`, so a brain running decide mode judges its pool again.

## FORK

The consolidation quote check reads quote and text without their markdown, and decide mode's version moves to 3 (SMD-3151)

**Why.** `evidenceIn` decides whether a model's quote is in a thought. It folded case, whitespace, curly quotes, dashes and the ellipsis, then looked for the quote as whole words in the raw text — markdown and all. Decide mode's writer (qwen3.8:27b) quotes the words it reads, without the markup, and most thoughts are markdown: Linear tickets, session summaries, change records. On SMD-3116's writer test (56 pairs, blind labels) the writer named a side on 18 of the 22 settling pairs, and `stands` refused 11 of them; 10 of the 12 quotes it missed there are in the text word for word once markdown is read away. Precision stayed at 64–67% with or without the check, so it refused true and false proposals at about the same rate.

**What changed.**
- **`withoutMarkdown`** (`server-portable/consolidate.ts`, new). The quote arrives on one line, so a link's text, a strike and a code span may cross one line break in the text, never a blank line.
  - An inline link or image, `[text](target)`, keeps its text and loses its target. A bare URL or an autolink stays as text.
  - Struck text goes with its `~~`, read as withdrawn, so a stale claim the current thought strikes through is not in it. GFM's rule applies: the markers flank the words and never sit inside a code span.
  - Asterisks, backticks and any bracket or `~~` left over go. An escape loses its backslash, and a bracket after a letter or digit reads as a space, so `argv[2]` keeps `argv` a word.
  - A heading's `#`s and a `>` run go where they open a word, not only a line. The hashes need their space, so `#433` stays, and so does `>=`; a comparison's `>` goes from both sides alike.
  - An underscore run at a word's edge is emphasis and goes; one inside a word (`snake_case`, `a__b`) stays.

  Every run a rule may take is bounded and tried from its start alone, so each costs a bounded scan.
- **`evidenceIn`** reads the quote and both texts through it: each text is cut to the characters the model was sent, then folded, then folded again by `plainText`. The whole-word rule, the three-character floor and "not in the other side" are as before, over the folded text. Every caller reads it so: `stands` (decide mode's gate), the decide note's `evidence_found` and `stale_found`, version 2's `quoteIn`, the judge's `evidence_found` and `eval-judge.ts`'s replays.
- **`DECIDE_VERSION` 3**, the pass's: version 1's writer, its quotes read without markdown. The docblock's rule is that the version moves when "the rules between" the decider and the writer change, and the quote check decides which judgements become proposals. Version 2 keeps its meaning (the shelved record rule, the eval's `--record-rule` and `--v2-quotes`). The key is `consolidate:<decider>+<writer>@p4d3`.

**Measured.**
- **Found on:** the 56 recorded writer answers of SMD-3116's test, re-checked with this tree's `stands`: recall 15/22 (was 7/22), precision 15/22 (was 7/11), the later state named in 14 of 15. Without my low-confidence settling labels: 13/16, 13/20, 13/13.
- **Verified on a fresh blind sample, its bars fixed on the ticket before it was drawn:**
  - **The sample:** 60 of the canary's `@p4d1` pairs where H2O's top verdict was outdates and the writer named a side, 30 standing proposals and 30 whose quotes failed, none read before.
  - **The writer,** asked again as the pass asks it, gave the pass's own outcome on all 60.
  - **Recall** on the 51 settling pairs: 26 (51%) → 45 (88%).
  - **Precision:** 26/30 (87%) → 45/53 (85%), against a floor of 82%.
  - **Nothing lost:** no proposal today's check lets stand is refused.
  - **Weighted** by the two populations (242 : 67): recall 79% → 95%, precision 87% → 86%.
  - **The six settling pairs still refused** are the writer's own quotes, not markdown: a stale phrase the current thought repeats (3), evidence found only in the other thought (3).

**Not here.**
- The judge path's proposals: `proposalVerdict` never read `evidence_found`, so the 7B pass proposes what it did; only the recorded flag reads truer.
- The writer's own quote errors: a stale phrase the current thought repeats (1 of the 11 in the writer test, 3 of the 6 still refused in the fresh sample), and evidence quoted from the other thought (3 of those 6).
- Not folded:
  - HTML entities and tables;
  - reference-style links, and links whose target holds a space or parentheses, whose text holds a bracket, or whose title is single-quoted;
  - an image inside a link, whose outer target stays;
  - single-tilde strikes;
  - `-`, `+` and `1.` list markers;
  - a fence's info string.
- Rare quotes still missed: one that copies half a link with its target, and one that starts inside a code span holding `~~`.

**Operators.**
- A brain in decide mode starts a fresh pass under `@p4d3` once it runs this tree: every pooled thought is judged again, its relations rewritten under the new key. Proposals stand as they are, since a pair is proposed once; the pass adds those its quotes now let through. A brain in judge mode is unaffected.
- Let a running `@p4d1` pass finish before upgrading. Any of its claims still pending, claimed or failed stay in preflight as an unfinished pass of this server's own models. The `bun consolidate.ts` that preflight suggests now works under `@p4d3`, which never takes them; with the sleep scheduler running, preflight shows the key as the scheduler's and suggests nothing. The canary's `@p4d1` pass was finished when this was written: 1,900 claims, all succeeded.
