## Changelog

Fork Checks' "Portable server" job starts `server-portable/test-server.ts` and `test-thoughts.ts` right after its first three installs, in the background, where they ran one after the other among its other steps; a step at the job's end waits on them and reports both. `test-server.ts` [17]'s keepalive ceiling case runs at 50 ms frames and a 500 ms ceiling, where it ran at 20 ms and 200 ms, so a loaded event loop's stall at its start no longer fails it (SMD-3470).

## FORK

Portable server runs its two timer-bound suites in the background (SMD-3470)

**What changed.**
- **fork-checks.yml, portable-server.** A step after the installs of `server-portable/`, `rest-client/` and `mcp-server/` starts `test-server.ts` and `test-thoughts.ts` in the background, each in a subshell with its own log, its exit code written by a rename, and a 300 s time limit. It is the data layer's pair (SMD-3195) in shape. The REST client's and MCP server's installs move up to before it, since `test-server.ts` drives an MCP server through both (SMD-2978); their comments go to the steps that run their suites. The two suites' own steps go. A last step, `if: ${{ !cancelled() }}`, waits on both up to the limit and the kill after it, and prints them as the data layer prints its pair: a passing log folded, a failing one open under an `::error` naming the suite, a hang as 124 (137 with TERM ignored), a start that never ran or a code never written each said, and both summary lines last.
- **`server-portable/test-server.ts` [17], two bounds a stall of the event loop broke.** Both are about timing under load, which the job's other suites beside this one now bring.
  - *The ceiling case:* a stream silent for 1250 ms with a 50 ms frame and a 500 ms ceiling, where it was 500 ms, 20 ms and 200 ms. The counts stay those its band was set for (nominally nine frames, about twenty-four uncapped, the band 4 to 10). `withSseKeepalive` measures its ceiling from the stream's start, so a first tick that lands past the ceiling sends no frame at all. At the old scale a stall of about 120 ms at the start failed the case, and with the job's other suites beside it, it did (below). The section's other requests take 13 s, so the longer stream costs the suite nothing.
  - *"…at the moment the client left":* the abandoned line's time must be at least 1 s, where it was 1.4. The MCP server times the request from its arrival, and the client's 1.5 s from its send, in one process, so a stall between the two reads short: a 200 ms one read 1.3 s. The floor tells the abort's moment from a line written at once; the upper bound of 3 s, against the stream's 13 s, is unchanged.

**Why.** The ticket: the two suites were 54 s and 33 s of the job's median 215, almost all of it waiting on real-clock timers (children's stop and flush bounds, [16]'s provider-state probes), one after the other on a runner whose cores sat mostly idle.

**Held.** The job's steps were run in order by a script generated from the workflow's own YAML, each under `bash -eo pipefail` in its working-directory, with GitHub's rule that a failure skips later steps except an `if: ${{ !cancelled() }}` one. Each run was in its own `oven/bun:1.4.3` container on 4 CPUs, as the image's non-root user, on a fresh copy of the tree, with the installs done by the job's own steps:
- **main's job: 189, 191 and 197 s.** The branch's, before the suite's two changes: 164 s, failed by the ceiling case alone (`0 frames in 500 ms at 20 ms with a 200 ms ceiling`; test-thoughts 845 of 845). The branch's after them: **96, 101, 110, 111, 119 and 135 s**, all green: test-server 1221 of 1221 and test-thoughts 845 of 845 every time, the last step's wait 0 s (both suites done before the job's last foreground step).
- **The wait step,** on stub suites in place of the two, under `bash -eo pipefail` on Linux:
  - both passing: exit 0, each log folded, both summary lines;
  - test-server failing with exit 3: the step fails, with `::error title=Portable server::test-server.ts failed (exit 3)` and test-thoughts' summary still printed;
  - test-thoughts hanging under a 3 s limit: `exit 124, over the 3 s limit` and "no summary line";
  - TERM ignored: `137, killed`;
  - the start step never run: "the two suites were never started";
  - a subshell that writes no code: "none, its subshell wrote no code by 1 s and 30 s".
- `bun scripts/check-fork-consistency.ts` passes: its reader reads both suites as run from `server-portable/`. Pinned actionlint 1.7.7 with shellcheck passes, and `tsc` in `server-portable/` is clean.

**Mutants** on `test-server.ts` [17], each the whole suite (`suite-mutant.ts`):
- the ceiling dropped in `sse.ts`: KILLED (`24 frames`, onStall 0, no stalled line);
- no keepalive frame sent: KILLED (8 rows);
- a 200 ms busy stall at the ceiling stream's start: passes at the new scale, and fails at the old one (`0 frames`), the case the change is for;
- in `mcp-server/app.ts`, which writes the abandoned line: timed at once (`0.0 s`), 13 s late (`14.5 s`) and 0.6 s short (`0.9 s`) are each KILLED by the floor and bound.

**Not taken.**
- *Every suite in the background.* `test-rest.ts`, `test-mcp.ts` and the runner's self-check wait on timers too (~17, 15 and 12 s), but more suites beside the typecheck's seven `tsc`s and svelte-check is more load on timing assertions like the two above. The ticket's two take the job from the ~210–262 s cluster at no more risk than the two fixes cover.
- *test-thoughts [16]'s probes side by side* (the ticket's optional second item): in the background they cost the job nothing, and its rows passed in all seven Linux runs of the job with it there.
