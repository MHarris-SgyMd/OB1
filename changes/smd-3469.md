**Departures from the ticket.**
- **The moves.** The ticket's moves came from a 10-28 run, when extract was the second-shortest cell. These come from the eight runs above.
- **~222 s.** The ticket's figure assumed a 796 s total. The cells now take about 898 job-s by the jobs API, a floor of 224.6 s a cell, and the split forecasts 226.6 s for its longest. The Verify's line is ~228 s by that measure, a little room over the forecast.
- **The run's length.** "Full stack: a second brain, the tier refresh and the canary" took a median of 243 s over the same runs, so the run's length drops by about 5 s, not the about 23 s the longest cell drops, until that job is shorter too. SMD-3392's line, 20 s under Full stack, no Supabase (202.5 s over these runs), is out of reach of any split of these sections into four cells.
- **The rest above Full stack, no Supabase.** The old reason for extract taking the rest's short sections, "to keep the rest under the full-stack jobs" (the SHARDS comment, and the workflow's "so no cell outlasts the full-stack jobs"), no longer holds once every cell is near the floor; both now say the cells are split to be about equal.
- **A CI-shift warning is expected on the rest's suite step.** ci-shift names a job or step whose last three runs on main all went over its line: the median of the ten before plus max(30 s, 20%). The rest's job moves from 192.5 to about 223 s by the API, under its line of about 231 s (in two of the eight runs, per run, it would have gone over). Its test-live step moves by the 30.2 s of [10h] and [10e], from a median of 165.5 to about 196.6 s, against a line of about 198.6 s; three of the eight runs would have gone over. Three in a row is likely, so ci-shift will probably warn on that step for a run or more after the merge, and then read the new times as its median. It blocks nothing.
