## FORK

A thought whose tickets are finished, priced before it is ranked: the eval and its verdict (SMD-2271)

**What changed.** PR 1 of SMD-2271 is the measurement; the ranking change is PR 2.
- `evals/eval-transitive-freshness.ts` — a thought's ticket references
  (central: `metadata.topics`, `metadata.action_items` and a session summary's
  header key; body: every key in its text, kept when the brain holds a head
  with a known status), six candidate rules for demoting a thought with no
  lifecycle of its own, and 059's re-sort with the transitive reason beside the
  reflexive one, as a TypeScript oracle. Read-only against a brain (every
  connection opens read-only): the census (what each rule moves among the
  thoughts nothing supersedes, each settled key's fan-out, the most-mentioned
  keys' passing mentions) and a panel of planning, held-out, control and
  topical queries scored against the labels. `--self-check` holds the rules,
  the oracle, the verdict's pick, (a)–(c)'s n/a cases, kappa and the labels'
  provenance with no database, and runs in Fork Checks.
- `evals/fixtures/transitive-freshness-labels.json` — 80 hits labelled stale,
  current or neutral (ids and numbers only), two graders per round given the
  rubric, the hits' text and the tickets' statuses and nothing of the rules:
  round 1 49 hits (κ 0.876; four adjudicated by the author), round 2 25 (1.000),
  round 3 6 (κ 0.250; two settled by a third blind grader).
- `evals/README.md` — the section with the measurement and the verdict.

**Why.** `prefer_current` (059) demotes a thought by its own lifecycle. A
session summary has none, and its one `supersedes` slot holds its own
checkpoint chain, so on "what should we work on next" the brain returned
summaries recommending finished tickets above the tickets' own Done rows.
SMD-2271 asks for that thought to inherit the lifecycle of the tickets it is
about, measured before it is built.

**Measured after.** The first registration failed: its four rules (central,
central without the header key, central with a body-share fallback, body share
alone) each flipped the measured case and each demoted a hit both graders call
current, every one naming an open ticket. A second registration, with an open
veto (never demote a thought naming an open ticket anywhere), was written before
it ran: `central+share-veto` qualifies and wins over `central-veto` (H1–H4 stale
hits 8 against 11; 17 under shipped search), with no labelled current hit
demoted anywhere in its windows and the plan's rank unchanged. It moves 146 of
the 563 lifecycle-less thoughts nothing supersedes (138 session summaries); one
settled key helps decide at most 6. The held-out queries share most hits with
P1–P4 — outside every P/C window both veto rules leave 0 stale hits and shipped
search 1 — so their stale comparison is not out-of-sample evidence; the
out-of-sample evidence is the round-2/3 current hits no veto rule demotes.

**Held.** The oracle's control rule equals `search_thoughts_current`'s top 10
on all 12 panel queries, and with every ticket head read as open every rule
gives that ranking. 21 self-check mutants killed, among them the open veto, the
lifecycle guard, the key pattern's word boundaries, each tie-break, the weight,
the share, the pick's margin, a shifted label decoding and a deleted fixture.
