## FORK

A thought whose tickets are finished, priced before it is ranked: the eval and its verdict (SMD-2271)

**What changed.** PR 1 of SMD-2271 is the measurement; the ranking change is PR 2.
- `evals/eval-transitive-freshness.ts` — a thought's ticket references
  (central: `metadata.topics`, `metadata.action_items` and a session summary's
  header key; body: every key in its text, kept when the brain holds a head
  with a known status), six candidate rules for demoting a thought with no
  lifecycle of its own, and 059's re-sort with the transitive reason beside the
  reflexive one, as a TypeScript oracle. Read-only against a brain: the census
  (what each rule moves, each settled key's fan-out, the most-mentioned keys'
  passing mentions) and a panel of planning, held-out, control and topical
  queries scored against the labels. `--self-check` holds the rules, the
  oracle, kappa and the labels' provenance with no database, and runs in Fork
  Checks.
- `evals/fixtures/transitive-freshness-labels.json` — 74 panel hits labelled
  stale, current or neutral, as ids and labels only. Each round had two graders
  who were given the rubric, the hits' text and the tickets' statuses, and
  nothing of the rules: round 1 (49 hits, κ 0.876, four adjudicated) and
  round 2 (25 hits, agreement 1.000).
- `evals/README.md` — the section with the measurement and the verdict.

**Why.** `prefer_current` (059) demotes a thought by its own lifecycle. A
session summary has none, and its one `supersedes` slot holds its own
checkpoint chain, so on "what should we work on next" the brain returned
summaries recommending finished tickets above the tickets' own Done rows.
SMD-2271 asks for that thought to inherit the lifecycle of the tickets it is
about, and asks that the rule be measured before it is built.

**The verdict.** The first registration failed. Its four rules (central, central
without the header key, central with a body-share fallback, body share alone)
each flipped the measured case, but each also demoted a hit both graders call
current, and every such miss names an open ticket. A second registration, with
an open veto (never demote a thought that names an open ticket anywhere), was
written before it ran and judged on four held-out planning queries.
`central+share-veto` qualifies and wins. Held out, its stale hits in the top
10s fall from 17 to 8; no current hit is demoted in any window; the release
plan's rank is unchanged. It moves 208 of the 747 thoughts that have no
lifecycle (199 of them session summaries), and one settled key decides at most
9 demotions. Two amendments to the first registration are disclosed in the
eval's header: the control's premise, and the check that first missed a current
hit pushed out of the top 10.

**Held.** The oracle's control rule equals `search_thoughts_current`'s top 10
on all 12 panel queries; with every ticket head read as open, every rule gives
that ranking exactly. Three mutants were each killed by the self-check: the open
veto dropped, the transitive rule read for a row with its own lifecycle, and the
key pattern without word boundaries.

**Left.** The rank arm (PR 2: the rule in `search_thoughts_current` under
`prefer_current`, its own reason on a demoted hit, the SQL held to this oracle).
About half the stale hits remain, because they name an open ticket somewhere.
The header key couples the rule to the session hook's header format.
Re-deriving a stale summary belongs to SMD-2243's pool.
