## Changelog

A batch or metric OTEL setting (`OTEL_BSP_*`, `OTEL_BLRP_*`, `OTEL_METRIC_EXPORT_INTERVAL`, `OTEL_METRIC_EXPORT_TIMEOUT`) that is ignored or held to its bound is now said at start by its name and the value used, never the value set. Before, the server's log and `preflight --deep`'s telemetry row said up to 32 characters of a value that was not a whole number and the whole of one held to its bound, and a queue size or metric interval the operator set was said as the bound or fallback of its export size or timeout, so a secret pasted into the wrong variable was printed (SMD-3235).

## FORK

A whole-number OTEL setting is said without its value (SMD-3235)

**What changed.**
- **`server-portable/otel-setup.ts`:** neither of `wholeNumber`'s notes says the value. A value that is not a whole number of at least the minimum and one held to its bound are each said as "(a value not shown)" after the variable's name, where telemetry.ts says an exporter or protocol value it does not know. A number is not shown either: a secret of digits alone parses as one, and was said in either note, whole when held (a card number) and quoted when under the least (a PIN). Nor is the other value of a pair: the export size is held to the queue and the metric timeout to the interval, so where the operator set the queue or the interval and it was neither ignored nor held, the note names it in place of its value, as the bound and as a fallback equal to it. A default's number, or a bound the queue or interval was itself held to, is said.
- **`deploy/README.md`** says so under the OTEL settings table.
- **test-server:**
  - The notes' rows say "(a value not shown)" in place of the value.
  - A row holds both notes for text, a fraction, digits past the bound and below the least, and an infinity; another holds a pair's set value named as a bound and as a fallback, and a default's or an unset, blank or held queue's or interval's number said.
  - A real `setUpTelemetry` with every signal on, a secret in each of the ten whole-number variables, once as text and once as digits: each variable named once, in a note of its whole shape, and no four characters of the secret in any line.
- **test-preflight:** `preflight --deep` with a secret in `OTEL_BSP_SCHEDULE_DELAY` warns, names the variable, and prints no four characters of it.

**Why.** SMD-2909's review pass 4, a secrets adversary, found `wholeNumber` saying `JSON.stringify(asked.slice(0, 32))`. The same line is on main since SMD-1849.

**Held.** test-server 1202, 4 more than main (1198); test-preflight 918, 1 more than main. Putting the old line back fails 10 test-server rows, the new ones among them; saying the number again in either note, four characters of the value in one variable's note, or a pair's value in place of its name, fails a row too.

**Not taken.** Settings outside telemetry still say their whole value when it is wrong: `OB1_STOP_GRACE` (shutdown.ts), `OB1_VECTOR_MEND_INTERVAL` (core/mend.ts), `OB1_PROVIDER_REPROBE_INTERVAL` (provider-health.ts), `OB1_PROPOSALS_WARN_DAYS` (preflight.ts, 40 characters), `OB1_TIER` and `OB1_BACKFILL_LIMIT` (db/config.mjs), the auth server's whole numbers (deploy/auth/config.ts) and jev's (jev/serve.ts). They are SMD-3261. `OTEL_SERVICE_NAME` (or `service.name` in `OTEL_RESOURCE_ATTRIBUTES`) is still said in the line naming where the signals go: the collector is sent it as a resource attribute anyway, so hiding it in the log would hide nothing. A value of `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_TIMEOUT` (or a signal's own) past a timer's longest reaches stderr in Bun's TimeoutOverflowWarning, since the SDK reads it unclamped: SMD-3277.
