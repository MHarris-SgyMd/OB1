## Changelog

What consolidation finds about two tickets the board does not link can now reach the board. With `LINEAR_COMMENT_API_KEY` set, board-sync ends each pass by posting one comment on the newer ticket of such a pair: the two tickets, what the judge found (a pending `outdates` proposal, or a standing `related`, `evolves` or `duplicate` relation), its confidence and how to act on it. It only proposes. It never adds a link, moves a status or edits a description. `db/board-findings.ts` runs the same step on its own, with `--dry-run`. At most `OB1_FINDINGS_POST_CAP` comments (5 by default, 0–100) go out in any 24 hours, counted from what this brain recorded posting. Nothing leaves until the egress gate allows it; under the default deny that takes `OB1_EGRESS_ALLOW=type:board-finding`. The key is a separate Linear key that can comment, not board-sync's `LINEAR_API_KEY`, which it only reads with; board-sync warns when the two are the same. Migration 086 adds `board_findings_posted`, one row per ticket pair and finding word, so a pair is told once per word. A marker line in the comment, read back from both tickets before posting, keeps a second brain from posting it again (SMD-2681).

## FORK

Consolidation findings between two unlinked tickets are posted as one Linear comment on the newer ticket, opt-in, capped and through the egress gate (SMD-2681)

**What changed.**
- **`db/board-findings.ts` (new).** The engine and a CLI (`--dry-run`, `--cap`).
  - **What it selects.** The UNION of pending proposals and standing relation facets whose two sides are Linear rows (`source = 'linear'`) filed under two different tickets the board does not link, using SMD-2680's board-pair rule from `brain-info.ts`:
    - 079's identity, `coalesce(ticket, issue)`, which must read as an identifier;
    - `NOT consolidation_tickets_linked`;
    - no active `duplicate_of` in either direction.

    It also drops anything already recorded for that pair and word. The rule is read when posting, because a link made after a verdict never moves the proposal. It is narrower than `brain_info`'s board counts, since a fork change record or a capture can carry a `ticket` too.
  - **One comment per ticket pair.** The findings are grouped by pair and ranked by confidence, then age. The comment goes on the newer side of the best finding. It names the two identifiers and, for each finding:
    - the word and its direction;
    - the confidence;
    - a proposal's one-line reason, as a code span, with `consolidate.ts --accept/--reject <id>`;
    - for a relation, the board link it suggests.

    It ends with the marker line `ob1-finding A B words`, made of letters, digits and hyphens. It is read back through `normalised()`, because Linear returns markdown derived from rich text, with identifiers autolinked and escapes added.
  - **Posted once.** Both tickets' comments are read first. A word a marker already names is recorded `found` and not posted.
    - Each post holds one transaction-scoped advisory lock for the brain, and re-reads the record and the day's count under it. Its rows are written after Linear answers, in that same transaction. So a failed post records nothing, and two posters at once neither double-post nor exceed the cap.
    - Board-sync passes its census, less any ticket the pass failed to fetch. A pair with a ticket off it is not asked of Linear, and a listed ticket the comment key can't find fails the pass. Without a census (the CLI), only Linear's "Entity not found" (or no issue and no error) counts as no such ticket.
    - The report gives each pair one outcome.
    - The key is the pair and the word, not a finding id, so a relation that a re-judge replaced at another score isn't posted again.
  - **Capped and gated.**
    - The cap counts the comments this brain posted in the last 24 hours.
    - Each comment is a subject of its own for `mayLeaveBox`: type `board-finding`, source `board-findings`, its text for `marker:`. These are values a capture is unlikely to carry. `EgressSubject.kind` gains `finding`.
    - A refusal before the board is read costs no Linear request. The CLI prints the comment; board-sync prints one line.
- **`db/sync-linear.ts`.** With `LINEAR_COMMENT_API_KEY` set, the step runs after each pass, never under `--audit` or `--only`, and dry under `--dry-run`.
  - A failed post fails the pass, so the heartbeat says `failed`.
  - A bad cap exits 2.
  - A refusing policy, a missing 079/086, or a role without the grants it needs is said once at start, and the step stays off.
  - It warns when the comment key is the read key.
  - A step that throws is named ("findings step failed") and fails the pass.
  - Its up-front egress refusal is judged without the findings' own terms: `type:board-finding` names a unit a section row carries, so before this it cleared the refusal and every ticket landed without a vector.
- **`db/linear-api.ts`.** `linearClient` takes an endpoint URL (a test's stub) and a per-request timeout; the step uses 20 s, since the post is made inside the recording transaction.
- **Migration 086.** Adds `board_findings_posted`:
  - one row per (ticket_a < ticket_b, word), with `posted_on` one of the two, `origin` posted or found, the comment id and the finding ids;
  - a partial index for the cap;
  - only ever inserted.

  The `structure` grant group gains `SELECT, INSERT` on it and `SELECT` on `supersession_proposals`, so a board-sync role needs `--groups capture,structure`.
- **Compose and env.** Board-sync forwards `LINEAR_COMMENT_API_KEY` and `OB1_FINDINGS_POST_CAP`. Both are documented, commented out, in `deploy/.env.example`.

**Why.** Board-sync only read the board. A finding about two tickets reached it only when someone read the queue. On October 1, someone read 98 proposals by hand to link 12 pairs. This is step 2 ("tell") of SMD-2682.
- **Why board-sync's loop.** That container already reaches Linear and stamps a heartbeat, and its pass has just refreshed the links that "not linked" is read on.
- **Why its own key.** Board-sync's key is a whole account used only to read.
- **Why the cap.** The judge's confidence does not yet rank real findings (SMD-2705).

**Tests.**
- **`db/test-board-findings.ts` (new, hermetic, in CI).** Covers:
  - grouping and rank;
  - the comment: what it carries, one-line reasons, the marker last;
  - the marker read back;
  - the cap's read;
  - a marker read back autolinked, escaped or emphasised, as Linear may return it;
  - the gate: deny, the two terms, `marker:`, another sink's term and the words a capture might carry, an allow policy's deny term.
- **test-live [40], against a stub Linear GraphQL endpoint.** One comment per unlinked pair on the newer ticket, including a section row and two words on one pair, and only ticket reads and comments in the request log. Nothing is posted for:
  - a session note on either side;
  - a fork change record's `ticket`, or a lower-case identity;
  - one ticket's two rows;
  - an accepted, rejected or stale proposal;
  - a pair linked after the verdict;
  - a `duplicate_of` in the reverse direction;
  - a closed relation.

  It also covers:
  - a marker already on the other ticket of the pair, as Linear returns it: recorded found;
  - a lost record: the marker stops a second post;
  - a re-run and a replaced relation: no request;
  - the cap, the day after it, and a found pair taking no place under it;
  - deny: no request, comment printed;
  - a dry run;
  - a failed post, `success: false` and a signal, then the retry;
  - a ticket Linear doesn't have: reported, not failed;
  - a body that lost a found word: gated again;
  - two posters at once: one place left under the cap posts once, and one pair is posted once;
  - the step's setup from the environment, its cap, a signal passed through, and a Linear that doesn't answer (given up on at the bound, exit 1; the comment it made anyway is found on the next run);
  - a duplicate either side holds, and a new word on a pair told before (posted, that word alone);
  - read failures other than "Entity not found", an empty answer, and "Entity not found" sent as HTTP 400;
  - the census: a ticket off the board asks nothing, a listed ticket the key can't find fails;
  - two posters on two different pairs with one place left;
  - a dry run under the cap and the gate;
  - a granted role (`capture,structure`), without INSERT, and a dry run's reads alone;
  - every run giving each pair exactly one outcome.
- **Schema and upgrade.** test-schema [76] (086's shape and constraints) and test-upgrade [20ah].
