## Changelog

test-live's re-embed-first cases, which had run at the end of [38], are a section of their own, [38r], in the long cell; [10g] moves from long to the sleep cell. The two sections share [38]'s scaffolding through one helper, `sleepScaffold(sql)`, which empties the brain and records what either section's cases need, so each runs alone in its cell. db-live's sleep cell had become the run's longest job (SMD-3392).

## FORK

test-live [38]'s re-embed cases split out as [38r], and the sleep and long cells rebalanced (SMD-3392)

**What changed** (`db/test-live.ts`).
- **`sleepScaffold(sql)`:** [38]'s setup, moved into a helper both sections call on a client of their own. It empties the brain of thoughts, entities, logged searches, heartbeats, the extraction key and the passes' claims; starts the stub model and embedding server; and returns the scheduler's env and the reads and runs the cases share (`capture`, `claims`, `beat`, `sleepRun`, `bounded`, `intercept`, `around`, the call logs, the keys).
- **The stub's switches are one object, `sw`.** They were fourteen `let`s the stub read per request, which a helper can't hand back. Their 130 references became `sw.<name>`, rewritten by the type checker's symbol resolution, so strings, property names and shadowing locals were left alone. Their comments moved onto the object's fields.
- **[38r]:** the `── Re-embed first (SMD-2679 PR 4b)` block under its own header, unchanged but for its indentation, its `sw.` switches and its try merged with the section's. It starts the outage schedule at 1 s, as [38]'s cases left it, so its waits stay as they were. In a full run [38] leaves the stub's extraction key recorded; the helper clears it, and extraction's first pass records it again. Its finally puts the embedding model back, as the block's own did, then stops the stub and clears the heartbeats, as [38]'s does. [38b]'s comment on the extraction key it sets aside names [38r], which it now follows in both the full run and the long cell.
- **`SHARDS`:** sleep is `[10g]` and `[38]`; long is `[9]`, `[38r]` and `[38b]`; extract and the rest are unchanged. The comment above it says why.
- **db/README.md:** the sleep shard's count is 117 (was 111) and long's 280 (was 286); the full run's 1736 is unchanged. The sleep scheduler's section no longer says a re-embed pass is "not yet here", which it has been since SMD-2679 PR 4b-1, and names [38r].
