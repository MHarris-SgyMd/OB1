## FORK

test-live [38]'s re-embed cases split out as [38r], and the four cells rebalanced (SMD-3392)

**What changed** (`db/test-live.ts`).
- **`sleepScaffold(sql)`:** [38]'s setup, moved verbatim into a helper both sections call on a client of their own. It empties the brain of thoughts, entities, logged searches, heartbeats and the passes' claims; starts the stub model and embedding server; and returns the scheduler's env and the reads and runs the cases share (`capture`, `claims`, `beat`, `sleepRun`, `bounded`, `intercept`, `around`, the call logs, the keys).
- **The stub's switches are one object, `sw`.** They were fourteen `let`s the stub read per request, which a helper can't hand back. Their 130 references became `sw.<name>`, rewritten by the type checker's symbol resolution, so strings, property names and shadowing locals were left alone. Their comments moved onto the object's fields.
- **[38r]:** the `── Re-embed first (SMD-2679 PR 4b)` block, unchanged but for its indentation, under its own header. It starts the outage schedule at 1 s, as [38]'s cases left it, so its waits stay as they were. It records no extraction key, which [38] left: extraction's first pass records one on a brain with none. Its finally puts the embedding model back, stops the stub and clears the heartbeats, as [38]'s does.
- **`SHARDS`:** sleep is `[10g]` and `[38]`; long is `[9]`, `[38r]` and `[38b]`; extract and the rest are unchanged. The comment above it says why.
- **db/README.md:** the sleep shard's count is 117 (was 111) and long's 280 (was 286); the full run's 1736 is unchanged. The sleep scheduler's section no longer says a re-embed pass is "not yet here", which it has been since SMD-2679 PR 4b-1, and names [38r].

**Why.** On main's runs to cf3fa56d, [38] took about 250 s of the sleep cell's 254 s suite step: 174 s of its own cases and 76 s of re-embed's, timed from the job logs. The sleep job took 268–278 s, against 261–288 s for Full stack, no Supabase, the usual longest job. A section runs whole in one cell, so [38] set that cell's floor.

**Held.** Locally, on throwaway Postgres 16 containers:
- the sleep shard passes its 117 assertions and long its 280;
- the full run passes all but [28]'s two replay rows, SMD-3342's, which fail on main's full runs too and pass in the rest shard. [doc] counts 72 headers, 16 listed sections run, the shards adding up to 1736;
- [38r] alone, with `SHARDS.long` set to it on a fresh database, passes its 23; after [9], with the helper's `DELETE FROM thoughts` dropped, it passes too.
