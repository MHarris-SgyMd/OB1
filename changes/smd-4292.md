## Changelog

The session hook (1.10.2) reads the tickets an ask names with the ask's secrets blanked, as the summary blanks them, so a ticket-shaped value a secret holds, such as `password=ABC-1234`, no longer reaches the brain in a summary's header, where the brain keyed it as the session's subject, or in an episode's "begun with" and "ended when … was taken up" lines, under `redact` or `refuse` (SMD-4292). Such a value also no longer begins an episode or decides where a session splits, so under `refuse` the episode holding it is refused whole, as one holding any other secret is. A real ticket the secret scan takes for a secret is dropped from the header with it. **Upgrading:** pull the hook's checkout; no settings change. Thoughts captured before 1.10.2 keep such a value in their header and episode lines: a later capture of the same session supersedes some, but a superseded thought still holds its text, and an episode the new split no longer has keeps its thought live. A clean episode can carry it too ("ended when ABC-1234 was taken up"), its asks showing no marker. Find them by the value itself, which you typed: a keyword search of the brain for it (`search_thoughts_keyword`), superseded thoughts included, and forget each session summary it finds with `forget_thought`.

## Record

An ask's tickets are read with its secrets blanked, so a ticket-shaped secret heads no summary and begins no episode (SMD-4292)

**Problem.** The capture hook blanks secrets in a session's prompts before it renders the summary (SMD-2127, `redactEpisode`). The tickets an ask names were read from the raw ask, before any blanking: `apply` filled the episode's `named` from them, and the segmenter read them to decide where an episode ends and what began the next. So a ticket-shaped value inside a secret (`set password=ABC-1234 and go`) was blanked in the listed prompt but:
- headed the summary (`headerTickets` lists `named`), the payload saying `tickets_from: asks`, and the brain's subject rule keyed it as one of the session's subjects (found by SMD-3306 PR 7a-3b-2's review pass 4);
- began a new episode, whose summary said "begun with ABC-1234", while the episode before said "ended when ABC-1234 was taken up" (found by this change's own end-to-end case, after a first fix that filtered `named` in `redactEpisode` alone left it there). Under `refuse` the episode holding the secret was refused, but the clean one before it was sent with that line (review pass 1): the ticket's premise, that `refuse` sends nothing, was wrong.

The rendered text's second scan cannot see it: a ticket-shaped word alone matches no secret pattern. A short password typed in that shape was stored in clear text in a thought every reader of the brain can find.

**Change** (`recipes/session-capture-hook/session-capture.mjs`, 1.10.2).
- **`askTickets`**, beside `ticketsIn`, reads an ask's tickets from the ask as the summary blanks it: flattened to one line, as `redactEpisode` blanks each prompt, with `redactSecrets` applied. It keeps only tickets the raw ask names, read with the same teams, since blanking can join a word to its marker into a ticket shape. Both readers of an ask's tickets go through it: `apply` (the session's rollup and each episode's `named`) and the segmenter's boundary check. So nothing downstream sees a ticket a secret held, as the scan of the whole prompt judges it: the header, `tickets_from`, an episode's `about`, its boundaries, the begun and ended lines, and the dry run's line per episode. (The rendered text's own scan can judge a clipped prompt otherwise: below.)
- **Both modes.** The ticket's Work limited the fix to `redact`. Under `refuse` too, read raw, the secret split off an episode of its own and the clean episode before it was sent naming it. Read blanked, it splits nothing, and the one episode holding it is refused whole, as an episode holding `password=hunter2` always was.
- **Its cost.** The scan runs once per ask, cached per event, and only for an ask whose raw text names a ticket, as the session's teams read it: it grows with the bytes of such asks, about 20 ms per MB, and an ask naming none pays nothing. `summariseTranscript` on 3,000 asks of 2 KB each naming an upper-case ticket (6.6 MB) took 55 ms before and 191 ms now (`~/.cache/smd4292/run/timing.mjs`, not in the repo; an earlier version's lower-case keys named no ticket, so its 43 and 161 measured asks that now skip the scan).
- The README's secret-scan paragraph and `metadata.json` (1.10.2) say so.

**Held.** The hook's suite:
- **Segmenter cases:** a password `ABC-1234` with no team known, and `smd-4321` on an `smd-610` branch, are in no `named`, head no summary (the second falls to the place's SMD-610) and are in no text. A second ask holding the secret splits no episode. A ticket named outside the secret in the same ask stays on the header, from the asks.
- **Through `prepare`:** a transcript whose ask sets a ticket-shaped password. The value is in no field of the payload, the prompt shows `[redacted:password assignment]`, one redaction is counted, and the payload has no `tickets_from`. Under `refuse`, a clean ask and then the password's: one episode ("the summary for session …"), refused, nothing sent, and the message names no value.
- **`--dry-run`** on a two-episode transcript whose first ask holds the password: both episode lines say "no ticket", and the output carries the value nowhere.
- **Pass 1 to 3 cases:** a quoted secret across a line break, as the first ask and a later one; a Slack webhook glued to a ticket-shaped word, with a real ticket beside it and with a lower-case mention of the word before a team is known; an ai-title holding a quoted secret across a line break, and one whose key the 120-character clip would cut.
- **Mutants,** each failing a case:
  - the asks read raw;
  - `apply` alone reading them raw;
  - the segmenter alone reading them raw;
  - the fast path inverted;
  - one cached text shared by every ask;
  - the ask blanked as written, not on one line;
  - no raw filter;
  - the title blanked as written (review pass 2);
  - the raw filter read with no teams, and the title flattened but not blanked (review pass 3).

**Review pass 1.** A secrets adversary, through `prepare` and `--dry-run` on Claude Code and Codex transcripts, and a cold reader who compared main's segmentation with the branch's over 39 realistic asks.
- **Limiting the rule to `redact`,** the cold reader's first remedy for `refuse` merging a clean episode into a refused one, sent the value: the clean episode said "ended when ABC-1234 was taken up". Run before the commit and reverted; the adversary confirmed it independently (caught: run-it, cold-read).
- **A quoted secret whose closing quote is on the next line** (`password = "hunter22⏎QQZ-9876"`, a shell continuation, CRLF, curly quotes, a Codex transcript) headed the summary: blanked as written, the scan cut it at its first space, while the summary, blanking the flattened prompt, blanked it whole. The ask is now blanked on one line (caught: run-it; held: two cases, first and later ask).
- **Blanking added a ticket:** a Slack webhook URL glued to `XY-12` is blanked from its first letter, leaving `XY-12[redacted:…]`. Only tickets the raw ask names are kept (caught: cold-read; held: a case).
- **The record and the code said `refuse` sends nothing.** It refuses per episode (caught: cold-read).

**Review pass 2.** A fresh secrets adversary on pass 1's two rules, and an operator walkthrough comparing main's hook with the branch's over 29 realistic sessions, both modes, the upgrade, and every claim of the record. No regression: the realistic sessions summarise exactly as on main.
- **The title was blanked as written but shown on one line,** the mismatch pass 1 closed for prompts: an ai-title's quoted value whose closing quote is on the next line was cut there and the rest sent. `redactEpisode` blanks the title flattened (caught: run-it; held: a case).
- **The Changelog said the value no longer reaches the brain,** but thoughts captured before 1.10.2 keep it: superseding keeps their text, and a chain the new split no longer has stays live. The Changelog's Upgrading line says how to find and forget them (caught: walkthrough).
- **The README said nothing of the new rule where an operator would look:** the ticket rules and the `redactions=N` entry now point to it; "in either mode" said of the summary's blanking is reworded. The record's cost is now per MB, with the function timed and where the script is (caught: walkthrough).

**Review pass 3.** An independent mutation tester (29 mutants of its own over every changed line) with a claims audit, and a decision table of 280 cells per hook over the segmenter's rules (place × where the secret sits × case × mode), main against the branch. The table found no defect. Its oracle, the branch on a ticket-shaped secret against main on `password=hunter22`, matched in all 260 comparable cells, and main sent the value in 162 payloads, the branch in none.
- **The raw filter read with no teams** let a lower-case mention of a key, before any team is known (no ticket), admit the one blanking made (`about abc-12: ABC-12https://hooks.slack.com/…`). It is read with the teams, which also skips the scan for an ask naming no ticket (caught: mutant; held: a case).
- **No case held the title blanked before its clip:** a mutant flattening it without blanking left a fragment of a key the 120-character clip cut (caught: mutant; held: a case).
- **The Upgrading line's way to find the stored values missed the main one:** a clean episode saying "ended when ABC-1234 was taken up" shows no marker. It now says to search for the value itself (caught: cold-read).
- **The cost said "none for an ask naming no ticket"** while the fast path skipped only asks with no ticket-shaped word, and the timing's lower-case keys named none; "233 ms scanned three times" did not reproduce. Reworded and retimed. Two Held claims were not asserted (the dry run's second line, "one episode" under `refuse`); both are now (caught: cold-read).

**Left as it is.**
- **The rendered text's scan can judge a clipped prompt otherwise than the whole prompt's.** Two rules read a cut run differently (the entropy bar and its word ratio; the Google key's trailing boundary): a key-like run with a long lowercase tail, cut by the 200-character clip, is cleared whole and blanked clipped. A ticket-shaped part of it then heads the summary, under `refuse` names the clean episode before it, and `--dry-run` prints it. The value is one the scan of the whole prompt judged no secret, and it needs a run cut just so. The fix belongs in the scan, a clipped run taking the whole prompt's verdict, and is a ticket of its own (review pass 2, secrets adversary).
- **`--dry-run` under `refuse`** prints a refused episode's text unblanked, on the operator's own terminal, before its `would REFUSE` line. Older than this change.
- **A real ticket the scan takes for a secret** is dropped from the header and from the split: `Password: SMD-1234 (reset flow)`, `passwd: SMD-1234`, a long mixed-case slug such as `SMD-1234-update-the-OAuth2-flow-for-GitHub-Apps-v3`, `SMD-1234=<token>`. Its ask was blanked in the text before this change too.
- **A thought already captured with such a value** keeps it. A session in progress across the upgrade splits differently once, so its episode chains renumber: some of its thoughts are superseded, which keeps their text; a chain the new split no longer has keeps its thought live, the value in its header, beside a new one of the same content. The Changelog's Upgrading line says how to find and forget them (review pass 2, walkthrough).
- **A branch or directory named like a secret** (`password=QQZ-9876`) still gives the place's ticket from its raw name; only the rendered `(branch)` is blanked. It needs a contrived name.
- **The secret scan's own limits:** a secret split across two asks (`set password=`, then `QQZ-9876 now`) is blanked nowhere, and a value under its length floor (`password=QQ-12`) is no secret to it.
