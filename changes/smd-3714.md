### PR 1: [38]'s pause cases on a scaled clock

**What changed** in [38] ([OB1 PR 627](https://github.com/MHarris-SgyMd/OB1/pull/627)):
- **A section clock, as [38b]'s:** `SCALE` 0.6 and `scaled(ms)`, passed as `quietMs` and `pollMs`. It covers:
  - the follow run's pause cases, whose call in hand runs 3.6 s and is asserted in hand at the pause, with only its extraction finishing while paused;
  - the drain with `--abort-after`;
  - the live write on a thought in hand.
- **Kept at their seconds:** cases whose margins rest on a pass's start, a read's 250 ms poll lag, or the 1 s poll. Among them is paused time off the clock, which two review passes couldn't scale cleanly.
- **The start-refusal case.** Since SMD-3379 PR 1c-3b it waited 10 s on every run for an `awake:` line that no longer comes, and asserted nothing about it. It now waits for the sleep, reads the pause or the wake, and asserts one: 15 s to 3 s.

**Why.** [38] grew from 179 s to 296 s (the median of two runs) with SMD-3379 PR 1c-3b (OB1 PR 617), most of it cases waiting out real timers: in run 41573992916 the 14 largest gaps were 141 of its 300 s span.

**Held, measured, reviewed.**
- **Mutants:** 1c-3b's twenty-one weren't kept, so twelve were rebuilt, and eleven are killed. The twelfth, the clock's rest from the sleep's start, survives the unscaled test too, as noted on SMD-3379.
- **Assertions:** 110, as before.
- **Times:** on CI's final head (run 41688428883), [38] spanned 267 s from 300, and the sleep cell's job took 292 s by GitHub's job time, from 317.
- **Review:** three passes and a boyscout. Passes 1 and 2 each found a test-teeth regression of its own, both fixed.

The lists, the mutant table and the review-pass table are the merged note's (`git show 53677299:changes/smd-3714.md`).
