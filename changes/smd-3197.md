## FORK

The session capture hook's suite: slow posts held by a gate the cases release, not 2.5 s sleeps (SMD-3197)

**What changed** (`recipes/session-capture-hook/test-session-capture.mjs`).
- **The gate:** a `[[slow]]` or `[[slow-down]]` post is held at the fake endpoint until its case lets it through. `releaseSlow()` lets through the posts held now, and `openSlow()` every post until the next case's `closeSlow()`. `slowArrived(n)` waits for the n-th arrival, so a case waits for the post it stages around. A post no case lets through goes on after 10 s and is counted, and the suite's last assertion fails on it.
- **Fourteen staged cases**, and [8], now wait for the slow post's arrival and assert it, where eleven slept 600 ms, 700 ms or 3 s and three waited on claims alone. Each stages its files, then lets the post through:
  - two release only the post held, for the second that follows: the chained compaction's follow-up, and the five tails;
  - [8] lets its detached post through only after the foreground's budget is read.
- **[6c]** (SMD-3022, carried over): its six-condition "both ends step aside" assertion is split one check a condition.
- **One neighbouring check** named "this session's" payload a second before now, and had relied on the 2.5 s between it and the stranger's payload to make that one older. It's named from the stranger's own timestamp now, and a second name, before the stranger's, gives "landed after" a check that can fail.

**Why.** The step took a median of 56.5 s over 30 runs, 52.7 s of it the endpoint's 2.5 s holds and the fixed sleeps around them. The hold proved no client timeout (the hook's is 90 s); it only kept a post open while the case staged.

**Held.** The suite's last assertion counts every slow post the gate let go on its 10 s fallback, so a case that never releases its post fails the run. Each case's assertion that its post arrived names the case's post when one never comes.

**Measured after.**
- The suite passes 795 of 795 in three runs, about 5 s each, against a median of 56.5 s on CI.
- **Four mutants each fail it:**
  - a hook that posts in the foreground runs into the gate's fallback, 10 s against the 1.5 s budget;
  - a foreground that outlasts its budget reads 1,623 ms;
  - a payload behind a cleared claim not followed up fails 17 checks;
  - a gate that holds nothing fails 24, so the cases need the hold the gate gives.

**Review pass 1.** A cold read, and the suite on Linux in a CI-like container, under load and starved.

- **Fixed:**
  - every wait for an arrival is asserted, where all but [6c]'s were bare: a post that never came waited 10 s and failed on a follow-up's words (caught: cold-read);
  - s-unbooked waited on its claim alone; it waits for its post too (caught: cold-read);
  - "landed after" by session held whatever the scoping did, the payload older than the name it was asked about; a name before it gives the check a tooth (caught: cold-read);
  - the final check's "none still held" always held after [8]'s open; it counts the fallback's releases alone (caught: cold-read);
  - the record counted [8] among the fourteen, and lacked the shape's Held and Measured after (caught: cold-read).
  - SMD-3313's multi-brain "detached" case waited until both fake endpoints had a post, then read the canary's state, which the child writes only after its answer: a flake on main too, 4 runs in 6 when starved, 1 in 20 unloaded. It waits for the state as well, and 22 starved or loaded runs passed (caught: run-it).
- **On Linux:** 42 runs of the branch (oven/bun:1.4.3; 20 unloaded, 10 under burners, 12 starved on two pinned CPUs) took 6–38 s each, against main's 61–89 s. Every gate assertion held in every run, and none let a post go on its fallback.

**Tidied while the files were open.** The fifteen arrival checks go through one helper, `slowInFlight()`, whose message names the case's slow session where it named a variable, and [8]'s no longer says "before it stages"; the stranger's timestamp is read once. No behaviour changed.
