## FORK

The session capture hook's suite: slow posts held by a gate the cases release, not 2.5 s sleeps (SMD-3197)

**What changed** (`recipes/session-capture-hook/test-session-capture.mjs`).
- **The gate:** a `[[slow]]` or `[[slow-down]]` post is held at the fake endpoint until its case lets it through. `releaseSlow()` lets through the posts held now, and `openSlow()` every post until the next case's `closeSlow()`. `slowArrived(n)` waits for the n-th arrival, so a case waits for the post it stages around. A post no case lets through goes on after 10 s and is counted, and the suite's last assertion fails on it.
- **Fourteen staged cases** now wait for the slow post's arrival, where eleven slept 600 ms, 700 ms or 3 s and three waited on claims alone, stage their files, and let it through:
  - two release only the post held, for the second that follows: the chained compaction's follow-up, and the five tails;
  - [8] lets its detached post through only after the foreground's budget is read.
- **[6c]** (SMD-3022, carried over): it asserts the checkpoint's post has arrived before the ends run, where it waited for the claims alone, and its six-condition "both ends step aside" assertion is split one check a condition.
- **One neighbouring check** named "this session's" payload a second before now, and had relied on the 2.5 s between it and the stranger's payload to make that one older. It's named from the stranger's own timestamp now.

**Why.** The step took a median of 56.5 s over 30 runs, 52.7 s of it the endpoint's 2.5 s holds and the fixed sleeps around them. The hold proved no client timeout (the hook's is 90 s); it only kept a post open while the case staged.

**Measured.**
- The suite passes 779 of 779 in three runs, about 5 s each (4.7 s timed).
- **Four mutants each fail it:**
  - a hook that posts in the foreground runs into the gate's fallback, 10 s against the 1.5 s budget;
  - a foreground that outlasts its budget reads 1,623 ms;
  - a payload behind a cleared claim not followed up fails 17 checks;
  - a gate that holds nothing fails 24, so the cases need the hold the gate gives.
