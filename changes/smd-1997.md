## FORK

The event log is the source of truth — the ADR, decided on two measured gates: `thought_audit` is the write-side truth, the `thoughts` row its projection kept as a table, the write functions the projector, and a three-step additive path from 053 (SMD-1997)

**What changed.** `docs/event-log-as-truth.md` is the architecture decision record the ticket asked for: the decision in eight numbered points, what the schema already held at 053, what the two gates measured (SMD-1998's replay, SMD-1999's writable projection), the event's columns and where each came from with the three additions the decision makes, the three verbs (ingested, comprehended, expressed) as a read over the columns the event already carries with a precedence, the write path from step 2 on (append, project, the trigger as the check, the projector's three announcing settings cleared before it returns, the cascade rule, the vector refresh and the snapshot's live-only trigger, the five "no event, no write" deltas accepted, the log faithful not corrective), a table of every projection with the key it records today and what the decision requires of it, the two clocks and the two as-of reads, deletion and forgetting, the consistency rule (atomic at the boundary, eventual off the row, convergent by rebuild; idempotent by the check; conflict preserved not resolved), the path in three steps with what coexists and how each rolls back, what is declined and why, what is not decided here, what holds it, and the prior art. One sentence in `CLAUDE.md`'s guard rail, one paragraph in `db/README.md` beside migration 046 and one line in `evals/README.md`'s Related point at it.

**Why.** SMD-1997 asked whether to restructure the data model around event sourcing and set two spikes as gates. Both opened: SMD-1998 measured that a rebuild reuses every vector by `(content_fingerprint, embedding_model)` and that the graph, not the index, is the expensive projection; SMD-1999 measured that the three write functions can append the event first and project the row in the same transaction with an empty contributor delta, and that a view named `thoughts` breaks the community schemas' DDL. The ticket's Verify names four things a decided ADR produces — the record with the taxonomy, the projector and snapshot strategy and the bitemporal model; the two spike results; a migration and compatibility plan naming how the log as truth coexists with the mutable path — and this is the first and the fourth, the second and third cited where they stand.

**Decided.** The log is the truth for the thought row and the row is derived from its event in the same transaction; `thoughts` stays a table (Postgres refuses `ADD COLUMN`, `CREATE INDEX`, `REFERENCES`, a row trigger and `ON CONFLICT` on a view, and the community surface runs all five); the functions are the projector and the audit trigger becomes the check under `ob1.projecting`, still appending for a raw write; read-your-writes by construction, no queue; the verbs are a read with a precedence — ingested by the trust ceiling the trigger enforces, then comprehended by a worker's door in `origin`, else expressed — and not a column, which would be a second declaration free to disagree with the columns that determine it; comprehension on the row is an update event from a worker's door, comprehension off the row (entities, chunks, proposals, facets) is a projection with a lineage row under SMD-1731, not a second log; every projection's key names the input's fingerprint and the recipe or its rebuild cannot be incremental, the vector snapshotted by its key in a table fed by live writes alone; transaction time is `created_at` + `seq` with `created_at` first (050's own rule for pre-050 rows), valid time the 046 window, the as-of read over the first the replay with a bound and over the second a filter SMD-2011 wires; deletion stays real, forgetting is the second named amendment of the log by `thought_id` over every row holding the text, a redacted thought projects to nothing, and the redaction lands no later than step 2 because from then the log holds every thought's text; the five deltas SMD-1999's record and bodies named against 053 are taken (no `updated_at` bump on an identical re-capture, a no-op edit or a vector refresh; the unreachable stale-read arm gone with the refusal kept; the 2-argument capture's row lock) and said to be the one caller-visible change.

**The path.** Three additive steps, filed as Phase 1d/1e/1f of SMD-1729 in the milestone with 1730–1732: SMD-2115 (the capture event carries the content and a backdating writer's `created_at`, the update event the key's move; 046's diff rule, its append and 050's stamp become functions; the payload backfilled onto earlier capture rows through a third arm in 046's immutability gate, or the replay seeds from the row store, decided there after measuring), SMD-2116 (the functions append then project, the trigger checks, the vector snapshot and the refresh, the accepted deltas; blocked by SMD-1723's redaction as well), SMD-2117 (`db/replay.ts` — the fold in `(created_at, seq)` order, bounded, into a tier or a schema, with a verify mode, defined over the events since step 1 until the backfill has run; the raw in-tree writers onto the projector; `thought_changes` reading a capture's head from the event). Each blocked by the one before. "Dual-write" is retired as a word: from step 2 there is one truth, and the period it named is the one in which raw writers still write the row first and are trigger-audited.

**Declined.** A view with `INSTEAD OF` triggers and a projection table behind a compat view (the DDL surface, measured); pure event sourcing with no snapshots (hours for the graph); an external bus or an asynchronous projector (Postgres-only, read-your-writes); a second log for comprehension; dual-write in application code (three functions are the one door); a verb column; making the record itself a checked artifact (its numbers live in the two README sections and the migrations, each held by its own suite — a second copy here would be one more thing to hold stale). Cryptographic shredding declined for forgetting on a single-operator brain; revisited if the log ever leaves the operator's database.

**Held.** By nothing in the page. The two gates' runners stay in CI (the pure rules self-checked in the portable-server job; gate 1's fixture and the prototype applied to a real Postgres at 053 in the data-layer job, the latter held to a recorded matrix) until SMD-2116 ships the bodies and their criteria move into the schema suites; test-schema's sentinel reads and preflight's recognisers pin the write functions and move with them at each step; check 21 keeps every `.sql` from destroying a row the brain holds; the version rules cut each step as a MINOR fragment with its migration. The record's own claims are those sections' measured results, cited, not restated as new numbers.

**Not decided here.** The log's partitioning (046 chose `RANGE` by month; SMD-1947 benches and decides); the redaction amendment's exact shape (SMD-1723; what the projector does with a redacted thought is decided); the graph's fingerprint and every recipe (SMD-1731); where a pre-step capture's text comes from on a replay (SMD-2115 measures the backfill first; until then the fold is defined over post-step-1 events); the door vocabulary `origin` holds and a helper that reads the verb (filed when a reader needs it); a Zep/DMR benchmark (SMD-1039).
