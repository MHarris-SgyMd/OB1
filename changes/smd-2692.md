## Changelog

The Kubernetes deployment's server now gives each embedding or chat call `OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT` seconds, 120 unless set, the core server's knob and default. A provider that stops answering fails the tool at that deadline with an error naming the knob. Before this, the only bound was Bun's own 300 s fetch cut, and with the reply kept alive a client waited through all of it (SMD-2692).

## FORK

kubernetes-deployment's provider calls end at OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT, as the core server's do (SMD-2692)

**What changed.**
- `integrations/kubernetes-deployment/index.ts` gains `withDeadline(what, base, call)`. It reads `OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT` per call, in seconds, with 120 for an unset, non-numeric or non-positive value: `server-portable/embed.ts`'s name, default and rule. It hands the call `signal: AbortSignal.timeout(…)` and `timeout: false`, so this deadline replaces Bun's 300 s idle cut instead of running under it. A `TimeoutError` becomes `Embeddings request to <base> timed out after N s (OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT)` (or `Chat completion …`), `embed.ts`'s wording. `getEmbedding` and `extractMetadata` run their fetch and their body read inside it, so a body that stalls after its headers is bounded too.
- The README's local-model section names the knob, and `k8s/openbrain.yml` lists it at 120. The README's stop paragraph said a call silent for 10 s is closed because "this server has no keepalive", which SMD-2001 made false. It now says a call still waiting on its provider at the drain bound is cut off, and the stop exits 1.
- `vendoredStalledLine`'s docblock in `server-portable/sse.ts`, with its three copies, no longer says no vendored server bounds its provider calls. The line it returns is unchanged.

**Why.** SMD-2001's keepalive stopped Bun resetting a silent reply at 8–12 s. That reset had been hiding a hung provider: the client failed fast while the tool hung on alone. Afterwards a client waited out Bun's fetch cut, about 300 s, and on SIGTERM the call held the drain to its bound. SMD-2001's review pass 3 found it, and put the bound on the fetches rather than shortening the keepalive.

**Held.** `extensions/test-auth.ts`, against a real provider server that stalls, reached through the server's own fetch with its init as given, under `OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT=2`:
- `search_thoughts`, whose embedding stalls after its headers, answers at about 2.0 s with the embeddings error naming the knob.
- `capture_thought`, whose chat call never answers, answers at about 2.0 s with the chat-completion error.
- *Mutants.* Four mutants each fail at least one case: dropping the deadline from the embedding call (no reply in 8 s) or from the chat call (the same), leaving the timeout unnamed (`The operation timed out.`), and reading the knob once at import (both cases, no reply in 8 s).

**Measured after.** test-auth 702/702; the new cases add about 4 s.

**Not taken.**
- *Falling back to default metadata when the chat call times out*, as the core does. A capture whose chat call times out still fails, as one whose chat call errors always has; only an answer whose content is not JSON falls back. Changing that is a behaviour change of its own.
- *A test of the 120 s default.* It would take two minutes per run; the rule is `embed.ts`'s.
- *A test of `timeout: false`.* Only a deadline past 300 s would show it.
- *Aborting provider calls in flight on SIGTERM.* A call within its deadline can still outlast the 28 s drain and be cut off; the core server's drain is the same.
- *agent-memory-api's and readwise-capture's embedding fetches*, which also carry no deadline. They are HTTP handlers whose replies are not kept alive, so Bun resets a slow one at 8–12 s and the client does not wait.
