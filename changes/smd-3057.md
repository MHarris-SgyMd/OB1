## Changelog

The `/canary/mcp` and `/working/mcp` routes of `deploy/compose.yaml`'s proxy, which reach a tier on the stack's mesh, are deprecated (the three-brain stack's, `compose.tiers.yaml`, are its own tiers and stay): every answer through them carries a `Deprecation` header and a `Link` to `deploy/README.md`'s "A canary beside the stack", where a canary answers on a port of its own and the three-brain stack routes its own tiers. They answer until **v2.0.0**, and no sooner than two weeks after this release. The session-capture hook's `--check` warns on a url at either route and says to set the tier's own port, not stable's `/mcp` (SMD-3057).

## FORK

Stable's tier routes deprecated before v2.0.0 removes them (SMD-3057)

**What changed.**
- `deploy/compose.yaml`: the route table's `canary` and `working` routers gain the `tier-deprecated` middleware, outermost after `not-legacy`, which adds `Deprecation: @1792108800` (2026-10-16, RFC 9745) and `Link: <…/deploy/README.md#a-canary-beside-the-stack>; rel="deprecation"; type="text/html"` to every answer, as `legacy-window` does for the root. The table's comment says so.
- `scripts/check-fork-consistency.ts`: the tier routers are built by `routeTierPaths(mw)`; check 28's `PROXY_ROUTE_TABLE` takes them with `tier-deprecated` and holds the middleware (`ROUTE_TIER_DEPRECATED`), while check 27's `TIER_ROUTE_TABLE` (`compose.tiers.yaml`, whose tiers are its own) keeps `ROUTE_TIER_PATHS` as it was.
- CI, "The tier routes reach a tier on the mesh…": the bodiless 404 at `/canary/mcp` and `/working/mcp` with no tier carries the header and the Link (so `tier-deprecated` stays outside `tier-absent`), as do each stand-in's 200, its own 401/404/429/500/503/504 and its 502 made the bodiless 404; stable's own `/mcp/health` answers 200 with neither a `Deprecation` nor a deprecation `Link`. Each read fails on a curl error rather than reading another answer's headers.
- `recipes/session-capture-hook`: `--check` told a url at a tier route, whose answers now carry `Deprecation`, that it was on the legacy root and to move to its `/mcp`, which is stable's. An answer whose `Link` names `#a-canary-beside-the-stack` is a tier route's, and the warning says to set the tier's own port; the test holds both warnings apart.
- `deploy/README.md`'s route table names the deprecation, of this file's proxy alone.
- `docs/one-brain-per-stack.md` records the decision, and that `tier-off` leaves compose.yaml's table only after SMD-2532 has closed the legacy route; `docs/operator-surface-tiers.md`'s as-built row names the deprecation.

**Not taken.**
- One helper building both `legacy-window` and `tier-deprecated`: two blocks, each held byte for byte by check 28.
- `db/bench-live.ts`'s default `--mcp`, stable's `/canary/mcp`: this Mac's canary answers there until the dogfood move (SMD-2874), which moves the default with it.
- A checker rule that the `Link` anchors name a heading of `deploy/README.md`: `legacy-window`'s carries the same exposure, and one rule over both is its own change. The 2026-09-27 client table in `docs/operator-surface-tiers.md` stays as read on that date.
- A `Link` to the release tag's README rather than `main`'s, and a `Sunset` header (RFC 8594): `legacy-window` links `main` too, and v2.0.0 has no date yet; the changelog's "no sooner than two weeks after this release" is the promise.

**Why.** CHANGELOG 1.6.0 shipped the routes as an addition of the released `compose.yaml`, and FORK.md's MAJOR rule lets a URL a configured client uses go only after a release has said so on every answer. Since SMD-2874 a canary is a brain of its own on its own port and joins stable's mesh no more, so the routes serve nothing `deploy/canary.sh` stands up. SMD-2874 PR 3 removes them, their services and `tier-absent` at v2.0.0, beside SMD-2532's closing of the legacy route; the maintainer chose (2026-10-16) this deprecation first.
