## Changelog

Migration 078 stops the consolidation pass from asking whether one ticket supersedes another: `consolidation_candidates` leaves out a pair whose two thoughts carry different ticket identities (`metadata.issue`), which made 93 of the stable brain's 128 proposals, all rejected; a full pass makes 26.6% fewer judge calls, and `db/consolidate.ts` says how many pairs a run left out (SMD-2448).

## FORK

Two different tickets are never paired for judgement (SMD-2448)

**What changed.** Migration 078 redefines `consolidation_candidates` on
066's body plus one NULL-safe condition: a pair whose two thoughts both carry
a ticket identity (`metadata->>'issue'`, exactly as 068 keys a ticket) and
the identities differ is left out. Two rows of one ticket (two writers of one
identity, SMD-1958) stay candidates, and a thought with no identity is judged
against a ticket row as before. The body carries the sentinel
`ob1:distinct-tickets-not-paired`; preflight's `lineage` check warns naming
078 when 063 or 066 is re-applied by hand over it (the next rung after 066's).
`consolidation_ticket_pairs_left_out(thought, floor)` counts the pairs the
rule removes for one thought, every other term met, no k cut. `db/consolidate.ts`
reports them: a run's summary line ("N pair(s) between two different tickets
left out"), and `--status` / `--dry-run` over the thoughts still to judge (or,
on a brain without 078, that such pairs are still judged). A stale proposal
on two tickets is settled by the pass as no longer a candidate, the reason
naming 078's rule. The sixth restriction is stated in 078's header and in
`db/README.md`, not in 029's, which release 1.0.0 froze.

**Why.** The first full p3 pass (2026-10-01) recorded 98 proposals and 68
paired two different tickets: a parent and its child, related or blocking
tickets, a ticket and its follow-up. Two tickets are two records, each with
its own status that board-sync keeps current and `node_state` reads; one
"superseding" the other archives a record that still holds. An always-on
consolidation follower (SMD-2424) would fill the review queue with them.

**Measured.** Read-only on the stable dogfood brain (2026-10-04): 93 of the
128 proposals ever recorded paired two different tickets — 24 of 24 under
`@p2`, 69 of 104 under `@p3` — all rejected; none paired two rows of one
ticket; neither accepted proposal involves a ticket row, so the rule keeps
both. Over the 1,238 thoughts in the pool at the shipped defaults (k 3,
cosine ≥ 0.6) a full pass makes 3,427 judge calls before the rule and 2,516
after (−26.6%); 9,896 of 42,026 eligible pairs are left out, and the read
took 3 s.

**Held.** test-schema [70]: one body, 078's, both sentinels and the
condition spelled NULL-safe; a newer SMD-1 row is judged under 066 against
every older row and under 078 against its own ticket's two rows, a note and
a null-issue row, never SMD-2 or SMD-3; the count is two and with the kept
list partitions 066's; a floor and an already-proposed pair each drop it by
one; a note is left nothing out; a re-apply is a no-op. test-upgrade [20ab]:
refused naming 063 on a schema at 062; onto a populated brain at 077 a newer
ticket row lists another ticket before the file and its own ticket's row and
the note after, an operator's REVOKE kept, no row, proposal or audit row
moved (a pending proposal on two tickets stands), a re-apply a no-op.
test-live [16]: `--status` and `--dry-run` count the one pair, and the run
judges the newer ticket row twice, never against the other ticket, and says
it left one out. test-preflight: 066 re-applied over 078 warns naming 078.

**Not taken.** The text rule (SMD-2448's optional third item: a note naming
ticket X paired with X's row is lineage): on the 128 it would leave out 10
more pairs, all rejected, none accepted — but the 35 pairs without two
identities are too few to call it clean, so it waits on a larger review set.
A `--status` clause for proposals already standing on two tickets (070's
shape for lineage pairs): every such row on the dogfood is already rejected.
