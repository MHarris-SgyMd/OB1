## Changelog

Migration 078 stops the consolidation pass from asking whether one ticket supersedes another: `consolidation_candidates` leaves out a pair whose two thoughts are filed under different tickets (`metadata.ticket`, else `metadata.issue`), which made 109 of the stable brain's 128 proposals, all rejected; a full pass makes 36.1% fewer judge calls, and `db/consolidate.ts` says how many a run did not spend (SMD-2448).

## FORK

Two different tickets are never paired for judgement (SMD-2448)

**What changed.** Migration 078 redefines `consolidation_candidates` on
066's body plus one NULL-safe condition: a pair whose two thoughts both carry
a ticket identity and the identities differ is left out. The identity is the
ticket `node_state` reads a thought under (058, 068, 071):
`coalesce(metadata->>'ticket', metadata->>'issue')` — a ticket's row by its
issue, a dated section filed under it by its ticket — the text exactly. Two
rows of one ticket (two writers of one identity, SMD-1958) stay candidates,
and a thought with no identity is judged against a ticket row as before.
The body carries `ob1:distinct-tickets-not-paired`; preflight's `lineage`
check warns naming 078 when 063 or 066 is re-applied by hand over it (the next
rung after 066's). `consolidation_ticket_pairs_left_out(thought, floor)`
counts the pairs the rule removes for one thought, every other term met, no k
cut. `db/consolidate.ts` turns that into judge calls not spent at `--k` (the
cut over 066's list less the cut over 078's) — read once per claim and added
when the thought is finished — for a run's summary line and for `--status` /
`--dry-run` over the thoughts the next run judges (pending, unpooled,
re-pooled for a stale proposal, failed under `--retry-failed`). It reports
the rule only where the candidate body carries the sentinel; otherwise it
says such pairs are still judged. A stale proposal on two tickets is settled
by the pass as no longer a candidate, the note naming 078's rule. The sixth
restriction is stated in 078's header and in `db/README.md`, not in 029's,
which release 1.0.0 froze.

**Why.** The first full p3 pass (2026-10-01) recorded 98 proposals and 68
paired two different tickets by their issue: a parent and its child, related
or blocking tickets, a ticket and its follow-up. Two tickets are two records,
each with its own status that board-sync keeps current and `node_state`
reads; one "superseding" the other archives a record that still holds. An
always-on consolidation follower (SMD-2424) would fill the review queue with
them.

**Measured.** Read-only on the stable dogfood brain (2026-10-04): 109 of the
128 proposals ever recorded paired two different tickets — 24 of 24 under
`@p2`, 85 of 104 under `@p3` — all rejected (93 by issue alone; the other 16
pair a dated section with another ticket's row); none paired two rows of one
ticket; neither accepted proposal pairs two tickets. Over the 1,238 pooled
thoughts at the shipped defaults (k 3, cosine ≥ 0.6) a full pass makes 3,427
judge calls before the rule and 2,190 after (−36.1%; 2,516 by issue alone).
Measured today, a non-stale proposal holds its pair either way; with every
proposal's pair counted as unjudged it is 3,431 → 2,194. The read took 3.3 s,
which is also what `--status` and `--dry-run` now spend on a pool that size.

**Held.** test-schema [70]: one body, 078's, both sentinels and the
condition spelled NULL-safe over the ticket-else-issue key; a newer SMD-1 row
is judged under 066 against every older row nothing supersedes, and under 078
against its own ticket's issue row, second writer and dated section, a note
and a null-issue row — never another ticket's row or section; for every
thought of a corpus exercising each shared term (two shared entities, a
superseded ticket, a superseded newer row, a same-day row, a lineage pair, a
stale and a pending proposal, the floor) the kept list and the count partition
066's; a re-apply is a no-op. test-upgrade [20ab]: refused naming 025, 029 and
063 in turn; onto a populated brain at 077 a newer ticket row lists another
ticket before the file and its own ticket's row and the note after, an
operator's REVOKE kept, no row, proposal or audit row moved (a pending
proposal on two tickets stands), a re-apply a no-op. test-live [16]:
`--status` and `--dry-run` count the one call saved; with 066 re-applied by
hand they say such pairs are still judged; the run judges the newer ticket row
twice, never against the other ticket's row or section, and says one call was
saved; a stale proposal on the two tickets is settled naming 078's rule.
test-preflight: 066 re-applied over 078 warns naming 078.

**Not taken.** The text rule (SMD-2448's optional third item: a thought
