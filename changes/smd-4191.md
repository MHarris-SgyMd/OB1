## Changelog

`plan_context` replies now stay within a budget. Each hit's text is cut to its share of about 1,600 characters, and the hit then says `fetch <id>` reads the whole. A hit's labels are capped at 2 claims, 2 relations and 3 priorities; the rest are counted, as `search_thoughts` counts them. The board lists its first 15 open tickets with titles cut to 80 characters, and settled tickets past the first 5 by key and status alone. A `trimmed` gap counts what was cut. MCP's structured value now keeps ids, keys, scores and counts, and leaves the labels to the text. A request that answered 63,108 characters on the canary would now answer about 15,000 (SMD-4191).

## Record

plan_context's reply held to a budget (SMD-4191)

On the canary on 2026-11-09, `plan_context` answered a "what next" request with 63,108 characters for 8 hits, too big for an agent to take in one tool result. The size was spread across the reply:
- the hits' whole text, at about 3,000 characters each;
- their labels in the text;
- the labels again as objects in MCP's value, mostly relations, at about 1,100 characters a hit;
- 51 named tickets with 200-character titles, in both the text and the value.

**What changed.**
- **`budgetPlan` (core/plan.ts),** applied in the core so REST and MCP still give one record:
  - each hit's text is cut to its share of HIT_TEXT_BUDGET (1,600 characters over the page, at least 160 a hit), and `contentChars` keeps the whole length;
  - a hit's labels are cut to HIT_LABELS (2 claims, 2 relations, 3 priorities), each cut added to its `…More`;
  - the board lists BOARD_SHOWN (15) open tickets with titles cut to BOARD_TITLE (80 characters), and settled or unknown-type tickets past OTHERS_TITLED (5) by key and status alone;
  - one `trimmed` gap counts hits cut, labels cut, tickets past the list, and titles shortened or left off.
- **The log** still names every hit and ticket read, cut or not.
- **The MCP text:**
  - a cut hit ends "… cut at N of M characters: fetch <id> for the whole text";
  - settled tickets past the first five are listed as "and N more: KEY (status), …";
  - the `trimmed` gap is said in words.
- **The MCP value:**
  - per hit, its id, time, similarity, superseded, trust, `contentChars`, and how many moved tickets, claims, relations and priorities it carries;
  - per open ticket, its rank, key, status type, effective priority, how many blockers it has, and whether it is claimed;
  - settled tickets as a list of keys; unknown-type ones as key and status type.

**Measured.** The canary-shaped fixture comes to 15,430 characters of MCP value, text included, against 49,500 unbudgeted.
