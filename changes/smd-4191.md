## Changelog

`plan_context` replies now stay within a budget: about 16,000 characters at the default 8 hits, and under 40,000 at the most a read can be. Each hit's text is cut to its share of about 1,400 characters, never through a character, and the hit then says `fetch <id>` reads the whole. A hit's labels are capped at 2 claims, 2 relations and 3 priorities; the rest are counted, as `search_thoughts` counts them. The board lists its first 15 open tickets with titles cut to 72 characters and the rest by key. Settled tickets past the first 5 are listed by key and status, and past 40 are counted. A `trimmed` entry among the gaps counts what was cut. MCP's structured value now keeps ids, keys and counts, and leaves the labels to the text. A request that answered 63,108 characters on the canary would now answer about 15,700 (SMD-4191).

## Record

plan_context's reply held to a budget (SMD-4191)

On the canary on 2026-11-09, `plan_context` answered a "what next" request with 63,108 characters for 8 hits, too big for an agent to take in one tool result. The size was spread across the reply:
- the hits' whole text, at about 3,000 characters each;
- their labels in the text;
- the labels again as objects in MCP's value, mostly relations, at about 1,100 characters a hit;
- 51 named tickets with 200-character titles, in both the text and the value.

**What changed.**
- **`budgetPlan` (core/plan.ts),** applied in the core so REST and MCP still give one record:
  - each hit's text is cut to its share of HIT_TEXT_BUDGET (1,400 characters over the page, at least 100 a hit), never through half of a surrogate pair (`headOf`), and `contentChars` keeps the whole length in UTF-16 units;
  - a hit's labels are cut to HIT_LABELS (2 claims, 2 relations, 3 priorities), each cut added to its `…More`;
  - the board lists BOARD_SHOWN (15) open tickets with titles cut to BOARD_TITLE (72 characters), and the rest by key in `moreOpen`;
  - settled or unknown-type tickets past OTHERS_TITLED (5) are listed by key and status, and past OTHERS_LISTED (40) counted in `othersMore`;
  - waiting tickets past WAITING_LISTED (10) are counted in `waitingMore`, and missing keys past MISSING_LISTED (20) in the gap's `more`;
  - one `trimmed` entry among the gaps counts hits cut, labels cut, open tickets past the list, titles shortened or left off, and tickets counted but not named.
- **The log** still names every hit and ticket read, cut or not.
- **The MCP text:**
  - a cut hit ends "… cut at N of M characters: fetch <id> for the whole text", and an exact match past the head reads "Contains (in the whole text, past what is shown)";
  - with only a cut among the gaps, the reply still says nothing else was found and to weigh the hits;
  - settled tickets past the first five are listed as "and N more: KEY (status), …";
  - the `trimmed` gap is said in words.
- **The MCP value:**
  - per hit, its id, time, similarity, superseded, trust, `contentChars`, and how many moved tickets, claims, relations and priorities it carries;
  - per open ticket, its rank, key, status type, effective priority, how many blockers it has, whether it is claimed, and whether a lapsed claim stands on it;
  - settled tickets as a list of keys; unknown-type ones as key and status type.

**Measured.** The canary-shaped fixture comes to 15,663 characters of MCP value, text included, against 49,905 unbudgeted. The worst the reach allows comes to 36,980: 20 hits of 60,000 characters with every label, 100 open, 400 settled, 100 waiting and 300 missing tickets. Before the budget that reply had no bound (review pass 1 measured 68,825 for one shape of it).
