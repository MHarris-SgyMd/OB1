## Changelog

test-rest's nine telemetry stop cases run side by side, each with its own collector and `bun api.ts`, where they ran one after another: the suite takes about 14 s where it took 44 s locally, and Fork Checks' "The REST core's routes and authorization" step falls with it. A test child now binds a port of its own and says which, so two servers started at once never share one (SMD-3263).

## FORK

test-rest [12]'s stop cases run side by side (SMD-3263)

**What changed.**
- **The nine `stopWith` cases start at once** and are awaited together: slow, hung, crowded, trickled, rejected, perSignal, noSpans, hungFirst and pushedBack. Each case is started under its name and its result read by that name, not by its place in a list. Their rows follow in the order they had. The three collectors written for them (trickle, partial, pushback) are defined above the starts. Each case's comment stays with its rows.
- **db/test-support.ts's `serveChild` starts a child at PORT 0** and reads the port it bound from Bun's start line on stdout. It picked a port by binding port 0 and freeing it, and nine starts at once let another server take a freed port in between; on Linux that failed a case in about 1% of runs. Its waits for `/health` and the SDK's line are 30 s and 20 s, from 15 s and 10 s: they are deadlines, which a quick child never meets, and nine starts at once are slower than one. `watchStream` gains `sofar`.
- **A case that throws is said, not thrown.** Its collector and child start inside the case's `try`. The error goes into the case's `missed` and a line naming the case, and the child's own output is kept, so its rows fail saying why. Thrown inside `Promise.all`, it would have ended the suite with the other eight children running.
- **pushedBack's row counts each signal's two attempts**, so a collector that never pushed back cannot pass it.
- **noSpans' and hungFirst's rows name every "not sent" line and the stop's line**, where a failure read "()" or named nothing.

**Why.** The step took 29–30 s on main until OB1 PR 473 (SMD-2902 PR 2) added six cases to [12], then 57–58 s. SMD-3201 overlapped [16]'s idle probes (58 → 43 s), which left [12] as nearly all of the suite: 38 of its 44 s locally.

test-rest's floor is now [16]'s 14 s idle probes, which start at the top.

- **Equal priority:** no timing bound moved. hung's flush was cut at 2999–3000 ms and its stop said 3 s in every run. The failures were noSpans once and hungFirst once, each before its message named what came instead.
- **Starved:** the suite runs below the burners. That fails main's serial cases as often, in the same rows: pushedBack's first answer late for its Retry-After, trickled and perSignal timed out, crowded's tally, and [16]'s probe. Nine children starting at once can also run out `serveChild`'s waits there, which fails every case at once; those waits were widened.
- **A barrier was tried and dropped.** Every child started before any case hit its own. Starved, 6 of its 10 runs had a failure, against 4 of 17 without it, so the cases run without one.

**Mutants**, each run against the concurrent cases:
- the flush's bound the pool's second: slow's and hung's rows;
- no cut said (`unsent` dropped from api.ts's drain): hung's, crowded's and perSignal's rows;
- every signal given the spans' deadline: perSignal's row;
- a partial success not counted: rejected's and noSpans' rows;
- the deadline at the timeout plus 2 s: pushedBack's row;
- no deadline: trickled's and perSignal's rows;
- the log batch's cut counting nothing held: crowded's row;
- a pushback collector that sends no 503, or counts every path as one: pushedBack's row;
- a review pass's 82 more, on the harness and the product: none survives here that main's serial cases do not let through too.

**Review pass 1.** A cold read; mutants on the harness and the product, each survivor run against main; and runs under load, several copies at once and on Linux at 4 CPUs.

- **Fixed:**
  - nine starts at once raced `serveChild`'s port pick: a freed port taken by another case's collector or child failed that case, about 1 in 100 runs on Linux, none on macOS, which hands ports out in sequence. A child binds its own port now (caught: cold-read);
  - the race was measured at 204 in 20,000 simulated starts and 12 in about 1,040 real ones on Linux. With each child binding its own port, [12]'s start pattern ran 699 times on Linux (6,291 children) with none (caught: run-it);
  - nine starts at once could run out `serveChild`'s SDK wait, a moved margin left as it was; widened (caught: cold-read);
  - a throw from a case's collector or child start was outside its `try`, so it could still orphan the other children (caught: cold-read);
  - the catch dropped the child's own output, and its line named no case (caught: cold-read);
  - the results were read by place, and slow's and pushedBack's rows each passed the other's output: swapping the two passed every row. Read by name now (caught: mutant);
  - pushedBack's row passed a collector that never sent a 503 (caught: mutant);
  - hungFirst's message named nothing when no line came (caught: run-it);
  - the record said PR 473 added four cases (six), "39 of 44 s" (38.3 in its own table), a CI saving measured only locally, and that the cases shared nothing (caught: cold-read).
- **Seen, not this change's** (each lets a mutant through main's serial cases too):
  - the span batch's cut can count nothing held: crowded's span batch holds nothing at the stop, so only the log batch's count is checked (caught: mutant);
  - the stop says "telemetry sent" once exports lost at their deadline before it were said on their own lines: trickled's row holds "not all sent" only because its metric export is cut too. Whether the stop's word should count those is SMD-2902's question (caught: mutant);
  - the export watcher's bookkeeping after a deadline or a cut is held by no row: a done(FAILED) that frees the batch, the guards that count an item once. Nothing in [12] answers an export after either (caught: mutant);
  - the 700 ms sleep past the batch's delay is unobserved, and no row asks that a case's waits did not run out (caught: mutant);
  - twice in about 100 local runs a server the suite started on port 0 was answered by something else with HTTP 404: the lone collector's exports, and [16]'s probe under one mutant. None in the review's runs, on either system (caught: run-it).
