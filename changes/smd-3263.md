## Changelog

test-rest's nine telemetry stop cases run side by side, each with its own collector and `bun api.ts`, where they ran one after another: the suite takes about 14 s where it took 44 s locally, and Fork Checks' "The REST core's routes and authorization" step falls with it. A child started by the suites' `serveChild` now binds a port of its own and says which, where the suite picked one and freed it for the child, so children started at once no longer race for a port (SMD-3263).

## FORK

test-rest [12]'s stop cases run side by side (SMD-3263)

**What changed.**
- **The nine `stopWith` cases start at once** and are awaited together: slow, hung, crowded, trickled, rejected, perSignal, noSpans, hungFirst and pushedBack. Each case is started under its name and its result read by that name. Their rows follow in the order they had. The collectors written for them (trickle, partial, pushback, slowly) are defined above the starts. Each case's comment stays with its rows.
- **db/test-support.ts's `serveChild` starts a child at PORT 0** and reads the port it bound from Bun's start line on stdout, its newline included. It picked a port by binding port 0 and freeing it, and nine starts at once let another server take a freed port in between; on Linux that failed a case in about 1% of runs. Its waits for `/health` and the SDK's line are 30 s and 20 s, from 15 s and 10 s: they are deadlines, which a quick child never meets, and nine starts at once are slower than one. An entry it serves must be served by its `export default` and read PORT, as api.ts and mcp-server/index.ts are. `watchStream` gains `sofar`.
- **A case that throws is said, not thrown.** Its collector and child start inside the case's `try`. The error goes into the case's `missed` and a line naming the case, and the child's own output is kept. A row after the starts asks that no case threw or ran out a wait. Thrown inside `Promise.all`, it would have ended the suite with the other eight children running.
- **slow's and pushedBack's rows read their own collectors**: the port each collector was asked on must be the case's, slow's must have been sent a span export, and pushedBack's retry must come the Retry-After's 4 s after its first attempt and its answer past the timeout plus 2 s, both timed at the collector.
- **A row for a child that binds but answers `/health` with a 503**: not up, its port read.
- **noSpans' and hungFirst's rows name every "not sent" line and the stop's line**, where a failure read "()" or named nothing.

**Why.** The step took 29–30 s on main until OB1 PR 473 (SMD-2902 PR 2) added six cases to [12], then 57–58 s. SMD-3201 overlapped [16]'s idle probes (58 → 43 s), which left [12] as nearly all of the suite: 38 of its 44 s locally.

- **Equal priority:** no timing bound moved. hung's flush was cut at 2999–3000 ms and its stop said 3 s in every run. The failures before pass 1 were noSpans once and hungFirst once, each before its message named what came instead.
- **Starved:** the suite runs below the burners. That fails main's serial cases as often, in the same rows: pushedBack's first answer late for its Retry-After, trickled and perSignal timed out, crowded's tally, and [16]'s probe. Nine children starting at once can also run out `serveChild`'s waits there, which fails every case at once; those waits were widened.
- **A barrier was tried and dropped.** Every child started before any case hit its own. Starved, 6 of its 10 runs had a failure, against 4 of 17 without it, so the cases run without one.

**Mutants**, each run against the concurrent cases:
- the flush's bound the pool's second: slow's and hung's rows;
- no cut said (`unsent` dropped from api.ts's drain): hung's, crowded's and perSignal's rows;
- every signal given the spans' deadline: perSignal's row;
- a partial success not counted: rejected's and noSpans' rows;
- the deadline at the timeout plus 2 s: pushedBack's row;
- no deadline: trickled's and perSignal's rows;
- the log batch's cut counting nothing held: crowded's row;
- a pushback collector that sends no 503, counts every path as one, answers its retry at once, or sends its 503 without Retry-After: pushedBack's row;
- slow's and pushedBack's names swapped: both rows;
- a throw after a case's waits: the no-misses row;
- `up` from the bind alone, no `/health` asked: the 503 child's row;
- the review passes' 112 more, on the harness, `serveChild` and the product: none survives here that main's serial cases do not let through too, bar those equivalent here (a port read from the first number on stdout, a picked port passed as PORT).

A child binds its own port now; [12]'s start pattern then ran 699 times on Linux (6,291 children) with none, and pass 2's 1,080 more children had none where main's had one (caught: cold-read);
  - nine starts at once could run out `serveChild`'s waits for `/health` and the SDK's line, a moved margin left as it was; widened to 30 s and 20 s (caught: cold-read);
  - a throw from a case's collector or child start was outside its `try`, so it could still orphan the other children, and the catch kept no output and named no case (caught: cold-read);
  - the record said PR 473 added four cases (six), "39 of 44 s" (38.3 in its own table), a CI saving measured only locally, and that the cases shared nothing (caught: cold-read);

- **Fixed in pass 2:**
