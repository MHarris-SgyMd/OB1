## Changelog

`db/migrate.ts`, `db/extract-entities.ts` and `db/consolidate.ts` are importable engines: importing one does nothing until its `run(opts)` is called, which is the CLI's run, returning its exit code, with its output going to the writer it is given; a claim worker's pass is stopped by a caller's AbortSignal, or by the stop its `onPass` hook hands over (SMD-2304, SMD-2134).

## FORK

migrate.ts, extract-entities.ts and consolidate.ts are engines: import one and call run() — nothing runs at import any more, PRs 1–3 of 5 (SMD-2304 / 2134)

**What changed.** SMD-2134's fourth cut, in five PRs.
- **PR 1, the migrator.** `run(opts): Promise<number>` and `MigrateOptions`:
  the flags typed, refused as the CLI refuses them, the migration files read
  per call. A caller's `sql` is used in place of the URL, never closed, and
  must be one connection (`max: 1`): the run leaves its session state on it
  (`lock_timeout`, pgvector's schema on `search_path`, `ob1.acl_*`), so it is
  a client dedicated to the run. `cli.ts` gains the `Writer` (`out`/`err`)
  and `consoleWriter`; run() refuses a missing URL with connect.ts's
  NO_DATABASE_URL and any other with its `databaseUrlProblem`.
- **PR 2, extract-entities.ts.** `run(opts)` and `ExtractOptions`: numbers
  held to the CLI's rules in its order and words (flag list included), the
  lease pair between them; `env` for the model, endpoints, egress and key. A
  caller's client needs workers + 1 connections (the heartbeat's spare), is
  refused as a reserved connection or a transaction's handle, and needs `url`
  beside it for a keyed run (the key resolves on its own connection).
  `lease.ts` gains `PassStop`, `stopOnSignals` (the CLI's handlers, installed
  from `onPass` where `process.on` was, removed when run() settles) and
  `sleepUnless` (a wakeable sleep that keeps nothing, re-armed past a timer's
  ceiling). The first stop, or a caller's AbortSignal, stops every worker
  after the thought in hand; a stop while already stopping — a second, or the
  first after the provider's refusal — is the hard stop: every lease
  returned, the thought in hand abandoned (nothing written or released), a
  paused worker and a follower's sleep woken, 130. Aborted before the pass,
  the signal stops the run before its next write.
- **PR 3, consolidate.ts.** The same shape, `ConsolidateOptions` adding `k`,
  `minSim`, `minConfidence`, `stale` and the review modes. The review flags'
  own rules (which combine, a --list word, a proposal id, the pass's note
  marker) are `reviewProblem`, an exported pure function the CLI and run()
  both refuse through; `cli.ts`'s `numberProblem` judges an in-process number
  by value in the scanner's words (readNumber now builds its refusal
  through it). A review has no pass and reads on under an aborted signal. The
  judge takes an AbortSignal, so the hard stop also aborts the call in hand.

**Why.** Four db/ scripts were top-level programs: importing one scanned
argv, connected and called process.exit at about fifteen sites. So SMD-2272's
`run_worker` had no pass to call, SMD-1930's runner no engine to wrap, and
test-live spawned each worker about eight times.

**Behaviour against main's CLI.** Measured with timings and ids masked: the
same on every run-it scenario and pre-connection case but these. The CLIs
exit through `closeThenExit`, which flushes before exiting. A URL Bun's client
rejects (`?sslmode=bogus`) exits 1 with connect.ts's refusal and no banner,
the client now opened before the banner and gates. In the hard stop's 3 s
grace a worker no longer writes the thought in hand into a row already back
in the pool (main wrote 4–6 mentions, measured). A follower's first signal
exits at once rather than after its sleep. A signal in the milliseconds after
run() settles ends the process by it. config.mjs still reads the embedding
model and width from the environment at first import.

**Held.** `db/test-engines.ts` (new, CI "Engines", no database): each engine
imports with no connection, output or listener; a transpiler-read census of
its code (all but its CLI block, which comes last) finds no exit, listener,
argv read, URL resolver, door, console call or stream write; every refusal
matches the spawned CLI's stdout and stderr byte for byte, before connecting
and without the password; the client rules, null as absent, `stopOnSignals`
with a fake exit, `sleepUnless`'s wake, ceiling and heap, `reviewProblem` and
`numberProblem`. test-live [1]/[2], [10] and [16]: run() in-process beside
the spawned CLI, byte for byte per stream (dry runs, no-op runs, `--status`
on a caller's client, `--list`, refusals, the drift report's streams pinned
as main's); an AbortSignal's stop, the hard stop (a release before the thought
in hand ends; consolidate's call aborted, run() back at once), a start-up
abort before each write, a provider-error pause and a follower woken, a
throwing Writer, the CLIs' one and two SIGINTs. Every fix has its mutant
killed but those named below.
