## FORK

graph-centrality reads the ticket lifecycle board-sync already stamps — a per-thought weight carries the filter and the decay, and the default is unchanged (SMD-1994)

**What changed.** `db/graph-centrality.ts` (change 115) printed, every run,
that the graph holds no ticket status. Since board-sync (SMD-1954) that is
false of the data: every synced ticket's row carries `status` ("Done"),
`status_type` (triage / backlog / unstarted / started / completed / canceled)
and `linear_updated_at`. The script now reads them through one rule. Every
thought has a **weight**, and every count of thoughts — mentions, support,
co_mentions, the per-relation counts — is a sum of weights over distinct
thoughts. `--status open|active|done` keeps the named lifecycles at 1 and
weighs the other known ones 0 (`open` = not completed or canceled; `active` =
unstarted or started; `done` = completed or canceled). `--decay-done` weighs a
completed or canceled thought `DONE_WEIGHT` = 0.25 — pre-registered in the
file before any number was read, one value, exact in binary so the sums are —
and is refused beside a filter, two answers to one question. A thought with no
lifecycle, or a `status_type` the file does not know, weighs 1 under every
flag: it passes every filter, and the output counts how many did rather than
calling it open. A row's lifecycle is its ticket's: a row carrying a ticket
(`issue`) or derived from one (`ticket` — SMD-2059's dated sections, which
carry no status) takes the status of the ticket's head — of the rows
carrying that `issue`, one nothing supersedes before one superseded, the
newest sync before an older — so a Done ticket's observations and its
superseded earlier rows are settled with it; a twin the sync chained under a
head without an `issue` of its own keeps its own lifecycle, which is none.
Degree counts neighbours, not evidence, so a filter removes
an edge with no kept evidence and decay leaves it; a thought is listed when it
weighs more than 0, ranks by its weight times its score, and shows its status.
The default — `--status all`, no decay — is every weight 1: today's counts by
construction, and the caveat now says so ("a Done ticket counts as a live one
until a flag says otherwise") beside the source, the latest
`linear_updated_at`, how many thoughts carry a lifecycle, how many are
settled, how many carry an unknown `status_type`, and under a filter how many
weighed in; each thought table says how many listed thoughts carry one.
`LIFECYCLE_CTE` is the one place the status comes from — today
`thoughts.metadata`, the scalar the sync overwrites each pass; when SMD-2074
folds `thought_audit`'s transitions (046) into a node-state projection, that
CTE reads it and the weights, the counts and the callers see the same three
columns.

**Why.** Measured 2026-09-24 over every High-priority issue in the eight Open
Brain projects, all states: 11 of 16 — 69% — are Done or closed and ranked

brain's largest project by attention (Core Data & Migrations, 82 tickets, 25%
of its footprint) is 57% done with nothing in progress. The "highest-value
open issues" question needed a hand join against Linear although the brain
held the status. `--status open` drops the 11 with no join.
