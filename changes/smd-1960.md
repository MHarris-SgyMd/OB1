## Changelog

Entity extraction streams every budgeted call's answer and aborts the call the moment the answer holds three copies of one item — the loop the runaways were measured to be, visible on the stream long before the answer budget — then retries under the penalty as a cut call is; on the fork's 32 stragglers a runaway is aborted a median 21 s into its call where it ran to its budget, the pass measured 1,796 s against 3,158 with a median 50 s per thought against 108 and 31 of 32 extracting — the 32nd extracts on a re-run under the shipped retry rule, so 32 of 32 is derived, not re-measured whole — and an answer that enumerates distinct ids is not a loop by the rule and still runs to the budget, which stays the bound; the penalised retry is read whole, since a penalised answer was measured to repeat an item three times and recover (SMD-1960).

## FORK

A runaway extraction call ran to its answer budget before it was known to be one — the answer is streamed now and the call aborted at the third copy of one item, then retried as before (SMD-1960)

**What changed.** `server-portable/entities.ts`: `extractOnce` requests
`stream: true` when the windowing says so (`ExtractWindowing.streamAbort`,
on for every budgeted call — `windowingFor` follows the budget, as the retry
does, since only a budgeted call has a retry to send an aborted one to), reads
the `text/event-stream` body frame by frame (`readStreamedAnswer`: `data:`
frames' `delta.content` reassembled, the last `finish_reason` kept, `[DONE]`
and comments passed over, frames split across reads buffered to their
newline), and feeds every piece to a `RunawayDetector`, which finds each
complete item — a `{…}` one level inside the top-level object, by brace depth
outside strings — and keys it as `parseExtraction` keys it, (type, name) or
(relation, from, to), the type and relation UNVALIDATED (a repeated item the
rules would reject is a loop all the same; three tails looped on `table`,
`document`, `issue`). At the `RUNAWAY_REPEATS`-th (3) copy of one key the
reader is cancelled — the connection closes and Ollama stops generating — and
the call returns malformed, a runaway, with `abortedMs`: how far into the call
it was aborted. `extractCall` retries it under the penalty as it retries a
call cut at the budget — the retry read WHOLE, never aborted: a penalised
answer was measured to repeat an item three times and recover, and the
retry is the last attempt. A provider that answers a
stream request with plain JSON is read whole, as one not asked — every stub in
the suites does. `max_tokens` stays on every call: the detector is the early
exit, the budget the bound, and an answer that enumerates distinct ids (12 of
the 31 captured tails: every ticket a `uses` edge, tickets counted down) is not
a loop by the rule and runs to the budget as before. `Extraction` and its
per-window record carry `abortedMs` (the longest call's); the worker's dump
line carries it, its summary counts thoughts aborted on the stream, and
`describeExtractWindow` — the banner and preflight's `extraction window` row
— names the abort beside the retry. `EXTRACT_STREAM_ABORT` (`db/config.mjs`,
on, not a knob, as the retry is not) reaches `EmbedConfig.extractStreamAbort`.
`evals/eval-extract-windows.ts` gains the `whole+s`, `w1200s` and `w600s` arms
and an `aborted (median s)` column; `evals/eval-entities.ts` gains
`--unstreamed` for the parity run. No migration, no knob, no compose change.

**Why.** SMD-1879 measured the failures as generation that does not converge
— one entity or relation repeated to the context's end — and bounded each
call with an answer budget (3× the text's estimated tokens + 1,536) and one
penalised retry. A runaway therefore cost the whole budget before it was
known to be one: about a minute on `qwen2.5:7b`, and the retry only started
then (median 106 s per straggler, 20 of 32 retried). On a stream the loop is
visible as it happens. Streaming does not fix the failure — the model loops
whatever the transport; the budget and the penalty are what rescue — it makes
the failure cheap. The rule was chosen against the 31 runaway tails the
SMD-1879 probe captured: 16 repeat one item, 3 alternate two (a third copy of
either), 12 enumerate distinct ids and must not trip it; two copies is one
duplicate, which a converging answer does hold and `parseExtraction` folds.

**Held.** `test-thoughts.ts` [8c]: the detector over a converging answer with
braces and escaped quotes inside a name, fed whole, seven characters or one at
a time (never fires; every item counted); two copies (quiet); the third copy
fires on the item that made it three, keyed as an entity and as a relation;
two items alternating fire at the fifth; copies differing in confidence,
aliases, case or whitespace are copies; a rejected type repeated is a loop;
forty distinct ids as entities and edges are an enumeration (quiet); an item
that is not JSON is skipped; a nested object is its item's. The shipped
windowing streams and the sentence says so; reasoning on streams nothing; the
merge carries the longest window's `abortedMs` and none when none.
`test-local-provider.ts` [10]: a stub that streams a loop one item per frame
and records, per request, how many frames it got out before the client hung
up — the runaway is aborted after the third copy and before the loop runs out,
the retry carries the penalty and is read WHOLE (no stream requested), the
answer says how far in; a streamed answer whose frames split
tokens mid-name and mid-brace parses to the SAME `Extraction` as the whole
one; plain JSON to a stream request is read whole; a stream still coming at
the per-call deadline is the `TimeoutError` the worker classifies, counted as
one call; a stream that ends with neither a `finish_reason` nor `[DONE]`
throws a socket error naming what arrived, not a malformed answer.
`test-preflight.ts` [3c]: the row names the abort and the retry.
Mutant (scratch copy): the detector's firing line dropped fails two named
assertions — the stub sends all 42 frames and nothing says when it was aborted.

**Not taken.** Streaming with reasoning on (no budget, so no retry to send an
aborted call to; the answer's deltas would sit beside the thinking's); the
same treatment for `providerCall`'s chat path and `judgePair` (neither was
measured to run away — the ticket's condition); a rule that catches the
enumerating runaways (distinct items, no repetition: only a bound on the
answer's length would, and the budget is that bound); a knob for the abort
(one rule for the worker and the eval); a smaller `RUNAWAY_REPEATS` (two
copies is a duplicate a converging answer holds).

**Follow-ups.** SMD-1962: preflight's probes and the evals' diallers still run
under Bun's idle cut; the stream here resets it per frame, which is the third
outcome for that ticket's one `providerFetch`. SMD-2000: a call aborted on the
stream is the cheapest possible signal to escalate to the larger model on.
