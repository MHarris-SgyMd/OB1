Two test-live checks that read state once, right after a log line printed before that state is written, now wait for it. ## FORK

Two test-live rows poll for the state they assert, where they read it once right after a line printed before it is written (SMD-3612, SMD-3627)

**What changed.** In `db/test-live.ts`:
- **`[9f]`'s "…is no suspect at the next outage"** (SMD-3612) polls the thought's claim until it is out of hand, bounded at 5 s, and asserts the value the poll saw: `pending` and, as the first outage case already asks, `last_error` null.
- **`[38]`'s "a live call during the sweep stops it between thoughts"** (SMD-3627) polls `heartbeat:sleep` until the sleep's mark has its end, bounded at 5 s, and asserts the value the poll saw: `to` later than `from`. Asserting the polled value, not a re-read, follows the awake stamp's poll earlier in `[38]`, whose re-read could catch the next sleep's stamp.

**Why.**
- **SMD-3612:** Fork Checks push run 40990711143 (7930d64b), extract cell: `claim {"status":"claimed","last_error":null}`. The follower prints `<id> goes back to the pool unrecorded` in `sendBack` (`db/reembed.ts`) and returns; the claim goes back to `pending` only in the worker's `finally` (`release_claims_for_worker`), after it.
- **SMD-3627:** push run 41078539185 (19caf3c8), sleep cell: `slept {"from":"2026-10-30T15:13:45.360Z","to":null}`. The sweep prints `awake: a live write … during the re-derive sweep — the sleep ends` as it returns (`db/sleep.ts`). The scheduler marks the sleep's end after `asleep()` returns, and `stamper.slept()` only sets a field the row's next write carries (`db/pass-stamp.ts`): `awaitQuiet`'s first stamp. The failing read came a few milliseconds after the line, and its `from` was the woken sleep's own.

**Held.** Each mutant ran on main 19caf3c8 and on this change, with the shard cut to the section (`[9f]` alone, `[38]` alone):
- **The claim handed back 300 ms late** (a sleep before `release_claims_for_worker`): main's row fails with CI's own message, `claimed`; with the fix `[9f]` passes 30 of 30.
- **The sleep's end stamped 500 ms late** (a sleep after a woken `asleep()`): main's row fails; with the fix `[38]` passes 89 of 89.
- **A thought kept a suspect** (`outage.settled` dropped, so the next outage records it failed): the fix's row fails, the poll ending at `failed`.
- **A mark whose `to` is its `from`**: the fix's row fails.
- Unmutated, `[9f]` 30 of 30 and `[38]` 89 of 89.

**Not taken.**
- **A larger delay for the sleep's mutant.** 1.5 s fails the row with the fix too, and a second row besides: by then the brain has been quiet for the case's whole 3 s window, so `awaitQuiet` returns at its first read without stamping, and the next sleep's open mark replaces the woken one's before it is written. The sweep's line comes about 1.5 s after the waking write, so a stamp within 1.5 s of the line lands; 500 ms is inside that.
- **The other single reads that follow a log line** in `[9f]` and the sleep section were checked. Most read after the run has exited; the backed-off retry's row is stamped `failed` before its line; and `[9f]`'s deadlock-at-the-write case reads its claim at once after its line too, but its check, `status !== "failed"`, passes on `claimed`, so it cannot flake.
