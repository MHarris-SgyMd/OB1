## Changelog

A brain can now read the priority and due time a captured note states in its own words. With `OB1_INFER_PRIORITY` on (it's off by default), a capture asks the metadata model, in one more call beside the tags', for the level ("urgent", "not important") and the deadline ("by Friday") the text gives, and the phrase that says each. A level or a due time stands only when the text holds its phrase, an inferred level is at most High, and a relative date is counted from the capture's day in UTC by fixed rules ("by Friday" on a Tuesday is that Friday). Only a phrase the rules can't read ("next Friday") takes the model's date.

The result is stated as a priority flagged inferred, under the capturing key, on each open item the capture proposed from the thought, or on the thought when it proposed none, its reason quoting the phrases. It never outranks anyone's stated priority, and nothing ranks on it yet: `effective_priority`, the agenda and search pass inferred statements over until the operator-labelled measurement clears its bar. That measurement is `evals/eval-inferred-priority.ts`. On stable's captures (mostly agent session summaries), the operator's blind labels agree with 0.188 of the model's level reads and 0.500 of its due days, far under the bar set before any label was read. So nothing ranks on an inferred statement, and the opt-in stays off by default (SMD-3670).

## Record

The priority and due time a captured text states, read by the metadata model and stated inferred, opt-in per brain (PR 1), and the operator-labelled measurement its ranking waits on (PR 2) (SMD-3670)

The maintainer decided the plan on 2026-11-09:
- **Where it runs:** a call of its own at capture, beside the embedding, the tags and the genre calls. It is opt-in, and the tags' prompt is unchanged.
- **What it's stated on:** the items the capture proposed, else the thought.
- **Due dates:** the rules first, the model's date as the fallback.
- **The order:** the writer first (this PR), then the labelled measurement (PR 2), then `count_inferred` turned on only if the bar clears (PR 3).

The maintainer also confirmed three rules proposed with the plan, on 2026-11-09:
- **An inferred level is at most High.** "urgent" costs nothing to write in a note, and the ticket's own example reads "Urgent: …" as High.
- **A due phrase alone states Medium.** 116 takes no statement without a level, and Medium is what unknown counts as. The reason says the level is the default.
- **Only fresh captures are read.** A re-capture, an edit, or an import that doesn't go through capture states nothing.

**What changed.**
- **`server-portable/priority-infer.ts`:**
  - `INFER_PROMPT` (version 1) and `inferPriority`, through the egress gate. Any failure the provider answers infers nothing.
  - `readAnswer` holds each half to a phrase the text holds as whole words (`quotedIn`), and caps the level at High (`INFERRED_CEILING`). It drops the level:
    - for a level word the text negates everywhere, up to two words before or with "non-" (`negatedIn`: "urgent" in "not very urgent"), but not across emphasis ("never been more urgent");
    - for a High read off a phrase that negates its own cue ("no rush");
    - for a level phrase with no word about how much it matters that names a time or is the deadline's own words. The local 7B read "I need the slides by Friday" as High, so a plain deadline would have been High, not the due phrase's Medium.
  - `resolveDue` reads the most specific thing first:
    - a written date, with its year if one is stated ("March the 5th" too), and "the 12th" with nothing after it ("the 3rd week" is the model's);
    - tomorrow, then "in N days" or weeks and "N days from today";
    - the end of the month;
    - a weekday, its abbreviations that are no ordinary word too ("Fri", "Thurs"; not "sun" or "sat");
    - today, tonight or EOD last, so "EOD Friday" is Friday.
  - `resolveDue` leaves some phrases to the model or states no due time:
    - a phrase that names its day more than one way goes to the model (`oneAnchor`). That covers two kinds of anchor ("Fri 20th", "by Friday in two weeks"), two weekdays, a "from" that isn't a count from today ("a month from today"), and a weekday picked by its place ("the second Friday"). The rules read one anchor and dropped the other, beating the model's right date;
    - a weekday moved on ("Friday next week", "the Friday after next") goes to the model;
    - "before" takes a day off ("before the trip on Friday" is Thursday) unless a time of day or an end is named ("before 5pm Friday", "before the end of the month"); a count before it ("2 days before the 20th") is the model's;
    - a start ("not before", "not until", "after") is no due time;
    - neither is a day before the capture's.
  - `modelDay` takes a real day from the capture's own to 366 days on, only on a phrase that names a time, and on the weekday the phrase names if it names one. The local 7B gave "next Friday", captured on a Tuesday, as the next Tuesday.
  - `inferredStatement` gives a due phrase alone Medium (`DUE_ONLY_LEVEL`), and its reason says so.
- **`OB1_INFER_PRIORITY`:** in `EmbedConfig`, root.ts's `Env`, compose's api service and `deploy/.env.example`.
- **`core/writes.ts`:**
  - The capture runs the call in its `Promise.all`, on the chat gate's decision. For a key that can read, it runs only for a text no thought holds yet (`keyAtText`), since a re-capture states nothing. A capture-only key is asked either way, so the call's time never tells it whether a text exists.
  - `proposeActionItems` returns the items it opened.
  - `stateInferred` asserts `inferred: true` on them, else on the thought, for a fresh capture only. A refusal or a fault is logged and never fails the capture.
- **docs/priority.md's Inferred row** says what states one.

**PR 2, the measurement.** The maintainer chose the design on 2026-11-10:
- **The population:** stable's current captures (`actor_kind` agent, 1,394 on 2026-11-10, 1,262 of them session-hook summaries). Ingested rows never reach the inference.
- **The draw:** 60 texts the model read a level or a due day from, and 60 it read nothing from. The operator labels them blind, and a second blind rater gives a κ.
- **The convention:** a ticket's label quoted beside it is a citation, not a stated level.
- **The bar:** at least 0.80 of level calls and 0.90 of due calls read as the operator reads them, and High where no level is read under 0.05. Each half is measured on at least 10 calls.

`evals/inferred-priority.ts` holds the rules. Its registered section (the bar, the conventions, the population's SQL, the draw and the score) was hashed and pinned as `1239b47ca93a5870` before the sheet was drawn. `eval-inferred-priority.ts` runs, draws, freezes and reports. Its `--self-check` runs in CI and ci-parity. The fixture holds ids and numbers only, and the run file and the sheet are refused inside the repo.

**The result (2026-11-11): the bar fails, so `count_inferred` stays off and PR 3 is not built.**
- **The run:** the model read something from 186 of the 1,394 captures.
- **Levels:** of 48 level reads in the sample, the operator reads 9, all ticket exports whose "Priority:" header the operator took as the ticket's own level. Over session summaries the model read 0 of 38 right, and 9 of the 48 reads were High where the operator read no level.
- **Due days:** of 16 due reads, the operator reads 8. Four of those match one day in a list of 4–11 days quoted from a board; reading such lists as none gives 3 of 16.
- **The raters:** κ is 0.173 on level, split on those exports' headers, and 0.829 on due. With the second rater as the gold, it fails too (level 0.021, due 0.313). An unblinded review of all 120 after the freeze sided with the operator on those headers (a text that is the ticket carries its own level, so convention 2 was ambiguous there), and scored the model the same against its own labels.
