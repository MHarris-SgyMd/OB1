## FORK

test-live [10h] holds the run's end hand-back to `RELEASE_WAIT` alone — a hand-back bounded at the budget no longer passes (SMD-3737)

**What changed.** A [10h] case beside the lost poll's stop cases, note 18 in `db/test-live.ts`:
- **The run.** Following, under `BUDGET_SESSION` (750 ms), `RELEASE_WAIT` at its default 30 s.
- **The hold.** The case takes the claim's row as the judge is asked, and sends a first stop once it holds it. The thought's release then waits on the row past the budget with the stop already in, so `lostPoll` ends the run at the stop and passes its own hand-back over. That leaves the claim to the run's end hand-back, in the loop's `finally`, alone.
- **The let-go.** The row goes `BUDGET_MS + 500` after that hand-back is seen waiting on it, or as the run ends. That is the margin `within`'s landing keeps.
- **The asserts.** The claim ends `pending`. The release's lost poll is said as ended by the stop. "The claims this run holds could not go back to the pool" is not said. The stop line says what the run held is back in the pool, and the run exits 0.

**Why.** `releaseHeld` (`db/subject-sweep.ts`) waits up to `RELEASE_WAIT` under a scheduler's session, whatever the budget. It runs as the second stop's hand-back and as the run's end hand-back. During SMD-3713's review pass 2, a mutant bounded only the run's end hand-back, at `min(RELEASE_WAIT, budget)`, and it passed every [10h] row:
- `within`: the stop's hand-back waits on the row too and covers for it;
- `past`: `RELEASE_WAIT` there is `BUDGET_MS + 100`, so both bounds fail alike;
- the every-kind case: `RELEASE_WAIT` there is 500 ms, under the budget.

The run it breaks is a scheduler's that ends without a second stop, with a claim's row held a little past the budget. That run would leave the claim leased to its end.

**Held.** [10h] alone, the change's test against the unchanged engine and two mutants of the run's end hand-back:
- **The ticket's mutant,** `min(RELEASE_WAIT, budget)`: 222 of 223 pass, and the new case alone fails, the claim left `claimed`.
- **The budget alone as the bound:** the new case fails, as does the hard stop's race case (review pass 7). Its `RELEASE_WAIT` is 1 s, under its 30 s budget, so the run is still waiting on the run-out lease's row at the case's 20 s bound.
- **The unchanged engine:** 223 of 223 pass.

**Measured after.** On the Mac, by its lines' timestamps, the case takes 2.27 s alone, at load ~32. It takes 2.3–2.7 s in six runs of [10h], three side by side beside eight CPU burners, all six passing. The floor is about 2.2 s: the release's wait to the budget, up to 250 ms of the poll, and the hold's 1.25 s.

**Not taken.** A hold of `BUDGET_MS + 250` would take 250 ms off the case. It would also leave a 250 ms margin over the mutant's bound, half of `within`'s, for a case a mutant must fail on every run.
