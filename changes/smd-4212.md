## Changelog

`plan_context` now says when the brain has little on a request: a `little` gap when none of its first 8 knowledge hits reaches a similarity of 0.74, the bar measured on qwen3-embedding:4b@1024 (on 40 held-out requests, 0.885 balanced accuracy and 0.789 precision). On another embedding a `little_not_measured` note says the bar isn't measured there. `evals/eval-little.ts` makes the measurement (SMD-4212).

## Record

Where "the brain has little on this" begins, measured (SMD-4212)

`plan_context`'s knowledge section is a search, and a search keeps its nearest matches. A request the brain knows nothing about still got eight hits and no gap. SMD-2684 asked for the signal and left its bar to its PR 2, which measured cites instead. This PR measures the bar, and its PR 2 raises the gap.

**What changed.**
- **`evals/eval-little.ts`**, with the shape the maintainer chose on 2026-11-10 and preregistered on the ticket before any measured run (hash `703eb62defe1`):
  - **the draw:** 30 Backlog tickets of the brain's own projects and 30 of SMD's other projects, by a seeded hash, plus 20 key-free paraphrases. A paraphraser who saw only the titles wrote them, and the harness checked each: no key, at most 25 words, no six words copied;
  - **the hits:** 8 per request, from `plan_context`'s own search, called through `search_thoughts` with the same arguments;
  - **the truth:** per hit, from two blind raters with κ; a request has little at 1 relevant hit or fewer;
  - **the candidates:** `top(t)`, `count(f)` and `literal`, t and f from 0.30 to 0.80; dev chooses by balanced accuracy, test confirms against the bar;
  - **the blind sheets:** a paraphrase's sheet names no key;
  - **a `--self-check` of 49 probes,** in CI.
- **`server-portable/core/plan.ts`:** `knowledgeGaps`' comment pointed at SMD-2684 PR 2 for the bar. It now names this measurement and SMD-4212's PR 2.

**Result: the bar is met by `top(0.74)`.**
- **On test:** 0.885 balanced accuracy and 0.789 precision of `little` (15 little said right, 4 wrong; 20 not-little right, 1 wrong). κ 0.789 over 640 hits.
- **Little, by kind of request:** brain tickets 3 of 30, other-project tickets 25 of 30, paraphrases 11 of 20.
- **The four false calls on test** are other-project tickets where the brain holds 2 relevant rows.
- **The one miss** is a brain ticket held mostly as its board record, whose own record scores 0.93.
- **Neighbouring thresholds:** `top(0.70)` and `top(0.72)` each score 0.906 balanced accuracy and 0.833 precision on test, so the bar sits on a plateau. evals/README.md has the table and the caveats.

- **The lean value:** the lean MCP value carries no literal matches, so they are read from the reply text's `Contains` lines.
- **PR 1's review pass 1:** no wrong number; a re-score on the fixed code gives the same result. These probes had no teeth, and now each does:
  - both of the choice's tie-breaks;
  - the window rule for other-project tickets;

  - the preregistration hash, now pinned;
  - the claim that the search is `plan_context`'s, now read from core/plan.ts and core/schemas.ts.

**PR 2, the gap** (`server-portable/core/plan.ts`, `render.ts`):
- **`knowledgeGaps`** takes the embedding the search ran on, as `<model>@<dim>` from `ctx.embedConfig()`. With hits and a vector, it raises:
  - `little` with the best similarity of the first `LITTLE_HITS` (8) hits and the bar (`LITTLE_BAR` 0.74), when that similarity is under it on `LITTLE_MEASURED_ON` (qwen3-embedding:4b@1024);
  - `little_not_measured`, a note and not a coverage gap, with the best similarity and both embeddings, on any other.

  A hit with no similarity counts as 0, as the measurement read it; when no hit read has one, the gap says so (`topSimilarity` null). With no vector, no hit, or no embedding named, there is no little gap.
- **The text** says the little gap as a coverage signal, with its similarity and bar. A bar not measured finds nothing, so "Nothing else found" and the weighing line still stand beside it.
- **The value** keeps the embedding ids: `guard` holds `embeddingId` and `measuredOn` to `<model>@<dim>` with no space, and nulls anything else.
- **The tool's description and server-portable/README.md** name the gap; the REST client is regenerated for the description.
- **Held:** test-server [37]:
  - the gap at the bar's edges (0.7399 little, 0.74 not), no similarity read as 0;
  - another embedding not measured, no vector or no embedding none;
  - the words, the value's ids, and a spaced id nulled.

  Through `planContext` with a stubbed embedding. A ticket the brain holds only as its board record still reads as covered: its own record is a close match.
- **PR 2's review pass 1:**
  - **The limit:** the bar was measured over 8 hits, and `plan_context` takes 1 to 20. The gap now reads the first 8. A smaller limit reads fewer; on the 80 measured requests, the best of the first 1, 3 or 5 hits made the same call as the best of 8 on every one.
  - **No teeth before:** no row put the best similarity anywhere but first, or varied the embedding's width, and none ran the gap through a real server. They do now: test-server [37], and test-e2e-sql [26] through the real search.
  - **The words:** a similarity just under the bar printed as the bar (0.740, under 0.74), and now rounds down. No similarity at all is now said as none. A model id starting with `@` is now kept. The description calls the not-measured note a note.
