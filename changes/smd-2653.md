## Changelog

The weekly digest ranks the window by recency alone and no longer reads `importance`, so a capture-only key's ten captures carrying a high value can no longer rank every other key's thought out of it (SMD-2653). **Upgrading:** `db/weekly-digest.ts` drops `--min-importance`; a scheduled run that passes it is refused as an unknown flag, so drop the flag.

## FORK

The weekly digest ranks by recency alone — a capture-only key's `importance` no longer decides what it reads (SMD-2653)

**What changed.** `db/weekly-digest.ts` stops reading `importance`, the native column and `metadata.importance` both, as SMD-3661 decided (`docs/priority.md`, decision 8: the key is retired):
- **The ranking.** `rankByRecency` replaces `thoughtImportance` and `rankAndTrim`: the window's rows newest first, capped at 200, of which the prompt takes the first 80 as before. The threshold, the widening below ten rows and `--min-importance` are gone.
- **The read.** `readThoughts` no longer probes for an `importance` column or selects it.
- **The prompt.** Each row in the synthesis prompt loses its `importance` field, and the prompt calls the rows "the N most recent thoughts", not "high-signal" ones.
- **The sort compares instants.** The old recency tiebreak compared `String(created_at)`, and a row from the database carries a Date, whose string leads with its weekday. With no importance anywhere (stable held none on 2026-11-02), that tiebreak was the whole order: a quiet week's widened pool of 60 began with the window's Wednesdays, not its newest rows.

`--min-importance` was a flag of maintainer tooling (`db/`, outside the version contract), so its removal is a `minor`.

**Why.** Any key could set `metadata.importance` on its own rows, a capture-only key included. SMD-2617's review pass 3 showed it on the real REST door: with a write key's 15 captures and a capture key's 10 carrying `importance: 9`, the pool held the capture key's 10 and none of the writer's. Only that key's text reached the synthesis model and the Telegram sink. Without its rows the pool held all 15 of the writer's.

**Held.**
- `test-weekly-digest.ts`:
  - Dates across three weekdays rank by the instant, and ISO strings too.
  - The repro, pure: a writer's 15 newer rows and a capture key's 10 older ones carrying importance 9 in metadata and as a column. The writer's lead, and the pool is the instants' order.
  - The cap of 200, and a prompt that carries a row's type and text but no `importance`.
- Four mutants, each refused: the string sort, importance ranked first again, `importance` back in the prompt, and the cap dropped.
- `test-live.ts` [36] runs the CLI without `--min-importance`. Its stub keeps the synthesis prompt, and the window's three newest rows, each carrying an importance, reach it with none of their importance.

**Not taken.** Refusing `importance` to a capture-only key at capture (the ticket's option 1). Once the digest stops reading it, nothing in the core or `db/` ranks by the key, and the vendored workers may still write it as data. Once SMD-3667 lands, the digest ranks by the effective priority.
