## Changelog

The project presents itself as Daemonic Brain, which grew out of Open Brain, not as a fork of it. `MAINTAINING.md` holds what FORK.md said in the present tense: versioning, cutting a release, deploying, CI, the review-pass convention and the known issues. FORK.md is the record of the fork era, and the index of numbered changes is in `changes/README.md` (SMD-3765).

## FORK

MAINTAINING.md is the front door and FORK.md the fork era's record — the project no longer presents itself as a fork (SMD-3765)

**What changed.** PR 1 of three: the front door and the doc split.
- **`MAINTAINING.md` (new)** takes FORK.md's present-tense sections. These are "Versioning" (with "What the version contract covers"), "Cutting a release", "Deploying", "Vendored content: audit once, hold the delta", "Checking CI", "Review passes", "The recurring defect: a value defined twice", "Known issues we did NOT fix" and "Before this touches anything sensitive".
  - The text moves as it was, except for the fork's voice and what the move itself changes: where the index lives, and the pin now being FORK.md's.
  - "Checking CI" takes the ruleset paragraph from "Landing a rebase on `main`".
  - "Before this touches anything sensitive" now states the brain as it is (scoped keys, the `auth` profile, local Ollama by default), where it described upstream's model, and its licence paragraph names Open Brain as the origin.
- **`FORK.md` is the fork era's record:** the pin, changes 1–17, "Files we own", the detach, how rebases ran and why nothing went upstream.
  - A section that moved keeps its heading with a pointer to MAINTAINING.md, so the 43 frozen migrations' citations, "Cutting a release" among them, still land.

**Why.** The pin was frozen on 2026-10-25, no parity is kept, v2.0.0 drops the pin from the version, and the project has its own name. FORK.md's first lines still said it was a soft fork that kept taking from upstream. The licence settles the wording: the code is a derivative of Open Brain, so the licence and its copyright notice stay, and upstream's name may be used only to identify it as the origin. So the text says "grew out of Open Brain", not just "inspired by".
