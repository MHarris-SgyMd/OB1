## Changelog

The project presents itself as Daemonic Brain, which grew out of Open Brain, not as a fork of it. `MAINTAINING.md` holds what FORK.md said in the present tense: versioning, cutting a release, deploying, CI, the review-pass convention and the known issues. FORK.md is the record of the fork era, and the index of numbered changes is in `changes/README.md` (SMD-3765).
Commits to the project's own code say `[core]` (`[fork]` is still accepted), a fragment's record sits under `## Record` (`## FORK` is still read), the version constant is `BRAIN_VERSION`, and the checker and the index renderer are `scripts/check-consistency.ts` and `scripts/changes-index.ts` (SMD-3765).
CI's workflow is `.github/workflows/brain-checks.yml`, "Brain Checks", and main's one required check is its job "Brain Checks passed" (SMD-3765).
