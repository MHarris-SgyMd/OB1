## Changelog

`evals/eval-priority.ts` measures whether a read puts what matters first: a seeded personal brain and board, seven prompts with a gold order each, scored by top-1, top-3 and Kendall's tau through the server's own search, keyless and in CI, with a pre-registered bar a priority read must clear and a dogfood set of "what next" sessions recorded by hand. Today's search gets top-1 on 1 of 7 prompts (SMD-3662).

## FORK

A "what matters now" eval: a seeded brain, a gold order per prompt, today's search as the baseline, and the bar a priority read must clear (SMD-3662)

**What changed.**
- **`evals/priority.ts`** holds the rules, all pure:
  - a seeded corpus of 18 items and 7 prompts, its board rows built by board-sync's own adapter (`renderIssue`, `issueFacets`), pinned by one hash with the file's source from the stub through the baseline reads (the stub, the seeding, the baseline call, the score, the mutant, the bar, the baseline reads' knobs), the bar's numbers pinned apart;
  - `MEASURED`, outside the hash: where a priority read joins, as a function from a prompt's key and words to thought ids, never seeing the gold;
  - a keyless stub embedder: a hashed bag of words over a shared background axis, with a hashed noise axis per text;
  - the score: top-1, top-3, and Kendall's tau-b over the gold items, with an item the read left out ranked 11;
  - the shuffled-gold mutant and its exact chance;
  - the pre-registered bar (`BAR`, `verdict`);
  - the dogfood rows' shape.
- **`evals/eval-priority.ts`** runs them:
  - `--self-check` probes every rule against hand-known answers, with no database.
  - `--seeded` resets the schema at a throwaway `DATABASE_URL` and plants the corpus under each writer's `ob1.actor`. It runs every prompt through the search tools themselves, over MCP on the server in-process (the write-path gate's `mcpUnderTest`), the query embedded by the server's own embedder through a loopback stub: `search`, `search_thoughts` with `prefer_current`, and `search_thoughts` with the filter `{"status_type":"backlog","priority":"High"}`. It prints the per-prompt hits, the table, the mutant beside chance and the bar on this baseline. It exits 1 when a baseline read errors, when a baseline read's scores differ from the recorded `BASELINE`, when the gold ceiling can't clear the bar, when the dogfood rows aren't sound, or when the mutant isn't at chance. It prints each measured read's verdict against the baselines of the same run; a measured read that errors on a prompt scores it false, false, 0, as the bar says. The gold ceiling runs through the measured path on every run.
- **`evals/fixtures/priority-dogfood.json`** holds the ticket's two cases, 10-14 and 11-02, as ids and numbers (check 9).
- **`db/test-support.ts`** gains `refuseHeldDatabase` and its decision, `heldRefusal`: refuse a database holding thoughts unless `OB1_FIXTURE_RESET=1`. `eval-projection-replay.ts --fixture-check` and `eval-priority.ts --seeded` share it, where each had a copy.
- **CI:** Fork Checks runs `--self-check` with the eval rules and `--seeded` in the data-layer job after the projection-replay fixture. `db/ci-parity.sh` mirrors both.

**Why.** On 2026-10-14 and on 2026-11-02, both brains answered "what is the highest-value unstarted issue?" with a ticket whose words matched the question: SMD-2540 over SMD-2888, then SMD-1853 over SMD-2908. Each was caught by hand. Nothing in `evals/` scored an ordering by importance, so SMD-3660's reads would have shipped with no number saying they helped.

**Measured** (main 8aa855f1; two runs identical). The table is in `evals/README.md`.

| read | top-1 | top-3 | mean tau |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| plain | 1 of 7 | 2 of 7 | 0.252 |
| `prefer_current` | 1 of 7 | 2 of 7 | 0.212 |
| filter | 0 of 7 | 0 of 7 | 0.055 |

Read against shuffled gold, every read scores within 0.005 of chance.

**The bar** (decided before any priority read is measured, and confirmed by the maintainer with the gold orders on 2026-11-02). On the seeded set at this corpus, a priority read helps only if:
- it gets top-1 on at least 5 of the 7 prompts;
- that is at least 2 more prompts than the best baseline read;
- its mean tau is at least 0.25 above the best baseline read's;
- no prompt that a baseline read kept in the top 3 leaves it.

On this baseline that means 5 of 7, and a tau of at least 0.502. The dogfood rows are reported, not gated.

**Held.**
- `--self-check` passes 79 checks.
- These mutants are each refused:
  - an order-blind top-1, by the self-check and by the seeded run's own chance check;
  - a constant tau, no background, a null tau counted as 1, and no regression check;
  - a changed noise weight, bar, absent rank or read knob, by the registration hash;
  - a changed `search_thoughts` `limit` or `prefer_current` default;
  - a recorded baseline that differs from the run, a baseline read that throws, an unreachable bar, and a fixture with no `rows` array, each by the seeded run;
  - a changed bar and board rows planted without board-sync's facets, by the self-check. An honest measured read added to `MEASURED` leaves the hash as it was;
  - a measured read that looks up the gold (defined outside `MEASURED`) or ranks by the items' facts, a broken answer-moving check, and an edited gold, each by the seeded run;
  - a second `--seeded`, or `--fixture-check`, on a database holding thoughts, by the shared guard (exit 2).
- The first stub had no background axis. It made texts that share no word orthogonal, so threshold 0 dropped them and the filter read returned nothing, where stable's returned all four High tickets. The probe now requires a background cosine well above 0.

**Review pass 2** (10 findings, 8 fixed and one in part):

Declined again: the copied "database holds thoughts" guard; and comment edits counting toward the hash, which is deliberate, since "nothing in the section moved" can be checked without judging the edit.

- The facts never reached the database, though the docs said later reads were seeded from them. Now the docs say what's planted: a later read's PR plants what its store reads, as a new registration, with the bar pinned apart.
- A non-object row before another still threw in `dogfoodProblems`.
- An exit inside the seeding's `try` skipped its `finally`.
- Problem lists were computed two or three times per assertion.

Declined a third time: the copied guard.

**Review pass 4** (7 findings, all fixed; and a claims audit of every doc the PR touched):
- A measured read returning one id twice could fail the whole run through the chance gate, under a message blaming the score. Ids are now taken once each, and the gate reads the baselines and the ceiling only.
- The check that a measured read doesn't read the answer scanned the `MEASURED` array's text, so a read defined above it passed. It is now behavioural: every measured read runs again with each gold reversed and the facts moved, and fails the run if its answer moves. The ceiling is the control.
- The seeding `INSERT` sat outside the hash. `plantRows` moved into the hashed section.
- `--seeded` printed verdicts without checking the registration. It now refuses an unregistered corpus.
- The hash read board-sync's rendered text, so a cosmetic change to the adapter would break this eval. A board item is registered by its issue's fields, and an adapter change that moves the numbers trips the drift check.
- The knob check matched one source line of `core/reads.ts` by regex. `reads.ts` exports the knobs; the baseline call imports them and parses `search_thoughts`' defaults from its schema.
- The guard was copied from `eval-projection-replay.ts`, and the header said `test-support` enforced it. It is `test-support`'s `refuseHeldDatabase` now, used by both.
- The audit fixed five claims: "a function of its arguments" for a file that reads itself; the dogfood question misquoted; the exit conditions incomplete; every read called a hybrid search; `DOGFOOD_READS` listed without the measured reads.

**Review pass 5** (10 findings, 6 fixed, 1 in part, 3 declined; and a mutation sweep over the whole diff):
