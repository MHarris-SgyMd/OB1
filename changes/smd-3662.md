## Changelog

`evals/eval-priority.ts` measures whether a read puts what matters first: a seeded personal brain and board, seven prompts with a gold order each, scored by top-1, top-3 and Kendall's tau through the server's own search, keyless and in CI, with a pre-registered bar a priority read must clear and a dogfood set of "what next" sessions recorded by hand. Today's search gets top-1 on 1 of 7 prompts (SMD-3662).

## FORK

A "what matters now" eval: a seeded brain, a gold order per prompt, today's search as the baseline, and the bar a priority read must clear (SMD-3662)

**What changed.**
- **`evals/priority.ts`** holds the rules, all pure:
  - a seeded corpus of 18 items and 7 prompts, pinned by one hash with the file's source from the stub through the reads (the stub, the score, the mutant, the bar, the reads' knobs);
  - a keyless stub embedder: a hashed bag of words over a shared background axis, with a hashed noise axis per text;
  - the score: top-1, top-3, and Kendall's tau-b over the gold items, with an item the read left out ranked 11;
  - the shuffled-gold mutant and its exact chance;
  - the pre-registered bar (`BAR`, `verdict`);
  - the dogfood rows' shape.
- **`evals/eval-priority.ts`** runs them:
  - `--self-check` probes every rule against hand-known answers, and reads the plain and filter reads' knobs from `core/reads.ts` and `search_thoughts`' block of `core/schemas.ts`.
  - `--seeded` resets the schema at a throwaway `DATABASE_URL` and plants the corpus under each writer's `ob1.actor`. It runs every prompt through `SqlStore.hybridThoughts` three ways: as `search` sends it, with `prefer_current`, and with the filter `{"status_type":"backlog","priority":"High"}`. It prints the per-prompt hits, the table, the mutant beside chance and the bar on this baseline. It exits 1 when a baseline read errors, when a baseline read's scores differ from the recorded `BASELINE`, when the gold ceiling can't clear the bar, when the dogfood rows aren't sound, or when the mutant isn't at chance. A measured read that errors on a prompt scores it false, false, 0, as the bar says.
- **`evals/fixtures/priority-dogfood.json`** holds the ticket's two cases, 10-14 and 11-02, as ids and numbers (check 9).
- **CI:** Fork Checks runs `--self-check` with the eval rules and `--seeded` in the data-layer job after the projection-replay fixture. `db/ci-parity.sh` mirrors both.

**Why.** On 2026-10-14 and on 2026-11-02, both brains answered "what is the highest-value unstarted issue?" with a ticket whose words matched the question: SMD-2540 over SMD-2888, then SMD-1853 over SMD-2908. Each was caught by hand. Nothing in `evals/` scored an ordering by importance, so SMD-3660's reads would have shipped with no number saying they helped.

**Measured** (main 8aa855f1; two runs identical). The table is in `evals/README.md`.

| read | top-1 | top-3 | mean tau |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| plain | 1 of 7 | 2 of 7 | 0.261 |
| `prefer_current` | 1 of 7 | 2 of 7 | 0.300 |
| filter | 0 of 7 | 0 of 7 | 0.055 |

Read against shuffled gold, every read scores within 0.005 of chance.

**The bar** (decided before any priority read is measured). On the seeded set at this corpus, a priority read helps only if:
- it gets top-1 on at least 5 of the 7 prompts;
- that is at least 2 more prompts than the best baseline read;
- its mean tau is at least 0.25 above the best baseline read's;
- no prompt that a baseline read kept in the top 3 leaves it.

On this baseline that means 5 of 7, and a tau of at least 0.550. The dogfood rows are reported, not gated.

**Held.**
- `--self-check` passes 62 checks.
- These mutants are each refused:
  - an order-blind top-1, by the self-check and by the seeded run's own chance check;
  - a constant tau, no background, a null tau counted as 1, and no regression check;
  - a changed noise weight, bar, absent rank or read knob, by the registration hash;
  - a changed `search_thoughts` `limit` or `prefer_current` default;
  - a recorded baseline that differs from the run, a baseline read that throws, an unreachable bar, and a fixture with no `rows` array, each by the seeded run.
- The first stub had no background axis. It made texts that share no word orthogonal, so threshold 0 dropped them and the filter read returned nothing, where stable's returned all four High tickets. The probe now requires a background cosine well above 0.

**Review pass 1** (10 findings, 9 fixed):
- A read that errored aborted the run, where the bar scores it false, false, 0.
- The order-blind check never called the scorer.
- The hash left out the stub's code, its noise weights and the bar.
- The knob check missed `search_thoughts`' `limit` and `prefer_current` defaults and wasn't anchored to its block.
- The mutant's tau was averaged over every prompt, diluting its tolerance.
- A malformed dogfood read threw, and a row whose alternative was its pick passed.
- An empty `blockedBy` counted as blocked.
- The bar's thresholds were computed twice; `barOn` is now the one copy.

Hashing `String(fn)` would have moved with a Bun upgrade, so the hash reads the stub's section of the source. Its first markers matched their own quotes inside the reader and hashed 53 characters; a mutant caught that. Declined: sharing the "database holds thoughts" guard with `eval-projection-replay.ts`, which belongs in `db/test-support.ts` and is a change of its own.

**Review pass 2** (10 findings, 8 fixed and one in part):
- The seeded step could exit 1 only on the mutant, which reads no database, so it couldn't catch a change to search. The recorded `BASELINE` is now asserted.
- A baseline read that errored was scored 0 and passed. It now fails the run.
- An unreachable bar only printed. It now fails the run.
- The hash left out the score, the verdict and the reads' knobs. It now covers the file from the stub through the reads.
- A dogfood read could take any name. Names come from `DOGFOOD_READS`.
- Every prompt reused one seed, so prompts whose golds had one length shared their shuffles. Each prompt now gets its own seed, from `seededRandom` (the fourth copy of mulberry32 is gone).
- A fixture with no `rows` array threw. It is now reported.
- A CRLF checkout would have hashed differently. The section is read as LF.

Declined again: the copied "database holds thoughts" guard; and comment edits counting toward the hash, which is deliberate, since "nothing in the section moved" can be checked without judging the edit.
