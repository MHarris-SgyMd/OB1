## FORK

The data layer's upgrade and preflight pair runs from the job's start, on a Postgres of its own (SMD-3195)

**What changed.**
- **`.github/workflows/fork-checks.yml`**, in the `data-layer` job:
  - A second service, `pair-postgres`: the same image, on port 5433, with `ob1upgrade` as its database. The pair runs there, and the job's other suites keep `postgres` and ob1test.
  - "Start the incremental upgrade and preflight, in the background", right after Install. It creates `ob1preflight` beside `ob1upgrade`, then starts each suite in a subshell that outlives the step, under its 600 s limit. Each subshell writes its suite's log, then its exit code by a rename, to `$RUNNER_TEMP`, with the start time and the limit beside them. The step sets no `DATABASE_URL`, so nothing it runs can reach ob1test.
  - "Incremental upgrade and preflight (started at the job's start)", the job's last step. It waits for both exit codes, up to the limit and 30 s more: the 10 s kill after the limit, and 20 s for the code. Then it prints the two logs and summary lines as the old step did, and fails on either. It runs after an earlier failure too (`!cancelled()`), so a failure beside one of the pair's is seen beside it. If the start step did not run, or died before it wrote the start time, it says so at once.
  - The old "Incremental upgrade and preflight, side by side" step is gone. Its comment moved to the start step and says what changed.
- **test-preflight runs on `ob1preflight`.** It ran on ob1test, after every other suite but the two extension suites. It changes the database it runs in, for a window each, putting each back after: `default_transaction_isolation`, `work_mem`, `jit`, CONNECT revoked from PUBLIC, USAGE on `public` revoked, an RLS policy on `thoughts`. Beside test-upgrade it needs a database of its own, as test-upgrade has had since SMD-2219.
- **`db/test-upgrade.ts`** waited until *any* backend in the cluster was blocked in an `upsert_thought` before letting its raw writer commit. Another backend waiting in one, in any database, released the writer early, and the fallback the case exists for went untested with every assertion passing. It now watches its own capture's backend by pid, as its other waits do.
- **`server-portable/test-e2e-sql.ts`:** its reads of what a Postgres's databases share are scoped to its own database. That covers [21]'s count of advisory locks, which counted test-upgrade's lock in `postgres` while the two shared a Postgres; its two lock-wait samplers in [14]; and its `pg_terminate_backend` by query pattern. With the pair on its own Postgres, nothing in CI reaches these any more. They are kept so a suite run beside it in another database of its Postgres, as this change's first build ran the pair, cannot fail them.
- **`server-portable/test-rest-sql.ts`** seeds its pending proposal judged an hour ahead. brain_info's oldest pending age is then 0 s at both doors: each door rounds its own read's age, and under load two reads either side of a half second answered 0 and 1. The race was there before; this change runs the suite beside the pair's load, where nothing ran beside it.
- **Headers and `db/ci-parity.sh`:** the three suites' headers say what they run beside and what they scope; the pair's role lists are complete. ci-parity's comment says CI runs the pair on a Postgres of its own. Its lines, which check 35 holds to CI's, are unchanged: locally the suites still run in series, in their places, on one database.

**Why.** Since SMD-3170 (b0f8b2c7), in the 7 green runs on main through 3c2575c7, the data layer was the run's slowest job, a median 313 s. The pair's step was a median 179 s of it (over the last 20 runs), and ran after 106 s of other suites it does not depend on. Expected: the job takes about 25 s of set-up plus the pair, about 205–215 s. The run's slowest job then becomes Portable server or the second-brain job, at about 265–270 s: Portable, a median 270 s, in the 3 runs since SMD-3193 shortened the other.

**Why a Postgres of its own.** The first build ran the pair on two more databases of the job's Postgres. An audit of what the pair and the job's other suites touch in common found four unscoped reads, now scoped. But review pass 1 found what no query can scope: a cluster's databases share its transaction horizon. An open transaction of the pair's held back the snapshot of test-plugins-sql.ts's `CREATE INDEX`, so Postgres marked the index too new for the plan's snapshot, and its plan read a sequential scan. That failed 9 runs in 30, and 4 in 4 with a transaction ID held in another database. That was the third collision through the shared cluster, after preflight's database settings and the reads, so the maintainer chose a second service over fixing each one.

**Not taken.**
- A `VACUUM` before test-plugins-sql's index, which passed 2 of 2 with the transaction ID held. It mends that one assertion, and leaves every later one that reads a plan after building an index, or relies on pruning, to meet the pair's horizon.
- Splitting the pair into a job of its own. That needs its own runner, checkout and install (about 25 s of runner time) and a nineteenth job, where two overlapping runs already exceed GitHub Free's 20 concurrent jobs. The second service costs another pull of a cached image and a container start, whose start-up overlaps the first's health wait.
- Stopping the poll early when a leg's subshell dies without writing its code. The step still fails then, at the deadline; the path needs the `cd` into a directory Install just used to fail.

**Held.**
- Built on main 66bd73f3.
- The audit found every role name disjoint between the pair and the job's other suites, and no `ALTER ROLE` on the connecting role.
- Replayed locally from the workflow, against `db/with-postgres.sh` databases with the same image, names and password as CI's services.
- Replayed with two Postgres containers standing in for the two services: every suite passed, test-upgrade 710/710 and test-preflight 892/892, the job in 113 s.
- A transaction holding an ID, while test-plugins-sql.ts runs: held on the pair's Postgres, 4 runs in 4 pass. Held in the same Postgres's `postgres` database, the layout the second service replaces, 2 runs in 2 fail at the index assertion, the flake reproduced.
- Review pass 1's run-it replays, the pair still sharing the cluster, are recorded under its pass below.
- A failing upgrade leg (exit 3) or preflight leg (exit 4) fails the last step, naming the leg. A hang ends as 124 at its limit. A start step that died first is named at once. Two passing legs pass.
- Check 35 still reads both legs: a renamed script, a dropped `cd`, an added flag and a dropped `DATABASE_URL` are each refused.
- An advisory lock held in `postgres`, as test-upgrade's is: test-e2e-sql passes 668/668 with the scoped count, and with the count unscoped (the mutant) fails [21] with 1 lock seen.
- The checker and actionlint 1.7.7 (with shellcheck) pass.

**Tidied after the last pass.** The start step's comment, edited in both passes, rewrapped to the file's 80 columns, no word changed.
