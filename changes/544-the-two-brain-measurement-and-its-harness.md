# 544. The two-brain measurement and its harness — one backlogged brain takes the slot broker's whole background room; model residency, not slots, stalls live calls (SMD-3379)

**What changed.** PR 3b-3b of the ticket, whose earlier PRs are change 495. The maintainer's cut of PR 3b-3 (2026-11-14) left 3b-3b the two-brain measurement, its arms decided 2026-11-16: the canary and a scratch brain (`ob1-3379`, the canary's model settings, sleep its one pass) on this Mac's Ollama (`OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=3`, `OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS=2`) and its h2o decide tier, both keyed to an `ob1-federated` broker (Ollama `slots: 3, reserve: 1`; h2o `slots: 1`, `per_model: false`), each brain's fixed share `chat=1,embed=1`, the two-brain share `.env.example` names, and stable left running and only read; three arms of 20 minutes (both brains backlogged under fixed shares, the same with the broker, one backlogged with the broker), each with the same ~150 generated notes in every backlogged brain (200 in the arm that ran) and `bench-live.ts` timing live calls on both from minute 12.

`db/bench-brains.ts` (new) is the harness. `load` captures an arm's notes (the same text in every brain, source `smd3379-measure`, ids kept). `sample` reads every brain's `brain_info` and `worker_status` and Ollama's loaded models every 15 s. `clean` reads each listed or found thought back and deletes it only where its whole text is the generator's own. `summary` reads from each brain's sleep heartbeat tallies (PR 2b-2a) the sleep's background calls, their mean number in flight and its slot waits, in all and by pass, over two windows of an arm, a tally that falls counted as the sleep begun again. `load` and `clean` run only on a brain that says its tier is canary or working. `bench-live.ts` takes `--key-file` beside `--mcp`, for a brain no connector names. A refusal in either harness names a flag or a brain and a failure's code, never a path typed, a file's text or a parser's words. The runs' samples, live calls and Ollama's scheduler lines are kept on the maintainer's machine; the canary's 386 notes and every probe were deleted after, and the scratch brain and the broker torn down.

**Why.** The ticket's Verify on two brains: with both backlogged, neither brain's live captures wait for a slot and their background work splits the background slots; with one backlogged, it uses its share alone under fixed shares, or every free slot under the broker. Nothing measured what a brain's background work held of a shared model server. **Version contract.** Maintainer tooling and a record, nothing a brain reads: a patch.

**Measured** (2026-11-16). A baseline, no notes and the broker unused: two `bench-live.ts` runs at once, a capture 3.2 s at the median on both brains, a search 118–141 ms. With the sleep's default of one worker a pass, the backlogged canary held 0.91 background calls in flight on the broker and never waited for a slot: a pass asks for no more than its workers, so neither a share of 1 nor the broker's 2 binds it, and both brains ran `OB1_SLEEP_WORKERS=3` from then (that arm stopped after 3.6 minutes and 16 calls).

One brain backlogged on the broker (arm C3, 200 notes in the canary, the scratch brain idle): over the 11.3 minutes of stamps before the live calls, extraction held 1.95 calls in flight on qwen2.5:7b, the broker's whole background room of 2, its 51 calls waiting 658 s for a slot — the Verify's "with one backlogged, every free slot (the broker)" holds — and 40 of consolidation's asks waited 173 s, for the h2o tier's one slot by the calls it finished then (a pass's tally does not part its servers); the canary's 2.17 in all is its sleep's alone, board-sync's calls not in it, and adds its passes over both servers, a call counting whole at the stamp after it ends. The second slot did not raise extraction's rate: 4.5 calls a minute against 5.3 with one worker (from that arm's sleep start, over three minutes), each call about 26 s against 11 s.

Through C3 Ollama held qwen2.5:7b alone (75 of 77 samples): a load of qwen3.8:27b (consolidation's and the subject pass's model, about 22 GiB; 7–14 GB free at its evictions) decided at 17:59:48 UTC waited until 18:23:54 for qwen2.5:7b to go idle, and another from 18:26:33 to 18:33:50, and requests for the evicted embedder waited behind them — the backlogged brain's own extraction, within the broker's room, kept qwen2.5:7b busy. Of each brain's timed live calls from 18:17 to 18:34, five needing an embedding timed out at 120 s (searches answered by exact matches alone, a capture saved without a vector), three answered after 22–65 s, three in under 0.4 s between the two waits, and two captures timed out on their metadata call, though the harness's direct metadata calls to qwen2.5:7b answered in 3.6–11 s then (not explained); stable's four captures ending 18:29–18:34 took 69–120 s (its api's log). That is no slot's: it is model residency, PR 3c's, and the broker's room does not prevent it. Ollama also runs qwen3.8:27b one call at a time ("does not currently support parallel requests") whatever a registry's `slots`. After the waits, the canary's backlog not yet drained (98 extraction rows at 18:19, unsampled after), its captures took 3.9 s at the median (3.4–7.4 s) and its searches 122 ms.

**Not measured.** Both brains backlogged (arms B and A), the Verify's split of the background slots between them. Another session upgraded the canary twice while the arms ran: to b47103f3 at 17:48 UTC, eight minutes into the first C3 load (named C2 in the evidence), and to a91a76e4 during B's load (begun 21:46:55, the new servers up at 21:53), 138 of its captures answered HTTP 500. The second build changes the claim loop, so B and A could not run on C3's build; and B's live captures took 66 s at the median on both measured brains while Ollama churned between qwen2.5:7b and qwen3.8:27b (35 evictions, 59 loads). The maintainer stopped the measurement there (2026-11-16): B and A wait for 3c's residency rule and an hour in which nothing else moves the canary.

**Held.** `bench-brains.ts --self-check`, 122 rows, in CI's latency harness step beside `bench-live.ts`'s and in `db/ci-parity.sh`: the notes and their fingerprint, the brains file, what a sample keeps, the windows' arithmetic and `summary`'s table from fixtures, each subcommand's flags, what a refusal says of a file, and the whole of `clean` against a brain in memory, the tier guard with it. Rows build the server's own replies for a thought that is gone with `render.ts`'s renderers, so a change to their words fails there, not on a brain. Of the author's 203 mutants over six review passes, 202 are killed; the one that lives is the grammar of a refusal. `main`'s wiring and the network paths stay outside the self-check, as the step's comment says: they were run against a real REST core and MCP server on a throwaway database in every pass from the second.
