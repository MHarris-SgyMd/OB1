## Changelog

The consolidation judge asks prompt version 4: five verdicts (unrelated, related, evolves, duplicate, outdates), with the words that show which thought is current quoted and checked. Only outdates and a duplicate become proposals, and a proposal's confidence is the model's token probability where the endpoint returns logprobs. On the dogfood brain the judge proposed again 2 of the 126 pairs a reviewer had rejected, where p3 proposed 119; `evals/eval-judge.ts` measures it on a brain's own labels (SMD-1873).

## FORK

The consolidation judge says something — five verdicts, a quoted direction, and a confidence from the model's own token probabilities (SMD-1873)

**What changed.**
- **The prompt** (`server-portable/consolidate.ts`, `CONSOLIDATE_PROMPT_VERSION` 4, so a new pool under `consolidate:<model>@p4`). Five verdicts where p3 had three:
  - `unrelated`;
  - `related`: compatible, neither continues the other;
  - `evolves`: one continues the other and both stand;
  - `duplicate`: the same claims;
  - `outdates`: one is no longer current, either a later state of the same thing or a reversal. This is p3's "conflict", renamed.

  Each word starts with a different letter, so the verdict's first token separates them. For a directed `outdates`, the judge copies the words from the current thought that show it is current. `judgePair` checks they are in that side's text (`evidenceIn`, a new `evidenceFound` on the judgement).
- **What becomes a proposal** (`proposalVerdict`): `outdates`, in the direction named or `conflict_undirected`, and a `duplicate`, recorded as `newer_supersedes_older`, with its reason starting `duplicate —`. The other three record nothing yet; SMD-1873's next PR stores them as relation edges, a duplicate included.
- **The confidence** (`proposalConfidence`). The pass asks for `logprobs` (`JUDGE_LOGPROBS`, 10). When the endpoint returns them, `valueDistribution` reads the model's probability over the five words at the token where the verdict starts, and the proposal records the mass on `outdates` plus `duplicate`. Otherwise it records the number the model wrote. An endpoint that refuses `logprobs` with a 400 is asked again without it for the rest of the process.
- **The record.** The proposal's recipe in `derivations` carries `judged`: the verdict word, `confidence_source` (`token` or `stated`), the stated number, and `evidence_found`. `--dump` lines carry `score`, `score_source`, `evidence` and `evidence_found`. The run summary counts the five verdicts.
- **`evals/eval-judge.ts`** (new). It reads a brain's reviewed proposals, the board's links between two tickets and the writers' `supersedes` pointers in one read-only transaction, along with a sample of today's candidates. It judges them offline and reports:
  - the verdicts by label;
  - the written confidence's spread;
  - the AUROC of each confidence against a right verdict and against "is it a supersession";
  - how often a supersession names a side and quotes words that are there;
  - what the pass would record.

**Why.** On the dogfood brain, 120 of the 128 proposals carried exactly 0.80 confidence and 117 named no side. 126 were rejected, mostly two related tickets the judge called a conflict. Re-judged under p3, the 7B called 119 of those 126 a conflict again. 0.80 was the model's own answer (the parser's default is 0.5), and among the proposals it was a coin, AUROC 0.53. The pairs that do make a thought out of date are mostly a later state of the same thing, and the 7B reads "conflict" as contradiction only.

**Measured after** (434 pairs from the dogfood brain, qwen2.5:7b, temperature 0; evals/README.md has the tables):

| | p3 | p4 |
|---|---|---|
| rejected proposals proposed again | 119 of 126 | 2 of 126 |
| linked ticket pairs read as related | 46 of 126 | 106 of 126 |
| writer-superseded and accepted pairs proposed | 3 of 62 | 22 of 62 |
| supersessions naming a side | 11% | 100% |
| AUROC, is it a supersession, by the recorded confidence | 0.12 | 0.92 (token) |

On qwen3.8:27b, p4 proposes 60 of the 62 true pairs, with 6 of 40 rejected pairs proposed again, at about four times the time per pair. Ollama returns that model's first-token logprobs only, so its proposals record the written number.

**Held.**
- `test-thoughts`: the five verdicts and p3's two old words refused; the evidence kept on a directed supersession only and found through case, line breaks and quotes; `valueDistribution` reading alternatives behind a lead-in quote, skipping a shared first token and refusing tokens that do not spell the answer; `proposalVerdict` and `proposalConfidence`; the version.
- `test-live` [16]'s stub judge answers in p4 and returns logprobs for one pair. That proposal records 0.90, the token mass, not the 0.92 the answer states, and its recipe says `token`. The pair without logprobs records its stated 0.70 under `stated`. The dump carries the quote, found in the newer text.
- `eval-judge.ts --self-check`: the AUROC arithmetic and what is right per label.

**Not taken.**
- **Asking the 27B by default:** its recall is the better one, but it costs four times the time per pair and records no ranking confidence. `OB1_JUDGE_MODEL` stays the operator's choice.
- **Dropping a direction whose quote was not found:** on the 27B the quote was found on 24 of 31 directed answers, and of those, 15 of 18 named the right side where labelled. That is too few labels to move the rule, so the recipe records the finding and the direction stands.

**Follow-ups.** SMD-1873's next PR: `related`, `evolves` and `duplicate` written as relation edges between two thoughts.
