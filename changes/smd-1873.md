## Changelog

The consolidation judge asks prompt version 4: five verdicts (unrelated, related, evolves, duplicate, outdates), with the words that show which thought is current quoted and checked. Only an outdates, and a duplicate one writer wrote both sides of, become proposals, and a proposal's confidence is the model's token probability where the endpoint returns logprobs. On the dogfood brain the judge proposed again 2 of the 126 pairs a reviewer had rejected, where p3 proposed 119; `evals/eval-judge.ts` measures it on a brain's own labels (SMD-1873).

## FORK

The consolidation judge says something — five verdicts, a quoted direction, and a confidence from the model's own token probabilities (SMD-1873)

**What changed.**
- **The prompt** (`server-portable/consolidate.ts`, `CONSOLIDATE_PROMPT_VERSION` 4, so a new pool under `consolidate:<model>@p4`). It has five verdicts where p3 had three:
  - `unrelated`;
  - `related`: compatible, and neither continues the other;
  - `evolves`: one continues the other, and both stand;
  - `duplicate`: the same claims;
  - `outdates`: one is no longer current, because the other is a later state of the same thing or a reversal. This is p3's "conflict", renamed.

  Each word starts with a different letter, so the verdict's first token separates them. For a directed `outdates`, the judge copies the words that show which side is current. `judgePair` counts them (`evidenceFound`) only when they appear as whole words in that side's text and not in the other's.
- **What becomes a proposal** (`proposalVerdict`):
  - `outdates`, in the direction named, or `conflict_undirected`;
  - a `duplicate`, only when one writer wrote both sides (050's `actor_name`, which a payload cannot set). It is recorded as `newer_supersedes_older`, with its reason starting `duplicate —`.

  A duplicate across two writers, or with either writer unknown, proposes nothing. `related`, `evolves` and every duplicate are relation edges in SMD-1873's next PR.
- **The confidence** (`proposalConfidence`).
  - The pass asks for `logprobs` (`JUDGE_LOGPROBS`, 10). `valueDistribution` reads the model's probability over the five words at the token where the verdict starts, and the proposal records the mass on `outdates` plus `duplicate`.
  - It records the number the model wrote instead when there is no distribution, when the token has no alternatives, or when the alternatives naming a verdict hold under half the token's mass (`MIN_COVERED`).
  - An endpoint and model that refuse `logprobs` with a 400 or a 422, then answer without it, are asked without it for the rest of the process. An error the retry gets too belongs to the pair and changes nothing for later pairs.
- **The record.** The proposal's recipe in `derivations` carries `judged`: the verdict word, `confidence_source` (`token` or `stated`), the stated number, `evidence_found`, the token distributions, and a duplicate's unused letter (`duplicate_keeps`).
  - `--dump` lines carry `score`, `score_source`, `evidence` and `evidence_found`.
  - The run summary counts the five verdicts and how many proposing verdicts were scored each way.
  - A verdict outside the five, such as p3's `conflict` from a model keeping to the old words, is reported by name.
- **`evals/eval-judge.ts`** (new). It reads a brain's reviewed proposals, the board's links between two tickets, the writers' `supersedes` pointers and a sample of today's candidates, in one read-only transaction. It judges them offline and reports:
  - the verdicts by label and the spread of the written confidence;
  - the AUROC of the recorded score against "is it a supersession", over all labelled pairs and, separately, among the pairs proposed, with their count;
  - how many supersessions the model directed;
  - what the pass would record.

**Why.**
- On the dogfood brain, 120 of the 128 proposals carried exactly 0.80 confidence and 117 named no side.
- 126 of them were rejected, mostly two related tickets the judge called a conflict. Re-judged under p3, the 7B called 119 of those 126 a conflict again.
- 0.80 was the model's own answer (the parser's default is 0.5), and among the proposals it was a coin, AUROC 0.53.
- The pairs that do make a thought out of date are mostly a later state of the same thing, and the 7B reads "conflict" as contradiction only.

**Held.**
- **`test-thoughts`:**
  - the five verdicts, and p3's words refused by name;
  - the evidence, kept on a directed supersession only, cut at a word, found through case, line breaks, curly quotes, dashes and an ellipsis, matched as whole words, and not in the other side, with no backtracking on a line of dots;
  - `valueDistribution`'s token edges;
  - `proposalVerdict`'s one-writer rule;
  - `proposalConfidence` and its coverage boundary;
  - the version.
- **`test-local-provider`:**
  - a 400 or 422 refusing `logprobs` is retried and remembered per model;
  - an error the retry also gets leaves `logprobs` on;
  - a 503 is not retried.
- **`test-live` [16]:**
  - The stub judge returns logprobs for two pairs. One records 0.90, the token mass, not the stated 0.92. The other states 0.9 but carries 0.30 of token mass, so it is set aside under the floor.
  - A pair without logprobs records its stated 0.70.
  - Each recipe says which source it used.
  - An agent's duplicate of the operator's thought proposes nothing, while one key's two copies are proposed with the newer standing.
  - An answer in p3's words fails its thought and names the word.
- **`eval-judge.ts --self-check`:** the AUROC arithmetic, and what counts as right per label.

**Not taken.**
- **Byte-fallback tokens.** An endpoint that sends one, or U+FFFD, anywhere in the answer makes the tokens not spell the content, so the written number is used. Ollama's qwen2.5:7b sent whole characters on every call measured.
- **Rounding at the floor.** The floor compares the two-decimal value 029 stores, so 0.495 passes 0.50.
- **One scale in the queue.** Token-scored and written-number proposals share one queue, ordered by confidence; the recipe says which each is.
- **p3 rows left pending.** The pass never re-judges a pair that already has a pending proposal, so an upgrade leaves p3's pending rows, at their written 0.80, for a reviewer.
- **The writer rule for "outdates".** An agent's thought outdating the operator's is held by the prompt alone, since an agent may state a later fact. The reviewer sees no writers (SMD-2707), and an accepted capture-scoped thought can still be repointed (SMD-2708). Both predate p4. The dogfood set has no operator-marked thought, so neither rule is measured there.
- **The evidence gates nothing.** The writer of a side can plant the quote in it, so a direction is never dropped or kept on the strength of `evidenceFound`.
- **The verdict word in a claim's error.** A model's off-vocabulary word, at most 40 characters on one line, is quoted in the failed claim's message, which no reply renders.
- **The table comment** on `supersession_proposals` (029, 067) still says "judged to CONFLICT". Those migrations are frozen; SMD-1873's next PR rewrites the comment.
- **Asking the 27B by default.** It costs four times the time per pair and records no ranking confidence. `OB1_JUDGE_MODEL` stays the operator's choice, and SMD-2705 decides.
