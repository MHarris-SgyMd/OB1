## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, consolidation now joins extraction once the thoughts captured before the work period began are extracted. Before, it waited for extraction's whole pool to drain, which never happened while captures kept arriving, so consolidation and the subject sweep beside it stayed out for as long as they came. A thought captured since is judged against neighbours captured on an earlier UTC date, all of them in that backlog, so the rule that a thought is judged against neighbours already extracted holds as before (SMD-4468).

## Record

Consolidation joins once the period's backlog is extracted, not extraction's whole pool — a steady inflow of captures no longer keeps it out of the period (SMD-4468)

**What changed.**

- `db/sleep.ts`:
  - **Extraction's pool read** (`poolOf`, given `before`, the period's start on the database's clock) carries `backlog`: the same counts — pending, in flight, failed, not yet pooled, stalled, this run's — of the thoughts captured before it. It is read in the same statement as the whole pool: the not-yet-pooled count comes from the same scan, and the claims are joined to their thoughts only where not done.
  - **The join** reads the backlog. Consolidation joins beside extraction once it is drained, saying `extraction has done the thoughts captured before the period began — consolidating beside it while it extracts the captures since` (`extraction drained — consolidating beside it`, as before, when the whole pool is). It joins past once the backlog holds nothing but stalled thoughts, at a period's first start too, the captures since waiting with extraction's set-aside. The period still ends as before, once the pools drain.
- `db/README.md` and `deploy/README.md` say the join and its log line.

**Why.** `db/sleep.ts` started consolidation beside extraction only once `drained(pools.extract)` held, or the pool held nothing but stalled thoughts. 016's trigger pools each capture as it is written, and extraction's follower claims at its polls, so under a steady inflow at least one thought was always pending or in hand. SMD-4384's review passes 2 and 3 measured consolidation joining only once the inflow stopped: a thought every 4 s for 300 s, judgements 158 s late at the median and 306 s at most (405 s with heavier extraction), and 0 consolidated in 480 s with an embedding outage mid-inflow.

The maintainer's choice (2026-11-19), of the ticket's three options: the backlog the period began with drained. Not "caught up" (no claim in hand, none pending since before the poll), which never holds while extraction is always busy, as on stable at about 19 s a call. Not a share of the period, which is SMD-3379's, and alone would judge thoughts before their neighbours are extracted. Migration 029 pairs a thought only with thoughts captured on an earlier UTC date. A capture made during the period is therefore paired only with thoughts in the backlog, and extraction takes the oldest claims first, so the wait is bounded by the backlog, whatever the inflow. The exception is consolidate.ts's: two captures either side of 00:00 UTC, the earlier still in hand when the later is judged. A period that runs on through midnight under an inflow meets it there, as captures beside a running `extract` follower always have.

**Held.**

- test-live **[38c]** (new, the sleep shard; its body a function's, the module being at TS2563's limit). Six older notes on one axis, two to seven days old, then a capture on the same axis every 300 ms, one extraction worker at 400 ms a call. While the inflow goes on, consolidation joins with the backlog's line, and judges an inflow note while extraction still holds work pending or in hand. "Extraction drained" is never said. Consolidation's first claim of the run comes after the backlog's last extraction finished. Once the inflow ends, every note is judged.
- Mutants, each with the files backed up outside the tree and restored, each failing the row: the join reading the whole pool (main's rule); the backlog read without its bound; the join at once.

**Contract.** No setting, flag or schema changes: a `patch`. The scheduler's log gains one line. Consolidation now judges during an inflow, where it waited for the inflow's end before.
