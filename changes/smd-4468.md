## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, consolidation now joins extraction once the thoughts captured before the work period began have been extracted. Before, it waited for extraction's whole pool to drain, which seldom happened while writes that pause nothing kept arriving (an `ingested` key's, board-sync's among them), so consolidation stayed out for as long as they came. A thought captured since is judged against neighbours captured on an earlier UTC date, and the join waits for every one of them not yet extracted, except across 00:00 UTC and except one dated an earlier day that lands after the join; board-sync's observations of the day, and tickets it re-renders, hold nothing back. The subject sweep still waits for the whole pool, as before. No migration. **Upgrading:** consolidation's judge calls now run while such writes arrive; on a model server of one slot they share it with extraction, which then falls behind a fast inflow: watch extraction's pool in `extract-entities.ts --status` during a sync. A thought from before the period that a pause has taken still holds the join until extraction reaches it, as before (SMD-4468).

## Record

Consolidation joins once the thoughts captured before the period began are extracted, not extraction's whole pool — a steady inflow of writes no longer keeps it out of the period (SMD-4468)

**What changed.**

- `db/sleep.ts`:
  - **The backlog** (`backlogCut`): the thoughts captured before the period began (its start on the database's clock) that have no entity under extraction's key yet, except a write during the period dated today (pooled since the start, and not dated ahead as a stopped thought is). One not yet pooled counts by its date alone. Extraction's pool read for the join (`poolOf`, `backlogSince`) reads whether any is left (`backlogLeft`), in the same statement as the whole pool: the not-yet-pooled ones from the same scan, and an `EXISTS` over the pending and in-flight claims, which stops at the first. No other read asks for it.
  - **The join:**
    - Once the backlog is drained, consolidation joins beside extraction, saying `extraction has done the thoughts captured before the period began — consolidating beside it while it extracts the writes since`. Once the whole pool is drained it says `extraction drained — consolidating beside it`, as before.
    - The join past stalled thoughts is main's, unchanged: only once extraction holds nothing else.
    - The subject sweep, joined on the backlog alone, waits until extraction's whole pool drains or is joined past, or a statement holds extraction, as on main; it reads extraction's claims each poll meanwhile, and the whole pool only once they are empty.
    - The period still ends as before, once the pools drain.
  - On a brain before 093, the start's line and the unreadable-stalled-read line say what the join waits for.
- `db/README.md` and `deploy/README.md` describe the join, the sweep's wait, the limit, edits and board-sync's writes, the 00:00 UTC exception and a write landing after the join, the judge calls during an inflow, and that a period under a steady inflow ends only once the writes stop; `deploy/README.md` gives the log line and how a held join looks.

**Why.** `db/sleep.ts` started consolidation beside extraction only once `drained(pools.extract)` held, or the pool held nothing but stalled thoughts. 016's trigger pools each write as it lands, and extraction's follower claims at its polls, so under a steady inflow a thought was pending or in hand at nearly every poll. SMD-4384's review passes 2 and 3 measured consolidation joining only once the inflow stopped, with a thought every 4 s for 300 s: judgements 158 s late at the median and 306 s at most (405 s with heavier extraction), and 0 consolidated in 480 s with an embedding outage mid-inflow.

The maintainer's choices:
- **2026-11-19**, of the ticket's three options: the backlog drained. The other two:
  - "Caught up" never holds while extraction is always busy, as on stable at about 19 s a call.
  - A share of the period is SMD-3379's, and on its own would judge thoughts before their neighbours are extracted.
- **Review pass 2, the bound.** The first cut took every thought captured before the period began. Board-sync dates an observation at 00:00 UTC of its day and a ticket at the issue's creation, so nearly all its writes were backlog, and on a Linear-fed brain the change did little. The maintainer chose the day bound offered: thoughts dated before today, never extracted. ci-parity then failed [38], [38b] and [38s]: with no inflow, a thought captured today before the period no longer held the join, so consolidation started beside a long or stalled thought of today's, against SMD-2694's and SMD-3008's reviewed order. The bound kept takes the period's start, and leaves out only what the day bound was for:
  - a write during the period dated today, which no thought captured since can pair with under 029;
  - a thought already extracted that an edit pools again, which keeps its entities until its next extraction replaces them, a neighbour meanwhile, whenever the edit came (the period's re-derive sweep runs orphans only, and clears none). One whose extraction found no entity counts.

  A write during the period dated an earlier day (a ticket created then, an import carrying its dates) stays backlog, as a neighbour should. The exception is consolidate.ts's: two captures either side of 00:00 UTC, the earlier still in hand when the later is judged. A period that runs on through midnight reads yesterday's writes since as backlog from then.
- **Review pass 2, the join past.** Joining past the backlog's stalled thoughts cut the capture in hand and set the inflow aside for consolidation's whole drain: in pass 2's run, 215 s, a median extraction wait of 345 s against about 30 s on main. The join past is main's again.
- **Review pass 2, the sweep.** On a model server of one slot, the sweep's judge calls beside consolidation's were about half of what slowed extraction. It waits for the whole pool, as on main. Consolidation's own share of the slot is SMD-4495's.

**Limits named, not fixed:**
- **A backlog thought a pause has taken** (review passes 1 and 2, the maintainer's choice) — stalled, or stopped short of a stall — is dated behind the newer writes (SMD-3008). While they come faster than extraction takes them, extraction does not reach it, and the join waits for it, as on main.
- **A thought captured today before the period that the period itself first pools** (a fresh brain, an extraction key's switch; review pass 4) counts as a write since: no one's neighbour under 029, but consolidation may start beside it, a long one included.
- **The join is one-way** (review pass 3): once consolidation has joined, a write dated an earlier day that lands after it (a ticket first synced, an import carrying its dates) is judged without a wait, and a newer thought judged before it is extracted misses its pair with it for good. Main has the same gap after its join, which under an inflow came seldom; pass 3's run-it saw 7 such claims with every fourth write dated three days back.

**Held.**

- test-live **[38c]**, a new section in the sleep shard. Its body is a function's, as the module is at TS2563's limit. Each case runs a capture every 300 ms, under the sync key, against one extraction worker at 400 ms a call. The inflow begins before the run, so no poll finds the pool drained.
  - **The inflow**, with the subject sweep on: six older notes two to seven days old, on the inflow's axis. While the inflow goes on, consolidation joins with the backlog's line and judges an inflow note while extraction still has work pending or in hand. "Extraction drained" is never said, and consolidation's first claim of the run comes after the backlog's last extraction finished. The subject sweep starts only once extraction's whole pool drains, here after the inflow ends. Every note is judged.
  - **The limit:** two backlog thoughts a pause has taken, one stalled (093's row seeded at three stalled stops) and one stopped once. No join of either kind comes during 8 s of inflow, and both are still pending. Every note is judged once the inflow ends.
  - **Board-sync's writes:** inflow notes dated 00:00 UTC today, and, once the period has begun, an older thought already extracted edited every 2 s under the sync key. Neither holds the join, the edited thought is pending at it, and an inflow note is judged during the inflow.
  - **An extraction model's switch:** sixteen older notes carrying only a retired key's entity, pooled again once the period has begun and dated a day ahead so extraction leaves them while the inflow lasts. No join comes during 8 s of inflow, all sixteen still to extract; once it ends they are extracted under the current key, no retired row left, and every note is judged.
- **Mutants**, each with the files backed up outside the tree and restored, and each failing a row:
  - same-day writes since counted as backlog;
  - edits counted as backlog;
  - the join reading the whole pool (main's rule);
  - the subject sweep not waiting;
  - the join at once;
  - the thoughts not yet pooled left out of the backlog;
  - another key's entities counted as extracted.
- One survives: a forward-dated claim (a stopped thought set last) read as a write since. It differs only for a stopped thought captured today before the period, which no row sets up.
- Pass 1's mutants on the backlog's stalled count, and on the restart before the backlog's join past, went with that code.
- [38], [38b] and [38s] hold the day bound's regression: ci-parity failed them under it, and passes with the bound kept.
