## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, consolidation now joins extraction once every thought dated before today (UTC) has been extracted. Before, it waited for extraction's whole pool to drain, which never happened while writes that pause nothing kept arriving (a sync key's, board-sync's), so consolidation stayed out for as long as they came. A thought dated today is judged against neighbours captured on an earlier UTC date, all of them extracted by then, except across 00:00 UTC. The subject sweep still waits for the whole pool, as before. No migration. **Upgrading:** consolidation's judge calls now run while such writes arrive; on a model server of one slot they share it with extraction, which then falls behind a fast inflow. A thought dated before today that a pause has taken still holds the join until extraction reaches it, as before (SMD-4468).

## Record

Consolidation joins once the thoughts dated before today are extracted, not extraction's whole pool — a steady inflow of writes no longer keeps it out of the period (SMD-4468)

**What changed.**

- `db/sleep.ts`:
  - **The backlog** (`backlogCut`): the thoughts dated before the current UTC day, by the database's clock, that have no entity yet. Extraction's pool read for the join (`poolOf`, `withBacklog`) also counts them — pending, in flight and not yet pooled — in the same statement as the whole pool. The not-yet-pooled count comes from the same scan, and only pending and in-flight claims are joined to their thoughts. No other read asks for it.
  - **The join:**
    - Once the backlog is drained, consolidation joins beside extraction, saying `extraction has done every thought dated before today (UTC) — consolidating beside it while it extracts today's`. Once the whole pool is drained it says `extraction drained — consolidating beside it`, as before.
    - The join past stalled thoughts is main's, unchanged: only once extraction holds nothing else.
    - The subject sweep, joined on the backlog alone, waits for extraction's whole pool to drain (or a statement to hold it) before it starts.
    - The period still ends as before, once the pools drain.
  - On a brain before 093, the start's line and the unreadable-stalled-read line say what the join waits for.
- `db/README.md` and `deploy/README.md` describe the join, its log line, the sweep's wait, the limit, edits and board-sync's writes, the 00:00 UTC exception, the judge calls during an inflow, how a held join looks, and that a period under a steady inflow ends only once the writes stop.

**Why.** `db/sleep.ts` started consolidation beside extraction only once `drained(pools.extract)` held, or the pool held nothing but stalled thoughts. 016's trigger pools each write as it lands, and extraction's follower claims at its polls, so under a steady inflow at least one thought was always pending or in hand. SMD-4384's review passes 2 and 3 measured consolidation joining only once the inflow stopped, with a thought every 4 s for 300 s: judgements 158 s late at the median and 306 s at most (405 s with heavier extraction), and 0 consolidated in 480 s with an embedding outage mid-inflow.

The maintainer's choices:
- **2026-11-19**, of the ticket's three options: the backlog drained. The other two:
  - "Caught up" never holds while extraction is always busy, as on stable at about 19 s a call.
  - A share of the period is SMD-3379's, and on its own would judge thoughts before their neighbours are extracted.
- **Review pass 2, the bound.** The first cut took the thoughts captured before the period began. Board-sync dates an observation at 00:00 UTC of its day and a ticket at the issue's creation, so nearly all its writes were backlog, and on a Linear-fed brain the change did little. Migration 029 pairs a thought only with thoughts captured on an earlier UTC date, so a thought dated today needs only earlier days' thoughts extracted. A thought already extracted keeps its entities until its next extraction replaces them, so it is a neighbour meanwhile. That is true of a board-sync re-render during the period; one edited before the period began loses its entities to the period's re-derive sweep, and is backlog until extracted again. The exception is consolidate.ts's: two captures either side of 00:00 UTC, the earlier still in hand when the later is judged. A period that runs on through midnight reads yesterday's writes as backlog from then.
- **Review pass 2, the join past.** Joining past the backlog's stalled thoughts cut the capture in hand and set the inflow aside for consolidation's whole drain: in pass 2's run, 215 s, a median extraction wait of 345 s against about 30 s on main. The join past is main's again.
- **Review pass 2, the sweep.** On a model server of one slot, the sweep's judge calls beside consolidation's were about half of what slowed extraction. It waits for the whole pool, as on main. Consolidation's own share of the slot is SMD-4495's.

**Limit named, not fixed** (review passes 1 and 2, the maintainer's choice): a backlog thought a pause has taken — stalled, or stopped short of a stall — is dated behind the newer writes (SMD-3008). While they come faster than extraction takes them, extraction does not reach it, and the join waits for it, as on main.

**Held.**

- test-live **[38c]**, a new section in the sleep shard. Its body is a function's, as the module is at TS2563's limit. Each case runs a capture every 300 ms, under the sync key, against one extraction worker at 400 ms a call. The inflow begins before the run, so no poll finds the pool drained.
  - **The inflow**, with the subject sweep on: six older notes two to seven days old, on the inflow's axis. While the inflow goes on, consolidation joins with the backlog's line and judges an inflow note while extraction still has work pending or in hand. "Extraction drained" is never said, and consolidation's first claim of the run comes after the backlog's last extraction finished. The subject sweep starts only after the inflow ends. Every note is judged.
  - **The limit:** two backlog thoughts a pause has taken, one stalled (093's row seeded at three stalled stops) and one stopped once. No join of either kind comes during 8 s of inflow, and both are still pending. Every note is judged once the inflow ends.
  - **Board-sync's writes:** inflow notes dated 00:00 UTC today, and, once the period has begun, an older thought already extracted edited every 2 s. Neither holds the join, and an inflow note is judged during the inflow.
- **Mutants**, each with the files backed up outside the tree and restored, and each failing a row:
  - the bound by time, not the day;
  - edits counted as backlog;
  - the join reading the whole pool (main's rule);
  - the subject sweep not waiting;
  - the join at once.
- Pass 1's mutants on the backlog's stalled count, and on the restart before the backlog's join past, went with that code.
