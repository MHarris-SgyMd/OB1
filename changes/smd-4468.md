## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, consolidation now joins extraction once the thoughts captured before the work period began are extracted. Before, it waited for extraction's whole pool to drain. Under a steady inflow of captures that never happened, so consolidation and the subject sweep beside it stayed out for as long as captures came. A thought captured since is judged against neighbours captured on an earlier UTC date, which are all in that backlog except across 00:00 UTC, so a thought is still judged only against neighbours already extracted. **Upgrading:** consolidation's judge calls, and the subject sweep's, now run while captures arrive. On a model server with one slot they share it with extraction, and on a hosted provider their spend starts mid-period. Two cases still wait as before: a backlog thought a pause stopped is dated behind the newer captures, so it is not reached while captures come faster than extraction takes them; and an edit to an older thought, or a write dated before the period began, counts as backlog (SMD-4468).

## Record

Consolidation joins once the period's backlog is extracted, not extraction's whole pool — a steady inflow of captures no longer keeps it out of the period (SMD-4468)

**What changed.**

- `db/sleep.ts`:
  - **Extraction's pool read** (`poolOf`, given `before`, the period's start on the database's clock; not on a claims-only read) carries `backlog`. It holds the counts the join reads — pending, in flight, not yet pooled and stalled — for the thoughts captured before that start, read in the same statement as the whole pool. The not-yet-pooled count comes from the same scan, and only pending and in-flight claims are joined to their thoughts.
  - **The join** reads the backlog:
    - Once the backlog is drained, consolidation joins beside extraction, saying `extraction has done the thoughts captured before the period began — consolidating beside it while it extracts the captures since`. When the whole pool is drained it says `extraction drained — consolidating beside it`, as before.
    - Once the backlog holds nothing but stalled thoughts, consolidation joins past them. Extraction is set aside until consolidation's pool drains, as before, and the captures since wait with it; the line ends `, N thought(s) captured since the period began waiting with it`.
    - Newer work behind a stalled thought in hand gets SMD-3008's restart first while one is due (at most once a minute), and the join past follows.
    - A period's first start still reads the whole pool, so the captures since go ahead of the stalled thoughts it dates last.
    - The period still ends as before, once the pools drain.
  - On a brain before 093, the start's line and the unreadable-stalled-read line now say what they wait for: the backlog extracted, never past a stalled thought.
- `server-portable/preflight.ts`: the stalled thought's fix says consolidation joins past it once nothing else captured before the period began is left to extract.
- `db/README.md` and `deploy/README.md` describe the join, its log lines, the two limits, the 00:00 UTC exception, the judge calls now running during an inflow, and that a period under a steady inflow ends only once captures stop.

**Why.** `db/sleep.ts` started consolidation beside extraction only once `drained(pools.extract)` held, or the pool held nothing but stalled thoughts. 016's trigger pools each capture as it is written, and extraction's follower claims at its polls, so under a steady inflow at least one thought was always pending or in hand. SMD-4384's review passes 2 and 3 measured consolidation joining only once the inflow stopped, with a thought every 4 s for 300 s: judgements 158 s late at the median and 306 s at most (405 s with heavier extraction), and 0 consolidated in 480 s with an embedding outage mid-inflow.

The maintainer's choice (2026-11-19) of the ticket's three options was the backlog the period began with, drained. The other two:
- "Caught up" (no claim in hand, none pending since before the poll) never holds while extraction is always busy, as on stable at about 19 s a call.
- A share of the period belongs to SMD-3379, and on its own would judge thoughts before their neighbours are extracted.

Migration 029 pairs a thought only with thoughts captured on an earlier UTC date, so a capture made during the period is paired only with thoughts in the backlog. The exception is consolidate.ts's: two captures either side of 00:00 UTC, the earlier still in hand when the later is judged. A period that runs on through midnight under an inflow meets it there, as captures beside a running `extract` follower always have.

**Limits named, not fixed** (review pass 1, the maintainer's choice):
- **A backlog thought a pause stopped** short of a stall is dated behind the captures since (SMD-3008). While captures come faster than extraction takes them, extraction does not reach it, and the join waits for it, as on main.
- **A write during the period dated before its start** counts as backlog and holds the join until extracted. That covers an edit, which 016 pools again (as when board-sync re-renders a ticket), and an import carrying its dates. A tighter bound (thoughts dated before the current UTC day and never extracted) was offered and declined.

**Held.**

- test-live **[38c]**, a new section in the sleep shard. Its body is a function's, as the module is at TS2563's limit. Each case runs a capture every 300 ms against one extraction worker at 400 ms a call.
  - **The inflow:** six older notes on one axis, two to seven days old, then the inflow on the same axis. While it goes on, consolidation joins with the backlog's line, and judges an inflow note while extraction still has work pending or in hand. "Extraction drained" is never said. Consolidation's first claim of the run comes after the backlog's last extraction finished, and once the inflow ends every note is judged.
  - **A stalled backlog thought:** the backlog's last thought is stalled (its 093 row seeded at three stalled stops) and dated behind the captures since. Consolidation joins past it while the inflow goes on, with the line naming the captures waiting, and judges an inflow note before the stalled thought is extracted. Every note is judged once the inflow ends.
  - **The named limit:** a backlog thought one pause stopped, plus a capture made during the period that stalls. No join comes during 8 s of inflow, and the stopped thought is still pending. Consolidation joins once the inflow ends.
- [38b]'s SMD-3008 row checks that a capture pending beside the stalled thought in hand restarts extraction, and that the join past follows in the same period. The first cut, which joined past at once, failed it; the restart now goes first while due.
- server-portable/test-preflight's stalled-thought rows and [38]'s before-093 row read the new wording.
- **Mutants**, each with the files backed up outside the tree and restored, and each failing a row:
  - the join reading the whole pool (main's rule);
  - the backlog read without its bound;
  - the join at once;
  - the join past reading the whole pool;
  - no restart first ([38b]);
  - the backlog's stalled count read unbounded (the third case: a stalled capture since read as an all-stalled backlog);
  - consolidation started but no judging after the join.
