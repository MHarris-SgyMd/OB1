## FORK

The observability step gets a job of its own, off the run's critical path (SMD-3170)

**What changed.**
- **`.github/workflows/fork-checks.yml`:** a new job, `deploy-stack-observability` ("Full stack: the observability profile"). It runs on a fresh runner:
  - the checkout and Bun;
  - `.github/ci/full-stack-up.sh`, as the other three full-stack jobs do;
  - "The server is configured for an authorization server that is not running", as `deploy-stack` and `deploy-stack-brains` run it. That is the stack the step met in `deploy-stack`. lgtm starts on an https public origin, with Grafana's root URL under it and its cookie secure; no other job starts it there;
  - "The observability profile's dashboard reads every series it names", moved from `deploy-stack` ("Full stack, no Supabase") with main's step body unchanged, SMD-3023's check of pg-exporter's front ([OB1 PR 456](https://github.com/MHarris-SgyMd/OB1/pull/456)) included;
  - logs on failure, which take the profile's lines from `deploy-stack`'s;
  - `down -v` with the profile named, so a step that failed before its own `rm` leaves nothing.
- **`fork-checks-passed`** needs the new job; check 20 refuses the aggregate without it. Since SMD-3168 the ruleset requires that job alone, so there is no ruleset change.
- **Comments:**
  - The ones in `deploy-stack` that placed the step ("the next step starts", "PR 3c's step later starts it", "the steps after it") now name the new job.
  - The step's own comment says what its restore is now for, and names the monitor role it leaves.
  - The jobs that run `.github/ci/auth-settings.sh` are named, in its header and in the authorization server step.
  - The stub's callers in `.github/ci/full-stack-up.sh` include this job, whose provider panels and alert read the stub's 404.

**Why.** Over the 24 green Fork Checks runs from SMD-3050's alert rules (2026-10-21 13:30Z), which lengthened the step, to 22:22Z:
- "Full stack, no Supabase" was the slowest job in 22 of them, a median 360 s (p90 377). The observability step was 134 s of it (p90 144, max 155), more than the ticket's 92 s. The step needs nothing of that job's but a running stack, configured for an authorization server as it was there.
- The slowest other job was "SQL data layer against real Postgres" in 17 of those runs, a median 310 s.

Predicted per run from those runs' step times:
- `deploy-stack` falls to about 232 s (p90 239).
- The new job takes about 191 s (p90 215): set-up, the checkout, Bun, the bring-up, the authorization settings, the step and the teardown, each timed in `deploy-stack`.
- The run's slowest job falls from a median 360 s to 310 s.

**Not taken.**
- **The step in `deploy-stack-auth`,** the ticket's other option, which it preferred while SMD-3171's runner-concurrency work is open. That job was 209 s, about 195–200 s since SMD-2288 removed its two Kubernetes steps ([OB1 PR 459](https://github.com/MHarris-SgyMd/OB1/pull/459), 8–16 s between them). With the step it would be about 330 s, still the critical path.
- **A trade,** with the import runner's image build and start (46 s) moving to `deploy-stack` in exchange. That was the first build, and it puts both jobs under the data layer job's 310 s, but `deploy-stack-auth` would sit at about 292 s and still be the slowest job in 10 of the 24 runs. It would also leave about 20 s of headroom, where the new job leaves a median 78 s between `deploy-stack` and the slowest other job:
  - the data layer job's next cut, its 143 s "Incremental upgrade and preflight, side by side", would have made `deploy-stack-auth` the critical path again;
  - so would any growth in the step itself.
- **The cost of the choice:** one runner more per run, 17 jobs at once against the free plan's 20. SMD-3171's fold of five short jobs into one would more than make that back.
