## Changelog

board-sync now waits out Linear's rate limit instead of asking again at every trigger. On HTTP 429, or an answer coded `RATELIMITED`, it makes no request until the reset time Linear sends with it. When Linear sends none, it backs off two intervals, then four, doubling up to an hour. Its heartbeat, `brain_info` and preflight say "rate-limited by Linear … until <time>", not just "failed". Each pass's log line now ends with its count of Linear requests. `deploy/README.md` has the sum of requests per hour when several brains share one Linear user (SMD-4133).

## Record

board-sync waits out Linear's rate limit, and says how much of the hour each pass spends (SMD-4133)

On 2026-11-06, after stable's 2.0.0 upgrade, both brains' board-sync failed every pass on Linear's 429. Each polled every 30 s under one Linear user, about 2,900 requests an hour against that user's 2,500. Nothing backed off: a refused pass was tried again at the next trigger, and the refused requests counted against the hour too. Work 1, raising `OB1_BOARD_SYNC_INTERVAL` to 300 on both brains, was applied that day, and both have passed since. This change is Work 2 and Work 3.

**What changed.**
- **`db/linear-api.ts`:** a 429, or an answer whose errors carry the code `RATELIMITED`, throws `LinearRateLimited`, with the reset Linear sent.
  - The reset is the exhausted limit's `X-RateLimit-Requests-Reset` or `X-RateLimit-Complexity-Reset` (epoch ms). When neither reads as exhausted, it is the later of the two; when neither is sent, `Retry-After`.
  - Any other failure is the error it was.
- **`db/sync-linear.ts`:**
  - The issue fetch rethrows a rate limit, so it ends the pass. Before, it counted as a batch refused, and the next pass asked again.
  - `triggerPasses` holds every trigger until the reset (an hour at most), with no request between. Without a reset it backs off two intervals, doubling up to an hour (`rateHold`). A rate limit on the start's board read holds the first pass the same way.
  - Each pass's line counts its Linear requests (`done (… ms, 15 Linear requests)`).
- **The heartbeat (`db/pass-stamp.ts`):** board-sync's row carries `limited { until, by }` while held. It is re-stamped at each held trigger, so it stays fresh, and cleared by the next pass and by any end, `db/supervise.ts`'s included.
- **`server-portable/brain-info.ts`:** reads `limited` on a live board-sync row and says the hold and its time left.
- **`server-portable/preflight.ts`:** its `workers` fix says the pass resumes on its own, and gives the hour's sum.
- **Work 3, the cost.** Measured 2026-11-11 by a read-only probe of the board's initiative and census: 13 projects and 1,315 issues. A quiet pass was 15 requests: 1 for the initiative and 14 census pages of 100. That is 180 an hour per brain at 300 s, and 1,800 at 30 s. `deploy/README.md` ("What a pass costs, and Linear's rate limit") and `deploy/.env.example` give that sum.

**Tests.**

- **Mutants:** seven, each killed on its own check. They drop the wait (the Verify's), the rethrow, the exhausted-limit rule, the body's code, the doubling, the streak and the start's hold.
- **`test-live` [37]:** the real stamper's row, held and then cleared, `brain_info`'s words, and the supervisor's end dropping the hold.

**Not done.**
- The hold is the process's own: a restarted board-sync asks once, and holds again if refused.
- The findings step's posts, under the comment key, are not held: a refused post fails that step, as before.
- A held pass runs none of its steps. The release of closed tickets' claims and the tickets' priorities wait for the hold too, an hour at most.
- The hold is on the wall clock: a host clock set back stretches it by as much.

**Review pass 1.** A cold read found no severe defect. Fixed:
- **The backoff ran one period long:** it counted from the refusal, so a pass's own seconds put the trigger at two periods just inside the hold. With passes of 5 s, requests went at 0, 900 and 2400 s, not 0, 600 and 1800. It now counts from the trigger that started the pass, and the self-check's passes take time. (caught: cold-read)
- **The reset's order:** with neither limit exhausted, `Retry-After` now comes before the hourly reset, which could hold a short refusal for an hour. (caught: cold-read)
- **An `errors` that is not a list** threw a TypeError in the client; it is read as the body it was. (caught: cold-read)
- **The words:**
  - preflight's fix said "no request until" a time already past; it now says the hold ended;
  - the start's refused board read went uncounted;
  - `deploy/README.md` gave a told finding one request (it is two reads and the comment) and claimed preflight gives the sum.

  (caught: cold-read)
- **test-live [37]** read the cleared hold only after the end, which clears it whatever the pass did; it now reads the row the pass stamped. (caught: cold-read)
