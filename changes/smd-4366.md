## Record

test-live's rest shard cut in two, and a stopped ci-parity row says how far it got — the rest 562 → 308 s (SMD-4366)

**What changed.**
- **A sixth shard, `claims`.** `db/test-live.ts`'s `SHARDS` lists it with [10], extraction through the claims, which was the rest's, and [38b], which was long's. Brain Checks' db-live matrix has its cell, `db/ci-parity.sh` its lane (`OB1_LIVE_SHARD=claims run db test-live.ts`), and `db/README.md` its line. No section is edited; each runs once, as before.
- **[26] to sleep, at the merge.** SMD-3318 PR 2b had moved [26], lint-sweep's ~72 s with its Tier 3 cases, from the rest to sweep, the rest having no room. With the rest cut, sweep would have been the longest cell, ~435 s of sections on main's runs to 45c6f4d1, so [26] runs in sleep: sleep ~364 s, sweep ~363, the rest ~303.
- **A stopped row says how far it got.** ci-parity's `reached` adds to the verdict of a suite its limit stopped: the last section header it printed, and the assertions it had passed and failed by its rows. A `(doc)` row is not counted, as `createAssert` counts it apart, and every other row is, so two that failed under one label are two. A suite that printed no ✓ row says what failed alone, never "0 passed": test-cli, test-connect, test-slot-relay and others print nothing for an assertion that held. A suite with no header says its counts alone, one with no row its section alone, and one with neither adds nothing.

  ```
  ✗ OB1_LIVE_SHARD=rest db/test-live.ts   exit 124, stopped at its 45 s limit in [16], 453 passed and 0 failed by then · 45 s
  ```
- **The limit stays 600 s.** Its comment says why it is not raised: a shard that nears it is cut or its sections moved.
