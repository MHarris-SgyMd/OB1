## Changelog

`db/ci-parity.sh`'s excerpt of a failing suite shows the lines that failed, not the first three that quote ✗ anywhere, and every failing row's whole output is kept in a file whose path is printed under the excerpt. Before, a passing test-preflight row that quoted ✗ took the excerpt, and a flake that did not come back could not be read (SMD-3607).

## FORK

ci-parity's failure excerpt names the failing lines, and keeps the whole output (SMD-3607)

**What changed.**
- **`excerpt`.** A failing suite's excerpt is the lines that begin, after their indent, with `✗` or `FAIL` and then a space or the line's end.
  - Every reporter the rows use but the checker's prints a failure that way, as a survey of all 77 scripts the rows run found: `createAssert`'s `  ✗  label` and eval-judge's, test-counts-page's, dashboard/test-oauth's and test-session-capture's, two spaces; the db/ suites' own `  ✗ label` (test-cli, test-connect, test-engines, test-worker-bootstrap, test-weekly-digest, test-board-findings, test-coordinator, test-brain-compare), deploy/test-tier-sh's, and ci-step-times' and suite-sections' self-checks, one space; and the other self-checks' `FAIL name` or `  FAIL name`.
  - A passing row that only quotes ✗, as test-preflight's `  ✓  … (✗ schema …)` rows do, begins with ✓ and is skipped. So is a closing summary that names no failure: the bare `FAIL` createAssert and three others print after their ✗ rows, and the checker's `FAIL — N violation(s):`, whose violations follow and so come through the tail.
  - At most three lines are shown, then "… and N more"; a line printed twice, on stdout and stderr, counts once. With no such line, the last three of a crash's errors as Bun prints them from the line's start (`error: …`, `TypeError: …`, `PostgresError: …`, `ENOENT: …`); with none, the last three lines, as before, less blank lines and Bun's `Bun v…` trailer. A failing suite that printed no assertion tally (a crash, or a script with a tally of its own) shows its last crash error under its rows. A row stopped at its limit reads no crash line, so an error it logged and lived through does not pass for the cause: its failure lines show, else its tail. Empty output prints nothing.
  - The ticket's `^\s*✗  ` (two spaces) would have missed the db/ suites' one-space form, and every self-check's `FAIL`.
- **`keep_output`.** Every failing row, suite, install or typecheck, writes its whole output to `$PARITY_TMP/ci-parity-failed.<row>.XXXXXX` and prints `full output: <path>` under the excerpt.
  - The file is outside the lanes' directory, which the exit removes since SMD-3647, so the path still holds once the run is over. Files stay until the system clears its temporary directory; cleared by hand, they are `ci-parity-failed.*`, never `ci-parity-*`, which takes a running run's limit markers too. The closing `FAILURES above` line says a failing row's file is named under it; a lane that fails before its rows, as with no container runtime, has no row and no file.
  - The two flakes SMD-3688 met, SMD-3706 and SMD-3712, lost their detail for want of this.
- Typecheck and install excerpts are as they were.
