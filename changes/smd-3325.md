## Changelog

test-upgrade, test-preflight and test-e2e-sql no longer run `migrate.ts --baseline` to build a ledger ahead of its schema: test-support's `plantLedger` writes the rows it wrote, every file at the migrator's sha, so the sections about the migrations' guards, `--reapply` and a ledger hole keep their cases when the flag is retired (SMD-3325, its first PR).

## FORK

The suites plant a ledger ahead of its schema directly, not through `migrate.ts --baseline` (SMD-3325)

**What changed.**
- `db/test-support.ts`: `plantLedger(url)` creates `schema_migrations` as the migrator does and records every file under `db/migrations` at `migrationSha` over its template, which is what `--baseline` wrote. A name already recorded keeps its row. It returns how many rows it added.
- `db/test-upgrade.ts`: the 32 sections that ran `--baseline` as a fixture call `plantLedger` instead. The guard sections ([9], [20] and its siblings) assert the remedy each guard names, `--reapply`, and no longer the guard's "(adopted with --baseline?)" parenthesis, which stays in the applied files. Their comments say "a ledger planted through N" for "a brain baselined at a ledger through N".
- `server-portable/test-preflight.ts` (the walk index remedy, the version module) and `server-portable/test-e2e-sql.ts` (the health ledger) plant theirs the same way.
- What still runs `--baseline` is what tests the flag itself: test-upgrade [7]'s `--reapply` beside it, [22]'s empty-database guard, `--force` and off-path adoption, and [23]'s adoption beside another schema's ledger. The second PR retires the flag and turns those into tests of the refusal.

**Why.** SMD-3325 retires `--baseline` (decided 2026-10-25). The suites used it about 35 times to make a schema older than its ledger, and the cases that state stands for (a guard refusing, `--reapply` restoring, a ledger hole) outlive the flag. Moving them first keeps the retirement's own diff to the flag.

**Held.** test-upgrade, test-preflight and test-e2e-sql pass as before. A planted sha that differed from the migrator's would read as a drifted file and fail every plain run the guard sections make.
