## Record

The session hook reads CLAIM_UNATTRIBUTED's `lapsedAt` — a claim made on a lapsed claim's work is marked while the registry is still away (SMD-3807)

**What changed.** In `recipes/session-capture-hook/session-capture.mjs` (1.7.2):
- **The hook's own renewal.** A CLAIM_UNATTRIBUTED carrying `lapsedAt` for the claim renewed records it as the claim's expiry. The next renewal finds the expiry past and runs the lapse check it runs for any claim it knows lapsed: it lists the ticket's claims, ended ones included, and a claim on overlapping work made or renewed since the lapse gets a `CLAIMED_WHILE_LAPSED` marker in place of a renewal. `list_claims` needs no agent id, so the check runs with the registry still away. With no claim since, the renewal is tried again and kept.
- **The agent's own call.** With the claims hook pasted, a `renew_claim`, or `claim_work` with the token, refused CLAIM_UNATTRIBUTED with the token file's claim's lapse records it the same way. The lapse is read from `structuredContent` where the harness hands it over, else from the reply's words ("It had lapsed at T, and stays lapsed until a renewal lands"), which are all a harness may hand a PostToolUse hook. Only the token file's own claim's lapse is recorded, by the id the reply names, and only a time.
- **The kept line** is written from the reply's facts: the code, the cause, whether renewals retry or an operator must act (tell the user), and the lapse with what the next renewal does about it. The brain's words, cut at 540 characters, lost the lapse time and the check's ending once a lapse was in them (648 to 686 characters).

**Why.** Since SMD-3705 a renewal, or a claim_work with the token, on the caller's own lapsed claim with the registry away answers CLAIM_UNATTRIBUTED with `lapsedAt`. The hook logged it and kept the token, recording nothing. When its record held no expiry, a claim another key made on the work since the lapse went unmarked until the registry answered and the renewal met it as WORK_CLAIMED, or as CLAIMED_WHILE_LAPSED on a revival. Found by SMD-3705's review passes 2 and 3.

**Held.** test-session-capture passes (949 assertions). The new rows in [13]:
- The hook's renewal refused CLAIM_UNATTRIBUTED with a lapse records it, and its line gives the cause and the lapse whole. At the next run, the registry still away, a claim made on the work since gets a `CLAIMED_WHILE_LAPSED` marker and no renewal is sent. With none since there is no marker: the renewal is tried again and kept.
- The registry set up wrong: the line says an operator must act and the user be told, and the lapse is recorded. A live claim's CLAIM_UNATTRIBUTED records nothing.
- The agent's own `renew_claim` (words alone) and `claim_work` (facts) record the lapse, and the next run marks the claim made since. Another claim's lapse, in words or in facts, and a `lapsedAt` that is no time record nothing.
- The fake brain's CLAIM_UNATTRIBUTED is now render.ts's words, the lapse sentence included.

**Mutants:** eleven run, all killed:
- the recording dropped, on the hook's renewal or the agent's call;
- the claim's id not checked, in the facts or in the words;
- the words not read, the facts not read, or the facts inside a result not read (it survived until the result row was added);
- the time not checked;
- the line's lapse dropped, or every cause read as the registry away;
- the kept branch dropped.
