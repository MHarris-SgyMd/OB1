## FORK

The second brain and the edge proxy move to the auth job — the brains job loses ~20 s (SMD-3471)

**What changed.**
- **The two steps**, "A second brain beside the stack, from its own env file" and "The edge proxy reaches both brains through their published ports", move from `deploy-stack-brains` to `deploy-stack-auth`, word for word.
  - They run straight after that job's "Bring up the compose stack", the same `.github/ci/full-stack-up.sh`. So they meet the stack as they met it in the brains job: just up, on 8010, as project `open-brain`, with `OB1_SMOKE_KEY` exported. That's before the auth job's own steps change anything.
  - They still take ports 8040–8043 and 8090–8094. The auth job uses none of them on the host: its import runner's egress check dials 127.0.0.1:8090 from inside its own container.
  - ob1b and the edge are removed by the second step's own trap, as before, so nothing of theirs is left for the auth steps. ob1b's images are tagged `ob1b-*` (compose's project prefix), so the auth job's later reads of `open-brain-server` are untouched; the `ob1b-*` tags stay on the runner, as they did in the brains job.
  - **A failure's reach moves with them:** a moved step failing now skips the authorization server's steps after it in that run, where before it skipped the tier refresh's and the canary's. The failure is still named in its own job.
- **The jobs' names** say what each now runs: "Full stack: the tier refresh and the canary", and "Full stack: a second brain, the edge proxy, the import runner and the authorization server". Only "Fork Checks passed" is a required check (SMD-3168), so no ruleset changes.
- **The comments** that named the brains job (or, already stale, `deploy-stack`) as the one running `instance.ts` and `edge.ts` beside a stack now name the auth job. So do both jobs' notes on what Bun runs.
- **The checker's** ci-parity excuses for the two jobs (`PARITY_NOT_RUN`) say what each runs.
- **The keys are masked where they are minted.** The edge step's `::add-mask::$OB1_SMOKE_KEY` was the brains job's only mask, and it moved with the step. So `.github/ci/full-stack-up.sh` now masks all four keys it mints, in every full-stack job, before any step prints. Each is a throwaway, and GitHub already prints a step's env values in its header before a mask, so this hides more than before, never less. The edge step's own mask is now redundant and stays.

**Why.** The brains job ran at a median of 238 s over main's last 20 push runs (10-29 to 10-31; the 2026-10-28 review, on pull-request runs, had 247 s). These two steps took about 20 s of it (medians 10 s and 10 s, 16–22 s a run), and they don't read the canary's state: ob1b runs on its own ports and is gone before the canary's steps start. The auth job ran at a median of 128 s over the same runs, on the same stack, with room for both: about 148 s after, 159 s at the window's worst. This shortens the brains job alone. It moves runner time rather than cutting it, and it shortens the run's wall time only once the longer jobs fall below brains: brains is now third, behind db-live's extract and long cells (250.5 s and 235.5 s over main's last 10 runs).

**Departures from the ticket.** None. The ticket's fallback, running them in the background inside brains, was not needed: `canary.sh` and `instance.ts` both scan ports and containers, and nothing here runs beside either.

**Held.**
- **The checker** (check 35 reads `PARITY_NOT_RUN`'s excuses by job) and **actionlint 1.7.7** with shellcheck pass.
- **The steps themselves** are Docker Engine on Linux checks (`edge.ts` picks the host shape there), so their rehearsal is the PR's own Fork Checks run.

**Measured after.** The job names changed, so `scripts/ci-step-times.ts --job` takes the old name for runs before this change and the new name for runs after. CI shift (SMD-3467) keys a series by job name (and `job › step`), so it starts both jobs, and every step in them, as new series, which name nothing until they have a window's history (20 runs); until then a slowdown in either job goes unwarned.
