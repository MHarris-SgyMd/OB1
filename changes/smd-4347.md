## Changelog

The MCP server can hold a 2026-07-28 `subscriptions/listen` stream open, and does not yet: its stop closes held streams itself, each with its closing frame, a stream's log line is written as it opens, and one caller's name holds at most 32 and the server 256, all behind `tools.listChanged`, which still says `false` (SMD-4347). No migration, and nothing the brain serves changes yet.

## Record

A server that can hold the listen streams, and does not yet — its stop closes them itself, each with its closing frame, and nothing it serves changes: PR 2a (SMD-4347)

**Why.** Change 545 decided, in `docs/mcp-listen-streams.md`, to hold the 2026-07-28 `subscriptions/listen` streams open and send nothing on them, so that a restart of the MCP server is what tells a connected client its tool list changed. It cut the build in two: 2a, what a held stream needs, with `tools.listChanged` still `false`; and 2b, the capability, the documents and a real client. This is 2a.

**What changed.**

- `mcp-server/listen-streams.ts`, new: `HOLD_LISTEN_STREAMS = false`, read by the entry alone; the bounds, 256 for the server and 32 for one caller's name; and the object the app and the entry share, a count of open streams and the app's close.
- `mcp-server/app.ts`: an app given streams to hold declares `tools.listChanged: true` on 2026-07-28. A request whose `Mcp-Method` header names `subscriptions/listen` then takes a place under the two bounds or is refused as the SDK refuses one past its own (a 200 carrying -32603; a notification bare), its line `refused` with a code that says which bound, `LISTEN_SERVER_FULL` or `LISTEN_CALLER_FULL`. A held stream's line is written as it opens, `ok`, its `ms` the time to open it; it has no stall bound, no keepalive frame of this server's and no abandonment warning; its place is given back when it ends, its client closes it, its connection drops or anything throws. At the stop such an app does two things: once the drain has ended it serves nothing that arrives; and from the cut a tool call's answer goes no further and does not end, and no request is begun, for the moment between the handler's close and the connections'. An app that holds none has neither gate.
- `server-portable/sse.ts`: `held`, a stream the wrapper neither keeps alive nor bounds; and `isCut`, after which it passes nothing on and does not end the answer.
- `server-portable/shutdown.ts`: `drainOnSignal` takes `heldCount`, `closeHeld` and `onDrainEnd`. Given them, it waits for the requests and the calls and not the held streams, and tells the app as that wait ends. It closes the streams itself, waits for their answers to have ended, then closes every connection: after the flush and the pool's close where the drain finished, at the bound and before the flush where it was cut. Its lines count the streams and say whether they were closed, or were not and were dropped, and why. The close's two bounds are 100 ms each; after a finished drain they come out of the flush's, so the latest a stop can end is unchanged at a grace of 2 s or more.
- `mcp-server/index.ts` gives the stop those three only where the server holds streams, so today's entry stops as it did.
- `mcp-server/test-listen-on.ts`, new: a preload that starts the suite's spawned entry with that one constant `true`. Its `test-` name keeps it out of the image, and `scripts/check-consistency.ts` names it among the suites not run, with why.

**Decided in the build, and written into `docs/mcp-listen-streams.md` under "As built in PR 2a".**

- The line at the open, not at the end with the stream's lifetime as the decision first had it. A record open at the stop must be ended before the flush, and the stream, where the drain finishes, closed after it; ended at the open, the record goes out like any request's and `server-portable/telemetry.ts` is untouched. Nothing records when a stream ended.
- Inert where the capability is off, where the decision said it would not be. The switch is one constant no setting reaches; the suite's spawned entry is started with a preload that changes the word.
- The caps are the route's count, of the requests whose header names the method, taken before the request is validated: a caller at its bound is refused whatever else is wrong with its request. The refusal's shape was fixed before anyone measured what Claude Code does with one. The handler's `maxSubscriptions` was set to the same number, taken out as a bound no row could tell from the first, and put back by the second review as what a stream the count has lost runs into.
- The stop closes the streams after the flush only where its drain finished; where it cut, at the bound. The decision had "after the flush" for both, and the build held to it through three reviews: the maintainer chose the other on 2026-11-19, in the fourth.
- For a request that is not a stream, on a server that holds them: a connection with no whole request on it no longer holds the stop to its bound; a request made whole on one after the drain has ended is dropped; and at a cut the connections are closed some milliseconds after the bound, not in its turn.
- A closing frame is best effort inside 200 ms, where the decision gave no bound: 100 ms for the close and 100 for the frames to leave, kept back from the flush after a finished drain. A stream dropped with none leaves its client the 7 seconds of a server that dies, and the stop still exits 0: its last line says what was dropped.
- Not built, and each named on the page as a change of its own: a count of open streams on a running server (SMD-4458), and a record of a stream's end (SMD-4459). Both come before PR 2b: the maintainer decided so on 2026-11-19, after the sixth review.
