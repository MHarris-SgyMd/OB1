## Changelog

The `--follow` workers' outage wait after a pass is one copy in `db/worker-bootstrap.ts`, on re-embed's rules, and extract's and consolidate's followers now act as re-embed's does. An outage back soon after the last one's answer, nothing having got past the provider since, carries the probe's schedule on rather than starting it over. A probe answered with a refusal — a 401, 402, 403 or 404 (a key revoked or credit run out while the provider was away, or a proxy answering 404 before its route is back), or a redirect, which no call follows — stops the follower, exit 2, nothing marked failed, where before the wait ended and the next call met the refusal. And the follower polls again at once after the provider answers, not after its interval (SMD-2636).

Extract's and consolidate's followers read a provider's error through the same one copy, and leave a 4xx or a redirect no rule reads to the probe: a 401, 402 or 403, a 404 naming no model, or a 400 neither the thought's length nor the request's shape. Answered, it is the thought's, recorded failed, and the follower runs on; refused, the request's, every worker stopped, exit 2; no answer, the provider's outage, waited out. Before, every such 4xx stopped every worker unprobed. A deadline whose probe is refused is now the request's too, exit 2, where extract recorded the thought failed and consolidate the pair's timeout. A 400 naming a field the worker sends (extract's answer budget, response_format, reasoning_effort, frequency_penalty; consolidate's response_format and reasoning_effort) is still the request's at once, and so is a 402 saying the call asks more than the account affords. A refusal of extract's escalation model names OB1_EXTRACT_ESCALATE_MODEL, and a refusal the probe found says the probe's words first. A judge's content refusal of a pair now fails the newer thought, and its newer neighbours paired with the flagged one, where it ended the run (SMD-2636).

Re-embed's follower reads a provider's error through the same copy. A call that meets its deadline with no answer is probed first, where it was an outage at once: answered, the thought is recorded failed and the follower runs on; refused, every worker stops, exit 2; no answer, the outage is waited out as before. A 402 saying the call asks more than the account affords stops every worker, exit 2. A 429, a 503 or the model loading now says the provider "is not answering", as every follower's outage does, where it said "is holding calls back". A refusal the probe found says the probe's words first. The remedy preflight gives for a provider's refusal says the provider "refused the embedding request" or "refused the chat request", where it named a probe, which a 402 "can only afford" is refused without (SMD-2636).

PR 3a: one reading of a provider's error for extract's and consolidate's followers — a 4xx no rule reads probed first, the request's shape still refused at once (SMD-2636)

The plan's PR 3, split in two (the maintainer's, 2026-11-08): this one, the helper with extract and consolidate on it; PR 3b, re-embed onto it.

**What moved.** `fault` in `db/worker-bootstrap.ts`, in place of SMD-3311's `followerFault`, reads an error on followClassify's split into one act and whether it makes a suspect:
- `pause`: a 429, a 503, any other 5xx, a 408, no connection;
- `outage`: the model not served, or consolidate's pair timeout already probed unanswered (`ProviderDown`);
- `probe`: a deadline with no answer, or a 4xx or a redirect no rule reads;

`probedFault` reads the probe made after a `probe` act: up the thought's, refused the request's, no answer an outage. `requestShapeRefused` is each worker's `requestFault`: a 400 naming a field it sends, in the provider's own error words, not an echo of the input. A refused tier probe names the tier's remedy. A refusal the probe found is said in the probe's own words first, the call's error after them. Extract's escalation model the start could not confirm is a `missing` reading, the request's. Consolidate's pair loop asks `fault` which timeouts are the caller's. **What changes, under --follow** (the maintainer's rules, 2026-11-05):

- **A gateway's 4xx that clears before the probe** (a 401 or 404 flapping) records the thought in hand failed, the probe answering: the maintainer's rule. A thought the probe reads its own carries it in its error: "a one-token call right after answered, so this thought's; --retry-failed revisits it".
- **A judge's content refusal of a pair fails the newer thought**, and with it every newer neighbour paired with the flagged one, again at each retry while it stands: the pair carries both texts. Before, it ended the run, exit 2, every restart.

PR 3b: re-embed's follower onto the same reading of a provider's error — a deadline probed first, every outage "is not answering" (SMD-2636)

**What moved.** Re-embed's catch reads a database error by followClassify, as before, and a provider error by `fault`, with no `requestFault` (its probe sends what its calls send, so a request-shape 400 meets the probe too, refused) and no `missing` (the model not served is an outage, as before). It has no pauses: `pause` is an outage at once, `fault`'s suspect deciding whether the thought in hand is one, as followClassify's backpressure did. `probe` is settled by `probedFault`. **Test change:** [9f]'s 401 row reads the probe's words first in the closing line; test-preflight's three re-embed remedy rows read "the embedding request". **Size.** Re-embed grows by 49 lines, most of them its header and the catch's notes.

**What changes, under --follow** (the maintainer's rules, 2026-11-05):
- **A deadline with no answer is probed first.** Answered: the thought's at once, "(the provider answered a probe right after, so this thought's; --retry-failed revisits it)", the follower running on. Refused: exit 2, refused `provider`. No answer: an outage, the thought a suspect, one probe timeout later, each worker probing first, said in the probe's words. The cost: a call that outruns the timeout while the tiny probe answers — the provider loading the model (Ollama's cold start), or a long document on a slow provider — fails that thought, retryable, where it was an outage.
