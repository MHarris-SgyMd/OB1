## Changelog

The `--follow` workers' shared outage glue — the start probe's refusal, the wait at the start, the database wait, the pass it does not end and the leases returned later — is one copy in `db/worker-bootstrap.ts`, where extract, consolidate and re-embed each kept their own of the pieces they share. The move changes nothing they do or say (SMD-2636).

The outage wait after a pass is one copy too, on re-embed's rules, so extract's and consolidate's followers now act as re-embed's does: a wait beginning within a minute, a poll and the pauses of the last one's answer, nothing got past the provider since, carries the probe's schedule on rather than starting it over; a probe answered with a refusal — a 401, 402, 403 or 404: a key revoked or credit run out while the provider was away, or a proxy answering 404 before its route is back — stops the follower, exit 2, nothing marked failed, where before the wait ended and the next call met the refusal; and the follower polls again at once after the provider answers, not after its interval (SMD-2636).

PR 1: the follower's start, database wait and leases moved into worker-bootstrap.ts — one copy for three workers, no behaviour changed (SMD-2636)

**The plan** (the maintainer's, 2026-11-05, on SMD-2636). A survey of the three copies found that unifying every piece on re-embed's rules would change behaviour and break up to 8 live rows, so the work is four PRs, the pure moves first:
- **PR 1, this one:** what is the same in every copy, moved as it is.
- **PR 2:** one outage wait, on re-embed's reference: the backoff carried across outages, a refused probe ending the follower, a poll at once after an answer.
- **PR 3:** one `fault()` on `followClassify` for all three: a deadline probed first, the 4xx the probe decides probed first with a request-shape 400 kept fatal, "is not answering" for every outage line.
- **PR 4:** the database errors extract and consolidate meet at write time, read with re-embed's rules.

**What moved**, into `db/worker-bootstrap.ts`'s new "A follower's waits" section:
- **`probeAtStart`:** the first probe, a refusal only of a probe that answered, else why it did not, kept for the wait (extract's, consolidate's; re-embed's start probe serves its one-shot runs too and stays its own).
- **`waitAtStart`:** the wait at the start, said, stamped through the caller's `startWait`, probed until anything but out, held to the start's refusals (all three).
- **`waitForDatabase`:** the database wait's two lines around `waitOut` (all three; re-embed's first-line message a parameter, and its own aftermath — the poll at once, the end stamp on a refusal — kept in reembed.ts).
- **`followedPass`:** the pass a database outage does not end, the provider wait after it a parameter (extract's, consolidate's; re-embed's poll loop is built otherwise).
- **`Unreturned`:** the leases a worker could not return, returned in turn before a pass, quietly before re-embed's admit gate, and all at once at the end (re-embed's end release unconditional, extract's and consolidate's only when the run did not end waiting for the database — each caller's own `if`).

Each worker passes its own words ("the provider" / "the embedding provider", what it holds back and how it probes), its probe and its refusal text. Every line each worker prints is the same, byte for byte.

**Not moved here:** the outage wait (`waitForProvider`) and the catch block's reading of a fault, which differ between the copies (PRs 2 and 3); the per-pass `halt`, the `--limit` give-back and `stampedPass`, which read the workers' pass internals; consolidate's pair-loop gate. subject-sweep.ts keeps a fourth copy of the start and outage waits, outside this ticket's three.

PR 2: one outage wait for three followers, on re-embed's rules — the schedule carried, a refused probe ending the follower, a poll at once after an answer (SMD-2636)

**What moved.** `waitOutProvider`, in worker-bootstrap.ts after `followedPass`: the wait's line, the stamper's `providerWait` around it, the probes on `outageWait`'s schedule from `ProviderOutage.resumeFrom`'s step, and its three ends — answered (the outage ended at the step reached and the settled count, said), refused (said, in the decision tier's name when its probe met it, and returned with whether it did, for the caller to stop on), stopped (null). It takes the follower's poll and whether it pauses before an outage, and makes the carry's window and words of them. Each worker's own `waitForProvider` keeps what is its own: re-embed's guard, its failed stamp, its probe subject and `stamper.during`; extract's escalation model, reset on an answer; each one's `pollNow`, `configError` and stop.

**What changes, for extract's and consolidate's followers** (re-embed's says and does what it did):
- **The schedule carries.** A wait beginning within the window of the last one's answer, nothing got past the provider since, goes on from the last one's step and says so. What got past is each worker's own count: a thought finished or recorded failed (re-embed's, one call each), extract's windows of a long thought answered and kept too (on a brain keeping them, migration 093; a window not kept, its save refused for a lease lost, is asked again), consolidate's pairs answered, a malformed answer among them, rather than its thoughts (one with no candidate finishes without a call), and its thoughts recorded failed. The window runs from the wait's start, after the pass: a call another worker had in flight can push it past, and such a call usually answers and moves the count anyway. Their window is a minute, a poll and the transient pauses (65 s as shipped), which they spend before an outage begins where re-embed does not (`pausesBefore`), so their line says "back within a minute, a poll and its pauses"; re-embed's keeps its words.
- **A refused probe ends the follower**, exit 2, its line "answers now, refusing the request itself", nothing marked failed. A refusal is any 401, 402, 403 or 404 the chat probe reads so, a proxy's 404 before its route is back among them, or the decision tier's probe refused; the closing remedy names OB1_JEV_BASE_URL for the tier's, OB1_EXTRACT_ESCALATE_MODEL for the escalation model's. Under the sleep scheduler the pass's exit 2 ends the scheduler with 2, where a period's start retries the same statuses. Before, the wait ended on any settled probe, the refusal too, and the next call met the refusal as its own.
- **A poll at once after an answer**, the follow interval skipped once.

**Size.** The helper and its two notes are 66 lines, most of it docblock; re-embed shrinks by 11; extract grows by 20 and consolidate by 16, the wrappers passing each parameter by name, with review pass 1's counts and remedies.
