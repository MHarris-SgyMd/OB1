## Changelog

The `--follow` workers' outage wait after a pass is one copy in `db/worker-bootstrap.ts`, on re-embed's rules, and extract's and consolidate's followers now act as re-embed's does. An outage back soon after the last one's answer, nothing having got past the provider since, carries the probe's schedule on rather than starting it over. A probe answered with a refusal — a 401, 402, 403 or 404 (a key revoked or credit run out while the provider was away, or a proxy answering 404 before its route is back), or a redirect, which no call follows — stops the follower, exit 2, nothing marked failed, where before the wait ended and the next call met the refusal. And the follower polls again at once after the provider answers, not after its interval (SMD-2636).

PR 1, the pure move, is change 487 (v2.0.0), which holds the ticket's four-PR plan; this is its PR 2.

**What moved.** `waitOutProvider`, in worker-bootstrap.ts after `followedPass`: the wait's line, the stamper's `providerWait` around it, the probes on `outageWait`'s schedule from `ProviderOutage.resumeFrom`'s step, and its three ends — answered (the outage ended at the step reached and the settled count, said), refused (said, in the decision tier's name when its probe met it, and returned with whether it did, for the caller to stop on), stopped (null). It takes the follower's poll and whether it pauses before an outage, and makes the carry's window and words of them. Each worker's own `waitForProvider` keeps what is its own: re-embed's guard, its failed stamp, its probe subject and `stamper.during`; extract's escalation model, reset on an answer; each one's `pollNow`, `configError` and stop.

**What changes, for extract's and consolidate's followers** (re-embed's says and does what it did):
- **The schedule carries.** A wait beginning within the window of the last one's answer, nothing having got past the provider since, goes on from the last one's step and says so. What got past is each worker's own count: a thought finished or recorded failed (re-embed's, one call each), extract's windows of a long thought answered and kept too (on a brain keeping them, migration 093; a window not kept, its save refused for a lease lost, is asked again), consolidate's thoughts finished having asked the judge at least once — not its pairs, which a thought put back asks again from its first, nor a thought with no candidate, finished without a call — and its thoughts recorded failed. The window runs from the last answer to the next wait's start, after the pass: a call another worker had in flight, or a wait at the admit gate, can take it past, and such a call usually answers and moves the count anyway. Their window is a minute and a poll, and the transient pauses besides (65 s of them as shipped), which they spend before an outage begins where re-embed does not (`pausesBefore`), so their line says "its pauses aside"; re-embed's keeps its words.
- **A refused probe ends the follower**, exit 2, its line "answers now, refusing the request itself", nothing marked failed. A refusal is any 401, 402, 403 or 404 the chat probe reads so, a proxy's 404 before its route is back among them, a redirect (since SMD-2908, merged beside this PR: no call follows one), or the decision tier's probe refused; the closing remedy names OB1_JEV_BASE_URL for the tier's, OB1_EXTRACT_ESCALATE_MODEL for the escalation model's. Under the sleep scheduler the pass's exit 2 ends the scheduler with 2, where a period's start retries the same statuses. Before, the wait ended on any settled probe, the refusal too, and the next call met the refusal as its own.
- **A poll at once after an answer**, the follow interval skipped once.
