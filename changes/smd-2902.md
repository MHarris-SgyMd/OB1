## Changelog

A server's stop now gives its telemetry flush up to 5 s, inside what `OB1_STOP_GRACE` leaves less a second, where it gave it the database pool's second. A collector that answers in a second or two gets the stop's last spans, metrics and log records, when the drain has left the time. One that is down adds at most 5 s to the stop. When the flush is cut short, the stop says, by signal, how many items were still on their way, where it said only "not all sent within 1000 ms" (SMD-2902).

Telemetry a collector never finished answering, or answered 200 but rejected in part (an OTLP partial success), is now counted as not sent. An export still unanswered at twice the exporter's own timeout plus 2 s is said as `no answer in time`, where a collector trickling its answer was never counted. What a partial success rejects is said as `rejected by the collector`, where it was read as sent (SMD-2902).

## FORK

The stop's telemetry flush gets a bound of its own and says what it cut by signal, and an export a collector never finishes answering, or rejects in part, is counted (SMD-2902)

**What changed.** In two PRs.

PR 1 (OB1 PR 467), the stop's flush:
- **`server-portable/shutdown.ts`.** `drainOnSignal` gives the flush its own bound: `FLUSH_BUDGET_MS` (5 s), at most the platform's grace period (`graceMs`) less `FLUSH_MARGIN_MS` (1 s) and less what the stop has used, and never less than the pool's close bound, which it had before. It still runs beside the close. When the flush is cut, at its bound or by a second signal, `unsent(kind)` is told before the stop's line.
- **`otel-setup.ts`.**
  - Each watched exporter's `cut(kind, held)` counts the items of every export still on its way, and `held` more its batch never handed over, as not sent for `kind`. It says them at once, and an export counted there is not counted again when it ends.
  - `countingLogProcessor` counts the log batch's records. That batch hands its queue over one batch at a time, behind any export already on its way, so with a collector that hangs it still holds the rest. The span batch hands everything over as the flush starts, and its drops are now readable (`dropCount().known`).
  - `Telemetry.cut` runs each signal's.
- **`telemetry.ts` `cutTelemetry`**, which the MCP server, the REST core and `mcp-server/` pass as `unsent`, with `graceMs` from `OB1_STOP_GRACE`.
  - The kind is "on its way or batched when the flush's N ms ran out" (or "at a second signal").
  - That is all that is known: a collector that refuses is retried until then, since the OTLP exporters retry `ECONNREFUSED`, 429 and 502–504 up to 5 times with backoff inside their 10 s timeout, and one that hangs is waited on.
- **`deploy/README.md`**: the stop's paragraph.
- **Tests:**
  - test-server [13d] gives the harness a flush budget and holds the new bound: inside the budget past the close's bound; capped by the grace; floored at the close's; after a cut; the longer of two stalled bounds, not their sum; and `unsent` said once before the line, on a second signal too, with a sink that throws harmless.
  - test-rest's slow-collector case is now the ticket's pair, through a real `bun api.ts` with `OB1_STOP_GRACE=4`: a collector answering in 1.5 s gets `; telemetry sent`, and one that never answers is cut at what the grace leaves, with its span, metric export and log record each said. A third case runs queues of one, with four requests behind the first's hung exports: each signal's lines add up to the five requests, dropped (`queue full`) or cut.
  - test-server [22] also stops a real `bun index.ts` (with `OB1_MCP_UNDER_TEST=client`, `mcp-server/index.ts`) against a collector that never answers: cut at what its 3 s grace leaves, with its spans and log records said. A watched exporter's row cuts twice and fails an export after: one line, the items on their way and the held ones once. [13d] sends a third signal at once (one `unsent`), and a second after a flush that rejected (none).

PR 2, the two losses said as nothing (the ticket's review pass 3 of SMD-1849 PR 2c):
- **A deadline per export** (`watchExporter`'s `deadlineMs`, `exportDeadlineFrom`): twice the exporter's own timeout, as the SDK reads it (`exportTimeoutFrom`: `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_<SIGNAL>_TIMEOUT`, else `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_TIMEOUT`, else 10 s), plus `EXPORT_DEADLINE_MARGIN_MS` (2 s), at most a timer's longest. The SDK's retrying transport retries only when its wait fits in what is left of the timeout, and gives that attempt what is left as its idle timer, so an export that is answered at all ends by twice the timeout.
  - An export still unanswered then is counted as not sent, `no answer in time`, and the SDK is handed a failure (`done`), so its batch moves on.
  - It leaves the exports on their way. A flush and `idle()` no longer wait on it, and the stop's cut no longer counts it: the watcher's `forceFlush` waits on its own exports, not the SDK's queue, which the log batch and the metric reader flush through. Its late answer counts nothing.
  - The SDK still holds its request, which nothing here can abort, in one of the 30 slots each signal's exporter has. Under a trickle that never ends, every export after 30 such is `too many exports at once`, until those requests end (review pass 2). That is measured: TIMEOUT 500, a request every 100 ms, all three signals full at about 2 min, memory flat at about 90 MB. Main fills the same slots, but at the batches' own 30 s export timeout rather than at this deadline: about 15 min at the defaults against about 11.
  - The exporter's timeout is an idle one, which a collector trickling its answer, a byte a second, never lets fire.
  - A collector that hangs still fails sooner, as `timed out`.
- **A partial success counted.**
  - The OTLP exporter says one only through the API's diag logger, `Received Partial Success response:` with the body's `partialSuccess` as JSON, in the same tick as the export's success.
  - The diag hook, now registered wherever a signal has a collector and not only with spans, reads `rejectedSpans`, `rejectedDataPoints` and `rejectedLogRecords` by signal (`partialRejections`).
  - The export's success callback then takes its signal's count (`takeRejected`) and says it as `rejected by the collector`, at most the export's own count. A metric export partly rejected counts as one, `partly rejected by the collector`.
  - It counts that export as failed, so a stop whose own flush meets one says `not all sent`. The collector's errorMessage is never read into a line.
- **`deploy/README.md`**: the kinds, and the partial success no longer "not counted".
- **Tests:**
  - test-server: a watched export past its deadline (once, the SDK handed one failure, no longer waited on by `idle()` or a flush whose inner one never ends, nothing more from its late answer or a cut); a cut then the deadline (counted once, let go); a partial success's rejected items (at most the export's own, an answer that rejected nothing sent, a metric export as partly rejected); `partialRejections`, `exportTimeoutFrom` and `exportDeadlineFrom` by case.
  - test-server also: an export answered at once says nothing at its deadline; a cut then a partial success is said once; a late partial success is its own export's, the next not blamed; `partialBuckets` sums by signal and gives a count once.
  - test-rest [15], through a real `bun api.ts`:
    - a collector trickling its answer, with `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_TIMEOUT=1000`, has its span and log record said as `no answer in time`, the case waiting on those lines (`serveChild`'s new `until`);
    - with only `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_TRACES_TIMEOUT` set, the span is past its deadline while the log record is still on its way at the stop;
    - one answering a partial success has the rejected span and log record said, the metric export as partly rejected, and its message never; with spans off, the log record's and metric export's are still counted;
    - a collector that hangs is `timed out`, never `no answer in time`;
    - one that answers 503 with `Retry-After: 4`, then 4 s on, at a 5 s timeout (past the timeout plus 2 s, inside twice it plus 2 s), is sent, with nothing said.

**Why.** SMD-1849 PR 2c's review measured the flush cut at the pool's second whatever the grace allowed. A collector down, answering 503 or hung was said only as `telemetry not all sent within 1000 ms`, with no signal, count or reason, and a collector answering in 1.5 s lost every stop's tail.

The bound is a fixed budget capped by the grace, by the maintainer's choice, not the rest of the grace that the ticket sketched. Nothing orders the observability profile's collector after the servers, so a `compose down` or an upgrade can stop it first. A refused collector's flush ends by itself, failed, after about 9 s, or 16 s with exports already on their way (the log batch and the metric reader wait for those first). A collector that hangs holds it to the bound. The rest of the grace would have spent those on every such stop, up to about 9.5 s under Docker's default (28 s with `OB1_STOP_GRACE=30` for a hung one).

**Measured after**, with a real `bun api.ts`, three requests before SIGTERM:
- `OB1_STOP_GRACE=30` and a collector answering in 1.5 s: `stopped in 1.5 s; database pool closed; telemetry sent`.
- `OB1_STOP_GRACE=30` and the collector down (refused): `stopped in 5.0 s; … telemetry not all sent within 5000 ms`. The stop said `3 spans`, `1 metric export` and `3 log records not sent to http://127.0.0.1:…`, each while still on its way when the 5000 ms ran out.
- test-rest's pair at `OB1_STOP_GRACE=4`: 1.5 s sent; never answering, cut at 3000 ms (a 4 s grace less the margin), with each signal's line.
- test-server: 1156 assertions, in both modes, this change's 7 on main 7b983289's 1149 (whose README said 1131). test-rest: 472, its 2 on main's 470.
- PR 2: test-server 1167 in both modes on main 3a6686aa (7 more than its 1160, whose README said 1156), test-rest 478 (6 more); a collector trickling a byte every 250 ms has its exports counted at the deadline as `no answer in time`, and one answering a partial success (rejecting 1 of each) has 1 span and 1 log record said as `rejected by the collector` and 1 metric export as `partly rejected by the collector`, its planted message never said (a real `bun api.ts`).
