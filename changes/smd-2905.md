## Changelog

A pass waiting mid-pass for a provider that stopped answering now says so on its heartbeat: the sleep scheduler's row, and an extraction or consolidation follower's own. Preflight's `sleep`, `consolidate pass` and `workers` rows name the provider as the cause, where they read "running a pass" and "its log says which" (SMD-2905).

## FORK

A pass waiting mid-pass for its provider says so on its row, the sleep scheduler's and a follower's own, and preflight names the provider (SMD-2905)

**What changed.**
- **`db/pass-stamp.ts`:**
  - A new value key, `waitingMidPass`, lists the passes waiting mid-pass for a provider that stopped answering. It sits beside the start's `waiting` and is left out while empty, so an older reader reads the row as before.
  - The sleep scheduler sets it by name. A follower's own stamper writes its own name through `providerWait`, at once and running.
  - The row's end clears it.
- **The engines:**
  - `db/extract-entities.ts`'s mid-pass `waitForProvider` now says `providerWait`, as `db/consolidate.ts`'s already did for SMD-3008.
  - Both say it through the stamper they stamp with: the scheduler's per-pass one, or the follower's own. consolidate.ts had said it to the scheduler's alone.
- **`db/sleep.ts`:**
  - The scheduler stamps the mid-pass wait on `heartbeat:sleep` beside the start's.
  - A wake clears it, as `stopPasses` clears the start's.
  - SMD-3008's poll reads the same set as before.
- **`server-portable/brain-info.ts`** parses the word. The scheduler's row takes the passes by name, each once. A follower's row takes its own name alone. Anything else is left off.
- **The state word** is "waiting for the provider mid-pass", or "…, at a pass's start and mid-pass" when both kinds of wait are on.
- **`server-portable/preflight.ts`:**
  - The `sleep` row says "asleep, consolidate waiting for the provider mid-pass", a warning whose fix names the provider.
  - The `consolidate pass` row says it on the scheduler's path and on a follower's ("…, waiting for the provider mid-pass").
  - The `workers` row's fix for a waiting follower names the provider.

**Why.**
- A provider is more likely to go down mid-pass than at a pass's start: a sleep runs for minutes to hours, and SMD-3008 made a judge outage a state the scheduler lives in.
- Through the whole wait, nothing ran and the cause was known, but every row read "running a pass, its last pass failed" with the generic "its log says which".
- SMD-2752 had given the start wait its word. SMD-3008 recorded the mid-pass wait for its own poll and kept it off the row for this ticket.

**Held.**
- **test-live [37]:** a follower's `providerWait(true)` puts `waitingMidPass: ["extract"]` on its own row, running. `false` clears it, and so does the row's end.
- **test-live [10] and [16]:** an extraction and a consolidation follower answered 503 past their pauses. Each one's own row carries its name while it waits, and none once the provider answers.
- **test-live [38]:**
  - extraction met with the model gone missing past its first call carries `["extract"]` on the sleep's row, running, until the provider answers;
  - cut again, a live write wakes the scheduler and clears it.
- **test-live [38b]:** in the judge-outage cases, the sleep's row says consolidation waits mid-pass in the mid-pass case and at its start in the start case, never both.
- **test-preflight:**
  - the `sleep` row's words and fix, alone and beside a start wait;
  - the `consolidate pass` row on the scheduler's path and a follower's;
  - the `workers` row's fix;
  - a word not of the shape (a pass it does not run, a name twice, a follower naming another worker) left off.
- **Counts:** test-live 1439 (1432 on main): `rest` 871, `sleep` 57, `long` 277, `extract` 237. test-preflight 777.
- **Mutants, each killed:**
  - extraction's wait not said;
  - consolidation saying it to the scheduler's stamper alone;
  - the scheduler recording the wait without stamping it;
  - the row's end keeping it;
  - a follower's stamper with no `providerWait`;
  - the parse taking any pass on a follower's row;
  - the state word ignoring it;
  - the `sleep` row's fix, the `consolidate pass` row's scheduler fix and follower words, and the `workers` row's fix, each ignoring it.
- **Equivalent, survived:** `stopPasses` not clearing the word. A wake stops the pass, whose own `finally` says `providerWait(false)`, and that stamps the row cleared. The clear is defensive, as the start's word's is.

**Not here.** A follower's start wait: its row does not exist until its first pass begins.
