## Changelog

Migration 148 adds the store for settled parts (SMD-4446): a statement in one thought that the other thought of a pair shows is no longer true or no longer open, kept with a quote from each, a reason and the word, as a proposal a reviewer decides. An accepted part writes no thought, so nothing is superseded or demoted. Nothing writes or shows a part yet: the pass and the review surfaces are the ticket's later pull requests.

## Record

Settled parts get a store — a statement in one thought that another settles is recorded with both quotes and decided by a reviewer, both thoughts left standing (SMD-4446)

**What changed.** PR 1 of four: the store. `docs/subject-anchored-claims.md`, rows 8 to 10, decides what it holds.

- **`settled_parts`** (migration 148), one row a part: the pair as a proposal names it, which thought holds the statement (`holder`, with `holder_id`), the statement and the words of the other thought that show it, the fingerprint of each text as judged, the word (one of SMD-3116's eight settling labels) and its group (`word_group`: `no_longer_holds` or `done_or_settled`), the reason, the judge's key, a status (pending, accepted, rejected, stale) and who decided it.
  - A pair may hold several parts. One statement of one thought stands once a pair (`settled_parts_one_statement`), so a statement a reviewer rejected is not proposed there again, while another statement of the pair may be.
  - Both thoughts are foreign keys that cascade, and a part's lineage row goes with it (`settled_parts_drop_derivation`). A deleted or forgotten thought leaves neither its parts' quotes nor their recipes.
- **`record_settled_part`**, the pass's one write. It refuses a part of the wrong shape with a RAISE (`invalid_parameter_value`): a holder, a word, a length, a missing key, fingerprint or recipe, a pair that is one thought. Each shape the table's CHECKs hold is refused before the row is built, so no refusal carries the words, in its message or in a constraint error's DETAIL. It returns NULL for a statement that already stands on the pair, replaces a stale row in place, and records the part's lineage row (kind `part`) with it.
- **`review_settled_part`**, the reviewer's decision. `EDITED_SINCE` refuses an accept when either text has moved since it was judged, unless forced. `ALREADY_ACCEPTED` and `ALREADY_REJECTED` refuse a decision that repeats the row's status, and a reviewer may reverse one. The reviewer columns are stamped by 114's trigger function. An acceptance updates the part's own row and writes no thought.
- **Lineage:** `part` joins `derivations`' kinds. `ob1_record_derivation` is 111's body with the kind, and `rebuild_derived` is 067's with the part beside the proposal: a moved text sets a pending part, or one the pass itself rejected, stale and queues its newer thought again under the part's key; a person's decision stands; a lineage row whose part is gone is an orphan. Its result gains `stale_parts` and `cascading.parts`, which `db/rebuild.ts` prints.
- **Grants:** the worker and server groups gain `settled_parts` (`db/config.mjs`), and 148 carries the same to every role holding `UPDATE` or `INSERT` on `supersession_proposals`. A role that only reads the proposal queue gains nothing.

**Why.** The maintainer marked every proposal decide version 5 had made on the canary by 2026-11-18 (SMD-3340): of 41, 2 were right as a whole, 32 right about a part and 7 neither. Accepted as built, the 41 would mark 39 thoughts superseded that still stand. Nothing stored a part.

**Held.** test-schema [133]: the shape; a part recorded with its lineage row; several a pair and one statement once; thirteen wrong shapes refused, none quoting the words; an accept that stamps the reviewer and moves neither thought's pointer, `updated_at` nor audit rows; a repeat refused and a reversal made; `EDITED_SINCE` and its override; a moved text staling a pending part and the pass's own rejection while a person's decisions stand; a stale statement replaced in place; the orphan sweep; the grants; a re-apply with parts standing; a forgotten thought leaving no part that names or quotes it; a deleted one taking its parts. test-upgrade [20ck], on Postgres: onto a populated brain at the file before, refused by name without 114's function, then the same beside a pending proposal on the pair. test-live [31]: `db/rebuild.ts` reports a part set stale, and a leaving thought's parts for the cascade. Twenty-five mutants of the migration, each failing a row of [133]: the three the ticket names (an accept that writes `supersedes`, the fingerprint check dropped, a repeated decision allowed) among them.

**Not taken.**

- **A rule that a part's proposer may not accept it,** as 117 has for a proposed close. The proposals' review has none, and it would be enforcement nobody asked for. It is put to the maintainer on the ticket.
- **The quote check in SQL.** Whether a part's words are in its two texts stays the pass's check, whose rule for a part SMD-4442 decides.
- **A settle function for stale parts,** as 067 has for proposals. The pass does not write parts yet; PR 2 adds what it needs. The rebuild's arm already reads the pass's note prefix.

**Follow-ups.** The ticket's PR 2 (the pass, as a new decide version), PR 3 (review and read) and PR 4 (the proposals pending today). SMD-4441 and SMD-4442 measure beside them. SMD-4474, found on the way: `migrate.ts --reapply` already fails at 064 on a brain holding a `relation` or `subject` lineage row, because each file that widens the kind list validates its own. A `part` row is one more such kind and changes nothing there.
