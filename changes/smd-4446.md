## Changelog

Migration 148 adds the store for settled parts (SMD-4446): a statement in one thought that the other thought of a pair shows is no longer true or no longer open, kept with a quote from each, a reason and the word, as a proposal a reviewer decides. An accepted part writes no thought, so nothing is superseded or demoted. Nothing writes or shows a part yet: the pass and the review surfaces are the ticket's later pull requests.

## Record

Settled parts get a store — a statement in one thought that another settles is recorded with both quotes and decided by a reviewer, both thoughts left standing (SMD-4446)

**What changed.** PR 1 of four: the store. `docs/subject-anchored-claims.md`, rows 8 to 10, decides what it holds.

- **`settled_parts`** (migration 148), one row a part: the pair as a proposal names it, which thought holds the statement (`holder`, with `holder_id`), the statement and the words of the other thought that show it, the fingerprint of each text as judged, the word (one of SMD-3116's eight settling labels) and its group (`word_group`: `no_longer_holds` or `done_or_settled`), the reason, the judge's key, a status (pending, accepted, rejected, stale) and who decided it.
  - A pair may hold several parts, and a whole-thought proposal beside them. One statement of one thought stands once a pair (`settled_parts_one_statement`), read as a thought's text is (`content_fingerprint_of`: case and runs of white space folded). So a statement a reviewer rejected is not proposed there again in other capitals, while another statement of the pair may be. The fold is that much and no more: a quote that differs by a word, a full stop, or a character a reader does not see is another part.
  - Both thoughts are foreign keys that cascade, and a part's lineage rows go with it (`settled_parts_drop_derivation`). A deleted or forgotten thought leaves neither its parts' quotes nor their recipes.
  - A part keeps its judgement (`settled_parts_keep_judgement`): no UPDATE moves its pair, its holder or its statement, and its quotes, word, reason, fingerprints and judge change only when a stale part is judged again. It does not hold the status, which a role holding `UPDATE` can move by hand as it can a proposal's.
  - Every write of a row moves its stamp, `changed_at`, and the table writes it (`settled_parts_stamp_change`): from the clock, and at least a millisecond past the stamp the row had.
  - The planner keeps no statistics on a part's words: `SET STATISTICS 0` on the statement, the showing words, the reason and the note, and on the identity index's fingerprint, so `ANALYZE` copies none into `pg_statistic`.
- **`record_settled_part`**, the pass's one write. It refuses a part of the wrong shape with a RAISE (`invalid_parameter_value`): a holder, a word, white space alone, a length, a missing key, fingerprint or recipe, a pair that is one thought. Each shape the table's CHECKs hold is refused before the row is built, and no refusal echoes an argument, so none carries a thought's words, in its message or in a constraint error's DETAIL. It answers `{ok, action, id, status}`: `recorded`, or `stands` with the id and status of the row that already holds the statement. It replaces a stale row in place, the judgement taken whole, and records the part's one lineage row (kind `part`) with it.
  - The write is of the texts as they stand. It takes the supersession advisory lock, as a decision, a rebuild and a delete do, and then holds the two thoughts' rows (`FOR SHARE`), both until its transaction ends, so an edit of either waits for it and it for an edit. A judgement of a text that has moved since the judge was sent it is not recorded: the write answers `{ok: false, error: "EDITED_SINCE", older_edited, newer_edited}`, for its caller to put the thought back for its new text.
- **`review_settled_part`**, the reviewer's decision. `EDITED_SINCE` refuses an accept when either text has moved since it was judged, unless forced. `ALREADY_ACCEPTED` and `ALREADY_REJECTED` refuse a decision that repeats the row's status, and a reviewer may reverse one: the row keeps the last decision alone. `STALE_READ` refuses a decision on a row that has changed since its caller read it (judged again, decided, set stale or written by hand), when the caller passes the row's `changed_at` as `p_if_unchanged_since`, `update_thought`'s parameter. The stamp given must be the row's own, to the millisecond: a later one, which no read gave, is refused too. A stale row may be decided as a stale proposal may: rejected, or accepted, which a moved text refuses unless forced. A decision asks no actor, as a proposal's asks none. The function runs under READ COMMITTED alone. The reviewer columns are stamped by 114's trigger function. An acceptance updates the part's own row and writes no thought.
- **Lineage:** `part` joins `derivations`' kinds. `ob1_record_derivation` is 111's body with the kind. `derivation_descendants` is 063's with a part beside the proposal in the walk's order. `rebuild_derived` is 067's with the part beside the proposal: a moved text sets a pending part stale and queues its newer thought again under the part's key; a decided part stands; a lineage row whose part is gone is an orphan. Its result gains `stale_parts` and `cascading.parts`, which `db/rebuild.ts` prints. The two censuses of stale lineage rows (preflight's `lineage` row and `db/rebuild.ts --status`) leave a part's row out, as they do a proposal's.
- **Grants:** the worker and server groups gain `settled_parts` (`db/config.mjs`), and 148 carries the same to every role holding `UPDATE` or `INSERT` on `supersession_proposals`. A role that only reads the proposal queue gains nothing. The write's lock on its two thoughts asks `UPDATE` on `thoughts`, the capture group's. `rebuild_derived` reads the table once a thought's walk holds a part's lineage row, so a role outside those groups that ran it before is refused on such a thought: measured for one holding capture, extraction and structure, whose orphan sweep answers `permission denied for table settled_parts` once the thought holds a part.

**Why.** The maintainer marked every proposal decide version 5 had made on the canary by 2026-11-18 (SMD-3340): of 41, 2 were right as a whole, 32 right about a part and 7 neither. Accepted as built, the 41 would mark 39 thoughts superseded that still stand. Nothing stored a part.

**Not taken.**

- **A rule that a part's proposer may not accept it, that a decision names its maker, or that it passes the stamp it read.** 117 has the first two for a proposed close. The proposals' review has none of the three, and each would be enforcement nobody asked for. They are put to the maintainer on the ticket.
- **The quote check in SQL.** Whether a part's words are in its two texts stays the pass's check, whose rule for a part SMD-4442 decides. Nor is the pair's capture order checked: the caller names it, as a proposal's does.
- **A settle function for stale parts,** as 067 has for proposals. The pass does not write parts yet; PR 2 adds what it needs, and the reopening of a rejection the pass made with it.
- **One part for a statement quoted two ways.** The fingerprint folds case and spacing. Of 20 spellings of one statement a person would call the same, 9 were recorded beside the rejected one: a no-break space, a zero-width space, a soft hyphen, an accent typed as two characters. Of 10 spellings the pass's own quote check finds in a text, straight quotes for curly ones among them, 9 were another part. Which characters the pass records for a span is PR 2's; a rule here would pre-empt SMD-4442.
- **The status held against a raw UPDATE.** A role holding `UPDATE` can decide a part by hand, or take a stale one back to pending with other words, as it can a proposal. The row's stamp moves, so a reviewer who passes the one it read is refused.
