## Changelog

`evals/eval-mediated-pairs.ts` measures whether the consolidation pass can ask the subject-anchored ADR's mediated-pair test: the typed-decision tier and the pass's chat model, against two blind raters' labels of 100 canary pairs. Neither meets its bar. The chat model's first step finds mediated pairs (κ 0.64), but no model's state step meets its bar, and the ADR's test is now version 2. So decide mode caps a mediated pair (decide version 4). The judge's model is asked the test's link step about a pair the decider calls outdates at the pass's floor, or evolves the pass would store. A pair linked only through a third subject, or by one thought naming the other's subject in passing, is stored as related at most, with no supersession proposed. The pass key moves to `@p4d4`, so a brain running decide mode judges its pool again (SMD-3203).

**What changed: decide version 4 (PR 2b).**

**What changed: the measurement (PR 2a).**
- **`evals/eval-mediated-pairs.ts`** (new). It asks the pass's own models the test's question about the same 100 pairs, rendered as prompt 4 renders them (`pairContext`).
  - **Version 1, one question:**
    - the tier (H2O-Lightning-4B, ob1-jev/1) gets one choice of the six labels, worded from the test;

- **`evals/README.md`:** the eval's section, with both rounds' results.

**Why.** Decide version 4 applies the ADR's rows 2 and 3, which act on the test's labels. A pass that labels pairs unlike the raters would cap, or make distinct, the wrong relations, so the measurement came first, as the ADR's path asks. On the canary's sample, the two raters found 43 and 44 of the 80 stored evolves and related pairs mediated: 34 and 33 through a third subject (F), and 9 and 11 half pairs (H). By their labels, 18 and 19 of the F-name and half pairs were stored as evolves, a continuation, which row 2 says they are not.

**What it found.** No candidate meets all three bars:
- κ ≥ 0.6 on mediated or not;
- F-state precision ≥ 0.8, with at least 5 predicted;
- F-state recall ≥ 0.5.

| Round | Candidate | Mediated κ | F-state predicted / right (of 11) |
|---|---|---|---|
| version 1 | tier, one choice of six | 0.04 | 1 / 0 |
| version 1 | writer, the test's text | 0.51 | 0 / 0 |
| version 2 | tier, both steps | 0.21 | 1 / 0 |
| version 2 | writer's link, tier's state | **0.64** | 10 / 4 |
| version 2 | writer, both steps | **0.64** | 5 / 0 |

The writer's link step finds mediated pairs. No state step meets the bars for F-state:
- the writer's own state step called 10 of the 11 gold F-state pairs F-name, as round 1's writer had;
- the tier, asked about the subject the writer named, got 4 of its 10 F-state calls right.

The writer's link step alone takes a median 14.3 s a pair.

So the maintainer chose to build the cap (2026-10-22). Decide version 4 asks the writer's link step of a pair it would store as evolves or propose as outdates. An H or F link is then related at most, with no supersession. Row 2's distinct waits for a state step that meets its bar.

**Not taken.**
- **Building the pass on version 1's numbers.** Its best mediated κ was 0.51, under the bar.
- **Re-rating the gold under version 2.** Only rule 1 is known to match both raters' working definitions. Rules 4 and 5 come from one rater each, and one gold F-state pair rests on a fixed spec counted as a value, which rule 1 calls a name. With every bar missed widely, no conclusion turns on it.
- **Reading the test from the ADR inside the pass.** The pass runs in a container without `docs/`, so decide version 4 embeds the text and holds it equal to the ADR's.
- **Moving the rest of H2O's mass onto a capped relation,** so that it would be stored at 1 − P(unrelated). On a mediated pair, much of that mass is about the subject that mediates it: an F-state pair's outdates is the third subject's later status. So a capped outdates stores a relation only where H2O's relation words clear the floor, as a declined proposal does.
- **Asking the link step of a duplicate, related or distinct verdict.** A duplicate states the same claims, so its two thoughts share their subject, and related and distinct are already within the cap. The maintainer's choice names evolves and outdates.
- **Leaving an unread link answer uncapped.** The guard's own answer is F, the safe side, and an answer naming no link gets the same.

**Operators.**
- **A brain in decide mode starts a fresh pass under `@p4d4`** once it runs this tree, as `@p4d3` did after SMD-3151 (its Operators note on the old key's claims holds here too). The sleep scheduler starts it at its first sleep.
- **The cost:** the link step adds a call to the judge's model on each pair H2O calls outdates at the floor, and on each evolves the pass would store. That is a median 14.3 s of the 27B a pair on SMD-3203's 100. The canary held 1,050 standing `@p4d3` evolves when its sample was drawn, so expect hours more of the 27B beside the tier's pass.
- **The deadline:** one `--timeout` (120 s by default) bounds a pair's three calls: the tier, the link step and the writer.
- **What a capped pair records:** related at H2O's P(related) when its relation words clear the floor, else no relation, and never a proposal. The recipe's `judged.decide.link` says why.
- **A brain in judge mode keeps its key, its proposals and its relations.**
