## Changelog

Eleven more places in test-live's sleep-scheduler sections that waited for one of the two lines consolidation joins on now take either, so a run no longer fails or waits out a bound on which was printed (SMD-4545). No shipped code changes.

## Record

Eleven places in test-live's [38], [38b] and [38s] take either line consolidation joins on, where they asked for "extraction drained" alone (SMD-4545)

**What changed.** In `db/test-live.ts`:

- Two patterns, written once above the sleep scaffold. `CONSOLIDATION_JOINED` is either of the two lines the scheduler joins consolidation on, from the line's start. `JOIN_SAID` is the looser form six waits already used ("extraction drained" anywhere in a line), with the other line added.
- **Five rows take `CONSOLIDATION_JOINED`**, the five the ticket names: [38]'s "the end reads consolidation's pool whole"; [38b]'s "joined because extraction drained, not past a stalled thought", which still asks that the stalled line is absent; [38b]'s two judge-probe cases; and [38s]'s first period.
- **Six waits in [38] take `JOIN_SAID`.** The ticket did not count them: they read a shorter form of the line, which the search behind the ticket did not match. Two of them, whose next step acts on extraction (the model made missing, the extraction key switched), also ask that no extraction claim is pending or in hand.

No file a release ships changes, and `db/sleep.ts` is as it was.

**Why.** The cause is the one measured for [38]'s "in the fixed order" row (SMD-4515's record). A thought's entities are written a moment before its claim is marked finished, and a thought with entities is extracted whatever its claim says. A poll in that moment reads no backlog left and a pool not drained, and the scheduler joins on the inflow rule's line ("extraction has done the thoughts captured before the period began …") where a poll a moment later joins on "extraction drained". Extraction is over either way.

[38s]'s first-period row failed so in `ci-parity` on 2026-11-23, load average 18: "joined at -1, swept at 25", everything else it asks held.

**Measured.** Each extraction claim's finish held a second through the row's own run, by a trigger made before it and dropped after, so a poll lands in that moment. One run of each shard a column:

| Row | Main's row, finish held | This change, finish held |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [38], the end reads the pool whole | fails, "not besideNote" | passes |
| [38b], joined because extraction drained | fails | passes |
| [38b], a pass that ends by itself | fails | passes |
| [38b], a consolidation refused at a later start | fails, "refused false; judged false" | passes |
| [38s], the first period | fails, "joined at -1, swept at 32", as the failure seen | passes |
| [38], a refusal retried, and its reset (two rows on two of the six waits) | fail, "recovered false" | pass |

The other four of the six waits fail no row when their line is not printed: each waits out its bound, 20 or 30 s, and the case goes on. They take the same pattern, as read and not held.

In [38b]'s case the held finish also failed its first row, on main and on this change alike: "0 judgement(s)" before the case's bound. With the bound raised for the run it passed, in the same nine periods. That was the hold's nine seconds against the case's clock, not the line.

**Held.** Each row asks what it asked beside the line. Two mutants of `db/sleep.ts`, one run each:

| Mutant | Result |
| --- | --- |
| The scheduler joins and prints neither line | all five rows fail, and the two rows on the six waits |
| The subject sweep starts before consolidation joins | [38s]'s row fails, "joined at 28, swept at 19" |

[38b]'s "not past a stalled thought" keeps its own clause for the stalled line, untouched. The stalled join's line ends "consolidating first", which neither pattern matches.

**Measured after.** The `sleep`, `claims` and `sweep` shards plain, at once: all three pass.
