## Changelog

A thought's judged relations can be read: the `relations` operation (MCP `relations`, REST `GET /v1/relations`, read scope) returns the consolidation judge's related, evolves, duplicate and distinct relations on a thought, from both sides, strongest first, a relation whose text was edited since it was judged marked stale. search_thoughts, search_thoughts_keyword, list_thoughts and fetch carry each result's relations (SMD-2726's read surface; SMD-3306 PR 7a-2b).

## Record

A thought's judged relations can be read: the `relations` operation, and relations on search_thoughts, search_thoughts_keyword, list_thoughts and fetch (SMD-2726 / 3306)

**What changed.**
- **`relations`** (read scope; MCP `relations`, REST `GET /v1/relations`), in `server-portable/core/relations.ts`. It reads one thought's standing judged relations, the facets on it (it the newer) and those naming it (it the older):
  - **Order:** strongest first, by the judge's confidence rounded to six places (absent counts as -1), then the newest recorded (when the relation was first written, which a re-judge keeping its word doesn't move), then the relation's id. A walk reads each relation standing throughout it once;
  - **Filter:** `relation` keeps one word;
  - **Paging:** `limit` a page (1–100, default 20). When more follow, the reply carries a `cursor`, passed back as `after` with the same id and word; it carries both, and a cursor passed with another is refused. `total` counts every relation under the word.
- **Each relation** carries:
  - its id and word;
  - the other thought's id, title, `trust`, time and `supersededBy`;
  - `otherIs`, whether the other is the older or the newer. A relation sits on the newer and names the older, so the text reads "evolves from" and "duplicates" on the newer, and "evolved into" and "duplicated by" on the older;
  - the confidence, and `judgedAt`: when it was last judged, the lineage row's time, which a re-judge keeping the word moves;
  - `stale`, when the relation's lineage row (084) holds fingerprints other than the two texts' now, as `consolidate.ts --list relations` marks EDITED SINCE JUDGED. A relation with no lineage row reads not stale, as there.
- **The maintainer's decisions (2026-11-07):**
  - every word shows, `distinct` included (102 had left showing it to a follow-up);
  - a stale relation shows, marked, until SMD-2726's `rebuild_derived` arm closes it;
  - the judge's relations alone (origin `judged`). A work thread's edges (origin `thread`, 120) stay with the `thread` read and fetch's `threads`.
- **Labels on the reads of a thought.**
  - `search_thoughts`, `search_thoughts_keyword` and `list_thoughts` carry `relations` and `relationsMore` on every result: the first 5, strongest first, with no title. MCP's text adds a `↔ Relations:` line naming another result on the page by its place (`Result 3`, `item 3`) and any other thought by its id, and a key to "edited since judged" once per page that shows one.
  - `fetch` carries the first 20, with titles, only where a thought has some, so a thought with none keeps ChatGPT's shape, as `claimed` is carried. An outside text's title is marked `[ingested]`. The ChatGPT-shape `search` carries none.
  - One read per page, beside the claimed label's.
  - A failed read gives `relations: null`, the text says so, and the log says why. The read goes on.
- **The store** has two new reads, `relationsAmong` (a page's thoughts, the first N each) and `relationsPage` (one thought's, a word and a cursor). They are one statement over 084's indexes: thought_facets' thought_id for the facets on a thought, 084's target index for those naming it, each thought's count and page aggregated once. `stale` reads the lineage row under the relation's judge key: a text's stored fingerprint settles a side where it is the one judged, and the text's own hash decides where it is not, or is NULL, each such thought hashed once a statement. The lineage row is read for the page's relations alone, and the other thoughts' text heads only for a read that shows titles.
- **MCP's structured value** leaves out the other thoughts' titles on the pages and the relations read: they are the thoughts' own words, and ride the relations read's text. fetch's value is its text's JSON, titles included, as its own title is.
- **Refusals:** another tool's cursor is `REFUSED_CURSOR` (`shape`), and one made for another thought or word too (`filters`); an id no thought has, or one that is no uuid, is `NOT_FOUND`; a word the judge doesn't record fails the input schema.
- `tools.json`, OpenAPI and `rest-client` are regenerated, and `server-portable/README.md` gains a "Judged relations" section.

**Why.** SMD-1873 PR 2 stored the judge's verdicts as relations (084), and 102 added `distinct`. Nothing but `brain_info`'s counts, board findings and `consolidate.ts --list relations` read them: a search, list or fetch never said that a thought had been judged a duplicate of another, or a later step of its work. On stable late on 2026-11-07, with its consolidation pass still judging, 3,186 thoughts stood in at least one of 6,524 judged relations: 3 per thought at the median, 7 at p90, 48 at most. 1,295 of those relations were stale. The maintainer cut SMD-3306's 7a-2 in three on 2026-11-06, and this is the second cut.
