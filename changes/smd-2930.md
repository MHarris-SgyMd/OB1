A finding comment on the board no longer repeats or contradicts itself: a relation the consolidation judge found between several rows of the same two tickets (a ticket and its dated sections) is one line per word, in its best finding's direction, saying how many row pairs back it and how many read it the other way round, where it was one line per row pair (SMD-2930).

## FORK

A board-finding comment says each relation word once, in its best finding's direction, with how many row pairs back it and how many read it the other way (SMD-2930)

**What changed.**
- **`db/board-findings.ts`'s `findingLines` (new)** gives a group's bullets in rank order:
  - a proposal is one bullet each, because each is its own pending decision with its own `--accept`/`--reject <id>`;
  - a relation is one bullet per word (a group is one ticket pair), in the best-ranked finding's direction and at its confidence, with "best of N row pairs" when N > 1 ("N row pairs" when the judge gave no confidence) and, for `evolves` and `duplicate`, "K the other way" when some row pairs were read the other way round. A ticket's dated sections fall on either side of the other ticket's dates, so the judge's direction per row pair says little about the tickets; one bullet saying so beats two that contradict each other.
- `commentBody` uses it; `findingLine` takes the row-pair count and the count read the other way.
- **Unchanged:** the record (`board_findings_posted.finding_ids` still names every facet), the marker, the egress subject and the cap.
- **Docs:** the poster's header, and db/README.md's "One comment per ticket pair".

**Why.** SMD-2682 measures how often the board accepts these comments, and SMD-2681's Verify has a person read the first ones. A dry run against the dogfood canary (548 unlinked ticket pairs) showed comments that repeated one line per row pair. The judge relates thoughts, and a ticket's dated sections are rows of their own, so one ticket pair can carry the same relation many times.

**Tests.**
- **db/test-board-findings.ts** (2 new): four `related` facets either way round are one bullet at 0.99, "best of 4 row pairs"; three `evolves`, one the other way, are one bullet saying "1 the other way", as is a `duplicate` read both ways; two proposals stay two bullets; the bullets come in rank order; the marker is unchanged and the group still names all nine findings; with no confidence, "2 row pairs".
- **db/test-live.ts [41]** (1 new): a stub-posted comment for a ticket row and its section, both related to another ticket, carries one bullet "(confidence 0.90, best of 2 row pairs)", and the record names both facet ids. The run that drains the pending pairs before the step cases now passes `cap: 100`: its default of 10 left a pair behind once this case posted, and that pair took the timed step's hang.
- **Mutants:** 7, all killed:
  - the collapse switched off;
  - proposals collapsed;
  - `related` counted the other way;
  - the other-way count never kept, or not shown;
  - the row-pair count not shown;
  - "best of" with no confidence.

  On the first build, two guards both kept proposals apart, so dropping either one alone changed nothing (equivalent mutants). The loop now has one guard, and dropping it is caught.

**Measured after.** A read-only dry run of the poster against the canary: 548 pairs, no comment repeating a bullet, none with two `evolves` bullets. 31 comments collapsed; 2 of them say "1 the other way", e.g. "SMD-2000 evolves from SMD-1973 (confidence 0.75, best of 2 row pairs, 1 the other way)", where they printed two bullets contradicting each other. The worst case is "SMD-2052 is related to SMD-1897 (confidence 0.99, best of 4 row pairs)".

- **A count of 2 when the judge wrote both directions on the same two rows.** That would need the pass to swap newer and older on one pair of rows.
