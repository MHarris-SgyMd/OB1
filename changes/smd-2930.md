## Changelog

A finding comment on the board no longer repeats itself: a relation the consolidation judge found between several rows of the same two tickets (a ticket and its dated sections) is one line, at its best confidence, saying how many row pairs back it, where it was one line per row pair, the same line up to four times over (SMD-2930).

## FORK

A board-finding comment says a relation once, at its best confidence, with how many row pairs back it (SMD-2930)

**What changed.**
- **`db/board-findings.ts`'s `findingLines` (new)** gives a group's bullets in rank order:
  - a proposal is one bullet each, because each is its own pending decision with its own `--accept`/`--reject <id>`;
  - a relation is one bullet per word, at the best-ranked finding, with "best of N row pairs" beside the confidence when N > 1. `related` and `duplicate` read the same either way round, so they collapse by word alone (a group is one ticket pair); `evolves` keeps its direction.
- `commentBody` uses it; `findingLine` takes the row-pair count.
- **Unchanged:** the record (`board_findings_posted.finding_ids` still names every facet), the marker, the egress subject and the cap.
- **Docs:** the poster's header, and db/README.md's "One comment per ticket pair".

**Why.** SMD-2682 measures how often the board accepts these comments, and SMD-2681's Verify has a person read the first ones. A dry run against the dogfood canary (548 unlinked ticket pairs) showed comments that repeated one line per row pair. The judge relates thoughts, and a ticket's dated sections are rows of their own, so one ticket pair can carry the same relation many times.

**Tests.**
- **db/test-board-findings.ts** (2 new): four `related` facets either way round are one bullet at 0.99, "best of 4 row pairs"; `evolves` stays one bullet per direction; two proposals stay two bullets; the marker is unchanged and the group still names all nine findings; with no confidence, only the count is shown.
- **db/test-live.ts [41]** (1 new): a stub-posted comment for a ticket row and its section, both related to another ticket, carries one bullet "(confidence 0.90, best of 2 row pairs)", and the record names both facet ids. The row is then dated out of the day's count, so the cap cases after it see the posts they were written against.
- **Mutants:** 5, all killed:
  - the collapse switched off;
  - `related` keyed by direction;
  - proposals collapsed;
  - the count dropped;
  - `evolves` direction dropped.

**Measured after.** A dry run of the poster against the canary, read-only: 547 comments, none with a repeated bullet, 29 collapsed. The worst was "SMD-2052 is related to SMD-1897 (confidence 0.99, best of 4 row pairs)".
