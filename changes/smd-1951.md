## Changelog

The whole dogfood brain (308 thoughts) is labelled by epistemic kind against a thirteen-value candidate axis and frozen as `evals/fixtures/thought-kinds.json` — ids and closed-vocabulary keys only — with `evals/eval-thought-kinds.ts` to label, freeze and score it against the live brain; 87% of the brain is a `plan` (an imported issue), the metadata model agrees with the hand label on 28% and calls half the brain `procedure`, and its confidence band carries no signal, so the enum change that follows is decided from this table (SMD-1951).

## FORK

Every thought in the dogfood brain labelled by kind — the distribution, the shipped type's confusion against it, and the metadata model's first pass, frozen as a content-free fixture the enum decision reads (SMD-1951)

**What changed.** `evals/eval-thought-kinds.ts` holds the candidate axis —
`observation`, `fact`, `idea`, `hypothesis`, `question`, `decision`, `lesson`,
`procedure`, `rule`, `event`, `plan`, `reference`, `compound` — the statuses
four kinds carry (`plan` open/done/canceled, `question` open/resolved,
`hypothesis` open/confirmed/refuted, `decision` standing/superseded), and three
modes: `--label` runs the metadata model over every thought in a brain and
appends one review line per id (resumable, content never written); `--freeze`
turns a hand-labels file and that review file into
`evals/fixtures/thought-kinds.json`; the default scores the fixture against the
LIVE brain — distribution per kind × source, the shipped `type` against the kind
with the share of the brain that has no slot in the five, the frozen first
pass's agreement per kind and per confidence band, and the same model run again
now so the number is reproduced rather than read. `--self-check` probes the
fixture's shape rules with thirteen mutants and validates the committed file;
the portable-server CI job runs it after the evals typecheck. The fixture is
inverted maps — a kind is the list of ids that are it, likewise `parts` (for
compounds), `status`, `source_kind`, `first_pass` and
`first_pass_confidence` — so every value is a thought id and every key a
closed-vocabulary identifier, the one shape check 9 admits without an allowlist
entry; `generated`, `origin` and `note` carry the provenance in words.

**Why.** `thought_stats` on 2026-09-22: 308 thoughts, 259 `task`. SMD-1949
proposes widening the enum; widening by intuition repeats the mistake. The
corpus was small enough to label whole.

**Measured.** Source is read from the text's shape, because every row says
`source: mcp` (the board was loaded through `capture_thought`, not SMD-1806's
ingester): 271 imported issues, 10 project records, 27 agent captures.

`plan` was added during labelling: the ticket's candidates had no honest slot
for assigned work carrying its problem and its acceptance test, which is what
an imported issue is, and `procedure` (how to do a repeatable thing) is not
that. Twenty plans are question-shaped (a spike whose work is to measure and
decide); two tickets are questions outright. No hypothesis exists in the brain
— nothing states a prediction with a checkable outcome — so SMD-1809's
calibration has no seed population here. The five compounds are the roadmap's
follow-up note, two DONE notes whose body is a decisions list, the posture
digest (rule + fact + event) and SMD-1729 (its decision + its program).

The shipped `type` is right for 249 of the 289 thoughts whose kind has a slot
in the five (86%, almost all of it `plan` → `task`), and 19 thoughts (6%) are a
kind the five cannot express at all — the lessons, decisions, questions,
compounds and the procedure; `event` lands on `reference` 7 of 9 times.

The metadata model (`qwen2.5:7b`, temperature 0) agrees with the hand label
on **86 of 308** (28%). It calls 149 thoughts `procedure` — 138 of them
imported issues, whose Problem / Work / Verify shape reads to it as "how to"
— and names `plan` for 81 (72 right). On the captures it is better: lesson
4 of 8 recalled at 4 of 5 precision, decision 2 of 3. Its confidence band is
`high` on 284 of 308 and right on 75 of those (26%), `medium` right on 10 of
22: the band does not separate right from wrong. Where it did say `plan`, it
read the issue's status right 45 of 72 times.

Also found: three tickets are in the brain more than once (SMD-1933 three
times, SMD-1867 and SMD-1903 twice) as successive versions of one description
— SMD-1720's stale-value case, live; and the `people` facet lists a package
and two ticket ids (SMD-1935).

**Held.** `--self-check` in CI: thirteen shape mutants each caught, the
committed fixture sound (a planted content string fails check 9 by name).
The scoring run against the live brain re-ran the model on all 308 and
reproduced the frozen first pass exactly: 86/308, no answer differing.

**Not taken.** Two types per thought with a ratio — the axis's answer to a
two-kind thought is `compound` with parts, then atomize. Committing the
`about` axis (people and projects named) — free text, out of a committed
fixture by check 9's own reasoning; it is computed from the text at report
time (issues per project, the tickets the captures cite). Sampling — 308 is
small enough to label whole, and a sample could not have found the three
duplicated tickets or the zero hypotheses. Any enum change — this ticket
measures; SMD-1949 decides.
