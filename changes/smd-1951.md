## Changelog

The whole dogfood brain (336 thoughts) is labelled by epistemic kind against a thirteen-value candidate axis and frozen as `evals/fixtures/thought-kinds.json` — ids and closed-vocabulary keys only — with `evals/eval-thought-kinds.ts` to label, freeze and score it against the live brain; 71% of the brain is a `plan` and 11% a `hypothesis` (all imported issues), the metadata model agrees with the hand label on 24% and calls half the brain `procedure`, and its confidence band carries no signal, so the enum change that follows is decided from this table (SMD-1951).

## FORK

Every thought in the dogfood brain labelled by kind — the distribution, the shipped type's confusion against it, and the metadata model's first pass, frozen as a content-free fixture the enum decision reads (SMD-1951)

**What changed.** `evals/eval-thought-kinds.ts` holds the candidate axis —
`observation`, `fact`, `idea`, `hypothesis`, `question`, `decision`, `lesson`,
`procedure`, `rule`, `event`, `plan`, `reference`, `compound` — the statuses
four kinds carry (`plan` open/done/canceled, `question` open/resolved,
`hypothesis` open/confirmed/refuted, `decision` standing/superseded), and three
modes: `--label` runs the metadata model over every thought in a brain and
appends one review line per id (resumable, content never written); `--freeze`
turns a hand-labels file and that review file into
`evals/fixtures/thought-kinds.json`; the default scores the fixture against the
LIVE brain — distribution per kind × source, the shipped `type` against the kind
with the share of the brain that has no slot in the five, the frozen first
pass's agreement per kind and per confidence band, and the same model run again
now so the number is reproduced rather than read (`--frozen` skips the re-run).
`--self-check` probes the fixture's shape rules with thirteen mutants and
validates the committed file; the portable-server CI job runs it after the
evals typecheck. The fixture is inverted maps — a kind is the list of ids that
are it, likewise `parts` (for compounds), `status`, `source_kind`, `first_pass`
and `first_pass_confidence` — so every value is a thought id and every key a
closed-vocabulary identifier, the one shape check 9 admits without an allowlist
entry; `generated`, `origin` and `note` carry the provenance in words.

**Why.** `thought_stats` on 2026-09-22: 308 thoughts, 259 `task`; 336 by the cut. SMD-1949
proposes widening the enum; widening by intuition repeats the mistake. The
corpus was small enough to label whole.

**Measured.** Source is read from the text's shape, because every row says

ingester): 271 imported issues, 10 project records, 27 agent captures as found; 336 by the cut, after the 16 rows this ticket wrote (below) and 12 a concurrent ingest added.

`plan` was added during labelling: the ticket's candidates had no honest slot
for assigned work carrying its problem and its acceptance test, which is what
an imported issue is, and `procedure` (how to do a repeatable thing) is not
that. A measure-first spike — "measure X, adopt only if it clears the bar":
GraphRAG, the vector-store ladder, the rerankers, quantisation, the Jev-class
and Edge0 spikes — is a `hypothesis`, and its status is the recorded outcome
(the fork record), not the ticket's text, which is the prediction: thirteen
refuted, four confirmed, twenty open. That is SMD-1809's seed population,
seventeen resolved. The five compounds are the roadmap's follow-up note, two DONE
notes whose body is a decisions list, the posture digest (rule + fact + event)
and SMD-1729 (its decision + its program).

The shipped `type` is right for 220 of the 263 thoughts whose kind has a slot
in the five (84%, almost all of it `plan` → `task`), and 73 thoughts (22%) are
a kind the five cannot express at all — every hypothesis (all 37 typed `task`),
the lessons, decisions, questions, compounds and the procedure; `event` lands
on `reference` 7 of 9 times.

The metadata model (`qwen2.5:7b`, temperature 0) agrees with the hand label
on **80 of 336** (24%). It calls 156 thoughts `procedure` — 138 of them
imported issues, whose Problem / Work / Verify shape reads to it as "how to"
— names `plan` for 83 (52 right) and never says `hypothesis`, `question` or
`compound`. On the captures it is better: lesson 4 of 8, and on the thirteen
atoms below it named the kind 13 of 13 times. Its confidence band is `high` on
310 of 336 and right on 68 of those (22%), `medium` right on 11 of 24: the
band does not separate right from wrong. Where it did say `plan`, it read the issue's status
right 30 of 50 times.

Also found: three tickets are in the brain more than once (SMD-1933 three
times, SMD-1867 and SMD-1903 twice) as successive versions of one description
— SMD-1720's stale-value case, live; and the `people` facet lists a package
and two ticket ids (SMD-1935).

**Atomized.** The decisions and rules inside the compounds and two DONE
notes were written back as their own thoughts, each `derived_from` its
source: SMD-1903's three egress decisions, SMD-1933's three, SMD-1917's
not-taken list and its fragment-cap rule, three standing rules from the
posture digest, SMD-1541's "no actor names a source", SMD-1729's
architecture decision — nine decisions and four rules, each naming what was
rejected. The extractor typed all thirteen `observation` and, asked for the
kind on the split text, got all thirteen right: the compound, not the model,
was the obstacle. The lesson digests (four to eight claims a row) are left
whole until SMD-1736 or SMD-1809 needs them one per row; a ticket's dated
"Update" sections (75 of 271 imports) go to SMD-1867's adapter.

**Held.** `--self-check` in CI: thirteen shape mutants each caught, the
committed fixture sound (a planted content string fails check 9 by name). The
scoring run against the live brain re-ran the model on all 308 and reproduced
the frozen first pass exactly, no answer differing.
