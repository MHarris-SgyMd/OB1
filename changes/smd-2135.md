`db/test-live.ts` [5d] loads 15,000 rows where it loaded 25,000, after a `VACUUM` that keeps the rows an earlier section deleted out of its gate's sample, and [5f] loads 6,000 where it loaded 12,000, the smallest table on which each plan it asserts is the one it names; [5f]'s user triggers stay off until its cleanup has emptied the table (SMD-2135).

## FORK

test-live.ts sizes [5d]'s and [5f]'s tables to what their planner decisions need — the two sections stop spending a third of CI's live step on rows and index builds (SMD-2135)

**What changed.** The third cut of SMD-2135, after #169 (the deadlock timer, the lease clock) and #176 ([9]'s slowed stub). The ticket asked whether [5d] and [5f] could share one corpus, and whether 25,000 and 12,000 rows are what the planner needs or margin. They are margin, and the corpus is not where the time goes.
- **[5f]: 12,000 rows → 6,000.** Its time was the two HNSW builds over the loaded rows and their chunks, 18 of its 22 s locally, and a build grows faster than its rows: the thoughts index took 9.4 s over 12,000 rows, 3.3 s over 6,000 and 1.1 s over 3,000, with 0, 2 or 4 parallel maintenance workers alike. Every assertion holds down to 2,000 rows, the mutant's buffer excess thousands of pages over the heap's size at each count. At 6,000 each mutant plan is also the one the section's header names, at both widths the suite runs (PostgreSQL 16). Below that, the unfiltered call's parent lookup becomes a Hash Join over the heap rather than a Merge Join over the whole primary key: between 5,000 and 5,600 rows at 1,024 dimensions, and between 5,500 and 6,000 at 768, so at 768 the count sits just above the switch. The assertion accepts either join.
- **[5d]: 25,000 rows → 15,000, into a vacuumed heap.** The skip needs the sample's scaled estimate at ten times `v_exact`, 10,000 here. Every full page holds the same number of rows (about 70), so a draw of d distinct pages estimates at least (d − 1)/d of the table, short only by the last, partly filled page. The gate's third condition admits no draw of fewer than three pages, and 15,000 is the smallest table on which a three-page draw still clears: 2/3 × 215 pages × 70 rows = 10,033. Measured over 5,000 draws of the deployed statement each: 11,000 missed 4.9%, since 7/8 of it is under 10,000 whenever its 10-row last page is drawn; 12,000, safe on every draw of six pages or more, missed none.
  - The bound holds only on an emptied heap. [5b]'s 2,000 rows are dead after the `DELETE`, and without a `VACUUM` the load went in after their 28 pages, which the sample draws and counts as seen with no hit (041's LEFT join, on purpose). At 15,000 that missed 49 of 5,000 draws, one call in a hundred, so about one run in six would have failed the section's exact band (twenty broad calls a run). At 25,000 the same draws missed none. The `VACUUM` runs after the index is dropped and before the load, and a new assertion after the load holds its effect: every heap page holds a live row.
- **[5f]'s triggers.** It disabled user triggers for the load and re-enabled them straight after, so its cleanup's `DELETE` wrote an audit row per loaded row, the rows [5d]'s comment says a load must not leave to the sections after. They stay off until the cleanup has emptied the table, as in [5d] and [5e]. Nothing after reads `thought_audit`'s delete rows by count ([25] reads capture rows only).

One assertion is added, [5d]'s page check (733 → 734), and `db/README.md`'s total and its lines naming the row counts follow.

**Why.** On main's push run for #176 (36178807862) and the runs since, "Run the live suite" took 129–134 s, [5d] 27.4 s and [5f] 22.4 s of it — the two largest sections once #176 cut [9].

**Held.** The sections' own teeth are their mutants and run on the smaller tables unchanged: [5d]'s 020 arm makes one more GIN scan per call on the broad filter, and [5f]'s pin `RESET` turns every branch's join into a Merge or Hash Join touching at least the heap's pages more. [5d]'s page check failed all three runs of a probe without the `VACUUM` (215 of 243 pages hold a row), where the gate's own band fails about one run in six by the draw rate (two of those three). The section comments carry the arithmetic above, so a later change to either count has to answer it.

**Measured after.** Main (82655a02) and this change before review pass 1 added the page check, 733/733 on every run:

**Not taken.**
- One corpus for [5d] and [5f], as the ticket proposed. The two need different tables — [5d] no HNSW index, no chunks, one row in 250 thin; [5f] both indexes, a chunk for every second row, the first 500 thin — and [5e], between them, loads its own 3,000. Sharing [5d]'s 15,000 rows would give [5f] a larger build, the cost this cut removes.
- Keeping each broad filter's exact answers across [5d]'s two bodies. The table does not change between them, but at 15,000 rows it saved about 0.4 s.
- [5f] at 3,000 rows (1.6 s less again). The unfiltered mutant becomes a Hash Join, and the section's text and messages would stop describing the plan they catch.
- The two-database split of the suite the ticket held in reserve, until CI says whether the step is under 120 s without it.
