## Changelog

The supersession proposal listing pages. `list_supersession_proposals` (and `GET /v1/proposals`) takes `after` — the `cursor` the page before ended with — and answers `cursor` (null once no more follow) and `remaining`, so any status or pass of more than 200 proposals can be read whole; `consolidate.ts --list <status> --after <cursor>` does the same, a capped page printing its cursor. A server or CLI from before this release reads a brain at 110 as missing migration 109: upgrade it, do not re-apply 109 (SMD-3249, migration 110).

## FORK

The proposal listing pages — a keyset cursor on the listing's own order, with the rows that remain counted (SMD-3249)

**What changed** (migration 110).
- **`list_supersession_proposals`, redefined on 109's body** — its columns, order, cap and the status, lineage and judge-key selectors — plus:
  - an after-cursor of three trailing parameters, `p_after_confidence numeric`, `p_after_judged_at timestamptz`, `p_after_id uuid`, all defaulted: the last listed row's values on the listing's order (`confidence DESC, judged_at, id`), the term `confidence < c OR (confidence = c AND (judged_at > t OR (judged_at = t AND id > i)))` applied before the order and the cap. All three or none: a partial cursor is refused by name, where it would list from the top without a word.
  - two trailing columns: `page_key`, each row's three values as text (numeric(3,2) exactly, judged_at to the microsecond, the id), and `remaining`, the selection's rows from this page's first on (`count(*) OVER ()`, before the LIMIT).
  - LANGUAGE plpgsql (a sql function cannot raise), `#variable_conflict use_column`. The sentinel `ob1:listing-pages` beside 070's and 109's.
  - 109's four-argument form dropped first (a RETURNS TABLE cannot gain a column), 070's and 029's with it if a hand re-apply left them; a call of two to four arguments resolves through the defaults.
- **Seven arguments, always,** in every fork caller: the store, `consolidate.ts`, `evals/eval-consolidate.ts`. 029, 070 or 109 re-applied by hand lands its form beside 110's, and a call both take — under three arguments beside 029's, four beside 070's, five beside 109's — is ambiguous.
- **The cursor** (`core/list-cursor.ts`, beside list_thoughts'): base64url of `["proposals", confidence, judged_at, id]`. The tag keeps one listing's cursor out of the other; the confidence is held to numeric(3,2)'s text, the time to Postgres's (the thoughts cursor's check, shared as `pgStamp`). A time of the right shape Postgres still cannot read is the caller's: `REFUSED_CURSOR`, as list_thoughts says it.
- **The tool and `GET /v1/proposals`** take `after` and answer `cursor` and `remaining`; the text says "of R" and ends "N more follow — pass after: <cursor> (with the same filters) for the next page". The value's guard keeps a proposals cursor by its own shape.
- **`consolidate.ts --list <status> --after <cursor>`**: a capped page's header says "the first N of R" and the page ends `next page: --after <cursor>`; `capNote` points at it. `--after` is refused alone, with `--list lineage` (two listings, pending then stale) or `--list relations` (another listing), and when it is no cursor of the listing's.
- **Preflight's `lineage` check** reads 110's seven-argument form and its three sentinels; its arms are 109's, one argument list on.
- **An older server or CLI on a brain at 110** lists through its shorter call, but its preflight and `--status` probe 109's signature and read the listing as from before 109. The fix is the new server, never re-applying 109, which would stand its form beside 110's.

**Why.** 070's listing caps a page at 200 with no cursor, and 109 kept that. A pending queue drains as it is reviewed, but every other listing stopped at 200: accepted, rejected, stale, all, and a look back at one pass's verdicts (SMD-3115's precision check is per pass). The canary on 2026-10-24 held 242 pending proposals under `@p4d1` alone.

**Why the values, not the row's id.** 063's recorder re-judges a stale row in place — `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`, the same id, `judged_at` now() and the new verdict's confidence — so a cursor naming the row would read its new place and jump the walk, skipping or repeating rows. (The plan on the ticket said the recorder deletes and re-inserts; test-schema [98] found it updates in place, and the values hold either way.)

**Held.**
- test-schema [98]: 230 rows under five confidences and three times walked in pages of 200 (remaining 230, then 30) and of 7, each row once and in order, page edges inside ties; page_key's text; the cursor's own row re-judged at a lower confidence — the next page is the seven that followed its old place, and a full walk meets it once at its new one; a partial cursor refused; a four-argument call resolving; 109 re-applied beside (four arguments ambiguous, seven resolving) and 110 again taking it away. [64], [84] and [97] name 110 as the last definer.
- test-upgrade [20bb]: refused without 016, 025 or 029; onto a populated brain at 109, the rows listing the same through a four-argument call, walked in pages of two; nothing moved; a re-apply a no-op. [20v] reads the flag in 110's body.
- test-preflight: 029's, 109's and 070's forms beside 110's; a brain at 109; a seven-argument body missing each of the three sentinels.
- test-live [16]: 51 proposals under one pass — a page of 50 "of 51" with its cursor, the cursor listing the last one and none after, in-process as spawned; `--after` refused alone, with lineage, with relations, and when no cursor; `capNote`'s cases.
- test-server: the reply's "of R", "N more follow", the value's cursor and remaining, a sentence in the cursor's place null, the refusal's words; the parser over its valid and ten invalid forms.
- test-store-sql [10] (a page's next and remaining, past the end, a cursor time Postgres cannot read), test-e2e-sql [9] in both modes (pages of one through the tool, list_thoughts' cursor refused), test-rest-sql (page two by page one's cursor through both doors alike; a bad cursor 400 REFUSED_CURSOR), rest-client's query.

**Not taken.**
- *Paging `--list relations`.* Another listing with its own cap of 50 and its own order (newest first); a cursor there is its own change.
- *The operator GUI's Next link on `/proposals`.* SMD-2280's page, which can now pass `after`.
- *A cursor naming the row (an id).* 063's in-place re-judge moves a row; a position does not.
- *Fetching one row past the cap to learn whether more follow.* The cap is 200 for every caller; `remaining` answers it without raising the cap or a second query.
