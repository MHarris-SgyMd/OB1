## Changelog

Migration 057 has the three write functions append the event first and one projector write the row in the same transaction — `upsert_thought` (2- and 3-argument), `update_thought` and `delete_thought` compute the after-image, append through `ob1_append_thought_event` and call `ob1_project_thought_event`; the audit trigger becomes the check under `ob1.projecting` (a projected row that is not its event's AFTER image is refused, SQLSTATE OB002) and appends as before for a raw write; a vector onto a row that has one is a refresh with no event and no `updated_at` (`ob1_refresh_thought_vector`); `ob1_embedding_snapshot` holds every vector by (key, model), seeded once and fed by live writes, so a fold rebuilds the vector without the provider; 001's stamp yields to the projector; the five accepted deltas land; the writable-projection prototype and its CI steps are retired, its criteria moved into the suites; step 2 of `docs/event-log-as-truth.md` (SMD-2116).

## FORK

The write functions append then project — the event first, one projector writing the row, the audit trigger the check; the vector snapshot the fold reads: migration 057, step 2 of the event-log decision (SMD-2116)

**What changed.** `db/migrations/057_append_then_project.sql`: `ob1_project_thought_event(event, vector, model, replay)` applies one `thought_audit` event to the row — capture → INSERT (the row's `created_at` from the event when it carries one), update → UPDATE by the event's afters, delete → DELETE — faithful, not corrective (a key the event does not move stays; a vector the event does not flip stays unless, on a replay, the snapshot holds one for the new text); it announces itself in `ob1.projecting`, `ob1.projecting_thought` and `ob1.projecting_replay`, runs 050's stamp under `ob1.actor_amend = 'backfill'`, clears `ob1.event`, restores every setting before it returns, refuses a content-less capture (OB003) and runs 042's guard in detach mode on a replay. The three write functions keep 046's and 042's bodies up to the write — the same locks in the same order (036's supersession lock, 033's fingerprint lock, 032's row lock) and every refusal before any append — then compute the after-image (050's two stamp arms and 046's diff rule, the functions 055 lifted out), append, and project; `delete_thought` appends and projects inside the block that catches the guard's OB001, so a refused delete leaves no event. `thoughts_write_audit` under `ob1.projecting` recomputes the diff from the row and refuses (OB002) unless the row is the event's AFTER image and moved only columns the event names — the vector's presence held live and not on a replay; a foreign row accepted as a bump (an empty diff) or as a tombstone's nulled successor pointer, appended live and skipped on a replay; under `ob1.projecting = 'vector'` only the vector may move; without a setting it appends as 055 did. `ob1_refresh_thought_vector` is the same-text-with-a-vector arm of `update_thought` (the re-embed's shape) and of a re-capture carrying a vector: no event, no bump. `ob1_embedding_snapshot (content_fingerprint, embedding_model) → embedding, dims, taken_at` is seeded once from every row holding all three and fed by `thoughts_snapshot_embedding` on live writes alone (nothing under a replay; nothing when none of the three moved; no vector operator in the body, so a session with pgvector off its path still writes). `update_updated_at` yields on `thoughts` for the row the projector names. As the last definer, 057 carries 046's ACL capture, DROP chain and replay for `update_thought`'s older forms and 042's DROP of the 2-argument `delete_thought`. The capture role gains SELECT on `thought_audit` (the check and the projector read the event) and SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE on the snapshot; preflight's `write privileges` names both with their triggers. Preflight's `atomic capture`, `audit events`, `edit signature` and `delete signature` name 057 as the last definer, read the new sentinels (`ob1:capture-appends-then-projects` in the writers, `ob1:projection-checked-against-its-event` in the trigger) and warn on a body from before it; every remedy that re-applies 046 or 055 names 057 after it. `db/reembed.ts` keeps calling `update_thought` (the stale-read guard, the chunk rewrite and 018's reports live there); its docblock says the pass moves no `updated_at` now. The five deltas the decision accepted: an identical re-capture writes nothing; an edit whose patch changes nothing writes nothing; a refresh leaves `updated_at` alone; the stale-read pre-check is the one guard (046's copy in the UPDATE's WHERE went with the UPDATE); the 2-argument form locks the row it lands on. The prototype (`evals/eval-writable-projection.ts`, `evals/writable-projection.ts`, `evals/writable-projection/`) and its two CI steps are retired: after 057 its baseline is option 2 and its teardown would drop shipped objects; C1–C6 and C10–C12 are test-schema [53], C7–C9 test-live [26] beside [6g]/[6h], C13 the header's cost line. The record stays in `evals/README.md`. The redaction arm — the projector skipping a redacted `thought_id` — is SMD-1723's, which lands after this file and owns the marker; the decision ordered redaction no later than this step, and the order is the maintainer's to keep or waive at the merge.

**Why.** `docs/event-log-as-truth.md` (SMD-1997) decided the log is the write-side truth and the row its projection; SMD-1999 measured the one shape that keeps the extension contract — the functions append first and one projector writes the row, `thoughts` a table, the trigger the check — with an empty contributor delta. Step 1 (055) put the payload in the event so a projector has something to project; this is step 2. From the day it lands there is one truth: the row is what its event says, and the trigger refuses a row that is not. The raw in-tree writers and every community schema's raw write are still trigger-audited; step 3 (SMD-2117) moves the in-tree ones onto the projector and builds the fold.

**Measured** on Postgres 16.15 in a container, width 8, no provider, medians of 200 calls over five rounds, 056's bodies against 057's on one database: a 3-argument capture with a vector 988 µs → 1.03 ms (×1.04), a content edit with a vector 942 µs → 1.08 ms (×1.15); the ratio moved ×0.89–×1.24 (capture) and ×1.06–×1.32 (edit) across rounds, inside SMD-1999's band (×0.65–×1.8) — noise over effect. 057's apply with the seed over 200 rows: 14–25 ms. On a copy of the dogfood brain (877 thoughts, 3,407 audit rows, 873 rows with key, model and vector at 1,024 dimensions): the apply with the seed 30 ms, 873 of 873 seeded byte-equal to their rows with the row's stamp as `taken_at`, no audit row written; the scripted writes (a capture, an identical re-capture, an edit, a re-embed, a delete) and a replay of one real thought's log through the projector behaved as [53] holds; preflight's four lines clean.
