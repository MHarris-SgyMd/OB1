The import runner's self-check (`bun deploy/orchestration/runner.ts --self-check`) takes about 12 s where it took 41 s: its stop and drain cases run on shortened bounds, side by side. The runner itself stops and drains a step as before (SMD-3198).

## FORK

The import runner's self-check runs its timed cases on shortened bounds, side by side (SMD-3198)

**What changed.**
- **`deploy/orchestration/runner.ts`:**
  - **A step's stop and drain bounds are a `Config` field**, `timings`, where they were module constants: SIGKILL's grace after the first SIGTERM, the second SIGTERM and the pipes' drain. The service's `configFrom`, and the start's probe that emitters reach no runner port (`emittersReach`), take `STEP_TIMINGS`: 6000, 1000 and 2000 ms, today's values. Nothing the runner does in the stack changes.
  - **The self-check's bounded cases** ("hangs", "twice", "deaf", "orphans", "floods") run on a 2 s deadline, a 1 s grace, the second SIGTERM at 0.2 s and a 0.3 s drain, each asserted bound written in them. They run side by side, each its own pipeline, after the 409 case. "slow" sleeps 0.6 s where it slept 1.5 s; the second call still lands while its run is busy. The reembed-turn case ("first" and "second") runs on the same bounds, and "second" starts 0.4 s behind "first", where it started 0.2 s behind: starved of CPU, a second's start could beat the first's to the turn.
  - **"twice" says so when it takes its second SIGTERM**, in its stderr, which the report's notes carry and a SIGKILL would not let it print. On a 1 s grace a time could tell the second SIGTERM (at 0.2 s) from the kill (at 1 s) only by 0.4 s either side, which a loaded runner can blur.
  - **"deaf" has a lower bound too**: it must die no earlier than the deadline plus the grace. A runner that killed at the deadline passed the old upper bound alone.
  - **The proxy section's sessions run as four groups side by side**, each on proxies of its own, each in its order. That is the main proxy's cases, a refused client that stays, tunnels to hosts that never close or end first, and the strict and loose proxies. Each group reads its own proxy's refusals, and every statement moved whole; no bound changed.

**Why.** The self-check runs twice per Fork Checks run. One is Portable server's "Orchestration import runner rules", on the host. The other is the import runner's image build in "Full stack: the import runner and the authorization server", as root with su-exec. Each took 41 s, of which 40.7 s was gaps of 0.4 s or more: its cases waited out the service's 6 s grace, 2 s drain, a 3 s deadline and 1.5 s sleeps, one after another. Portable server, 256–288 s since SMD-3195 (main's one run and its PR's two), is the run's slowest job. Expected: each self-check about 12 s, and Portable server and the image build each about 29 s shorter, about 58 runner-seconds a run. The run itself gains 8–29 s: Schema against real Postgres (rest), at 243–250 s, is then about level with Portable server. The image build's job, 195–201 s, is off the critical path, and the build has no layer cache, so its saving holds on every run.

**Not taken.**
- Dropping one of the two runs. As root with su-exec it proves a DATABASE_URL without a password is refused. On the host it proves an imports root the runner cannot search reads "blocked", and that as root without su-exec the runner refuses to start. Each run proves branches the other cannot reach.
- Running the bounded, review pass 4 and reembed-turn groups side by side. They share the module's one reembed turn and the stand-in ingester's count: the turn case holds the turn for 3 s, which "slow" would wait on.
- The ticket's 1 s deadline and 0.4 s "slow". "slow" must finish its emitter, ingest and reembed inside the deadline that "hangs" and "deaf" overrun. That would leave it about 0.2 s on a loaded runner, against 1 s at 2 s, for 1 s of the run.

**Held.**
- Built on main 70c81854.
- The self-check passes locally in 12.0 s, from 41.0 s, and in 10 runs of 10: six as is, and four beside 14 CPU burners (about a 4-vCPU runner's share left), each 11–12 s.
- In the runner's image, built from `deploy/orchestration/runner.Dockerfile`: the build's self-check layer passes, and the self-check run there as root with su-exec passes in 12 s.
- Mutants of `step()`, each failing the case that holds it:
  - never sending SIGKILL fails "deaf" (30.0 s);
  - never sending the second SIGTERM fails "twice" (no line in its notes);
  - draining forever fails "orphans" (30.0 s);
  - killing at the deadline with no grace fails "deaf" (2.0 s, under its new lower bound) and "twice".
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` in evals/, which imports runner.ts, passes.
- The checker passes.
