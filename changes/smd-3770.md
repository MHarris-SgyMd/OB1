## Changelog

CONTRIBUTING states how a contribution names the product (SMD-3770): Daemonic Brain for the product, "a brain" for one memory, Open Brain only for the origin, and identifiers unchanged. The skills, and the PR and issue templates, now follow it.

## Record

The contributed skills and the contribution templates say Daemonic Brain, under CONTRIBUTING's new naming rule (SMD-3770)

**What changed.** PR 1 of several, one or two directories each.
- **The rule** (the ticket's Work 1): CONTRIBUTING's new "Naming the product".
  - Daemonic Brain names the product: its setup, its tools, its connector and what it does.
  - One memory is "a brain".
  - Open Brain names only the origin: a "grew out of", a link to upstream, a credit or attribution, a quote.
  - OB1 used as the product's name follows the same rule, and OB1 as a repository's name stays.
  - Identifiers stay until SMD-3771 decides them.
  - A `metadata.json` `author` is never changed.
- **CONTRIBUTING's own seven mentions** follow the rule. They are on SMD-3793's list, but taken here, since the rule now sits in that file. Two `metadata.json` examples say "your brain".
- **`skills/`:**
  - "Open Brain" goes from 165 occurrences to 12, and OB1 as a word from 37 to 23. The files holding either go from 50 to 9.
  - The product's name becomes Daemonic Brain, and "an Open Brain" becomes "a Daemonic Brain".
  - OB1 as the product's name became Daemonic Brain or "the brain", reviewed one at a time (14). `OB1-connected mode` is `Brain-connected mode`, beside `Direct-chat mode`.
- **Contribution templates:** the PR template's test line and the two issue templates.
- **Work 3, the connector's server name in prompts.** A prompt that names a tool does it by the connector's server name, as in `mcp__open-brain__capture_thought`, and that name stays with the connector (SMD-3771). The prose beside it names the product.
- **What stays in `skills/`, on purpose:**
  - `deleting-thoughts`' attribution of "the Open Brain maintainer's stance", which is upstream's maintainer's;
  - the `open-brain` tags in `metadata.json` files;
  - `heavy-file-ingestion`'s `.ob1/` output folder.

**Why.** The maintainer decided on 2026-11-04 that the live text says Daemonic Brain now (SMD-1929, SMD-3765). Contributors had no rule for which "Open Brain" goes and which stays.
