## Changelog

A new read tool, `agenda`, answers "what should I work on next?". It lists open tickets and open items ranked by effective priority, each with the terms behind its place (the stated level, urgency from its due time, what it unblocks), over MCP and as `GET /v1/agenda`. By default it shows only work that can start and nobody holds. Blocked items, items under a live claim and items not yet startable are left out and counted. It also says how many of the items it found carry no priority, and when the board last synced.

Scope it by `source` (linear, items, or a plugin's name), by Linear `project` or `label`, or with `holder: "mine"` for the work your key holds a claim on. Proposed open items (extractions nobody confirmed) show only with `include_proposed` (SMD-3669).

## Record

The agenda: open, startable, unclaimed tickets and open items, ranked by effective priority, each with why; query_log's agenda kind (PR 1 of 2) (SMD-3669)

The maintainer decided the plan on 2026-11-07:
- the candidates are unsettled tickets and open items with status `open`, with proposed items only when asked;
- a live claim hides its ticket, while a lapsed one never released is named and hides nothing;
- the scope is source, Linear project and label, or `holder: "mine"`;
- a migration logs each call.

`plan_context`'s board section (point 4) waits for SMD-2684, which isn't built; a note on it says to read `agenda`. PR 2 is the measure: SMD-3662's eval scores `agenda` against plain search.

**What changed.**
- **Migration 123:** `agenda(p_scope, p_holder, p_limit, p_include_blocked, p_include_claimed, p_include_proposed, p_weights, p_now)`, and `ob1_item_blockers`, both STABLE.
  - It takes every ticket whose head has a known, unsettled status type and every open item with status `open`, in the scope or held by the calling key.
  - It scores them with 122's `effective_priority` in chunks of 1000, and sorts by effective, then the sooner due time, then kind and key.
  - It leaves out, and counts, a ticket blocked by an open item (`include_blocked` lists its blockers), a ticket under a live claim (`include_claimed` names the holder), and always an item not yet startable.
  - It returns `{items, total, unknown, left_out, board_sync, claims_read, now}`; `unknown` counts the items in `total`, not only the page.
  - `query_log` takes the kind `agenda` and gains `result_items`, the typed items a call returned. Its kind CHECK widens, and an agenda row must carry its items.
- **The `agenda` tool** (read scope) is wired through the schema, `core/agenda.ts`, the store's `agenda` and `logAgenda`, `GET /v1/agenda`, the MCP render, `tools.json` and the regenerated REST client. Its query_log row, best-effort as a search's, carries the scope and the items.
- **Docs:** db/README.md (123, the count, the map) and server-portable/README.md's tool table.
