## Changelog

The opt-in priority inference (`OB1_INFER_PRIORITY`, off by default) reads less of what isn't the text's own. Its prompt (v2) says whose work a level and a deadline must be about, and names what is neither: a label quoted beside another ticket, where an item ranks among others, a finding's severity, an expiry, the earliest something can happen. Two code guards drop what the model still reads that way. A level quoted as another ticket's label (a key's "(Medium)" tag, a board line, a table row with its key, a rank among tickets) is dropped only when an agent-kind key wrote the capture, such as a session summary: a person's capture keeps every level the prompt reads, since syntax can't tell another ticket's label from their own "fix the login ticket (urgent)". A due phrase that is the earliest something can happen ("can't happen before 11-03", "doesn't open until Monday", "starting Monday") is dropped the same way, for an agent-kind writer alone, and a deadline stays one ("isn't due until Friday", "not later than 15 November"). Nothing ranks on these statements yet: `count_inferred` stays off until a fresh measurement clears its bar (SMD-4226).

**What changed.**
- **`server-portable/priority-infer.ts`:**
  - `INFER_PROMPT` v2. A note's level is how much its writer says their own work matters. A note that is itself a ticket or task carries its own priority field. It names what is no level and what is no deadline.
  - `citedIn` finds a level phrase every place of which has a shape that cites another item:
    - a ticket key earlier in its clause, its words ending in "(", "[", ",", ":", "·" or a dash just before the level;
    - a key beside the level inside the phrase (the key case-sensitive, so "mid-2025 high" is no key);
    - a table row that holds a key;
    - on a line that holds a key, a board status (capitalised) before or after the level ("Backlog, Low", "High, Todo");
    - a board field's name before it ("Owner, Urgent priority");
    - a level directly before a key;
    - a rank among items by level or priority ("the only High-priority open child", "the highest-priority open issue").

    A ticket's own "Priority:" field, at a line's start or after "·" or "|" as an export lists it, is never cited; "Filed SMD-4300 (Priority: High)" is. A key followed by "is", "to", "at" or nothing, then a capitalised level, cites too ("SMD-12 is High", "Moved SMD-12 to High", "SMD-12 High"); "SMD-12 is urgent" stays the writer's own. Common key-shaped tokens that name no ticket are passed over: "COVID-19", "ISO-8601", "UTF-8", "GPT-4", a quarter ("Q4-2026") and a few airline prefixes. A line break (U+2028 too) or a sentence's end bounds a clause. Places are found on a copy lower-cased character by character, so a text holding "İ" is read like any other, and a phrase's curly quotes are straightened as the text's are. Each place reads a bounded stretch around it: 380 KB with 20,000 keys takes 41 ms, where review pass 2 measured 12.8 s.
  - `readAnswer` marks such a level `cited`, and a floor's due day `floored`. `forWriter(reading, kind)` drops a marked half only when the kind is "agent" (the maintainer's choices after review passes 2 and 3). An operator, an importer ("ingested"), a key never classified and a stamp that can't be read all keep both. The capture's `stateInferred` checks the key's scope first, then reads the row's `actor_kind` only when a half is marked.
  - `flooredIn` finds a due phrase only in a floor's own construction:
    - a negation (the contractions, "nothing", "no one") then a verb of something starting, then "before", "until" or "till" ("can't happen before 11-03", "doesn't open until Monday", "isn't ready until Monday", "nothing ships until Friday");
    - "not until" or "not before" with nothing between;
    - "no sooner than" or "no earlier than";
    - "starting", "from" or "(only) after" just before it, unless the phrase opens with "by", "before" or "until";
    - "at the earliest", "earliest", "or later", "at the soonest" or "onwards" after it, except "or later if …" and "earliest possible".

    Nothing ahead of it may be a condition ("if it doesn't ship before Friday"). A negated deadline stays one ("isn't due until Friday", "don't need it until Friday", "not later than", "if it's not paid before the 15th"), as do "start by Monday", "from now until Friday" and "after the meeting but before Friday".
- **`evals/eval-inferred-priority.ts`:**
  - `--run --only-fixture` runs the shipped prompt over SMD-3670's frozen texts, and `--dev` scores that run against the frozen labels. It is labelled a development check, not a verdict: the 120 were drawn from v1's calls. It counts the items where the run reads a half the gold doesn't, each half apart.
  - Each run line records a hash of `priority-infer.ts` (the prompt and its guards), and a run mixing two drafts is refused.
  - The run applies the agent-kind rule to every line, since the population is actor_kind agent alone.
  - SMD-3670's report still reads its recorded verdict, and now says it gates nothing, since the shipped prompt is v2.
