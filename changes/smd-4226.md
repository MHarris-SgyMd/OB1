## Changelog

The opt-in priority inference (`OB1_INFER_PRIORITY`, off by default) reads less of what isn't the text's own. Its prompt (v2) says whose work a level and a deadline must be about, and names what is neither: a label quoted beside another ticket, where an item ranks among others, a finding's severity, an expiry, the earliest something can happen. Two code guards drop what the model still reads that way: a level phrase cited for another item (a ticket's "(Medium)" tag, "Backlog, Low", a table cell, "the only High-priority child"), and a due phrase that is a floor ("can't happen before 11-03"). A ticket's own export keeps its "Priority:" field as its level. Nothing ranks on these statements yet: `count_inferred` stays off until a fresh measurement clears its bar (SMD-4226).

## Record

The priority inference's prompt v2 and its guards against cited labels and floors, checked against SMD-3670's frozen labels: PR 1 (SMD-4226)

SMD-3670 PR 2 measured prompt v1 on stable's captures. The operator agreed with 0.188 of its level reads and 0.500 of its due days. Most of the rest were a label quoted beside another ticket, a ranking, an expiry or a floor. The maintainer decided the plan on 2026-11-12:
- **Two PRs:** this one fixes the inference and checks it against the frozen labels; PR 2 is a fresh registered measurement, started only if this check holds.
- **The fix:** the prompt plus a code guard, since the local 7B follows a prompt loosely.
- **PR 2's population:** stable's captures only, with the frame fixed to leave the ingester's rows out.
- **This PR's gate:** the ticket's Verify. On the frozen 120, v2 makes no level read on a session summary where the operator read none, or names each one it still makes. Its due reads exclude expiries and floors.

**What changed.**
- **`server-portable/priority-infer.ts`:**
  - `INFER_PROMPT` v2. A note's level is how much its writer says their own work matters. A note that is itself a ticket or task carries its own priority field. It names what is no level and what is no deadline.
  - `citedIn` drops a level phrase every place of which is cited for another item:
    - a ticket key in it, or in the 40 characters before it;
    - parentheses, or a table's cell;
    - a level word in a list of labels;
    - a rank among items ("the only", "the highest", "one of two", near a ticket, issue, child, item, bug, task, row or pull request).
  - `flooredIn` drops a due phrase that is the earliest something can happen. That is a negation up to two words before it, "at the earliest" or "or later" after it, or those words inside the phrase.
- **`evals/eval-inferred-priority.ts`:**
  - `--run --only-fixture` runs the shipped prompt over SMD-3670's frozen texts, and `--dev` scores that run against the frozen labels. It is labelled a development check, not a verdict: the 120 were drawn from v1's calls.
  - SMD-3670's report still reads its recorded verdict, and now says it gates nothing, since the shipped prompt is v2.
