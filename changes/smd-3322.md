## Changelog

The operator GUI edits and deletes a thought: its page links to an edit page, which saves through `update_thought` and refuses an edit from a stale read rather than overwriting the change, and to a delete page, which confirms first and asks again before deleting a thought other thoughts cite. Each calls the REST core under the signed-in visitor's credential, and a read session is offered neither (SMD-3322).

## FORK

The operator GUI edits and deletes a thought, each through its REST-core operation under the visitor's credential (SMD-3322)

**What changed.**
- **The session** (`dashboard/src/lib/server/session.ts`) keeps the operations whoami named at sign-in, by a key or the authorization server's token. A page offers a write only where they list it (`rest.ts` `may`); the REST core still decides each call. `rest.ts` `write` calls an operation under the visitor's credential and hands its answer to the form action, so a refusal is said beside the form.
- **A thought's page** links to its edit and delete pages for a session that may call `update_thought` and `delete_thought`.
- **The edit page** (`/dashboard/thoughts/<id>/edit`) saves the text through `update_thought`, sending the `updated_at` it read as `if_unchanged_since`:
  - a change made since (another tab, a connector, a worker) is refused in words, never overwritten;
  - the form keeps the stale time it posted, since the page's load reads the thought afresh after a refusal; with the fresh time in the field, saving again would overwrite the change the refusal reported;
  - the form posts as multipart, so the text goes as it is, not percent-encoded; the GUI's body limit (`serve.ts`) rises from 64 kB to 1 MB for an edit's post alone (every other request, a sign-in signed out among them, is held to 64 kB by its declared length in `hooks.server.ts`), and a thought whose text would post past 1,000,000 bytes — its UTF-8, each line break sent as CRLF (`writes.ts` `postedBytes`) — is not offered for editing, the page saying so;
  - an empty text, a NUL and a time altered to anything but the core's time form are refused before any call; a browser's CRLF breaks are saved as one break each, as the plugin pin saves them, and the field opens with the line break a browser drops, so a text's own leading break survives.
- **The delete page** (`/dashboard/thoughts/<id>/delete`) names the thought and asks first; a delete sends the visitor to the list, which says what was deleted, once, from a flash cookie named after the listener's session cookie, never from its address. A thought that statements in other thoughts cite is refused, the page saying how many (the core's `citedBy`), with a second confirm to delete it anyway (`detach_citations`).
- **The smoke** (`dashboard/smoke.ts --writes`) captures a thought of its own over the proxy's `/mcp`, edits it as the page's multipart form posts and deletes it on its pages, and holds a read key to neither. Its keys may come from the environment (`OB1_SMOKE_KEY` and the two beside it) rather than its command line.
- **CI**, the full-stack job's GUI step, runs the smoke with `--writes` and its keys from the environment, then reads the arm's thought's audit rows.

**Why.** The GUI was read-only: SMD-2280's write verbs went with none of its children (SMD-3307, SMD-3309, SMD-3310), and SMD-3322 took them. SMD-3064 ([OB1 PR 560](https://github.com/MHarris-SgyMd/OB1/pull/560)) went first, so a write through the authorization server is audited as the GUI's own principal, `oauth:operator+gui`, not as every connector's one name. This is the first of three PRs: capture, and worker retry and release, follow; accept and reject wait on SMD-3338.

**Held.**
- The smoke, in CI against the full stack:
  - the write key's thought page links to both pages; the edit page holds the text and the time read, its form multipart and its field opening with a line break; an empty text and an altered time are refused in words;
  - an edit of 160 kB saves (303 back to the thought, each CRLF break saved as one) and a second edit from the first read is refused as stale, its form keeping that stale time and the typed text, the thought keeping the saved edit;
  - a read key sees neither link, gets 403s from both pages and from posts to them, and leaves the thought as it was;
  - a link naming a thought as deleted makes the list say nothing; the delete asks first, sends to the list with its flash cookie, which the list names and clears with the attributes it was set with, and the thought is then a 404;
  - a body past 64 kB is refused signed out (a sign-in's) and signed in to a page but an edit (the delete's, the thought kept); a thought of 400,000 short lines, 800 kB stored and 1.2 MB as a browser posts it, is offered no edit form.
- CI then reads the thought's audit rows: its capture through the MCP server, then one update and one delete through the REST core, each under the smoke key's name (Verify 1 and 3).
- Verify 2 (a read session sees no write verbs) is the smoke's read-key arm.
- **Rehearsed** on a fresh scratch stack: the CI step as written, its port moved off the dogfood stack's. It seeded its thoughts, the smoke passed 116 checks, and the audit-row check held.
- **Mutants**, each rebuilt into the stack and run through the smoke, each failing its write arm:
  - the form taking the fresh time after a refusal fails "its form keeping the stale time";
  - the edit sent without `if_unchanged_since` saves the stale edit, failing three checks;
  - the links offered to every session fail the read key's "no Edit and no Delete";
  - the body limit back at 64 kB fails the 160 kB save and five checks after it;
  - the list reading `deleted` from its address fails "a link naming a thought as deleted makes the list say nothing";
  - the edit page measuring a text as stored, not as posted, fails the many-lined thought's check;
  - the 64 kB guard dropped from `hooks.server.ts` fails both big-body checks (and the arm after them, the big post to the delete then deleting its thought).
- Not held by a test: the edit and delete actions' own gates. They spare the core a call it would refuse, but the load that runs after a refused action answers the page's 403 either way, so the smoke cannot tell them from the core's refusal (a mutant dropping the edit's gate survived, as it must).

**Not taken.** The edit and delete pages read the thought through `fetch`, which logs a click-through, as the thought's own page does; SMD-3212 has the GUI's click-throughs. A refused edit of a thought deleted meanwhile shows the load's 404, not the typed text. An edit typed past 1 MB still meets the server's bare 413: with no script on the page, nothing can measure the field before it is posted. Two tabs deleting within a minute share one flash cookie, so one list may name the other's thought.
