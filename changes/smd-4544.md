## Changelog

The session hook (1.11.7) finds a work-claim token (`wc_` and 64 hex) glued to a letter or digit before it, such as `xwc_<hex>` or a URL-encoded `%3Dwc_<hex>`, or to a hex character after it, such as `wc_<hex>expires`, which no rule took before: it went to the brain in the summary and to the model in the excerpt (SMD-4544). ## Record

The work-claim rule takes a token glued to its neighbours (SMD-4544)

**What changed** (`recipes/session-capture-hook/lib/secrets.mjs` and `db/sensitivity.ts`, the same literal; 1.11.7).
- **The `work-claim token` rule** drops its lookbehind `(?<![A-Za-z0-9])` and its trailing guard `(?![0-9a-f])`: the token is `wc_` and a run of 64 or more hex, whatever precedes or follows it, blanked whole. A run of 63 is still no token.
- **What it reverses:** SMD-3615 read `xwc_` and `9wc_` before 64 hex as a word's, and a run of 65 as no token, near misses both. The suite's three near-miss probes for them become catches; the 63-digit near miss stays. The trailing guard went on the maintainer's word after review pass 1.
- **The 64-hex rule's own lookbehinds** are unchanged, so a digest beside an underscore or a letter reads as before.
- **1.11.7**, in `lib/hook.mjs` and `metadata.json`; the README's Testing list names the cases.

**Why.** SMD-3656's review pass 1 (secrets adversary) found a claim token with a letter or digit before it taken by no rule, and this ticket's review pass 1 the same with a hex character after it: the work-claim rule's lookbehind refused it, the 64-hex rule's lookbehind refuses a run after `_`, and the entropy rule skips hex. A token proves a claim to anyone holding the claim's key, so it went out whole. A name ending in `wc` right before `_` and a digest is unlikely, and no text in this repository holds one.

**Held.**
- **The suite** passes under Bun: 1,095 assertions — three near-miss assertions gone, one new.
  - **Glued tokens:** after `x`, after `%3D`, after a digit, in upper case, and with `expires`, `Done` or `2026` glued after, each is blanked in a prompt and in the outcome, the gate passes the blanked text, and the model's excerpt is withheld.
  - **Near miss:** 63 hex after `wc_` is still no claim token.
- **Four mutants each fail the suite:** the lookbehind restored, a digit before the token still refused, the trailing guard restored, and a run of 63 taken as a token.
- **A differential against main** (the hook as at 73404178, unchanged since), through the episode path, over 4,536 texts in a prompt and in the outcome, a token with every kind of neighbour before and after it: no finding main makes under refuse is lost, nothing main does not send is sent, no episode main's gate passes under redact is refused, and no excerpt main withholds is sent. What the branch newly blanks is, every time, a token after a letter or digit, or one with a hex character after it.
- **The brain's copy:** the table equals the hook's; the near-miss samples `db/test-live.ts` holds standard keep their tiers.
- `tsc --checkJs --noUnusedLocals` and `scripts/check-consistency.ts` pass.

**Not taken.**
- **Contrived false positives.** A name ending in `wc` right before `_` and a sha256 (`libwc_<sha256>.so`, `srcwc_<sha256>`) is now blanked, refused under refuse, and restricted in the brain, where main sent it (review pass 1). Main already blanks `wc_`, `_wc_`, `-wc_` and `/wc_` before a sha256, so a left anchor buys little.
- **A token split or spelled otherwise** is read as before, in the hook as on main: split by a line break or a space (`wc_<32 hex>⏎<32 hex>`, `w⏎c_`), its underscore URL-encoded (`wc%5F<hex>`), a zero-width character inside it, or in fullwidth letters. The brain's copy reads the last two after its NFKC fold and its hidden-character drop, so the hook and the brain disagree there (review pass 1).
