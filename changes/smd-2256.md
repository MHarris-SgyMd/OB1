## Changelog

`node_state` reads a stored projection kept current on write: migration 060's `ob1_ticket_head` (each ticket's head) and `ob1_superseded_by` (each thought's newest successor), fed by statement triggers on `thoughts`, so `search_thoughts`' `prefer_current` costs a lookup, not the brain — signatures and rows unchanged, the capture group gains the writes on both tables (SMD-2256).

## FORK

node_state reads two tables kept current on write, so prefer_current costs its window, not the corpus (SMD-2256)

**What changed.** `db/migrations/060_node_state_projection.sql`:
`ob1_ticket_head` (keyed by `md5(issue)::uuid`, the issue beside it: the head
row, its status, status_type and watermark) and `ob1_superseded_by` (each
superseded thought's newest successor), no foreign keys. The rules once each —
`ob1_ticket_heads_of(text[])`, `ob1_superseders_of(uuid[])`, NULL for every
key, a one-time-filtered branch for NULL beside an `unnest()`-keyed one — and
`ob1_node_projection_reconcile` (superseders, then heads, since the head rule
reads "nothing supersedes it" from the table). Three statement triggers on
`thoughts` (AFTER INSERT / UPDATE / DELETE, transition tables): a statement
touching no row with an issue key or a pointer returns at once; otherwise the
keys it moved — both sides of a key's move, a pointer's targets and their
issues, a successor's `created_at` — are locked (advisory, classes
22560–22562, hash order, a re-read of the targets' issues after the grant) and
reconciled; past `ob1.node_projection_bulk_keys` (256) the global lock is
taken exclusively instead. Outside READ COMMITTED every key is rewritten, so a
concurrent writer conflicts. `ob1_node_projection_drift()` (058's formulas
verbatim) and `ob1_rebuild_node_projection()` (the seed, and the repair after a
write with triggers disabled). `node_lifecycle()` and `node_state()` keep their
signatures and rows and read the tables; `node_state` has no top-level WITH,
so a caller's planner pulls it up and drops the dependency joins it does not
read (their tables are then not permission-checked either: the search runs
without SELECT on `thought_sources`). `search_thoughts_hybrid` is estimated at
100 rows. Grants: the capture group gains SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE on
both tables; preflight names what a role without them breaks. The tool's
description, its grant hint, both READMEs, the ADR's `node_state` row and the
bench's arm follow.

**Why.** At 059 `prefer_current` cost `node_state`'s whole-brain read per
call — +10.7 ms at 10,000 thoughts, +129 ms at 100,000 — over the budget it
was pre-registered against. A narrowing that kept the reads met the budget and
was declined for a stored projection, SMD-1997's direction (the ticket holds
its numbers). Fed by the row store, not the log: every writer reaches
`thoughts`, raw ones included, and the log carries no `created_at` move; the
fold later replaces the feed, not the tables.

**Held.** test-schema 1860 — new [56]: the catalog; two seeded sequences of
150 raw statements each (every kind of write, multi-row and whole-table, the
bulk path, rollbacks, successors competing for one target) with drift() empty
and `node_lifecycle()` and `superseded_by` equal to 058's own text after each;
the plan (pulled up, primary-key probes); the grant; the replays; the rebuild;
the rewrite outside READ COMMITTED. [55]: the capture group alone now runs the
search. test-upgrade 340 — [20m]: 060 refused without 058, naming it; applied
over tickets and pointers, `node_state()` row for row what 058's read, the
window guard thirty-one. test-live 771 — [28]: same-ticket writers serialise
and the later recomputes from the earlier's commit; a pointer write re-reads a
target's moving issue; two successors at once; a ticket write on 20,000
thoughts (5,000 of them pointers) scans `thoughts` no times and reads 12 of its
rows; the suite leaves no drift. test-preflight 470 (the projection's writes
missing name ticket writes and lifecycle reads, not captures); test-server's
hint.

**Measured after.** `db/bench-hybrid.ts`'s arm, now interleaved and paired:
`prefer_current` adds +1.05 to +1.86 ms at 10,000 thoughts over three runs,
inside the pre-registered budget (the hybrid's own median) on every one, and
+2.4 to +3.2 ms at 100,000 — about 1.6 of it the hybrid at the 4N window, not
`node_state` (the join itself measured +0.01 to +0.15). Whole-brain
`node_state()` 11.6 → 0.5 ms at 10,000, about 130 → 12–16 ms at 100,000. A
writer, triggers on against off: a plain capture +0.00 to +0.07 ms (budget
0.1); a ticket's status update +0.40 to +0.58 ms at 10,000 and +0.46 to +0.50
at 100,000 against a budget of 0.5 — met on the last run, over on the one
before (and +0.66 before the lookup was skipped for a pointer-less write), so
call it borderline; stamping 40% of the rows −3% and +10% (budget +20%). The
dogfood brain (a read-only dump in a throwaway Postgres, 1,001 thoughts,
migrated to main's 059, then 060 applied: 449 heads, 75 superseders, drift 0):
graph-centrality in 300 modes, `node_state()` and `node_lifecycle()` byte for
byte the same; whole-brain `node_state()` 31 → 0.5 ms, the dependency read
34 → 3–4 ms. Mutants: seventeen, sixteen killed — [56] kills every rule and
trigger mutant (a trigger event dropped, the old key or pointer skipped, the
target lookup skipped on either path, the created_at move, the watermark move,
the pointer target, the superseded term of the head rule, the keyed delete,
heads before superseders), test-live [28] the four concurrency ones (no issue
locks, no superseder locks, no re-read, NULL for "no keys" — the last only once
[28] counted rows read, not just scans); the md5 recheck survives, reachable
only through a collision.

**Not taken.** The narrowing (an ids overload of `node_lifecycle`, a plpgsql
wrapper). A thought_audit fold (the log misses `created_at` moves and
trigger-less loads). One row per thought (a status sync would rewrite every
note under the ticket). SECURITY DEFINER triggers (the repo's policy).

**Follow-ups.** SMD-2267: the dependency columns as a stored fold.
