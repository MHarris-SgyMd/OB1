A write-path eval: twenty fictional sessions with planted facts and planted errors run through the real server with the provider scripted, four deliverables written by a fixed reader, scored as salient survival, error catch by mechanism and citation coverage, paired per mechanism (helped / hurt, McNemar); `evals/baselines.json` gains a `write_path` floor and the data-layer job a keyless gate on it (SMD-1713).

## FORK

The third layer measured — what survives capture into a later deliverable, and which planted errors are caught, by which mechanism; a keyless gate in the data-layer job (SMD-1713)

**What changed.** `evals/eval-write-path.ts` runs `evals/write-path-corpus.ts` — twenty fictional sessions, thirty-six captures by an operator's key and an agent's over twelve fictional subjects, with 27 salient facts, three stale decisions each reversed later, three numbers the operator later mistyped and three unsourced inferences by the agent planted among them, plus the cases where a mechanism should hurt — through the real `server-portable/index.ts` booted in-process against a throwaway Postgres, the provider scripted as `test-chunking.ts` scripts it: one `Bun.serve` answering `/embeddings` (an axis per subject named, a hashed tie-break) and the three chat prompts the write path sends (the capture's metadata, the extractor's entities, the consolidation judge's verdict by one blunt rule: digit runs that differ under one subject conflict). Four arms, one process each — `default`, `-supersedes` (the newer decision never names the older), `-judge` (no extraction, no consolidation pass), `-actor` (the keys never classified) — then a fixed reader searches each deliverable's subjects (k=5), leaves out a hit marked superseded, leaves out an agent's word where the operator's stands, writes a hit in a pending conflict proposal as *contested*, and captures each deliverable with `derived_from` naming the ids it used. `evals/write-path.ts` holds every rule pure: the stub's answers, the parsers, the reader, the scorer, the pairing, the comparison, the corpus's shape rules. `baselines.json` gains `write_path` (the floor as counts and the ids of the items behind them, the corpus's label); `.github/workflows/fork-checks.yml` gains `--self-check` in the portable-server job and `--gate` in the data-layer job.

**Why.** Every number under `evals/` was retrieval, or since SMD-1719 use; nothing measured layer three, and change 90 pointed here for it. The full form the ticket names — a deliverable carrying citations — needs SMD-1715's `capture_deliverable`, not built, and 042's `record_citation`, which no tool calls (SMD-1733); so the reduced form the ticket's Notes allow ran, with a deliverable as what SMD-1719 already scores as a citation: a capture whose `derived_from` names its sources. The corpus is code, not a fixture: check 9 reads every committed fixture as an allowlist with no exception, and an exception would have had to tell fiction from an export by file name; the rule stays whole and the corpus is source, typechecked with the evals.

**Held.** `--self-check` (portable-server job, no database): the corpus's shape rules probed with broken copies — a duplicate text under 003's fingerprint (whitespace runs collapsed), an inference where the operator never spoke (uncatchable), a digit on a subject with a decision pair (029 keeps a superseded thought out of the judge's pool, so the `-supersedes` arm would move the judge's pairs too: a rule, not an accident of the corpus), any conflicting pair whose newer side has more earlier neighbours than the shipped candidate count, two texts of one subject under 1e-6 apart in cosine to the query (a tie falls to the row's id; the query is the bare axis, so the weight alone orders); the stub's three answers against the REAL prompt builders (both prompts name their delimiters in their rules before the wrapped text; the first draft read the rule as the text and extracted nothing); the parsers on rendered blocks, a forged `ID:` line in content included; the reader's five rules; the scorer's partitions (plain / contested / dropped / unseen; an error never retrieved is outside the catch population and outside the pairing); McNemar on hand-known values, a p per population; the comparator: the floor is counts and item ids, so a corpus that grew, an error that stopped being retrieved (the rate stays 1; the population, the unseen ceiling and the id do not), a fact lost for a fact gained (every count kept), a lost citation behind a changed line count (the ratio is compared as well) and the ticket's mutant — one planted fact dropped from its deliverable — each fail by name, and an improvement passes with a note. `--gate` (data-layer job, keyless; one mode at a time): the corpus's rules, the record's label and k, the default arm at or above the recorded counts over the recorded population with every recorded item still right, no more errors unseen than recorded, and the blind reader below both the recorded catch and this run's; a missing cost line is a fault. Live mutants, restored by copy: the reader dropping the first hit of every subject fails survival (9/27); a deliverable captured with no `derived_from` fails coverage (0/26); the reader ignoring the superseded mark fails catch (6/9); the run-it reviews' further mutants — the actor rule dropped, the judge always agreeing or always conflicting, the pointer never written, the keys never classified, the sessions undated, extraction skipped, the blind reader as the default, a worker path broken, a corpus grown by one item, a planted error removed, k set to 1 (the label and the unseen ceiling catch what the rate at 3/3 cannot), a digit on a decision's subject, two texts a bucket apart — each fail on the right rate with the right words.

**Measured after** (the committed corpus, 2026-09-23; deterministic, three gate runs identical): default arm survival 17/27 (63.0%), catch 9/9, coverage 26/26; `-supersedes` catch 6/9; `-judge` survival 23/27, catch 6/9, nothing contested; `-actor` survival 19/27, catch 7/9; the blind reader survival 25/27, catch 0/9. Paired against default, a McNemar p per population: supersedes errors 3/0 (p 0.250), facts 0/0; judge errors 3/0 (0.250), facts 0/6 (0.031) — the mixed p over both is 0.508, two effects in opposite directions cancelling, which is why it is not the number to read; actor errors 2/0 (0.500), facts 0/2 (0.500). Each stale decision rests on the supersedes pointer, each slip on the judge, two inferences on the actor mark and the numbered one on either. Ten facts did not survive: two to the k cut, six contested by the judge (the slips' true twins, the two true facts with different numbers, the fact whose agent inference carried a number), two the agent's true facts dropped where the operator spoke. The judge's hurt is the read's design, not the rule's alone: a pending proposal contests both sides until a reviewer decides (029's never-apply-unreviewed), so the read returns a question where SMD-1735's belief-state read would return the reviewed value plain. Labels are what the catch costs: the blind reader states 25 facts and catches nothing. Coverage is 1.0 by construction of the reader, which always cites what it used; what it holds is the store's side — that `derived_from` comes back as given (035's re-capture path and the capture-key trim would show) — and it is billed as that, not as a measure of the writer.
