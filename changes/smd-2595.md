## Changelog

The seven vendored HTTP servers that read their own URL (`agent-memory-api`, `open-brain-rest`, `rest-api`, `smart-ingest`, and the `bio`, `metadata-norm` and `entity-extraction-worker` workers) now answer a request whose `Host` the URL parser refuses, or that has none, userinfo or a path, as they answer it at `localhost`, where they used to give a 500 (at five of them, to a keyed request only). `agent-memory-api` and `open-brain-rest` no longer leave a request pending for good when a client hangs up mid-upload. Upgrade `integrations/_shared/auth.ts` and `integrations/consolidation-workers/_shared/auth.ts` with these servers: beside v1.5.0's copies each of the seven fails at start (Bun's `SyntaxError: Export named '…' not found`, naming `requestAt`, `routable` or `queryOf`), and `rest-api` and `smart-ingest`, which now import the first, fail without it (SMD-2595).

## FORK

The seven vendored servers that read their URL answer a `Host` the URL parser refuses, or none, as `localhost` gets answered, not with a 500; a hang-up mid-upload no longer leaves a request pending (SMD-2595)

**What changed.**
- **`routable` moved from `root.ts` to `auth.ts`,** beside `queryOf`. `routable` rebuilds a request whose `req.url` will not parse at `http://unparsable-host.invalid`, keeping its path and query, and passes any other as it came; its from-parts constructor is split out as `requestAt(req, url)`. `auth.ts` is the module whose byte-identical copies sit in each vendored tree, so the five `_shared/auth.ts` copies now carry both, and no second synced module was needed. `index.ts` and `api.ts` import it from there; check 25, the tripwire on what the server entries may import, lets `api.ts` import `routable` from `./auth.ts`.
- **`agent-memory-api` and `open-brain-rest`** hand their Hono app `requestAt(routed, url)`: the request after `routable`, at the URL with the path prefix stripped. Before, they parsed the raw URL with `new URL(req.url)`, which threw at a bad `Host` before the key check, and handed on `new Request(url, req)`.
- **`bio`, `metadata-norm` and `entity-extraction-worker`** read `dry_run`, `name` and `limit` through `queryOf(req.url)`; they read nothing else of the URL.
- **`rest-api` and `smart-ingest`** route `routable(req)`. It is the one name they take from `../_shared/auth.ts`; their key is still their own compare, and their READMEs say so.

**Why.** Bun builds `req.url` from the `Host` header without checking it. A `Host` the parser refuses leaves a URL that will not parse; no `Host` (HTTP/1.0), or one with userinfo or a path, leaves the bare request target (`/health`), which will not parse either. Measured on main, served in-process (`agent-memory-api` and `rest-api` also as `bun <file>`) and asked over a raw socket at `x:99999`, `[::1`, `brain.example.test:abc`, `::1:8000`, no `Host` and userinfo:
- `agent-memory-api` and `open-brain-rest` gave a 500 to every request, the keyless 401 and `/health` included;
- the other five gave a 500 to every keyed request, while their keyless 401 held.

Traefik forwards the first four Hosts as sent (SMD-2535). The stack's proxy routes none of the seven, but an operator's own Traefik in front of one would let the 500s through.

The hang-up was a second fault in the same entry. `new Request(url, req)` copies a body that never settles when the client leaves mid-upload, as measured in SMD-2535. So every route of `agent-memory-api` and `open-brain-rest` that reads a body (`POST /writeback` and `POST /capture` among them) left a request pending for the life of the process each time a client hung up, even at `Host: localhost`. Such a hang-up now ends the handler with an `AbortError`. Hono's default error handler logs it at `agent-memory-api` and at `open-brain-rest`'s `POST /thought/:id/reflection`, as `rest-api` already logged its own; `open-brain-rest`'s other body routes catch it into an unlogged 500, and its `/ingest` reads it as an empty body.

**Not here.**
- **`open-brain-rest`'s route handlers** still call `new URL(c.req.url)`. Behind the entry every such URL parses, so they are left as they are.
- **Three vendored Hono servers that misroute a bare target** (SMD-2614): `ob-graph`, `work-operating-model-activation` and `meal-planning/shared-server.ts` export `app.fetch` raw. They parse no URL, so no 500, but with no `Host` or a userinfo `Host` Hono reads the bare target's path wrong and their own routes are missed (`GET /health` a 401 or a 405, `POST /mcp` a 404). That happens only off the proxy. Nine more export `app.fetch` raw but route by wildcard alone, so nothing there is missed.
- **The authorization server** (SMD-2615): `deploy/auth/server.ts` throws uncaught on an absolute-form target with a bad authority, through `node:http`, and the process exits; reachability through Traefik is unmeasured.
- **The internal servers**: `deploy/orchestration/runner.ts`, `provision.ts`, `jev/serve.ts` and the `evals/` stubs parse `req.url` too, and so does `recipes/email-history-import`'s one-shot loopback OAuth callback. None is a public route.

**Held by**
- **extensions/test-auth.ts** (33 new, 818 in all; not server-portable's test-auth), in a new section. Each of the seven servers is served in-process, and each request is sent at `Host: localhost`, the control, and at seven others: the six above and a `Host` with a path.
  - **21 rows** (three per server) need every Host to get the control's status, and every answer to hold the word that names the route the control reached. That catches a prefix not stripped, a route missed and a dry run that read no query.
  - **12 rows**, one per Host (`localhost`, `x:99999`, none) at each of the four servers that read a body, have the client hang up mid-upload. Each needs one request pending as it leaves and none after.
