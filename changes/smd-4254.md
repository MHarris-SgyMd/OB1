## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, a thought whose embedding crashes the provider is now recorded failed at its third outage alone, and the backlog behind it is re-embedded in the same period: re-embed waits out an outage that made the thought in hand a suspect, probing every 5 s, up to 2 minutes a wait and 4 minutes of isolation a period — its waits and the retries between them — rather than being set down for the period with its suspects forgotten (SMD-4254). A crashing thought is failed in one period if the provider is back under about 80 s after each crash, and its backlog re-embedded too under about 60 s; the claim passes are held 4 minutes and a poll at most a period from the first wait's start. One that outlasts the limits backs re-embed off by time: such an outage met in the next 4 minutes sets it down at once, as before, then 8, 16, 32, then an hour at each between tries, its suspects moved behind the pool so the backlog pooled before is re-embedded meanwhile, until re-embed drains or a thought is recorded failed alone; a live write's pause counts toward neither limit. No migration.

## Record

Re-embed under the sleep scheduler waits out an outage that made the thought in hand a suspect — the thought crashing the provider is failed and the backlog behind it re-embedded, not stalled every period (SMD-4254)

**Why.** SMD-2641 PR 2 has re-embed's `--follow` follower retry an outage's suspects one at a time and fail a thought that crashes the provider at its third outage alone. Under `db/sleep.ts`, the deployed path, the poll that saw re-embed waiting mid-pass ended it and set it down for the period (`setReembedDown("outage")`), and the run's `ProviderOutage` went with it, suspects and counts. The suspect was handed back at the pool's front, so each period's first claim crashed the provider again and the period re-embedded nothing. SMD-2641 PR 2's review pass 1 measured it under the real scheduler: no isolated drain ever started, the thought stayed pending, and from the second or third period each re-embedded 0 of its backlog, on main as on that branch.

**Decided.** The maintainer's choice (2026-11-18): don't set re-embed down on an outage that made suspects; let the run wait it out and isolate, as extract and consolidate already do under the scheduler. An outage with no suspect (a 429, a 503, the model loading) and backpressure keep the set-down. The bound is the build's: re-embed runs alone at a period's start, so an unbounded wait would hold the claim passes for the period whenever the embedding endpoint is really down with a thought in hand. Review pass 1 found the bound a wait's while the probe schedule carried across an isolation's waits, and no limit on a period's waits; the maintainer's decision (2026-11-18): under the scheduler, probe every 5 s from each wait's start, no doubling and no carry; keep the 2-minute bound a wait and cap a period's such waits at 4 minutes, begun again each period. Review pass 2 found the cap counting the waits alone — re-embed runs alone between them too, the retry's call and its blame probe, two OB1_LLM_TIMEOUTs a round with a provider that hangs (about 16 minutes a period at the defaults) — and a crasher whose crash outlasts the limits costing the cap every period, where main set it down in a poll. The maintainer's decision (2026-11-18): the cap counts the whole isolation from the first wait's start, the waits and the retries and probes between them, and sets re-embed down between waits too; and a period set down at a limit without the thought failed backs off later periods — set down at once at such an outage, as main does, skipping 1 period, then 2, 4, …, a try between, reset when re-embed drains or a thought is recorded failed alone, for the scheduler's life. Review pass 3 found the back-off counted in periods, which are capture-driven — about 30 s apart on a busy brain: each period backed off claimed the crashing thought first, handed back at the pool's front, and crashed the provider again, so the periods after it met the provider still down and were set down at once, using up the skip without a try — a crash of 80 s or more held the claim passes 40-63% of the time over 35 minutes (main 16.7%), and a shortened crash waited out the skip count (370 s in one run); and a live write's pause counted toward the cap, setting re-embed down at the resume with nothing in hand. The maintainer's decision (2026-11-18): keep the waiting design and patch it — back off by time, 4 minutes from a try's limit, then 8, 16, …, reset as before; move the outage's suspects behind the pool at a limit's set-down and a backed-off one, as the cap-between abort's set-back does; and stop the limits' clock while the period's gate is closed. Review pass 4 found the window doubling with no ceiling — after about ten limits the next try was some 68 hours away; within the maintainer's back-off decision, it is held at an hour, the hourly rescan's scale.

**Limits.** A crashing thought takes four waits under the scheduler — its batch's outage, two alone, then the outage it leaves once recorded failed — so with the 4-minute cap on the isolation it is failed in one period only if the provider is back under about 80 s after each crash, and its backlog re-embedded in that period too only under about 60 s; the retries between the waits count, so a provider slow to fail them lowers those edges. Longer crashes are set down at the cap (or the 2-minute bound), the thought retried at a later period, forgotten as before, and re-embed backs off by time — such an outage set down at once for 4 minutes after a try's limit, then 8, 16, 32, then an hour at each (review pass 4's ceiling), its suspects moved behind the backlog pooled before the set-down — so a crash that ends between about 80 and 120 s is tried at 0, 8, 20, 40, … minutes, each try held to the 4-minute cap; one longer than the 2-minute bound that ends, at 0, 6, 16, 34, … minutes, each held to the bound, plus the wait for the provider to come back; one that never ends, once, held to the bound, every period after finding the provider down at its start; and past the ceiling a try comes at most an hour after the last one's limit, a poll a period otherwise. With a provider that hangs a try costs about 6 minutes when it hangs for good (two OB1_LLM_TIMEOUTs and the bound) and about 8 for a hang of about 240-360 s (the two and the cap). Thoughts captured after the move land behind the crashing thought, so under a steady inflow each period still claims it before that period's new captures, a new note waiting about one crash length (a median 120-228 s measured); on an idle brain the move pays at the next rescan. The back-off spares the claim passes, not the provider: a crasher outlasting the limits crashes it every crash length, as on main (about 24 an hour at 150 s, 40 at 90 s). Over 60 minutes at the shipped settings with a steady capture every 30 s (review pass 4's run lane), the claim passes were held 27.4% of the time with a 150 s crash (48, 33, 17, 33, 17, 17% by 10 minutes) and 38.4% with a 90 s one (57, 42, 49, 17, 49, 17%), main 16.7% throughout, the backlog re-embedded meanwhile (main none); on an idle brain a try comes only with the hourly rescan, 5.5-10.7% held. A crash shortened meanwhile is failed at the first try after the back-off in hand ends, which can be up to its length away; preflight's remedy names that case, and its fix the back-off. The back-off is the scheduler's: a restart tries at once. While re-embed isolates such an outage the claim passes wait too, 4 minutes and a poll at most a period from the first wait's start; what comes before that wait — the call that met the outage, and its probe, each up to OB1_LLM_TIMEOUT with a provider that hangs — is as before SMD-4254. A set-down between waits aborts the retry's call or probe in hand and sets its thought back in the queue, behind the backlog. A crash of a few seconds costs about 20 s once. An endpoint simply down, first met mid-pass with a 5xx, a refused or reset connection or a timeout, makes a suspect and so costs up to the 2-minute bound once (a 300-thought backlog's extraction took a median 132-137 s against 12 s on main in the review's run), before the next period's start finds it down and sets re-embed down at once.
