## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, a thought whose embedding crashes the provider is now recorded failed at its third outage alone, and the backlog behind it is re-embedded in the same period: re-embed waits out an outage a thought's call brought down, up to two minutes, rather than being set down for the period with its suspects forgotten (SMD-4254). No migration.

## Record

Re-embed under the sleep scheduler waits out an outage a thought brought down — the thought crashing the provider is failed and the backlog behind it re-embedded, not stalled every period (SMD-4254)

**Why.** SMD-2641 PR 2 has re-embed's `--follow` follower retry an outage's suspects one at a time and fail a thought that crashes the provider at its third outage alone. Under `db/sleep.ts`, the deployed path, the poll that saw re-embed waiting mid-pass ended it and set it down for the period (`setReembedDown("outage")`), and the run's `ProviderOutage` went with it, suspects and counts. The suspect was handed back at the pool's front, so each period's first claim crashed the provider again and the period re-embedded nothing. SMD-2641 PR 2's review pass 1 measured it under the real scheduler: no isolated drain ever started, the thought stayed pending, and from the second or third period each re-embedded 0 of its backlog, on main as on that branch.

**Decided.** The maintainer's choice (2026-11-18): don't set re-embed down on an outage that made suspects; let the run wait it out and isolate, as extract and consolidate already do under the scheduler. An outage with no suspect (a 429, a 503, the model loading) and backpressure keep the set-down. The bound is the build's: re-embed runs alone at a period's start, so an unbounded wait would hold the claim passes for the period whenever the embedding endpoint is really down with a thought in hand.

**What changed.**

- `db/worker-bootstrap.ts`: `ProviderOutage.byThought` says whether the outage in progress is a thought's doing — begun or joined over an error that made a suspect, a suspect's outage alone (`blame`), or the outage a suspect blamed at its third leaves (`begin`'s new `blamed`, passed by `db/reembed.ts` and `db/claim-loop.ts`, since that one makes no suspect). An outage begun while isolating that meets none of the kept suspects is not. `waitOutProvider` passes it with the wait: `stamper.providerWait(true, byThought)`.
- `db/pass-stamp.ts`: `PassStamper.providerWait(on, byThought?)`. A follower's own stamper and the subject sweep's call leave it out.
- `db/sleep.ts`: the per-pass stamper keeps, for re-embed, when such a wait began, taken as its word arrives. The re-embed poll leaves a mid-pass wait on a thought's outage running, said once a wait, up to `REEMBED_THOUGHT_WAIT_MS` (120 s, beside `DEFAULT_POLL_S`, its comment saying why) from that start. Past it, it says so and sets re-embed down as before. A wait at the start, or on an outage no thought made, is set down at the first poll as before. `reembedThoughtWaitMs` is the test's override. Extract's and consolidate's handling is unchanged.
- `server-portable/preflight.ts`: the `outage` set-down's remedy names a thought that crashes the provider for longer than the wait as the cause of one that comes back every period, and how to find it: the sleep log's "goes back to the pool unrecorded" line naming the same thought at each period's start.
- `db/README.md`'s scheduler section and its "The thought's own fault" bullet, and `db/sleep.ts`'s docblocks, say the rule and the bound.

**Held.**

- test-live **[38p]** (new, the long cell): a hemlock thought pooled first, five vectorless thoughts behind it, the stub's provider down until two probes find it so. Under one `--follow` scheduler run at a 250 ms poll and 1 s outage waits, the thought is recorded failed "at 3 outages" on its fourth crash. All five are re-embedded, "re-embed drained" is said, and no set-down is. A nightshade thought that keeps the provider down, at a 3 s bound: re-embed is left waiting about the bound, then set down `outage`, said. Extraction runs on, and the thought stays pending.
- test-live [38r]'s 503 row also asserts the wait is not left running: a 503 makes no suspect.
- `db/test-worker-bootstrap.ts`: eight rows on `byThought` through an isolated outage's life.
- test-preflight [5b]: the idle `outage` set-down's fix names the crashing thought and the log line.
- Mutants, each with the files backed up outside the tree and restored to their sha256: the poll's new branch dropped (always set down) fails both [38p] rows; the condition inverted fails [38r]'s 503 row and both [38p] rows; the third-alone outage not marked (`begin(why)`) fails [38p]'s first row, its backlog 0 of 5 re-embedded; `blame`'s mark dropped fails the unit row.

**Not taken.** Keeping one `ProviderOutage` for the scheduler's life (the ticket's first option): the maintainer chose the wait.

**Limits.** A thought that keeps the provider down past two minutes still sets re-embed down at each period's first claim, forgotten as before; preflight's remedy names that case. While re-embed waits on such an outage the claim passes wait too, two minutes at most a wait. A provider that crashes on a thought each time costs one wait per crash, four in all, each ended by its probe.
