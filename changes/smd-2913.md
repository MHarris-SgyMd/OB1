## FORK

A finding's first take-back since its post decides what became of it — migration 091 keeps every settle by the pass (SMD-2913)

**What changed.**
- **Migration 091.** `supersession_proposals.pass_settled_at timestamptz[]` holds each time the pass settled the row (067's `settled by the pass:` rejection), oldest first. Its one writer is the BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE trigger `supersession_proposals_keep_settles`. The trigger appends `reviewed_at` when the row becomes settled and otherwise carries the column over: through 067's reopen (which clears `reviewed_at`), 063's replace in place, a person's review, and a caller's own value. Rows settled at the upgrade are backfilled; a settle already reopened left no time. No grant: the trigger writes the row being written, and 087's table stays insert-only.
- **The read (`server-portable/brain-info.ts`).**
  - An outdates finding is taken back once the pass has settled every proposal it named that is still present at least once since the post, at the latest of those first settles. A settle from before the post was of an earlier verdict and does not count.
  - A relation finding is taken back at the first close since the post that no relation of its word outlived, on any of the named row pairs. A successor written in the closing transaction starts at the close's instant, so a re-judge at the same word is no take-back. The facets already keep this history (084: only ever closed), so relations need no migration.
  - `findingOutcome` puts the take-back ahead of open, so a finding taken back within the window stays withdrawn after a reopen, a re-judge that finds the conflict again, or the relation said again. An acceptance, a person's rejection and a link still come first. Past the window it is unanswered, as before.
  - `FindingRow.withdrawnS` covers both kinds; `proposals.settledS` and the unused `settled` count are gone.
- **`board-findings.ts --report`** names what the judge did since: "(reopened since)" or "(said again since)".
- **Docs:** findingOutcome's JSDoc, db/README.md (091's paragraph, the map, the count, "What became of it"), and server-portable/README.md's `Findings`.

**Why.** SMD-2682 review pass 3 found that a finding the pass took back on day 5 read open again after any ticket edit (ticket texts carry `Status:`, `Parent:` and `Labels:`), and unanswered once 30 days old. It could only understate the rate.

**Choice of record.** The ticket offered a `withdrawn_at` column on `board_findings_posted`, stamped either by triggers on proposals and facets or by board-sync each pass. Either would need UPDATE on a table that is insert-only by design, and grants for every role that settles or closes. Keeping the settles on the proposal row needs neither, and the relation history was already there.
