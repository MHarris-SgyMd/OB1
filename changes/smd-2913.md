## Changelog

A board finding the consolidation pass took back within the 30-day window now stays withdrawn when a later ticket edit reopens its proposal and the pass settles it again, instead of reading open and then unanswered. A re-judge that finds the conflict again, or a relation said again, still reads as the judge asserting it once more. Migration 091 keeps every settle by the pass on its proposal (SMD-2913).

## FORK

A settle by the pass survives 067's reopen — migration 091 keeps every settle, and a posted finding reads by what the judge last asserted (SMD-2913)

**What changed.**
- **Migration 091.** `supersession_proposals.pass_settled_at timestamptz[]` holds each time the pass settled the row (067's `settled by the pass:` rejection), oldest first. Its one writer is the BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE trigger `supersession_proposals_keep_settles`. The trigger appends `reviewed_at` when the row becomes settled and otherwise carries the column over: through 067's reopen (which clears `reviewed_at`), 063's replace in place, a person's review, and a caller's own value. Rows settled at the upgrade are backfilled; a settle already reopened left no time. No grant: the trigger writes the row being written, and 087's table stays insert-only. The header says a data-only reload with triggers enabled rebuilds the column from each row's status.
- **The read (`server-portable/brain-info.ts`).**
  - An outdates finding is taken back once the pass has settled every proposal it named that is still present, each by its first settle since its `judged_at`, at the latest of those. `judged_at` moves only when a re-judge finds the conflict again (063's replace in place). That is the judge asserting the finding once more, so a settle before it no longer counts, while a reopen by a text move leaves the settle standing.
  - The poster posts pending rows only, so a settle since `judged_at` came after the finding was chosen. One made before its comment was posted counts, at 0.
  - `findingOutcome` ranks the take-back ahead of open, which matters only for a stale proposal: a pending one carries no take-back.
  - Relations read as on main: a relation said again stands and reads open. A relation has no reopen, so the ticket's relation case was the judge's verdict, not a lost take-back.
  - The Findings row reads `? (migration 091 is not applied)` on a brain without the column.
- **`board-findings.ts --report`** adds "(reopened since)" to a take-back whose proposal is stale again, and names 091 (exit 2) on a brain without it.
- **Docs:** findingOutcome's JSDoc, db/README.md (091's paragraph, the map, the count, "What became of it"), and server-portable/README.md's `Findings`.

**Why.** SMD-2682 review pass 3 found that a finding the pass took back on day 5 read open again after any ticket edit (ticket texts carry `Status:`, `Parent:` and `Labels:`), and unanswered once 30 days old, understating the rate.

**Choice of record.** The ticket offered a `withdrawn_at` column on `board_findings_posted`, stamped either by triggers on proposals and facets or by board-sync each pass. Either needs UPDATE on a table that is insert-only by design, and grants for every role that settles. Keeping the settles on the proposal row needs neither.
