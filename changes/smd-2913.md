## Changelog

A board finding the consolidation pass took back within the 30-day window now stays withdrawn when a later ticket edit reopens its proposal and the pass settles it again, instead of reading open and then unanswered. A re-judge that finds the conflict again, or a relation said again, still reads as the judge asserting it once more. Migration 091 keeps every settle by the pass on its proposal (SMD-2913).

## FORK

A settle by the pass survives 067's reopen — migration 091 keeps every settle, and a posted finding reads by what the judge last asserted (SMD-2913)

**What changed.**
- **Migration 091.** `supersession_proposals.pass_settled_at timestamptz[]` holds each time the pass settled the row (067's `settled by the pass:` rejection) since the judge last asserted it, oldest first. Its one writer is the BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE trigger `supersession_proposals_keep_settles`; a value a caller writes is replaced. The trigger:
  - appends `reviewed_at` when the row becomes settled;
  - empties the column when `judged_at` moves or a stale row turns pending (063's replace in place: a re-judge found the conflict again, the judge saying it again — the status catches a replace in the transaction that last wrote `judged_at`, where `now()` is unchanged);
  - otherwise keeps it, through 067's reopen (which clears `reviewed_at`) and a person's review.

  The assertion is read from the write itself, never by comparing times two transactions wrote, so two passes at once cannot misorder them. Rows settled at the upgrade are backfilled; a settle already reopened left no time. No grant: the trigger writes the row being written, and 087's table stays insert-only. The header says a data-only reload with triggers enabled rebuilds the column from each row's status.
- **The read (`server-portable/brain-info.ts`).**
  - An outdates finding is taken back once the pass has settled every proposal it named that is still present, each by its first settle in the record, at the latest of those. The poster posts pending rows only, so a settle in the record came after the finding was chosen; one made before its comment was posted counts, at 0.
  - The proposals partition into accepted, rejected (by a person), settled, reopened (stale again after a settle in the record), stale (waiting on its re-judge) and pending. A reopened proposal is taken back: it neither keeps a finding open nor holds off a person's rejection beside it.
  - Relations read as on main: a relation said again stands and reads open. A relation has no reopen, so the ticket's relation case was the judge's verdict, not a lost take-back.
  - The window still runs from the post (the tracker's "a month of posted findings"): a finding the judge asserts again late in it leaves the board less time, and past it reads unanswered at once. The docs say so.
  - The Findings row reads `? (migration 091 is not applied)` on a brain without the column.
- **`board-findings.ts --report`** adds "(reopened since)" to a take-back with a reopened proposal, says "the pass settled its proposal" for a present proposal with no time kept (never "gone"), and exits 2 naming 091 when the table on the role's search path lacks the column.
- **Docs:** findingOutcome's JSDoc, db/README.md (091's paragraph, the map, the count, "What became of it"), and server-portable/README.md's `Findings`.

**Why.** SMD-2682 review pass 3 found that a finding the pass took back on day 5 read open again after any ticket edit (ticket texts carry `Status:`, `Parent:` and `Labels:`), and unanswered once 30 days old, understating the rate.

**Choice of record.** The ticket offered a `withdrawn_at` column on `board_findings_posted`, stamped either by triggers on proposals and facets or by board-sync each pass. Either needs UPDATE on a table that is insert-only by design, and grants for every role that settles. Keeping the settles on the proposal row needs neither.
