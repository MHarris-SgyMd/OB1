## FORK

Fork Checks' full-stack job split three ways by what each group of steps stands up — every step kept, each on a stack of its own (SMD-3051)

**What changed.**
- **The bring-up** ("Bring up the compose stack": four scoped keys into `$GITHUB_ENV`, the provider stub on the runner's :11434, `deploy/.env`, compose's up) moved byte for byte into `.github/ci/full-stack-up.sh`, which each job runs.
- **`deploy-stack`, "Full stack, no Supabase"** keeps its name, so a PR's required check is the one it was. It keeps the stack's own checks: the `.env` read, the profiles' refusals, ports, networks, Bun and the proxy's route tables, `smoke.sh`, the REST core, the plugin webhook, `/grafana`, pg_stat_statements, the tier routes, the dashboards and the SvelteKit smoke, the Docker-daemon restart and the SIGTERM stop.
- **`deploy-stack-brains`, "Full stack: a second brain, the tier refresh and the canary"**: the second brain, the tier refresh, and the canary's refusals and life. The tier step logs the search the canary's `--diff` replays, so the two stay together.
- **`deploy-stack-auth`, "Full stack: the import runner and the authorization server"**: the Kubernetes image and its stop, the import runner's image, role and egress, the authorization server, and the proxy's discovery, log and stream step. That last step reads the MCP server the auth step recreated for an origin, as it did when it ran after it.
- **One step is new.** The brains job writes into `deploy/.env` what the auth step left there: the four settings that configure the authorization server, and `auth/provision.ts --init`'s secrets. The canary step holds `canary.sh` to leaving every `OB1_AUTH_` line out of the canary's file, so stable's file has to carry them. Nothing starts the authorization server there.
- Comments that named a step now in another job say where it is. Each `Logs on failure` prints the canary's logs only in the job that runs one. The ruleset record requires the two new names, and check 35 excuses the two new job ids, as it does `deploy-stack`.

**Why.** The job's median went from 318 s (runs before 2026-10-13) to 471 s, mostly from new steps. Once SMD-3048 shards test-live (about 300 s for its longest shard), this job alone would hold the run's median near 500 s.

**Held.**
- **Step comparison.** A comparison of main's job against the three found every one of its 26 steps in exactly one job: checkout, Bun, the bring-up, the log and the teardown in each. Every step keeps main's order within its job. The only `run` bodies that differ are two comment lines and one message naming the auth step, and the canary log lines dropped from two jobs. The script also holds the old bring-up body verbatim.
- **Check 20** fails without the two ruleset entries, and **check 35** fails without the two excusals: each naming what is missing, by mutant.
- actionlint 1.7.7 (with its shellcheck 0.10.0) passes, and so does shellcheck on the script.

**Measured after.** To fill from the PR's first green run: each job's time, against 471 s.

**Not taken.**
- **An aggregator job** under the old name. Check 20 already requires every job, so it would add a job and nothing else.
- **YAML anchors** for the shared steps. actionlint is pinned at 1.7.7.
- **A local composite action.** Check 23 pins every `uses:` to a commit.
- **Building the images once** for the three jobs. Each job's build is inside its 34 s bring-up, so measure first.

**Follow-ups.** The live ruleset is re-applied after the merge (`gh api -X PUT repos/MHarris-SgyMd/OB1/rulesets/22189960 --input .github/rulesets/main.json`). Until then the two new jobs run but are not required.
