Fork Checks' full-stack job is three jobs, each on a stack of its own: the stack's own checks, a second brain with the tier refresh and the canary, and the import runner with the authorization server; the bring-up they share is `.github/ci/full-stack-up.sh` (SMD-3051).

## FORK

Fork Checks' full-stack job split three ways by what each group of steps stands up — every step kept, each on a stack of its own (SMD-3051)

**What changed.**
- **The bring-up** ("Bring up the compose stack": four scoped keys into `$GITHUB_ENV`, the provider stub on the runner's :11434, `deploy/.env`, compose's up) moved into `.github/ci/full-stack-up.sh`, which each job runs. Its code is unchanged; its comments name the job each later step is in.
- **`deploy-stack`, "Full stack, no Supabase"** keeps its name, so a PR's required check is the one it was. It keeps the stack's own checks:
  - the `.env` read, the profiles' refusals, ports, networks, Bun and the proxy's route tables;
  - the import runner's database role, which runs on migrate's image and ran before pg_stat_statements' "no role statement" check on main;
  - `smoke.sh`, against a stack configured for no authorization server (the default, which no job held before), then the REST core, the plugin webhook, `/grafana`, pg_stat_statements and the tier routes;
  - the dashboards and the SvelteKit smoke, the Docker-daemon restart and the SIGTERM stop.
- **`deploy-stack-brains`, "Full stack: a second brain, the tier refresh and the canary"**: the second brain and SMD-2876's edge proxy in front of both brains (which uses the second brain and removes it), then `smoke.sh`, the tier refresh, and the canary's refusals and life. The tier step and the smoke log the two searches the canary's `--diff` replays, so they stay together.
- **`deploy-stack-auth`, "Full stack: the import runner and the authorization server"**: the Kubernetes image and its stop, the import runner's image and egress, the authorization server, and the proxy's discovery, log and stream step. That last step reads the MCP server the auth step recreated for an origin, as it did when it ran after it.
- **One step is new, in the other two jobs.** It goes where the authorization server's step ran on main, and leaves behind what that step left:
  - `deploy/.env` configured for it (`.github/ci/auth-settings.sh`, which the auth step runs too);
  - the MCP server recreated on that `.env`;
  - no authorization server running.
  
  So the steps after it meet the stack they met on main. In deploy-stack-brains, `smoke.sh` takes its configured-but-not-advertising branch and `canary.sh` has `OB1_AUTH_` lines to leave out of the canary's file. In deploy-stack, the REST core, the webhook, the daemon restart and the stop meet the configured server. Both scripts in `.github/ci/` refuse to run outside Actions, since each writes `deploy/.env`.
- **Smaller changes:**
  - Comments that named a step now in another job say where it is.
  - Each `Logs on failure` prints the canary's logs only in the job that runs one.
  - Workflow lint shellchecks `.github/ci/*.sh`, since actionlint reads only `run:` bodies.
  - The ruleset record requires the two new names, and check 35 excuses the two new job ids, as it does `deploy-stack`.

**Why.** The job's median went from 318 s (runs before 2026-10-13) to 471 s, mostly from new steps. Once SMD-3048 shards test-live (about 300 s for its longest shard), this job alone would hold the run's median near 500 s.

**Held.**
- **Step comparison.** A comparison of main's job against the three found each of its 38 steps at 91840d15 (36 named, 2 `uses:`) in the new jobs, and none lost:
  - **In each job:** checkout, Bun, the bring-up, the log and the teardown.
  - **In two jobs:** the smoke, in deploy-stack and in deploy-stack-brains.
  - **Exactly once:** every other step.
  - **Order:** each job keeps main's order.
  - **`run` bodies that differ:** the auth step's settings lines, which became its call of `auth-settings.sh`; two comment lines and one message naming the auth step; and the canary log lines dropped from two jobs.
  - The script holds the old bring-up body's every line, in order. Only its comments changed, to name the job each later step is in.
- **Check 20** fails without the two ruleset entries, and **check 35** fails without the two excusals: each naming what is missing, by mutant.
- actionlint 1.7.7 (with its shellcheck 0.10.0) passes, and so does shellcheck on both scripts.

**Not taken.**
- **An aggregator job** under the old name. Check 20 already requires every job, so it would add a job and nothing else.
- **YAML anchors** for the shared steps. actionlint is pinned at 1.7.7.
- **A local composite action.** Check 23 pins every `uses:` to a commit.
- **Building the images once** for the three jobs. Each job's build is inside its 34 s bring-up, the repository is public, so the extra builds cost no minutes, and a save/load hand-off adds a job every run would wait on. Measure first.

Every other open PR must merge `main` under the strict policy anyway, which brings the two jobs to it.
