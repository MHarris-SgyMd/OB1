## Changelog

Migration 058 adds `node_state`, one SQL read of a thought's lifecycle, blockers and supersession for every ranking surface — `node_lifecycle()`, `node_dependencies()`, `node_state(ids)` and the two status sets — and `db/graph-centrality.ts`'s `--status`, `--startable` and `--decay-blocked` read it, their reports byte for byte what they were; every mode now needs 058 (SMD-2074).

## FORK

node_state: a thought's lifecycle, blockers and supersession are one SQL read every ranking surface shares, and graph-centrality is its first reader (SMD-2074)

**What changed.** `db/migrations/058_node_state.sql`, five functions, all
`LANGUAGE sql`, SECURITY INVOKER, no SET, not STRICT (a caller's planner inlines
them; string bodies, so a reset drops them in any order):
`node_lifecycle_types()` and `node_settled_types()` (IMMUTABLE: the six status
types this schema knows and the two that settle a node, written once);
`node_lifecycle()` (per thought: status, status_type, the source watermark
`synced_at`, created_at — SMD-1994's ticket-head rule, moved verbatim; it reads
`thoughts` alone); `node_dependencies()` (one row per `blocks` / `blocked_by`
facet, open or closed — system, blocked and blocker identities, `active`,
`changed_at`, and whether the system gates, SMD-2218's own-row rule);
`node_state(p_ids)` (every thought, or those named: the lifecycle beside `open`
— known and not settled, NULL when unknown — `blocked`, `blockers` — the
ticket's open blockers from gating active links, kept on a settled thought as
raw fact — `unknown_blockers`, `in_dependencies` and `superseded_by`, the
newest superseder by the rule the server's `supersededAmong` labels with).
Coverage and freshness are columns, not a count function: each consumer counts
over what it ranks. `graph-centrality.ts`: `LIFECYCLE_CTE` reads
`node_lifecycle()`, and under either dependency flag `STATE_CTE` reads
`node_state()` instead; `dependencySql` is gone. `weightsSql` binds one
parameter (the kept types), the known and settled sets being 058's.
`coverage()` counts from the same rows and `node_dependencies()`. A new exported
`schemaProblem()` refuses (exit 2) a brain without 016 or 058, or whose 058's
status sets are not the script's `LIFECYCLE_TYPES` / `LIFECYCLE_FILTERS.done`.
`metadata.status_type` stays a transitional, lossy scalar: SMD-1997's fold of
`thought_audit`'s transitions replaces `node_lifecycle()`'s body and no
signature (`docs/event-log-as-truth.md`'s projection row says so now).

**Why.** SMD-1994, SMD-2061/2181 and SMD-2218 built the lifecycle and
startability reads as SQL private to one CLI, and attention is not
actionability in search either. The server cannot import a `db/` script — its
image carries `db/config.mjs` and `db/version.mjs` alone — and its PostgREST
store reaches only RPCs, so a second consumer would have copied the joins. The
one definition both can call is SQL.

**Behaviour.** Every report and JSON is what it was (Held, Measured after).
Two things move: every mode needs 058 — a brain without it is exit 2 naming
the migration, where the default modes ran on 016 alone — and the default
modes still read `thoughts` alone (`node_lifecycle()`), so a role without the
`structure` group's `thought_sources` runs them; the dependency flags read
`node_state()`, which needs it, as they needed it before.

**Held.** `test-schema.ts` 1832 (1817 before). [44] asks `node_state` directly
beside the reports on the same fixture, the assertions landed against the old
CTEs first and passing there: `open` per thought and `node_lifecycle()` equal to
node_state's lifecycle columns both ways; `superseded_by` — t1 over tPrev, a
later second superseder named instead, a same-`created_at` tie to the greater
id; per ticket `blocked`, `blockers` and `unknown_blockers` (tX's SMD-7999),
the Done tD unblocked with its blocker kept, the seven `in_dependencies`,
`node_dependencies()`'s seven facets, six active, the board gating; jira not
gating until a row states a status, then tJ1 blocked by `jira:PROJ-2`; a named
read equal to the whole read's rows; a closed facet moving the line's freshness.
The SQL-text control now says the no-flag text reads `node_lifecycle()` and no
`node_state`, link facet or source row, `weightsSql` binds one parameter under
every flag, and the lifecycle counts are one set under all four filters with
and without either flag. New [54]: the five signatures and their columns in
order (the contract search is written against), volatility and the inlining
properties, `EXPLAIN` showing no function scan, the SQL sets equal to the
script's, `node_state('{}')` and `('{NULL}')` empty, a role without `structure`
rendering every no-flag report exactly as the owner does and refused on
`thought_sources` under `--startable` and `--decay-blocked`, and
`schemaProblem` on a dropped `node_state` and on skewed known and settled sets.
`test-upgrade.ts` [20k] (325): 058 on a pre-025 schema names 025, on 025
without 053 names 053, and applies once both are there; the window guard is
twenty-nine. `test-support.ts` drops the five. 27 mutants of 058 and the
script each fail an assertion — among them the heads' supersession term, the
settled filter, `active` always true, a borrowed gate, the direction swapped,
`superseded_by` oldest or tie-broken low, `triage` dropped, STRICT / SET /
VOLATILE on `node_state`, `p_ids` ignored, the no-flag path on `node_state`, a
stale known slot in `coverage()`, and either check in `schemaProblem` dropped.

**Measured after.** The drop-in, live (2026-09-25): the dogfood brain dumped
read-only into a throwaway Postgres (904 thoughts, 171 dependency facets, all
`linear`, gating). Before `migrate.ts` the branch's script exits 2 naming 058;
after it, main's `graph-centrality.ts` and this one render 300 modes — five
subjects (none, two names, a hub, a fuzzy near-miss) × fifteen flag sets
(`--status` × either dependency flag, the decays, `--no-edges`,
`--keep-numeric`) × text and `--json` × two limits — byte for byte, exit codes
included. Again with a gating `jira` (an unknown and a settled blocker beside an
open one), an ungated `acme` and a closed-only `zeta` added: 300 more, the
same. Timing, mean of five: `--status open --json` 1.11 s on main, 1.01 s here;
`--status open --decay-blocked --json` 1.06 s and 1.03 s.

**Not taken.** A view (none ships; it needs a grant row and cannot take ids).
A coverage function: the default modes could not call one without reading
`thought_sources`, and search counts over its candidates, not the brain. One
function for everything: the default path must stay on `thoughts`.

role needs SELECT on `thought_sources` (a `ROLE_GRANTS` row), and `synced_at` is

`node_lifecycle_types()`. Not built: entity-level state and `derived_from` in
