## Changelog

Effective priority orders by level first (SMD-4177, migration 127): an item's level decides its place, and urgency, overdue and what it unblocks (now +0.4, +0.2, +0.6, +0.1 or +0.2) order items of one level without lifting one past the next. An overdue note with no stated level no longer outranks an Urgent ticket, and a High due tomorrow (3.4) passes `min_priority` at High, not at Urgent.
**Upgrading:** the agenda's order and search's `priority_weight` and `min_priority` move with it. A caller who wants the old order passes the old weights by name (`soon` 2, `week` 1, `overdue` 3, `unblocks_one` 0.5, `unblocks_many` 1).

## Record

`effective_priority` orders by level first: the terms after the level are scaled to add at most 0.8, so an overdue note with no stated level no longer outranks an Urgent ticket (SMD-4177)

**Why.** Migration 122's weights (docs/priority.md decision 5) added urgency in whole levels: +2 due by the end of tomorrow, +1 within the week, +3 overdue, +0.5 or +1 for what an item unblocks.
- On SMD-3662's seeded set the overdue gutters note (unknown 2 + overdue 3 = 5) outranked the Urgent ticket that unblocks another (4 + 0.5). The operator's gold puts the ticket first on four prompts.
- Both priority reads stopped at top-1 on 2 of 7.
- On a real brain an open item left overdue and never closed outranked every stated level below Critical, indefinitely.

**What changed.** The maintainer chose option C on 2026-11-09, posted on SMD-4177 before the build and the run:
- **The rule:** an item's level (stated, read from its open items, inherited, or unknown's 2) decides its place. Urgency, overdue and unblocks order items of one level.
- **Migration 127** replaces `effective_priority` with 125's body and the default weights scaled by 0.2: `soon` 0.4, `week` 0.2, `overdue` 0.6, `unblocks_one` 0.1, `unblocks_many` 0.2. Together they add at most 0.8, so they never reach the next level, and their ratios keep 122's order within a level. Every weight is still a setting a caller overrides by name.
- **Search's blend** divides effective priority by 5.8, the new largest (`PRIORITY_SPAN`, from 9).
- **docs/priority.md** amends decision 5 and rewrites its examples: a Medium bill due tomorrow (2.4) now stays below a High with no date (3). The ADR had declined a lexicographic order on 11-02, and this adopts it in effect.
- **The tool descriptions,** `server-portable/README.md` and `db/README.md` say the new weights. The eval's `plannedNoteReads` reads tenths.
