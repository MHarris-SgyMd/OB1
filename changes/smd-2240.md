## Changelog

`db/extract-entities.ts` extracts a thought over the per-thought bound over its first windows and records it succeeded with a `partial:` caveat, where it was failed with nothing in the graph; the bound is a knob (`OB1_EXTRACT_MAX_WINDOWS`, 24 unset), `--status` and the run's summary count and list partial rows apart, and `--retry-partial` re-extracts them after a raise (SMD-2240).

## FORK

A thought over the extraction bound is extracted over its prefix, not failed — its coverage on the claim, the bound a knob (SMD-2240)

**What changed.** `server-portable/entities.ts`: `extractEntities` no longer
throws `over EXTRACT_MAX_WINDOWS (24); not extracted` before any call.
`boundedWindows` takes a thought's windows in order up to the per-thought bound
— at most `maxWindows` windows, and a text bound of `maxWindows × windowTokens`
estimated tokens in which a window of twice the size or more (only a run
`chunk.ts` cannot split makes one) counts its whole length and any other its
length up to the size — and the answer's new `coverage {windows, of, cut}`
says how much was read; each call's part marker still counts the thought's
windows ("Part 3 of 67"). The bound is `ExtractWindowing.maxWindows`, from
`EmbedConfig.extractMaxWindows`: `OB1_EXTRACT_MAX_WINDOWS` when a positive
safe integer once floored, else `db/config.mjs`'s `EXTRACT_MAX_WINDOWS`, 24
(`resolveExtractMaxWindows`). `describeExtractWindow` states the bound, so the
worker's `window:` banner and preflight's `extraction window` row both do.
`db/extract-entities.ts` releases a thought with coverage **succeeded with a
caveat** — migration 028's rule, `last_error` on a succeeded row — reading
`partial: N of M windows extracted, …`, or `… sent, the last cut short …`
(`partialCaveat`, `PARTIAL_CAVEAT_PREFIX`); one predicate (`partialRow`)
drives the counts (`12 extracted (1 over a prefix only), 0 failed`), the list
`--status` and the end of a run print, and the new `--retry-partial`, which
returns those rows to the pool so a raised bound re-extracts them
(`record_thought_entities` replaces the prefix's rows). `--dump` lines carry
`coverage`. The knob is forwarded by `deploy/compose.yaml` and documented in
`deploy/.env.example`, as `OB1_EXTRACT_CHUNK_TOKENS` is.

**Why.** The bound was sized at four times the longest thought on the fork's
brain, and a thought over it was failed with the count, retried identically by
`--retry-failed`, and gave the graph nothing. Ingested documents (SMD-1867,
SMD-2136) broke the sizing: a live pass over the dogfood brain on 2026-09-25
failed 8 of 53 queued thoughts on it, research PDFs and session summaries of 26
to 74 windows, which sat in the pool looking like defects. The cost bound was
right and the all-or-nothing policy was not.

**The blob.** The text bound is what holds a run `chunk.ts` cannot split
(SMD-1974). `chunk.ts` sends a whitespace-free blob whole however long, in one
window or, carried by the overlap, two, and the count bound never saw it. It
is now cut where the thought's text reaches the bound, with a caveat saying
the runs used it up. A window under twice the size counts no more than the
size — the word that filled it can run past, and unspaced CJK prose, whose `。`
`chunk.ts` does not split at, sits one paragraph a window just over it — so a
multi-window thought's text is under twice `maxWindows` windows' worth;
charging those in full would cut thoughts of 24 windows that extracted whole
before (review passes 1 and 2). A one-window thought is sent with its blank
lines, as before. A single call over a window's size stays SMD-1974's to fix.

**Beside migration 028.** 028's header says this worker is clear of
`reembed.ts`'s caveat readers because it released success with NULL; partial
rows now carry a caveat, so `reembed.ts --job extract:… --retry-fallbacks`, a
key `reembed.ts` warns about but accepts, would sweep them. SMD-1311, which
refuses such keys, is the fix; the exposure is noted there.

**Held.** `server-portable/test-local-provider.ts` [10]: a 30-window thought is
24 calls in order, its coverage and part markers stated, the opening sent and
the tail not; the caveat's exact text; `OB1_EXTRACT_MAX_WINDOWS` 2, 40 and
eight unset spellings (1e21 and 1e308 among them); the one-window blob cut to
the bound in one call; a run mid-thought ending the prefix; windows a word or
an unspaced paragraph over the size, whole; a one-window thought behind blank
lines, whole; a run of exactly the bound leaving no empty call; no cut between
surrogate halves. `server-portable/test-preflight.ts` [3c]: the row's closing
clause at the default and at 80. `db/test-live.ts` [10]: under
`OB1_EXTRACT_MAX_WINDOWS=2` a six-paragraph thought is succeeded with the
caveat, its opening's entities and edge in the graph and its closing's entity
not; `--status` counts and lists it; `--dry-run --retry-partial`, then
`--retry-partial` under the default reads it whole and clears the caveat; the
windowed ledger thought under the default has no caveat (743 → 753).

**Measured after.** Through the worker against a stub model: `db/README.md`
itself (81 windows at 1,200) is `24 of 81 windows extracted`, each call marked
"Part k of 81"; a note with a 105,000-character base64 run in its middle
(windows of 772, 27,015, 26,250 and 720 tokens) is `3 of 4 … sent, the last
cut short`; an edit clears the caveat and a run under 500 reads all 81.
`boundedWindows` fuzzed over 3,000 random mixes of prose, blobs, unspaced CJK,
emoji and blank lines: no invariant broken.

**Not taken.** Bumping `ENTITY_PROMPT_VERSION`: the prompt and the rules for
a whole extraction are unchanged — what changes is a thought over the count,
which stored nothing, and a thought whose runs pass the text bound, which is
now cut — and a bump would re-extract every thought under `--switch-key`. A
column on `thought_work_claims` for the coverage: 028's caveat already means
"the write stands, and this is what the worker could not do", so no
migration. Splitting an over-window run by characters at extraction time:
that is SMD-1974's decision, for embedding and extraction together.

(the 028 exposure above) and SMD-1974 (the per-call size, and `chunk.ts` not
splitting at `。`). The re-run of the 2026-09-25 pool on the stable brain is
the operator's.
