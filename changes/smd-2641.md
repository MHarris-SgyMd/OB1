## Changelog

Extract's and consolidate's `--follow` followers no longer blame the wrong thought for a provider crash. After an outage, once the provider answers, the thoughts that were in hand at it are retried one at a time with one worker, and the follower says so; normal passes resume once each has settled. A thought retried alone that meets the error again is recorded failed only on a one-token call made at once: if it answers, the thought is its own; if not, the follower waits again, and the thought's third such outage alone makes it its own. Before, with several workers, a thought that crashed the provider could fail the innocent thought beside it, and a provider crashing every few minutes failed whichever re-claimed thought it met first. A thought that crashes the provider now costs four crashes before it is failed, where it cost two; one the provider errs on while it answers still costs two (SMD-2641).

## Record

PR 1: an outage's suspects retried one at a time, and blamed only on a probe — extract's and consolidate's followers (SMD-2641)

The maintainer's rules (2026-11-10, on the ticket): the isolation reaches the suspects in the pool's order, with no migration; a suspect alone whose probe gets no answer is its own at its third outage alone (`ALONE_OUTAGES`, N = 3); two PRs, re-embed's follower in PR 2.

**What changes** (`ProviderOutage`, `db/worker-bootstrap.ts`, made `isolate` by extract and consolidate; re-embed and the subject sweep keep its rule unchanged):
- **Isolation.** Once an outage that made suspects ends, `isolating()` holds the next passes to one worker and a batch of one, the pool's order, said ("retrying the last outage's N suspect thought(s) one at a time"), until each suspect has settled (finished, or recorded failed), a pass finds nothing to claim (`unpooled`: another process holds them, or they were deleted), or the 15-minute window passes. The isolated pass ends as its last suspect settles, the next polled at once with every worker. An outage that begins while isolating keeps the suspects not yet retried, with their counts.
- **Blamed on a probe.** A suspect alone that errs again past the pauses (`blames`) is probed at once, or read off the probe just made (a deadline's, or consolidate's pair timeout's `ProviderDown`): answered, it is its own, recorded failed ("…alone in flight, and a one-token call right after answered…"), and the worker goes on; refused, the request's, every worker stopped, exit 2; no answer, an outage it was alone at, said "(n of 3)", and at the third its own ("…at 3 outages running, the one-token call after each unanswered…"), the outage begun with no suspect and the worker stopping after the record. Before, a suspect that erred again within 15 minutes of a probe answering was its own, whatever else was in flight.
- **A halted thought joins.** A worker whose pauses another worker's outage cuts short makes its thought a suspect of that outage when its streak could be its own: which worker's pauses end first, and so whose thought began the outage, is a race. Before, only the thought whose worker began or joined the outage past its own pauses was one.
