## Changelog

The `observability` profile's Postgres exporter no longer answers its `/probe` endpoint. That endpoint dials any Postgres target with credentials its caller supplies, so a caller on the `telemetry` network could try passwords against Postgres through it. Containers on `data` can no longer read its metrics. The exporter now listens on its own loopback. In front of it, the image's busybox `httpd` answers on the container's `telemetry` address alone and serves one path, `/cgi-bin/metrics`, which the collector now scrapes (SMD-3023).

Upgrading with the profile on: the first `up -d` recreates `pg-exporter`, and `lgtm`, whose scrape path changed, so the whole `lgtm` container restarts once, Grafana with Prometheus, Loki and Tempo, on the same data (SMD-3023).

## FORK

The observability profile's Postgres exporter answers one path, on `telemetry` alone — its `/probe`, which dials any target and credentials, is unreachable (SMD-3023)

**What changed.**
- **`pg-exporter`** (`deploy/compose.yaml`):
  - postgres_exporter now runs on `127.0.0.1:9188`. Its `/probe` cannot be turned off.
  - The entrypoint resolves the container's `telemetry` alias, `pg-exporter.ob1.internal`, to its address there. It then starts busybox `httpd` on that address at 9187, with `PATH` its whole environment, serving one CGI, `/cgi-bin/metrics`. The CGI fetches the exporter's `/metrics` itself. If the exporter does not answer, or `/tmp` has no room for the copy, it answers 502.
  - The CGI is written to a tmpfs of its own, `/var/www`, mounted `exec,mode=1777`. Docker mounts a tmpfs `noexec` unless told, and httpd then answers 404 for a CGI it cannot run. Podman gives a directory that exists in the image its root-owned 755. The entrypoint refuses, saying so, if it cannot write the CGI.
  - Every other path on 9187 is httpd's 404.
  - If either process ends, the container exits within 35 s and restarts. A loop polls both processes with `kill -0` every 5 s, then waits 30 s.
  - The service gains `/tmp` as a tmpfs at the engine's defaults (it stays read-only) and `init: true`. The healthcheck reads the exporter's landing page on loopback.
- **`lgtm`**: the collector's `prometheus/postgres` scrape has `metrics_path: /cgi-bin/metrics`.
- **Check 28**: `PG_EXPORTER_SERVICE` and `LGTM_SERVICE` follow these changes. The seccomp probe is re-anchored on a line only the authorization server has: pg-exporter's confinement block matched auth's until pass 1 gave it a second tmpfs.
- **CI** (the observability step, before pg-exporter's stop):
  - from `telemetry`, `/cgi-bin/metrics` must answer `pg_up 1`;
  - `/probe` (a wrong password for `ob1_monitor`, without TLS), `/`, `/metrics` and `/cgi-bin/..` + `/probe` must each be a 404;
  - a `/probe` sent after a scrape on the same connection must get no answer;
  - from `data` (the server), nothing may answer at the exporter's address on either network, on 9187 or 9188;
  - Postgres must log no failed `ob1_monitor` sign-in.
- **`deploy/README.md`**: the "Two gaps are open" paragraph and both table rows are replaced by what answers now. The data-source paragraph names the one path a Grafana admin reaches on `telemetry`.

**Why.** SMD-1849 PR 3b shipped the exporter with two known gaps (the maintainer's call, 2026-10-14):
- `/probe` from `telemetry` dialled Postgres with a guessed password, and Postgres logged it;
- every container on `data` could read the statements' text.

The ticket's candidate fix was httpd's proxy, `P:/metrics`. It fails: httpd reads the first request line, then pipes the connection. A `/probe` pipelined after a `GET /metrics` reached the exporter, which dialled the target (measured). A CGI is a separate request the script makes itself, so nothing the caller sends reaches the exporter. busybox's `wait -n` never returns for a process a signal ends (measured: httpd killed, the shell still waiting minutes later), hence the poll.

**Held.** check 28's `PG_EXPORTER_SERVICE`, whole; CI's assertions above. Each assertion was run against a mutant front on the scratch stack, and each mutant was killed by its assertion:
- httpd on every interface, and the exporter on every interface, by the check from `data`;
- the ticket's proxy, by the pipelined request;
- the same with that check dropped, by Postgres's log;
- a `/probe` proxy beside the CGI, by the 404 loop.

**Measured after.** On the scratch stack (podman 6):
- httpd listens on the `telemetry` address alone (10.89.23.3:9187) and the exporter on 127.0.0.1:9188.
- From lgtm, `/cgi-bin/metrics` answers 200 with `pg_up 1` (1,874 `pg_` lines). `/probe`, `/`, `/metrics` and `/cgi-bin/../probe` answer 404, the exporter's own port refuses, and Postgres logged no `ob1_monitor` failures before or after.
- From the server, on `data`: `pg-exporter:9187` and `:9188` refused, and the `telemetry` address timed out on both ports.
- Prometheus: `up` and `pg_up` for job `postgres` are 1, with 100 `pg_stat_statements_calls_total` series.
- A stop takes 0.3 s. httpd killed: restarted 37 s later on its new `telemetry` address, healthy.
- An upgrade from main and a rollback to it (twice each): only `lgtm` and `pg-exporter` were recreated, `lgtm-data` was kept, and no alert rule changed state. With main's `lgtm` beside this `pg-exporter` (one service updated), `up` read 0, and `ob1-postgres-unreached` went pending in 22 s and firing a minute on.
- The exporter killed: scrapes answered 502 at once, and the container restarted 35 s after the kill. Postgres paused: wget's 10 s cut the scrape, and `/tmp` was empty after interrupted and timed-out scrapes. 20 parallel scrapes: all complete. An IPv6-enabled network: httpd bound to the IPv4 address.
- With `/var/www` root-owned 755 (podman's copy of the image's directory), the entrypoint said it could not write its CGI and started nothing.

On Docker Engine 29.9 (in dind; review pass 1's walkthrough), this tree:
- `/tmp` `noexec`, `/var/www` exec and 1777;
- the alias resolved to the `telemetry` address alone;
- `/cgi-bin/metrics` answered `pg_up 1`, and every command of the CI block passed;
- a stop took 0.34 s, and a killed httpd restarted 31 s later.

CI's observability step holds the same on the runner's Docker.

**Review passes.**

| Pass | Finding | Caught | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the CGI on `/tmp`, which Docker mounts `noexec`: httpd answered 404 on Docker Engine 29.9 (measured in dind), so every Docker install would lose the Postgres series | cold read, walkthrough | pass 1: `/var/www:exec,mode=1777` |
| 1 | podman mounts `/var/www` root-owned 755, so `mkdir` failed and the entrypoint went on to serve nothing behind a healthy healthcheck | run-it | pass 1: the mode, and the entrypoint refuses when the CGI cannot be written |
| 2 | with `/tmp` unwritable, the CGI answered 200 with an empty body (the collector read an unexpected EOF) | run-it | pass 2: `mktemp` takes the 502 path |

**Not taken.** Replacing the exporter with one that has no multi-target endpoint: the dashboard's Postgres row and the alert rule read this exporter's series names. Basic auth on the exporter's web config: the collector must scrape it, and it would still leave `/probe` open to whoever holds the scrape credentials.
