## Changelog

An egress gate decides, before any model call, whether a thought's text may reach an endpoint not declared local: `OB1_EGRESS_POLICY` (deny by default, allow, off) with `OB1_EGRESS_ALLOW` / `OB1_EGRESS_DENY` terms over actor, source, type, topic and marker; `OB1_LLM_LOCAL` / `OB1_CHAT_LOCAL` declare an endpoint on the box. A refused capture lands without a vector and says why; the decision is recorded on the audit row; preflight reports the mode and what leaves per endpoint (SMD-1903). Every stack pointing at a local provider by address now sets `OB1_LLM_LOCAL=1`.

## FORK

Nothing decided which content may leave the box for a model call — a deterministic egress gate, deny by default, with "local" declared and never guessed, before any cloud tier (SMD-1903)

**What changed.** `server-portable/egress.ts` is the one rule:
`mayLeaveBox(subject, endpoint, policy, secondOpinion?) → { allowed, rule,
reason }`, pure, over a subject (the call's kind, the actor, the row's `source`,
`type` and `topics`, the text), an endpoint's `base` and `local`, and a policy
resolved from `OB1_EGRESS_POLICY`: `deny` (the default — nothing reaches an
endpoint not declared local unless an `OB1_EGRESS_ALLOW` term matches), `allow`
(everything does unless an `OB1_EGRESS_DENY` term matches), `off`. Terms are
comma-separated `unit:value` over `actor`, `source`, `type`, `topic` and
`marker` (a substring of the text), matched exactly and case-insensitively; a
knob that does not parse fails CLOSED — the mode is deny whatever it said, and
preflight fails the row naming the entry. "Local" is **declared, not guessed**:
`OB1_LLM_LOCAL=1` says the embeddings endpoint is on this machine or its private
network, `OB1_CHAT_LOCAL=1` the same of a chat endpoint of its own (a chat
endpoint at the same base is the same box); a loopback address, the
container-to-host alias and the compose service name are all remote to the gate
until the flag says otherwise. Preflight's hostname guess (`isLocalHostname`)
still decides whether a credential is needed, never whether text may leave.
Every dialler asks first: `providerCall` takes the subject as a fourth argument
and throws a `ProviderError` of kind `egress` before the request exists;
`judgePair` refuses a pair when EITHER row may not leave; `extractEntities`
refuses on the row's; the embedder threads the subject through every window and
its whole-content fallback rethrows a refusal; a dialler without a subject does
not compile. A classifier may attach as a second opinion that can only make the
answer stricter — consulted on an "allowed" alone, a hook that throws refuses —
because a detector right 98% of the time is a compliance failure twice in a
hundred; the Jev-class detector SMD-1897 spikes would attach there, at most.

**What a refusal does.** `capture_thought` decides once for both endpoints and
makes only the allowed calls: a refused embedding lands the row with its text
and fingerprint and NO vector (`captureThought` takes `embedding | null`, the
one path that stores a row vectorless on purpose) and the reply leads with
"saved WITHOUT a vector", the rule, the host, and the two ways back (exact text
through `search_thoughts_keyword`; a later `db/reembed.ts` pass against an
endpoint the gate allows); a refused tagging call lands
`metadata_extraction_failed: egress_denied` and no placeholder tags — the call
never happened, so it produced none; a refused search names the keyword tool
and the remedy; an edit with new text is judged on the row's own metadata and
stored vectorless when refused; a re-capture keeps the thought's tags and vector
and the reply says so. The decision is provenance on the write's audit row,
`thought_audit.actor_context.egress` — `{ policy, embeddings: { to, allowed,
rule }, chat: { … } }`, one entry per endpoint not declared local, nothing when
both are (no migration: the trigger keeps every actor key it does not read),
where SMD-1729's program will find which rule fired and which provider was
dialled; the thought's own `metadata` was declined as the record — state merged
by edits, not the event. `reembed.ts`, `consolidate.ts` and
`extract-entities.ts` carry the same subject from the row, print an `egress:`
banner, and stop before claiming under a policy that refuses every row they
carry. Preflight reports the mode (a knob that does not parse fails, `off`
warns) and, per endpoint, "declared local", "looks local but is not declared so
— the gate treats it as remote" with the one-line fix (the upgrade case: every
stack that points at Ollama by address), or what leaves under which terms.
`deploy/compose.yaml` forwards the five knobs with no fallback — the file must
not declare local on the operator's behalf, so the `local-models` profile now
needs one line; `deploy/.env.example` has the block; `SETUP.md` gains "What may
leave the box" and `OB1_LLM_LOCAL=1` in both local blocks, with the paragraph the
ticket asked for: with PHI in the brain, keep every endpoint on the box.

**Why.** Every embedding and chat call sent a thought's full text to whatever
`OB1_LLM_BASE_URL` — and, since SMD-1902, `OB1_CHAT_BASE_URL` — named, and the
only policy was the URL the operator typed. For a brain holding protected health
information that is a compliance boundary, and the routing ladder (SMD-1898)
makes the gap concrete: a router says "this item needs a stronger model";
nothing said "this item may not leave regardless" — which is the actual reason a
local-large tier (SMD-1880) is needed. Deny by default is the decision the
ticket left to stakeholders, made here and stated: the shape SMD-1813 already
chose for the connector path, and the one that fails safe on the upgrade — a
stack that upgrades unread loses no text, captures land vectorless and say so,
preflight names the line; `allow` and `off` are one word away. Declined: routing
a refused call to a local tier instead (two endpoints, not a tier list — the
route is SMD-1897's); a compose fallback declaring the profile's Ollama local (a
false declaration for anyone who sets a hosted URL and forgets the flag).
