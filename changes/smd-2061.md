## Changelog

`db/graph-centrality.ts --startable` reads the board's `blocks` / `blocked_by` link facets (migration 053) and weighs a thought whose ticket has an open blocker 0 in every count, so `--status open --startable` ranks the graph as the tickets you could start now build it; a completed or canceled blocker does not block, a parent is not blocked by its children, and without the flag every mode's output is unchanged byte for byte (SMD-2061).

## FORK

graph-centrality reads the dependency edges 053 already stores — `--startable` weighs a blocked thought 0, a settled blocker blocks nothing, and the default is unchanged (SMD-2061)

**What changed.** SMD-1994 made `db/graph-centrality.ts` lifecycle-aware: a
per-thought weight every count sums, `--status open` dropping Done tickets. It
still could not answer "what can I start now". A ticket blocked by an open one
ranked like a startable one, although migration 053 (SMD-1867) stores the
board's relations as `link` facets on the row holding a ticket's identity —
`blocks` on the blocker, `blocked_by` on the blocked. `--startable` reads both
directions (an edge the sync stated on one side only still counts) and
multiplies a second factor into the same weight: a thought whose ticket has an
**open blocker** weighs 0. A blocker is open unless its own lifecycle, resolved
through `source_thought()` (053's resolver) to a thought and read through
SMD-1994's ticket-head rule, is completed or canceled: a settled blocker is not
a blocker. A blocker the brain does not hold, or one with no lifecycle, still
blocks — the source said "blocked" and nothing says it is settled — and the
output counts such blockers. A row derived from a ticket (`ticket`) or carrying
one (`issue`) takes its ticket's blockers, as it takes its ticket's status.
Only an active link counts (053 closes a relation the source dropped), and only
`blocks` / `blocked_by`: `child_of` makes nobody a blocker. A thought with no
link facet counts as unblocked. Under the flag the whole-graph lists hold only
the entities a startable thought mentions, as under a `--status` filter, and a
dependency caveat line says where the edges come from, the latest link written
or closed, how many active dependency facets there are, how many thoughts
belong to a ticket whose relations were read, how many weighed 0, and how many
blockers are unsettled only for want of a lifecycle; the JSON carries the same
numbers as `coverage.dependencies`. The flag composes with `--status` and
`--decay-done`: the weights multiply. Without it the dependency read is not in
the SQL, so every other mode's output is byte for byte SMD-1994's and a brain
without 053 still runs them; with it, a brain without 053 is exit 2.
`dependencySql` is the seam, beside `LIFECYCLE_CTE`: when SMD-2074's node-state
projection holds startability, it reads that and the callers see the same
weight.

**Why.** Measured 2026-09-24: of the three unstarted open High-priority Open
Brain issues, two were blocked (SMD-1997 by SMD-1999, SMD-1999 by SMD-1998),
and priority-only ranking put the least actionable, SMD-1997, on top. SMD-1994
closed the status half of that divergence; this closes the dependency half. The
three have since completed, so the case is a fixture. On the dogfood brain
(752 thoughts, 165 active dependency facets, 0 unresolved targets, links
written up to 2026-09-25T12:55Z), `--status open --startable` over the whole
graph weighs 32 thoughts 0 and lists 431 of the 459 `--status open` lists. The
28 dropped are the hand join's blocked tickets exactly — the wiki connectors
SMD-1814–1818 behind the open SMD-1812/1813, the claim-log phases
SMD-1732/1734/1794 behind the open SMD-1731, SMD-2116–2118, SMD-2147–2150 and
the rest. SMD-1731 stays (its one blocker, SMD-1730, is completed), and so do
SMD-1806 (started, every blocker completed), SMD-1812 and SMD-1813. The read
adds about 100 ms (610 → 711 ms for the whole report).

**Held.** `test-schema.ts` [44], 20 assertions over tickets with 053's own
source rows and link facets: the 2026-09-24 board as tP (backlog) blocked by
tQ (unstarted) blocked by tR (started), with tS startable under the open tP
(`child_of`). `--status open` lists all of them; `--startable` drops tP and tQ
and keeps tR and tS. A section derived from tP is blocked with it. A `blocks`
stated on tR's side alone blocks its target. A closed `blocked_by` blocks
nothing. A blocker not in the brain blocks and is counted. Hand captures and
tickets with no link facet pass. Once tR completes, tQ and the `blocks` target
are free and tP still waits on tQ. `--decay-done --startable` multiplies (tP 0,
the settled t1 and tR 0.25, the freed tQ 1). The control: the default and
`--status open` reports rendered before the links were written are
byte-identical after, `weightsSql` without the flag carries no `thought_facets`,
and `coverage.dependencies` is absent. Plus the coverage numbers, the
dependency line and header text, the no-neighbour line naming the flag, and
`parseArgs`. Mutants, each killed: a settled blocker still blocking (fails and
crashes the section); the `blocks` direction dropped (4); closed links read
(4); `child_of` read as `blocked_by` (fails and crashes); derived rows not
given their ticket (5); an unknown blocker not blocking (5); the blocked factor
dropped from the weight (8).

**Not taken.** *Down-weighting a blocked thought instead of dropping it*
(Work item 3, a pre-registered `BLOCKED_WEIGHT` behind `--decay-blocked`):
cut at the plan stage as its own PR, to be judged on this read's numbers.
*Treating an unresolved or lifecycle-less blocker as settled*: it would make
startability depend on what the brain happens to hold; the count is printed
instead. *A column naming each thought's blockers*: under the filter a blocked
thought is not listed, so the column would be empty; it belongs with the
down-weight. *Reading `child_of` as a gate*: an epic's children are its work,
not its blockers. *The shared node-state projection* every ranking surface
reads: SMD-2074.

**Upstream status:** not sent — upstream has no entity graph, board sync or
link facets.
