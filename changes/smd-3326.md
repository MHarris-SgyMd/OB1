## Changelog

H2O-Lightning-4B's decision tier (`jev/h2o/serve.py`, the tier decide mode calls) reads one request at a time on a worker thread of its own, so `/health` and `/info` answer during a decide. It admits at most `JEV_MAX_QUEUED` requests (8) and `JEV_MAX_QUEUED_BYTES` of body (32 MiB), answering 503 with `Retry-After` past either (the consolidation pass reads that 503 as an outage, so `JEV_MAX_QUEUED` should exceed the decide workers of every brain sharing the tier), and drops a waiting request whose caller has left. A body too large for that byte bound on its own is a 413. A context it would read only in part (over the model's 32,000 tokens) is now refused with the contract's 422, naming the decision and its token count; it was cut to its head and tail and answered. The consolidation pass records that pair on the thought's claim with the tier's words, judges the thought's other pairs and goes on, rather than stopping (SMD-3326).

## Record

H2O's decision tier reads one request at a time, bounds its queue, and refuses a record it would read in part (SMD-3326)

**What changed.**
- `jev/h2o/serve.py`: a `Gate` runs each request's read behind a lock on a single worker thread, never on the event loop. Its admission bound is SMD-2082's knobs, `JEV_MAX_QUEUED` (8) and `JEV_MAX_QUEUED_BYTES` (32 MiB). Past either, the answer is 503 with `Retry-After`: the admitted requests times the recent read time, at least 1 s. A request is refused before its body is read when its Content-Length shows it would pass the bound, and otherwise as soon as the bytes read pass it. A caller that leaves while its request waits gets 499 then, its admission and bytes released, and its request is never read; so does one that leaves mid-body. A body that does not arrive within 30 s is a 408, so a caller that declares a body and stalls cannot hold its place. A body over `JEV_MAX_QUEUED_BYTES` on its own is a 413, since no wait would admit it.
- `handle` is the whole of `POST /decide`, free of FastAPI and the model; the FastAPI route only adapts it.
- A context over `max_state_tokens` gets a 422 before it queues, and every answered result says `truncated: false`. Every context is encoded to count it, on a thread off the event loop: the tokenizer normalizes to NFC first, so a text's UTF-8 length is no bound on its tokens (10,666 × U+FB2C is 31,998 bytes and 42,664 tokens). A context it cannot encode is a 400 naming the decision. The shim's head-and-tail cut stays as `Reader.truncate_state`, for `conformance.py` alone, since H2O's published reads were made with it.
- `server-portable/jev.ts`: a 422 from `/decide` throws `DecisionRefused`, a `ProviderError`. `db/consolidate.ts`'s pair loop records it as it records an egress refusal: `pair with <older id>: <the tier's words>` on the claim, the thought's next pair still judged, and the claim failed at the end (`1 of 2 pair(s) not judged: …`), so `--retry-failed` revisits it. As a plain `http` error it stopped every worker, and the next pass met the same pair. `db/worker-bootstrap.ts` classifies `DecisionRefused` as the thought's failure in `classifyError` and `followClassify` too, for a worker that lets it reach them.
- `server-portable/jev-contract.ts` and `jev/README.md`: an adapter may refuse a context it would cut, with the 422; the status list gains 503.
- `jev/h2o/test_serve.py`: the request path with a fake reader and the standard library alone. Portable server runs it (`python3 jev/h2o/test_serve.py`).
- `jev/h2o/README.md`: a "Serving" section with the knobs, the 422 decision and the measurements.

**Why.** Decide mode (SMD-3115) judges every pair through this adapter, and it was an eval-grade server:
- `async def decide` ran the synchronous MPS read on the event loop with no lock, so two requests interleaved on the model and `/health` waited behind a decide.
- Nothing bounded its queue, and stable and the canary share the one :8022 process.
- A record past the model's tokens was cut and answered as `truncated`. The maintainer decided on 2026-11-08 that no such cut is a faithful read, so the contract's 422 applies.

**Held.**
- test_serve.py, 42 assertions:
  - a burst serializes and every request answers;
  - a request cancelled mid-read still leaves the next read waiting for the thread;
  - the event loop turns while a read runs;
  - the count and byte bounds, each a 503 with Retry-After, refused before the body when Content-Length shows it;
  - 413 declared and streamed, and for a body over the byte bound alone;
  - 499 for a caller gone while waiting, at its turn, and mid-body; a caller check that raises reads as still here;
  - the 422 with nothing read, and the token count taken by encoding;
  - 400, 409 and 500 as before, and the gate empty after every path;
  - a knob or Content-Length of `²` (a digit to `str.isdigit`, not to `int`) refused by name or read as undeclared;
  - a request naming the model it serves is read, and one cancelled while it waits is never read and leaves the next request read;
  - a body that stalls, or trickles past the deadline though each chunk is quick, is a 408 with its slot released; the token count runs off the event loop; a context the tokenizer cannot encode is a 400;
  - each prompt holds the decision's record, and an unset knob is its default.
- Twenty-one mutants, one per mechanism, each fail it: eight worker threads, the read on the event loop, no count bound, no byte bound, no refusal before the body, no drop while waiting, no check at its turn, no 422, bytes never released, a body over the byte bound alone answered 503, a broken body stream escaping, a raising caller check escaping, tokens estimated from bytes, a 409 for its own model's name, a cancelled waiter's acquire left running, no body deadline, the token count on the event loop, an encode failure escaping, a per-chunk deadline, the prompt built without the record, and an unset knob refused. The suite passed 20 runs of 20 on macOS and on Linux under Python 3.12.
- `db/test-worker-bootstrap.ts`: `DecisionRefused` is the thought's failure in both classifiers; any other 422 is not.
- `db/test-live.ts` [16d]: a thought with two older neighbours, the first refused with 422 and the second read. The claim records the refused pair by the older thought's id with the tier's words, the second pair is judged, and another thought's pair in the same run is settled. The pass exits 1 for its failed row, where the refusal read as the provider's stopped every worker and exited 2. With the pair loop's catch removed, the case fails.

**Measured.** On the dogfood Mac, the real model, the adapter on a spare port with `JEV_MAX_QUEUED=4`:
- 7 requests at once: 4 answered one after another at 0.46, 0.84, 1.24 and 1.63 s, and 3 got 503 with `Retry-After: 4`.
- `/health`: 31 of 31 polls answered during the burst, the slowest in 1 ms.
- 19,999 × U+A000: 422 in 10 ms, naming 59,997 tokens.
- `server-portable/jev.ts`'s `jevDecideMany`: 70 decisions, split under the 64 cap, every one answered, and the 422 reached it as its `http` error.

**Not here.** These are PR 2's (SMD-3326):
- decide mode's own tier setting;
- preflight's second tier row;
- the LaunchAgent template and the host-process docs.

The co-load measurement (Work 4) runs once the hardened adapter is deployed. `jev/serve.ts`'s own bound stays SMD-2082's, and decider-4b's stays SMD-2265's.
