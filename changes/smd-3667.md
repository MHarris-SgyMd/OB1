## Changelog

A new read, `effective_priority`, orders any item (a thought, a ticket, an entity or an open item) by how much it matters now, and names every term behind the number. It takes the item's stated level, or a level inherited from what the item serves (a parent ticket, the ticket a thought is about, an open item's origin thought), or medium when nothing is known. It adds urgency from the due time, counted in calendar days in UTC (+2 by the end of tomorrow, +1 within seven days, +3 once overdue), and +0.5 or +1 for the open items the item blocks. So a Medium bill due tomorrow now outranks a High project with no date, and the read says why. A ticket's own row, read as a thought, reads as its ticket. The weights are docs/priority.md's, and a caller can override each by name (SMD-3667).

Migration 120 adds the read and stores nothing; `node_state` is unchanged. Search and the agenda will call it.

## Record

Effective priority: one read over any item, every term named — the counted level, else one inherited from what it serves, else unknown's 2; urgency in calendar days in UTC; overdue; the open items it blocks (SMD-3667)

The maintainer chose on 2026-11-05 a read of its own beside 116's `priority_of`, with every term computed at read time, rather than new columns on `node_state`. `node_state` covers thoughts alone, and reshaping it would drop and recreate it under `search_thoughts_current`, `ticket_status_inputs` and a pinned test. A projection for unblocks and inheritance follows only if timing asks for one.

**What changed.**
- **Migration 120** adds `effective_priority(p_items jsonb, p_weights jsonb, p_now timestamptz)` and nine helpers, all STABLE: `ob1_priority_counted`, `ob1_priority_open`, `ob1_priority_subject`, `ob1_ticket_row_of`, `ob1_ticket_rows`, `ob1_item_settled`, `ob1_node_settled`, `ob1_item_serves` and `ob1_item_unblocks`. It takes up to 1000 `{kind, key}` items, in the order asked.
  - **Level:** 116's winner. A tier-4 (inferred) statement is left out unless `count_inferred` is set, per decision 5, and is listed in `passed_over`.
  - **A ticket and its own rows are one item:** a row, read as a thought, reads as its ticket, and its first term names it (`as`). The statements on the ticket and on its rows compete by 116's rule as one pool. A served item or a due reference that is a ticket's row is read as the ticket too.
  - **Inherited:** for an item with no level of its own, the highest effective level among what it serves, less 1 per hop, at most 2 hops, and only above unknown's 2. A served item's effective level is its stated one when it states one, so the walk goes past it only when it doesn't. A settled served item passes nothing down. A ticket serves its parent (an active Linear `child_of` link). A thought serves its ticket (metadata `ticket`, else `issue`, else its standing `ticket:` subject). An open item serves its origin thought.
  - **Due time:** the winner's `due_at`; else its `due_ref`'s, one hop (the referenced item's winning `due_at`, else an open item's own); else an open item's own `due_at`. `due_from` says which. A referenced item since settled keeps its date, so an item that took a release's due time reads overdue until it is restated.
  - **Urgency:** calendar days in UTC. +2 by the end of tomorrow, +1 by the end of the seventh day, and +3 overdue in their place once the due time has come. None at a stated level 0, and none for a settled item.
  - **Unblocks:** open items blocked through active `blocks` / `blocked_by` links in a gating system, transitively to depth 3, never through a settled item, each counted once however many paths reach it. +0.5 for one, +1 for three or more. A settled item blocks nothing.
  - **Also returned:** `settled`, `not_before` with `waiting`, `terms` (each named, with what it read), `winner` and `passed_over`.
  - **Weights:** a jsonb of overrides by name. An unknown name, a wrong type or a value out of range is refused with 22023.
- **The store's `effectivePriority`** (`server-portable/store.ts`, `store-sql.ts`) reads it, with its columns named (SMD-3302). It throws `PriorityRefused`, with `problem` `weight` for a bad weight and `kind` or `key` for a bad item.
- **docs/priority.md:** the urgency row counts calendar days. The table names its weights, adds the inferred row and an open item's own due time, and "A read" names the function. db/README.md (120, the count and the map) and the version follow.
