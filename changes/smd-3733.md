## Changelog

test-live's [38], the sleep scheduler's section, runs its six one-shot sleeps at a 300 ms quiet and poll, meets a model gone at the first sleep's start without an 8 s quiet, and runs its N12 and retry case at a 3 s capture and 500 ms polls, so db-live's sleep cell takes about 27 s less (SMD-3733).

## FORK

test-live's [38] ~27 s shorter: the one-shot sleeps at 300 ms, the model gone at the first sleep's start by the stub's probe count, and the N12 run's quiet and capture (SMD-3733)

**What changed** in `db/test-live.ts`'s [38]:
- **The six one-shot sleeps** (once, the sweep, the held row, the poisoned row, the budget's settings and the drained pools before the clock's sweep cases) run at `ONE_SHOT`, a 300 ms quiet and poll, not a second of each.
  - Each asserts its end state alone: rows swept, settings seen, the order of calls, its exit.
  - No line's regex names their quiet or poll, and neither `--poll`'s bound nor N12's is involved: this is not the blanket scale SMD-3714 declined.
  - `drained()` counts what isn't pooled yet, so a faster poll ends no sleep early.
- **The model gone at the first sleep's start.** The stub's new `missingAfterProbes` answers 404 to every request once its probe count reaches it. The case sets it two past the count, so the start's check gets its two probes served (one for each pass) and the first sleep's start meets the model gone.
  - Before, the model went at the scheduler's first stamp, and an 8 s quiet kept the scheduler awake past the check however long a loaded runner took over it. Now nothing races, and the run takes the section's 1 s quiet.
  - The assertion's message gives the probes served.
- **The N12 and retry run** runs a 3 s capture in a 3.5 s quiet, polled every 500 ms, where it ran 4 s in 5 s at 1 s polls. The outage schedule's 2.5 s and 5 s and the 3 s re-stamp read are as they were.

**Why not the ticket's 2.5 s capture.** At 2.5 s in a 3 s quiet with 1 s polls, consolidation's cap was said after the first pause had resumed, failing its row once in the first run. The line comes a poll after the abort, and the abort can come a poll after the pause and a second into the call: up to about 3 s after the write. A 4 s capture had about a second's margin. At 500 ms polls the line comes by about 2 s, which keeps that margin inside a 3 s capture.

**Held.**
- **Locally**, [38]'s 110 assertions pass in five sleep-shard runs, two with every CPU busy (one `yes` per CPU). The section spans 239.3–240.4 s in each, against 266.9 s on the same machine at SMD-3714 PR 1's last review pass:
  - the one-shots ~11 s;
  - the model gone 8.3 s → 0.3 s;
  - the N12 run's rows ~8 s.
- **Seven mutants** of `db/sleep.ts` and `db/lease.ts`, each run against the sleep shard, all killed:

  | mutant | the row that failed |
  | -- | -- |
  | no N12 | the judgement in hand aborted and set back, and the cap's row |
  | no cap on consolidation's aborts | the cap's row: no cap line |
  | the cap's line every poll | the cap's row: 9 cap lines |
  | no move behind newer work (`setBack`'s date) | the set-back, 4th of 6 in the queue |
  | the start's check gets no pass through its start | the model gone at the first sleep's start: exit 2, 2 probes served |
  | no re-stamp through the retry wait | the refusal retried, the row stale mid-wait |
  | a sleep that ran keeps the schedule | the reset: 10 s, not 2.5 |

  The first four are SMD-3714's kept-at-seconds mutants that reach the N12 run, rebuilt. The last three hold this change's other rows: the probe count, the 3 s re-stamp read, and the reset the quiet's flip lands before.

**The run's length.** On main's run at 7e6e8897 the db-live jobs were sleep 290 s, rest 289, long 286 and extract 266. With [38] ~27 s shorter, long and the rest set db-live's length, a few seconds under sleep's before. So this change gives the sleep cell room, and SMD-3734's timers for long and the rest will shorten the run. Moving [17] and [5b] (~21 s) from the rest into sleep would leave long at ~286 s, so the cells stay as they are.
**Not re-run.** SMD-3714 named seven mutants on cases kept at their seconds. Four reach the N12 run and are above. The other three (the late commit timed from its own date, no move to the tail on a grace wake, the statement stop's rest) are on cases this change leaves alone.
