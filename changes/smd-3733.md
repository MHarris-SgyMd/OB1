## Changelog

test-live's [38], the sleep scheduler's section, runs its seven one-shot sleeps at a 300 ms quiet and poll, meets a model gone at the first sleep's start without an 8 s quiet, and runs its N12 and retry case at a 3 s capture and 500 ms polls, so the section takes about 27 s less locally (SMD-3733).

## FORK

test-live's [38] ~27 s shorter locally: the one-shot sleeps at 300 ms, the model gone at the first sleep's start by the stub's probe count, and the N12 run's quiet and capture (SMD-3733)

**What changed** in `db/test-live.ts`'s [38]:
- **The seven one-shot sleeps** run at `ONE_SHOT`, a 300 ms quiet and poll. They are once, the drained backlog whose start line says `--abort-after`, the sweep, the locked row, the poisoned row, the budget's settings, and the drained pools before the clock's sweep cases. Each ran at the section's 1 s, but the drained backlog's at SMD-3714's scaled 600 ms.
  - Each asserts its end state alone: rows swept, settings seen, the order of calls, a start line, its exit. The drained backlog's exit is now asserted too, and the run bounded, since the next case relies on it.
  - No line's regex names their quiet or poll, and neither `--poll`'s bound nor N12's is involved: this is not the blanket scale SMD-3714 declined.
  - `drained()` counts what isn't pooled yet, so a faster poll ends no sleep early.
- **The model gone at the first sleep's start.** The stub's new `missingAfterProbes` answers 404 to every request once its probe count reaches it. The case sets it two past the count, so the start's check gets its two probes served (one for each pass) and the first sleep's start meets the model gone.
  - Before, the model went at the scheduler's first stamp, and an 8 s quiet kept the scheduler awake past the check however long a loaded runner took over it. Now nothing races, and the run takes the section's 1 s quiet.
  - The assertion's message gives the probes served.
- **The N12 and retry run** runs a 3 s capture in a 3.5 s quiet, polled every 500 ms, where it ran 4 s in 5 s at 1 s polls. The outage schedule's 2.5 s and 5 s and the 3 s re-stamp read are as they were. Its read of the queue after the first abort is taken in the poll that sees the abort, one statement, where two reads after it could straddle the resume.

**Why not the ticket's 2.5 s capture.** At 2.5 s in a 3 s quiet with 1 s polls, consolidation's cap was said after the first pause had resumed, failing its row once in the first run. The line comes a poll after the abort, and the abort can come a poll after the pause and a second into the call: up to about 3 s after the write. A 4 s capture had about a second's margin. At 500 ms polls the line comes by about 2 s, which keeps that margin inside a 3 s capture.
- **At 1 s polls with the 3 s capture** the run passed three times of three in review pass 1, so the 500 ms poll is a margin, not a fix for a failure seen at that capture.
- **The quiet.** A 3 s quiet would allow the 3 s capture too, since the capture is at most the quiet. The extra half second is for the reset case, which flips the model away once a live write ends the recovered sleep: the flip must land before the next sleep's start. Its room is now about 2.75 s, against about 3.75 s at the 5 s quiet.
