## Changelog

A re-embed follower run with no admit gate now stops claiming once any of its workers has seen the model record move, as one under a gate already did (SMD-4413).

## Record

A re-embed follower's "record moved" rule holds with a gate or with none: it is the engine's check before each claim, and the loop's afterGate option is gone (SMD-4413)

**Problem.** A re-embed follower ends when it finds `ob1_config` recording a model other than its own. A worker that reads the record moved sets `switchedTo` and ends the drain. A second rule covered the case that read cannot: the record moved back while another worker was mid-thought. That worker then reads its own model recorded, and the rule stopped it all the same, because the follower had already decided to end.

`db/claim-loop.ts` asked that rule, `afterGate`, only after the admit gate. A follower with no gate, which is how `reembed.ts --follow` runs by hand, never asked it. In the same case its second worker went on claiming until the pool was empty, and the follower then ended 2, "never switches the model", with the pool re-embedded after it chose to stop. SMD-4401 kept the rule under a gate and named this gap.

**Decision.** The maintainer's, 2026-11-18: the rule holds everywhere, and it is re-embed's own.

**Change.**

- **`db/reembed.ts`.** The check before each claim (`beforeClaim`) returns at once when `switchedTo` is set, before it reads the record. The loop asks that check before every claim, with a gate or with none.
- **`db/claim-loop.ts`.** The `afterGate` option, its docblock and its read after the gate are removed. Only re-embed passed it, and its check now does the same work. `beforeClaim`'s docblock says it is asked with a gate or with none.
- **`db/test-engines.ts`.** The row that held `afterGate` ("not asked where there is no gate") holds the check in its place: it ends a worker with nothing claimed past an open gate. The row before it already holds that with no gate.
- **`db/test-live.ts` [9f].** A row beside SMD-4401's, with no gate: two workers, the record moved while both calls are in flight, and moved back a second after the quick worker's thought lands. The second worker's thought lands, it claims nothing more, and the follower ends 2 with two calls made and three thoughts pending.

**What changes for a caller.** Under a gate, nothing: the worker stopped after the gate before, and stops at the check now, one line later, with nothing claimed either way. With no gate, in the one case, the second worker stops where it drained the pool. Batches already in hand still land, as before.

**What the ungated row cannot see.** No gate means no signal that says when the quick worker has read the record moved. The row waits for that worker's thought to land and one second more before it moves the record back. A move that landed after the quick call ended, or a move back that came before the quick worker's read, would leave no worker having seen the move and the follower running, and the row would fail saying so. It would not pass wrongly. The row's first draft used the gated row's calls, 1.5 s and 4 s, which left about 1.2 s for the move to land; they are 3 s and 6 s, for about 2.7 s (caught: cold-read).

**Verify.** The check without its first line, run against test-live's long shard, fails two rows and no other: SMD-4401's gated row and this one, each re-embedding what was left (caught: mutant). The long shard is at 374 assertions, one more than main. `db/test-engines.ts` passes, 738 of 738.

The new row adds about 7 s to the long shard: its slow call is 6 s.

**Left as it is.** SMD-4401's record describes `afterGate`'s docblock, which this change removes with the option. That record is the history of its own PR.
