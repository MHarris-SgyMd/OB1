## Changelog

A test of extraction's stop during a provider probe has room for a slow CI runner, and says where the time went when it misses (SMD-4399). No behaviour changes.

## Record

test-live [10]'s "a first stop during a probe after a timeout ends it" stops sooner and is bound at 3 s: the return is milliseconds, and CI once took 2,054 ms against 2 s (SMD-4399)

**Problem.** The row starts an extraction follower with a 4 s call timeout and makes the stub hang. Once the thought's call has timed out and the probe after it has been asked, it waited 1 s, aborted the follower's signal, and held the return to 2,000 ms. It failed once in CI at 2,054 ms, with everything else it checks true: exit 0, the claim pending. Its message gave the time and nothing of what the follower was doing.

**What it was.** The runner, not the path.

| The case alone, repeated | Return after the stop |
|---|---|
| 40 times, one at a time | 4 to 25 ms, median 7 |
| 75 times, three copies at once | 3 to 13 ms, median 9 |

The follower says "stopping after the current thought" in the same millisecond as the stop, prints its summary within about 5 ms, and returns. No step on the way out waits on a clock. A return at 2,054 ms is a runner that stopped for about two seconds.

**Change.** In `db/test-live.ts` [10]; no engine changes.

- **The stop comes 200 ms into the probe, not 1 s.** The probe has the call's 4 s. A stop that did not end it would return when that ran out: about 3 s after a stop made 1 s in, about 3.5 s after one made 200 ms in. Stopping sooner leaves more room under that for the bound.
- **The bound is 3 s, not 2 s.** It is the runner's room: the path needs none of it.
- **A miss says where the time went.** The follower's lines after the stop, each with its milliseconds from it.

**Verify.** A caller's stop that leaves the pass's halt signal alone, so the probe runs out its timeout, run against test-live's rest shard: the row fails at 3,563 ms, and its message shows the stop's line at +0 ms and the summary at +3,558 ms (caught: mutant). The same mutant fails one other row, "a caller's AbortSignal during a provider-error pause", which waits on the same signal.

**Not done from the ticket's Verify.** "The rest shard passes 20 runs in a row under `ci-parity`'s load" was not run: it is over three hours. The case itself was run 115 times, 75 of them under load, and never took more than 25 ms.

**Not known.** Why that CI runner stopped for two seconds. One miss in the job's history is all there is.
