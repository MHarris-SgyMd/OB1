## Changelog

`db/bench-brains.ts`, the two-brain measurement's harness: `summary` reads a restart from the coordinator's marks where the tallies cannot show one, prints board-sync's calls in rows of their own, and says the thoughts not yet pooled and the rows failed on the extraction line; `sample` keeps each pool's counts and the start of its run (SMD-4382).

## Record

bench-brains.ts's summary sees a restart the tallies hide, board-sync's calls, and the extraction rows that failed or were never pooled, before the two remaining two-brain arms are run (SMD-4382)

**What changed.** `db/bench-brains.ts` and its section of `db/README.md`. No brain's code.

- **A restart.** A sample already kept the coordinator's children and the time of its stamp; a child marked `running` for `for_s` seconds began that long before the stamp (`beginsOf`). A pair of stamps in which a tally falls is a restart, as before. A beginning that lies between a fall's stamps is that fall's restart and counts once. A beginning with no fall is a restart at the pair of stamps it lies between: the one the tallies cannot show (`restartsOf`). Restarts are read over every stamp of the arm, then each window counts its own pairs.
- **The pair across the split** is a row of its own, `between`: it was in neither window, so its calls and a restart in it were nowhere in the table.
- **Board-sync.** Rows under each brain's, from board-sync's own stamps and its own mark, where it stamped tallies. They name no brain and say `its board-sync`. This is the ticket's "decide which": rows of their own, not a sum, because the two processes stamp at other times. Board-sync stamps at least as each pass starts and ends, a pass every five minutes unless set.
- **The extraction line.** `sample` keeps every pool's pending, claimed, failed and unpooled counts, whether the key is the brain's own (`poolsOf`), and the thoughts the brain holds. The line reads the brain's own key alone (`extractionLine`): rows pending or claimed, thoughts with no row under the key, rows failed, each first to last. It ends `ran dry`, `never dry at the end` (with when the pool was first seen empty, where it was), `nothing to do throughout`, that more rows had failed under the key than it held failed at the arm's first sample, or that the last sample had no row under the key. An older key's rows are said apart, and a key that changed in the arm is said. A brain with no row under its key is said to be unread, with the thoughts it holds.
- **Marks that were not `running`.** A line under a brain's rows says in how many of the samples that kept a dated mark the coordinator's mark for a pass said another state; or that no sample kept one.
- **The live files.** A live row ends with its file's place among the `--live` flags. Each file has a line: its name, when its calls began, and how many began in a sampled arm's window after the split.
- **Smaller.** A sample keeps the start of its run of `sample`, and two runs of one arm name in one file are a table each. Samples are read in the order of their times. `--ollama` is read as a url: `/api/ps` goes after its path and a query stays behind it; what is no http or https url is refused before a round. `--brains` may not name a brain `(ollama)`. An answer whose result is no object, or whose reading threw, is called "an answer of a shape the harness does not read" (`readOf`). Ollama's answer with no list of models is a failed read, not a server with none loaded. A sample whose pools are not pools is refused by its line.

**Why.** Each gap was recorded as not taken in SMD-3379 PR 3b-3b's record. A restart whose first stamp passes the old one's on every tally undercounts a window in silence. Board-sync's calls hold the same slots and were in no figure. "Ran dry" could not tell extracted from failed, and an older key's rows made "never dry" permanent. A sample taken without the new fields cannot be read again with them, so this lands before arms B and A.

**Held.** `bun db/bench-brains.ts --self-check`, which CI runs: 193 rows, 122 before, its floor at 180.

- A fixture with a restart the tallies cannot show counts one restart and 30 calls (16 by the tallies alone); the same samples without the stamp's time read as before. One with that restart in the pair across the split says it on the row between.
- A fixture with a board-sync heartbeat prints its 1,000 and 1,500 calls apart from the sleep's 9 and 20; one whose board-sync began again reads that from board-sync's own mark.
- A fixture whose pool emptied while 12 rows failed says so and does not print "ran dry"; so do rows that failed after it emptied, between two samples, or under a key that had no row at the arm's first sample.
- 211 mutants of the new decisions, run in a copy of `db/` outside the worktree: 209 killed. The two left print the same on anything `sample` writes: `stampsOf`'s sort, now that samples are read in time order, and a second turn of `beginsOf`'s last loop, which the rule before it takes first. The readers' own mutants on inputs `sample` does not write (an infinite count, a heartbeat with no time) are not in the set.

**Not taken.**

- **A run no sample saw running** has no mark: one that lived wholly between two samples is read by its tallies. The summary says where a mark was not `running`; it cannot say what was missed. Where some samples kept no dated mark and the rest said `running`, it says nothing.
- **A stamp half a second or less after a beginning** is taken for the old process's last, unless the tallies fall into it. Where it was the new one's first, the next pair reads as a restart, and that stamp's tallies less the old process's last are counted beyond the truth.
- **A beginning read late with no fall beside it,** after its own process's first stamp, counts a restart a pair late, or one that never was, or none where no stamp follows the time read.
- **A beginning taken for another run's fall:** one read early, at or before the old process's last stamp where that stamp ends a fall, or one read up to ten seconds after a fall's later stamp. Its own restart, one the tallies hide, is not counted. A beginning read late beside a fall another beginning lies in counts as a restart of its own instead.
- **A run whose first mark reads within fifteen seconds of the run before** is read as that run, and a mark more than fifteen seconds late on a run last seen running more than fifteen seconds before the time it reads is a run of its own until a mark near enough the truth is sampled.
- **A brain with no coordinator** is read by its tallies alone.
- **A restart the tallies cannot show was not produced on a real brain.** At both real restarts every pass's calls fell (15 to 3, and 22 to 3). The fixtures, the simulator and the enumeration hold the case.
- **From the walkthrough, left:** the count of restarts is not said as "at least"; one `--split` is applied to every arm of a file; older samples, which name no run, read two runs of one arm name as one; `1 time(s)` and the like; a refusal names a brain as typed; `cli.ts` checks a flag's value after the brains file.
- **`bench-live.ts`'s own reading still throws on such an answer.** This harness catches the throw; that one's callers are outside this ticket.
- **The api's vector mender and detached jobs** stamp no tallies, so there is nothing to read.
- **Wiring no self-check row reaches.** A reviewer's five mutants there survived: `main`'s refusal of an `--ollama` that is no url (run by hand: exit 2, nothing written), the run's start `sample` stamps, the file names `summary` passes, `sample`'s failed read of a model list, and `tool`'s use of `readOf`.
