## Changelog

`db/bench-brains.ts`, the two-brain measurement's harness: `summary` reads a restart from the coordinator's marks where the tallies cannot show one, prints board-sync's calls in rows of their own, and says the thoughts not yet pooled and the rows failed on the extraction line; `sample` keeps each pool's counts and the start of its run (SMD-4382).

## Record

bench-brains.ts's summary sees a restart the tallies hide, board-sync's calls, and the extraction rows that failed or were never pooled, before the two remaining two-brain arms are run (SMD-4382)

**What changed.** `db/bench-brains.ts` and its section of `db/README.md`. No brain's code.

- **A restart.** A sample already kept the coordinator's children and the time of its stamp; a child marked `running` for `for_s` seconds began that long before the stamp (`beginsOf`). A pair of stamps in which a tally falls is a restart, as before. A beginning that lies between a fall's stamps is that fall's restart and counts once. A beginning with no fall is a restart at the pair of stamps it lies between: the one the tallies cannot show (`restartsOf`). Restarts are read over every stamp of the arm, then each window counts its own pairs.
- **Board-sync.** Two rows under each brain's, from board-sync's own stamps and its own mark, where it stamped tallies. They name no brain and say `its board-sync`. This is the ticket's "decide which": rows of their own, not a sum, because the two processes stamp at other times. Board-sync stamps as each pass starts and ends, a pass every five minutes unless set.
- **The extraction line.** `sample` keeps every pool's pending, claimed, failed and unpooled counts, whether the key is the brain's own (`poolsOf`), and the thoughts the brain holds. The line reads the brain's own key alone (`extractionLine`): rows pending or claimed, thoughts with no row under the key, rows failed, each first to last. A pool where more rows had failed by the last sample than at the first is not said to have run dry. An older key's rows are said apart, and a key that changed in the arm is said. A brain with no row under its key is said to be unread, with the thoughts it holds.
- **Marks that were not `running`.** A line under a brain's rows says in how many samples the coordinator's mark for a pass said another state.
- **Smaller.** A sample keeps the start of its run of `sample`, and two runs of one arm name in one file are a table each. A live row ends with its file's place among the `--live` flags, and the files are named under the table. `--ollama` is read as a url: `/api/ps` goes after its path and a query stays behind it; what is no http or https url is refused before a round. `--brains` may not name a brain `(ollama)`. An answer whose result is no object, or whose reading threw, is called "an answer of a shape the harness does not read" (`readOf`). Ollama's answer with no list of models is a failed read, not a server with none loaded. A sample whose pools are not pools is refused by its line.

**Why.** Each gap was recorded as not taken in SMD-3379 PR 3b-3b's record. A restart whose first stamp passes the old one's on every tally undercounts a window in silence. Board-sync's calls hold the same slots and were in no figure. "Ran dry" could not tell extracted from failed, and an older key's rows made "never dry" permanent. A sample taken without the new fields cannot be read again with them, so this lands before arms B and A.

**Held.** `bun db/bench-brains.ts --self-check`, which CI runs: 170 rows, 122 before, its floor at 160.

- A fixture with a restart the tallies cannot show counts one restart and 30 calls (16 by the tallies alone); the same samples without the stamp's time read as before.
- A fixture with a board-sync heartbeat prints its 1,000 and 1,500 calls apart from the sleep's 9 and 20; one whose board-sync began again reads that from board-sync's own mark.
- A fixture whose pool emptied while 12 rows failed says so and does not print "ran dry"; so do rows that failed after it emptied, or between two samples.
- 150 mutants of the new decisions, run in a copy of `db/` outside the worktree, all killed. The set has the first reviewer set's surviving edits where the rewritten lines still take them.

**Not taken.**

- **A run no sample saw running** has no mark: one that lived wholly between two samples is read by its tallies. The summary says where a mark was not `running`; it cannot say what was missed.
- **A stamp half a second or less after a beginning** is taken for the old process's last. Where it was the new one's first, the next pair reads as a restart and that stamp's tallies count twice.
- **A beginning read late beside a fall another beginning lies in** counts as a restart of its own.
- **Two runs that began within fifteen seconds** read as one, and a brain with no coordinator is read by its tallies alone.
- **A restart the tallies cannot show was not produced on a real brain.** At both real restarts every pass's calls fell (15 to 3, and 22 to 3). The fixtures and the simulator hold the case.
- **`bench-live.ts`'s own reading still throws on such an answer.** This harness catches the throw; that one's callers are outside this ticket.
- **The api's vector mender and detached jobs** stamp no tallies, so there is nothing to read.
- **Wiring no self-check row reaches:** `main`'s refusal of an `--ollama` that is no url (run by hand: exit 2, nothing written), the run's start `sample` stamps, the file names `summary` passes. A reviewer's five mutants there survived.
