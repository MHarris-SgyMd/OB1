## Changelog

`db/bench-brains.ts`, the two-brain measurement's harness: `summary` reads a restart from the coordinator's marks where the tallies cannot show one, prints board-sync's calls in rows of their own, and says the thoughts not yet pooled and the rows failed on the extraction line; `sample` keeps each pool's counts and the start of its run (SMD-4382).

## Record

bench-brains.ts's summary sees a restart the tallies hide, board-sync's calls, and the extraction rows that failed or were never pooled, before the two remaining two-brain arms are run (SMD-4382)

**What changed.** `db/bench-brains.ts` and its section of `db/README.md`. No brain's code.

- **A restart.** `windowOf` takes the times each run of a process began (`beginsOf`). A sample already kept the coordinator's children and the time of its stamp; a child marked `running` for `for_s` seconds began that long before the stamp. A pair of stamps in which a tally falls is a restart, as before. A beginning with such a fall beside it is that fall's restart and counts once. A beginning with none is a restart at the pair of stamps it lies between: the one the tallies cannot show. A stamp half a second or less after a beginning is taken for the old process's last.
- **Board-sync.** Two rows under each brain's, from board-sync's own stamps and its own mark, where it stamped tallies. They name no brain and end `its board-sync`. This is the ticket's "decide which": rows of their own, not a sum, because the two processes stamp at other times and board-sync stamps every five minutes while it is idle.
- **The extraction line.** `sample` keeps every pool's pending, claimed, failed and unpooled counts and whether the key is the brain's own (`poolsOf`). The line reads the brain's own key alone (`extractionLine`): rows pending or claimed, thoughts not yet pooled, rows failed, each first to last. Thoughts not yet pooled are work to do. A pool that emptied while more rows failed is not said to have run dry. An older key's rows are said apart. A brain with no row under its key is said to be unread, not empty.
- **Smaller.** A sample keeps the start of its run of `sample`, and two runs of one arm name in one file are a table each. A live row ends with its file's place among the `--live` flags, and the files are named under the table. `--ollama` is read as a url: `/api/ps` goes after its path and a query stays behind it; what is no http or https url is refused before a round. `--brains` may not name a brain `(ollama)`. An answer whose reading threw is called "an answer of a shape the harness does not read" (`readOf`), and Ollama's answer with no list of models is a failed read, not a server with none loaded.

**Why.** Each gap was recorded as not taken in SMD-3379 PR 3b-3b's record. A restart whose first stamp passes the old one's on every tally undercounts a window in silence. Board-sync's calls hold the same slots and were in no figure. "Ran dry" could not tell extracted from failed, a brain with no first pass showed no backlog, and an older key's rows made "never dry" permanent. A sample taken without the new fields cannot be read again with them, so this lands before arms B and A.

**Held.** `bun db/bench-brains.ts --self-check`, which CI runs: 148 rows, 122 before, its floor at 140.

- A fixture with a restart the tallies cannot show counts one restart and 30 calls (16 by the tallies alone); the same samples without the stamp's time read as before.
- A fixture with a board-sync heartbeat prints its 1,000 and 1,500 calls apart from the sleep's 9 and 20; one whose board-sync began again reads that from board-sync's own mark.
- A fixture whose pool emptied while 12 rows failed says so and does not print "ran dry"; unpooled thoughts keep a pool from dry; an older key's rows are said apart.
- 91 mutants of the new decisions, run in a copy of `db/` outside the worktree: 90 killed. The one left changes which stamp of a falling pair is compared with the beginning, and the rule then picks the same pair by its second clause, so no input tells them apart.

**Measured after.**

- **The evidence of 2026-11-16** (kept on the maintainer's machine) summarises as it did. Every arm's rows are byte for byte the same: arm C3's canary reads 94 calls, 1,473 s over 679 s, 2.17 in flight, extraction 51 calls and 1,322 s (1.95). The differences are the six board-sync lines added under the canary's rows (four windows of 0 calls and two with fewer than two stamps: its board-sync called no model in those arms), the legend, and each live row's file. Those samples kept no stamp time, so their restarts are read by the tallies, as before.
- **Two real restarts,** on a scratch brain of the measurement's own (sleep its one pass, this Mac's Ollama, torn down after):
  - Its coordinator restarted between a load of 3 notes and one of 8. Seven marks of the new sleep put its beginning within 12 ms of each other. The summary: one restart, 31 calls. Counted by hand from the stamps: 9 by the old process after its first stamp, 22 by the new.
  - Its sleep killed and run again by the coordinator a minute later: one restart, the new process's 3 calls. A mark written 5.5 s into that run read `for_s` 5, which puts the beginning 0.48 s late: the rounding the rule allows for.
- **What the first of them found.** The first rule gave each stamp to the run that began a second or more before it. The restarted sleep stamped its first tallies 0.88 s after it began, so that stamp went to the old run and one restart was counted twice: 34 calls for 31. The rule above replaced it.
- **Read-only on the live brains:** two minutes of `sample` on the canary and stable. Pools, both marks and board-sync's tallies read; `--ollama` with a query on its url answered.
