## Changelog

The core docs describe the compose stack as the fork's own setup, not as a migration off Supabase. `SETUP.md` and `deploy/README.md` lose "without Supabase" from their titles, the deploy service table says what each service is rather than what of Supabase's it replaces, and provider examples speak of managed Postgres generally; history, the shim's own text and CI's job names keep their mentions (SMD-3443).

## FORK

The core docs stop teaching and framing Supabase (SMD-3443)

**What changed.** The how-to and the framing goes; history, the shim's text, CI's job names and one upstream line stay.
- `SETUP.md`:
  - The title is "Setting up Open Brain".
  - The opening says what the stack is, with one parenthesis that upstream's guide builds on supabase.com and this fork needs no Supabase account or CLI.
  - The query log's note says "on that host" rather than "a hosted (Supabase) deployment".
  - The prerequisites' "No Supabase account. No Supabase CLI. No Deno." is gone, said once in the opening.
- `deploy/README.md`:
  - The title is "the compose stack", and the opening describes the stack rather than "Phase 4 of the migration".
  - The env file's line no longer names `supabase secrets set`.
  - The service table's column is "What it is": each row names the service, not the Supabase piece it replaced.
  - The credentials note says the env file's settings are hand-written, without "on Supabase this mattered less".
  - The `vector extension` note names managed providers generally.
  - The caveats lose the bullet on upstream's Edge Function failing smoke checks 2 to 4. The fork retired that build in SMD-1800, and FORK.md changes 42 and 75 keep the record.
  - The scheduled-jobs and auth caveats no longer say "off Supabase".

- `server-portable/README.md`: the opening says "on Bun" rather than "no dependency on Supabase's function host", and "the runtime decision in the Supabase migration" becomes "built runtime-neutral so the runtime could be chosen last".
- `docs/03-faq.md`, `docs/agent-memory-portability.md` and `docs/open-brain-assistant-gpt-context.md` read the brain through its REST core, or call it Postgres-backed, where they said Supabase.
