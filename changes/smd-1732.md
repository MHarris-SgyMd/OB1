## Changelog

Migration 063 adds `rebuild_derived`, the one primitive over the lineage table: it walks `derivations` forward from a thought, restores a vector from the snapshot where the database can, hands every other stale artifact to the worker that owns its recipe with the reason marked on the row, deletes a lineage row whose artifact is gone, sets a pending proposal whose texts moved to a new `stale` status the next consolidation pass re-judges in place, and reports the derived prose it cannot reproduce; `db/rebuild.ts` is the operator's door and preflight's `lineage` check counts the marks and warns on orphans (SMD-1732).

## FORK

`rebuild_derived` — the one operation over the lineage table: walk forward from an input, re-derive what the database can, hand the rest to the workers with the reason on the row, delete what has no artifact, report what cannot be reproduced (SMD-1732)

**What changed.** Migration 063 (Phase 1c of SMD-1729). Two functions: `derivation_descendants(input, max_depth, node_cap)` — 026's iterative walk with a walk-global seen set over 061's GIN index, one `input_ids && frontier` probe per level, the `derived_from` children (025) listed as prose and not expanded — and `rebuild_derived(input, reason, input_gone, fingerprints, force) → jsonb`, which holds the walk whole and acts on every row: ORPHAN rules first (a chunks row with no windows, an entities row with nothing standing under its key, a vector row on a NULL embedding, a metadata row on an untagged thought → the row deleted), then STALE rules — an input's fingerprint moved, or `--force` — by kind: a vector whose current text has a snapshot row at the model is restored by `ob1_refresh_thought_vector` (the one re-derivation the database owns; a snapshot vector identical to the row's is 060's copy of the old vector under the new key and is not one); a vector without one and the windows go to the reembed pool under `ob1_config`'s model and width; an `extract:` pass to the configured extraction key; a pending proposal takes the new `stale` status and its newer thought is requeued under the judge's key; a `source:` pass and a decided proposal are kept; the tags are marked with no pool to feed. Every hand-off is 016's `requeue_thought_work` (a live lease revoked, as its content-edit trigger does) and every marked row carries `stale_since` / `stale_reason` — two new columns 061's writer clears on the producer's next upsert. With `input_gone` (SMD-1723's forget, BEFORE the row delete, in one transaction): the windows, the input's mentions and edges — the entities locked first, then the prune (016's rule) — and their lineage rows go; the snapshot rows at the input's own fingerprints (its position on each row, never the other thought's; the caller's from the log; none a standing thought holds) go; the proposals and the vector's and tags' rows are counted for the cascade. The `stale` status: 029's status CHECK widened, the unreviewed CHECK reads pending-or-stale, `consolidation_candidates` yields a stale pair again, `record_supersession_proposal` (061's body) replaces a stale row in place and re-records it; `review_supersession_proposal` needs no change (a stale row is a reviewer's, with `p_force`). Preflight's `lineage` check counts the marked rows and WARNs on orphan rows naming `db/rebuild.ts --orphans`; `db/rebuild.ts` (`--input`, `--gone`, `--force`, `--dry-run` as BEGIN/ROLLBACK, `--orphans`, `--status`) prints the report and the command that drains each pool it fed; the worker group gains `DELETE` on the snapshot; the ADR's three "not built" sentences move to the present tense.

**Why.** 061 recorded every derived artifact's inputs and recipe and said staleness is a read; nothing acted on it. SMD-1723's forget, SMD-1734's trust, SMD-2243's invalidate-on-supersession and SMD-1794's scheduler all wanted one primitive that reaches every tier, tested once. The database cannot re-run a model, so the honest shape is: re-derive the one thing it holds the inputs for, hand the rest to the workers that own the model calls under the keys they actually drain, and say on the row why.

**Held.** The graph the producers write is one level deep (every mechanical artifact's inputs are thoughts; a proposal is a leaf; a `derived_from` child is prose), so level order is topological order; the clamps and the seen set are for a producer that one day chains, and the header says so rather than sells it. Staleness stays a read; the mark is a request. Nothing runs under a replay (`REPLAYING`). The locks are delete_thought's (the supersession advisory lock first — a reviewer accepting the same proposal waits, then acts on the row the rebuild left). No arity moves; three bodies redefined on their own text; every DELETE names its rows.

**Measured after.** On a read-only copy of the dogfood brain (1,025 thoughts, 2,472 lineage rows — 1,025 vectors, 1,394 extraction pairs, 24 chunk sets, 24 proposals, 5 tag rows — 15,520 mentions, 12,057 edges; PostgreSQL 16): the walk from the most-fed thought (nine rows) 3.3 ms warm, its GIN probe a bitmap scan on `idx_derivations_inputs`; `rebuild_derived` on it 3.4 ms with nothing moved, 3.7 ms after a text move (two pools fed, two rows marked), 4.4 ms for the forget arm (three rows deleted, six proposals and one lineage row counted for the cascade, two derived_from children reported); every thought in turn, 1,025 calls, 523 ms — 3,242 rows walked, 2,452 current, nothing to do; the orphan census (preflight's and `--status`'s read) 36 ms; the table 1.2 MB. What the copy showed: six of its 24 proposals are stale by fingerprint and all six decided — kept, as the ticket's "a reviewer's decision stands" says; 2,460 of 2,472 rows are 061's legacy backfill, which read current by construction (`--force` is the door to re-run them); 88 thoughts sit pending in the extraction pool already.

**Not taken.** Deleting a stale pending proposal and requeueing the pair (029's file forbids a DELETE and a verdict is a record) or marking it alone (the pair could never be judged again): the maintainer chose the `stale` status. A retag pool for the tags (a second worker inside this ticket): marked and reported, a follow-up. Deleting the proposals in the forget arm (the cascade does it at the row delete, an RI action needs no grant, 029 forbids it). Taking the input's survival from the reason string: an explicit parameter. Re-deriving through the walk's depth (nothing chains today). The ticket's "re-counts the entity": no counter exists (016: evidence is the edge), so the prune is the re-count.
