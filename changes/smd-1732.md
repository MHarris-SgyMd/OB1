## Changelog

Migration 063 adds `rebuild_derived`, the one primitive over the lineage table: it walks `derivations` forward from a thought, restores a vector from the snapshot where the database can, hands every other stale artifact to the worker that owns its recipe with the reason marked on the row, deletes a lineage row whose artifact is gone, sets a pending proposal whose texts moved to a new `stale` status the next consolidation pass replaces in place when it finds the conflict again, and reports the derived prose it cannot reproduce; `db/rebuild.ts` is the operator's door and preflight's `lineage` check counts the marks and warns on orphans (SMD-1732).

## FORK

`rebuild_derived` — the one operation over the lineage table: walk forward from an input, re-derive what the database can, hand the rest to the workers with the reason on the row, delete what has no artifact, report what cannot be reproduced (SMD-1732)

**What changed.** Migration 063 (Phase 1c of SMD-1729). Two functions: `derivation_descendants(input, max_depth, node_cap)` — 026's iterative walk with a walk-global seen set over 061's GIN index, one `input_ids && frontier` probe per level, the `derived_from` children (025) listed as prose and not expanded — and `rebuild_derived(input, reason, input_gone, fingerprints, force, orphans_only) → jsonb`, which holds the walk whole and acts on every row: ORPHAN rules first (a chunks row with no windows, an entities row with nothing standing under its key, a vector row on a NULL embedding, a metadata row on an untagged thought → the row deleted), then STALE rules — an input's fingerprint moved, or `--force` — by kind: a vector whose current text has a snapshot row at the model is restored by `ob1_refresh_thought_vector` (the one re-derivation the database owns; a snapshot vector identical to the row's is 060's copy of the old vector under the new key and is not one); a vector without one and the windows go to the reembed pool under `ob1_config`'s model and width; an `extract:` pass to the configured extraction key; a pending proposal takes the new `stale` status and its newer thought is requeued under the judge's key; a `source:` pass and a decided proposal are kept; the tags are marked with no pool to feed. Every hand-off is 016's `requeue_thought_work` (a live lease revoked, as its content-edit trigger does) and every marked row carries `stale_since` / `stale_reason` — two new columns 061's writer clears on the producer's next upsert. With `input_gone` (SMD-1723's forget, BEFORE the row delete, in one transaction): the windows, the input's mentions and edges — the entities locked first, then the prune (016's rule) — and their lineage rows go; the snapshot rows at the input's own fingerprints (its position on each row, never the other thought's; the caller's from the log; none a standing thought holds) go; the proposals and the vector's and tags' rows are counted for the cascade. The `stale` status: 029's status CHECK widened, the unreviewed CHECK reads pending-or-stale, `consolidation_candidates` yields a stale pair again, `record_supersession_proposal` (061's body) replaces a stale row in place and re-records it; `review_supersession_proposal` needs no change (a stale row is a reviewer's, with `p_force`). Preflight's `lineage` check counts the marked rows and WARNs on orphan rows naming `db/rebuild.ts --orphans`; `db/rebuild.ts` (`--input`, `--gone`, `--force`, `--dry-run` as BEGIN/ROLLBACK, `--orphans`, `--status`) prints the report and the command that drains each pool it fed; the worker group gains `DELETE` on the snapshot; the ADR's three "not built" sentences move to the present tense.

**Why.** 061 recorded every derived artifact's inputs and recipe and said staleness is a read; nothing acted on it. SMD-1723's forget, SMD-1734's trust, SMD-2243's invalidate-on-supersession and SMD-1794's scheduler all wanted one primitive that reaches every tier, tested once. The database cannot re-run a model, so the honest shape is: re-derive the one thing it holds the inputs for, hand the rest to the workers that own the model calls under the keys they actually drain, and say on the row why.

**Held.** The graph the producers write is one level deep (every mechanical artifact's inputs are thoughts; a proposal is a leaf; a `derived_from` child is prose), so level order is topological order; the clamps and the seen set are for a producer that one day chains, and the header says so rather than sells it. Staleness stays a read; the mark is a request. Nothing runs under a replay (`REPLAYING`). The locks are delete_thought's (the supersession advisory lock first — a reviewer accepting the same proposal waits, then acts on the row the rebuild left). No arity moves; three bodies redefined on their own text; every DELETE names its rows.

**Measured after.** On a read-only copy of the dogfood brain (1,025 thoughts, 2,472 lineage rows — 1,025 vectors, 1,394 extraction pairs, 24 chunk sets, 24 proposals, 5 tag rows — 15,520 mentions, 12,057 edges; PostgreSQL 16; every write in a rolled-back transaction): the walk from the most-fed thought (nine lineage rows and two derived_from children, eleven walked) 0.6–3.3 ms, its GIN probe a bitmap scan on `idx_derivations_inputs`; `rebuild_derived` on it under a millisecond to 3 ms whatever the arm — nothing moved (`current 3, kept 6, legacy 9`), a text move (two pools fed, two rows marked), the forget arm (three rows deleted, six proposals and one lineage row counted for the cascade, two children reported), `--force` (the vector re-recorded, the extraction pooled), orphans only (nine current, nothing listed); every thought in turn, 1,025 calls, 120–523 ms — 3,242 rows walked, 2,452 current, 44 kept (22 stale proposals seen from both sides); a `--force` over every thought 218–544 ms — 1,025 vector records renewed, 969 (thought, pool) claims (1,017 pending in the extraction pool with the 88 already there, 24 in the reembed pool), 974 rows marked, 5 tag rows with no pool, legacy rows 2,460 → 1,440 (every vector's gone), and a second force repeats the same counts; the orphan census 20–37 ms (the `to_jsonb` read of the mark's column costs about a third of it at 10,001 rows); the table 1.2 MB. What the copy showed: 22 of its 24 proposals are stale by fingerprint (an older side moved under 21, a newer under 17) and all 24 rejected — kept, as the ticket's "a reviewer's decision stands" says; 2,460 of 2,472 rows were 061's legacy backfill, which read current by construction until `--force` renews them; 88 thoughts sat pending in the extraction pool already.

**Boyscout.** The three LOWs above; the leftover review databases dropped.

**Not taken.** Deleting a stale pending proposal and requeueing the pair (029's file forbids a DELETE and a verdict is a record) or marking it alone (the pair could never be judged again): the maintainer chose the `stale` status. A retag pool for the tags (a second worker inside this ticket): marked and reported, a follow-up. Deleting the proposals in the forget arm (the cascade does it at the row delete, an RI action needs no grant, 029 forbids it). Taking the input's survival from the reason string: an explicit parameter. Re-deriving through the walk's depth (nothing chains today). The ticket's "re-counts the entity": no counter exists (016: evidence is the edge), so the prune is the re-count.

**Follow-ups.** No suite drains a rebuild-fed reembed pool with the real worker end to end: [31] proves the enqueue and the pool's key, [9] the worker's write path under its own job key, and the mark's clearing by the trigger is proved on the label-move and vector-move arms — a tooth joining them needs [9]'s stub provider under the configured key. A worker-side settle for a stale proposal the pass no longer finds in conflict (a reject with the pass's note, or a fourth verb) — today it waits for a reviewer under `--list stale`. SMD-1723 (forget: the `input_gone` / `fingerprints` contract, the order "event, rebuild, row delete", the entity lock order); SMD-1734 (`derivation_descendants` is the walk to reuse); SMD-2243 (`rebuild_derived(superseded, 'superseded')`); SMD-1254 / SMD-2139 (no pool re-tags a thought — the mark waits for one); SMD-2117 (the mark columns ride the fold's copy of `derivations`).
