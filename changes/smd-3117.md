## Changelog

The consolidation pass now records a pair it judges unrelated, as a `distinct` relation on the newer thought: two near neighbours about different subjects, kept apart (SMD-3117). Before, an unrelated verdict only closed the pair's relation and kept nothing, so nothing could tell the two had been judged apart. `consolidate.ts --status` and `brain_info` count the distinct pairs beside the relations, and board findings never post one.

## FORK

The consolidation judge's distinct is stored: 084's relation vocabulary gains `distinct`, so a pair the judge calls unrelated is a standing relation on the newer thought (SMD-3117)

**Why.** Every pair the pass judges is a near neighbour (029's candidate rule), so an unrelated verdict says two look-alikes are about different subjects. 084 closed the pair's edge on one and kept nothing. Nothing told a reader, a merge or a later judge that the two were judged apart, and SMD-3116 — the typed-label spike, which renamed unrelated to distinct (differentiation: look-alikes kept apart, never merged) — had no pool of such pairs to measure. The maintainer decided on 2026-10-19 that the pass records it.

**What changed** (migration 100).
- **`thought_facets_validate` and `record_thought_relation`** are 084's bodies, verbatim but the word list: a relation's relation is one of related, evolves, duplicate or distinct. Their comments and `thought_facets`' and `supersession_proposals`' say so. 084's set per pair stands — one standing relation per pair, so a relation replaces a distinct and a distinct a relation; a delete closes or drops it; its lineage row is written with it.
- **A sentinel, `ob1:relation-distinct`,** sits in the door's body. 100 keeps 084's signature, so `consolidate.ts` reads the sentinel to know whether to send the word: on a brain short of 100, 084's door would refuse it and fail every thought with an unrelated pair.
- **The pass** (`server-portable/consolidate.ts`'s `relationVerdict`, in judge and decide mode): a well-formed unrelated verdict records `distinct` — under decide mode only H2O's own top unrelated, not one taken after a proposal failed. `relationConfidence` cuts it at its own probability — the word's token probability, or H2O's P(unrelated) under decide mode — not the relation words' mass, which a confident unrelated leaves near nothing. Prompt 4 still asks for "unrelated", so no prompt version and no pass key moved.
- **A brain short of 100.** `consolidate.ts` reads the sentinel at start, on every poll of a follower, and when the door refuses the word mid-pass (084 re-applied by hand under a running pass). Short of 100, an unrelated verdict closes the pair's edge as before, unless that edge is a distinct, which stands: the verdict agrees with it, and a closed one would not come back with 100. The run says so at its start and in `--status`, naming both remedies (`migrate.ts`, or `--reapply` where the ledger records 100), and the summary counts the verdicts at the floor it could not store. A refusal mid-pass stops sending the word rather than failing the thought; a door that keeps the sentinel and still refuses fails it.
- **The readers keep it apart.** The run summary says how many of each action's writes were distinct, beside the action's own count (`relations: 1 added (1 distinct), 0 kept, …`), never as a second count. `--status` counts the distinct pairs after the relations, outside their count and its clauses; `--list relations` leaves them out. `brain_info` gains `relations.distinct` and says `; N pairs judged distinct` after the Relations row, never as a board pair. Board findings never post a distinct pair: two tickets judged apart are nothing to link.

**Not here.**
- A pair judged unrelated before 100 has no distinct: nothing recorded the verdict. It gains one when the pass judges it again.
- The pass still judges a pair again whenever its newer thought is pooled again; skipping one whose distinct stands at the texts judged, and showing distinct at retrieval, are follow-ups if wanted.
- `--list distinct`: the pairs are counted, not listed; SQL reads them (`thought_facets`, kind `relation`, `payload->>'relation' = 'distinct'`).

**Operators.** Apply 100 before running a worker from this tree, or its unrelated verdicts are counted, not stored (the summary says so). 084 re-applied by hand takes the word away; 100 after it, or `bun migrate.ts --reapply`, puts it back.
