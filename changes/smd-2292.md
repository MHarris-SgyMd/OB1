## FORK

**What changed.** Migration 065 redefines 029's `consolidation_candidates` on 063's body plus two NULL-safe conditions: a candidate `o` is left out when the judged thought's `derived_from` names it, and when `o`'s `derived_from` names the judged thought. The body carries the sentinel `ob1:lineage-excludes-the-pair`; preflight's `lineage` check reads it and warns, naming 065 as the remedy, when 029 or 063 is re-applied by hand over it. The fifth restriction beside 029's four is stated in 065's header and in `db/README.md`'s consolidation paragraph, not in 029's — that file is frozen by release 1.0.0, comments included (the checker hashes a released migration) — and `evals/README.md`'s rule sentence names it (the eval's table stands as measured under 029's rule alone). Direct members only: the array is one level, and a deeper read belongs to the lineage table once every derived thought has a row there (SMD-1731).

**Why.** 064 made a page a thought whose `derived_from` names the evidence its sections were generated from. Once the re-embed worker gives the page thought a vector and the extractor gives it entities, 063's body returned the page's own evidence as its first candidate at cosine 1 (measured in SMD-1812's third review pass, and again here before the file lands: test-upgrade [20q]). The judge would then be asked whether the page supersedes the thought it was derived from, and a reviewer accepting the verdict would archive the evidence while the page still named it — a derivation eating its input. The same shape stood for any thought captured with `derived_from`; pages make it routine. Re-deriving is `rebuild_derived`'s door (063), not supersession's.
