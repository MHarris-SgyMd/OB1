## FORK

A derivation and its inputs are never paired for judgement — consolidation_candidates leaves out every thought a thought's derived_from names, from either side (SMD-2292)

**What changed.** Migration 066 redefines 029's `consolidation_candidates` on 063's body plus two NULL-safe conditions: a candidate `o` is left out when the judged thought's `derived_from` names it, and when `o`'s `derived_from` names the judged thought. The body carries the sentinel `ob1:lineage-excludes-the-pair`; preflight's `lineage` check reads it and warns, naming 066 as the remedy, when 063 is re-applied by hand over it (029 re-applied is caught earlier by the producer-count arm, and the remedies run 061, 063, 066 in turn). The fifth restriction beside 029's four is stated in 066's header and in `db/README.md`'s consolidation paragraph, not in 029's — that file is frozen by release 1.0.0, comments included (the checker hashes a released migration) — and `evals/README.md`'s rule sentence names it (the eval's table stands as measured under 029's rule alone). Direct members only: the array is one level, and a deeper read belongs to the lineage table once every derived thought has a row there (SMD-1731) — expect the transitive shape from SMD-2143's writers (a page citing an earlier page or a digest is judged against that page's evidence; SMD-2314 is the deeper read, and until then a reviewer reads `trace_provenance(newer)`). Siblings, two pages from one evidence, are still judged. A proposal already standing on a lineage pair is left as it is and is not marked — the listing reads nothing of `derived_from`, the pass never replaces it, the recorder has no lineage guard; SMD-2313 counts and flags such rows, no verdict is written at apply time.

**Why.** 064 made a page a thought whose `derived_from` names the evidence its sections were generated from. Once the re-embed worker gives the page thought a vector and the extractor gives it entities, 063's body returned the page's own evidence as its first candidate at cosine 1 (measured in SMD-1812's third review pass, and again here before the file lands: test-upgrade [20r]). The judge would then be asked whether the page supersedes the thought it was derived from, and a reviewer accepting the verdict would archive the evidence while the page still named it — a derivation eating its input. The same shape stood for any thought captured with `derived_from`; pages make it routine. Re-deriving is `rebuild_derived`'s door (063), not supersession's.

**Held.** test-schema [60]: one body, 066's last, the sentinel and 063's stale clause in it, both directions read NULL-safe; a page generated from evidence E, re-embedded through the 11-argument `update_thought` (a vector and its label) and extracted sharing an entity with E is judged against an unrelated near-duplicate D (cosine 1, one shared entity) and never against E; moved twenty days back so it is the older side, E never lists it while D does; a digest captured with `derived_from [E, X]` is judged against D alone; a thought naming nothing is judged against every older thought sharing the entity, as 063 had it; with E deleted the digest still names it and X stays out; a re-apply a no-op. test-upgrade [20r]: onto a populated brain at 064 the page's candidates are E and D before the file and D alone after; a proposal planted on the page and its second evidence under 063's body is pending and unmoved after the file, one on a third evidence set stale stays stale (and is re-found under 063's body before it), the proposals table read whole; no audit row, no thought, no lineage row moved; the ledger baselined, a re-apply a no-op with each condition named on failure; refused by name without 025, 029 or 063. `server-portable/test-preflight.ts`: 063 re-applied over 066 warns on the candidate body naming 066; 066 after it is the clean census. Mutant, run by hand: the two conditions removed from the body, six of [60]'s assertions fail (the page's candidates read E, D, X — the measured bug — the reverse, the digest, the deletion, the shape and the re-apply) and three of [20r]'s (after the file, the reverse, and the re-apply). Suites: test-schema 2211, test-live 857, test-upgrade 452, test-preflight 568, test-replay, the checker, tsc on db and server-portable.

**Not taken.** An auto-reject of proposals already standing on a lineage pair at apply time — a rejection is a verdict with a reviewer's name on it, and the file promises no data step; the flag and a listed reject (SMD-2313) come first. A remedy naming this file when preflight's older-bodies arm reads `consolidation_candidates` alone — 029 re-applied by hand always lands its 8-argument proposal writer beside 061's, so the producer-count arm fires first and that state is not reached.

**Follow-ups.** SMD-2313: preflight counts unreviewed proposals standing on a `derived_from` pair and the listing flags them `lineage`. SMD-2314: exclude transitive lineage — ancestors through a bounded trace, or a `derivations` walk once every derived thought has a row there — the shape SMD-2143's writers make routine. SMD-2315: 032's `validate_derived_from` refuses a self-reference and decides what a cycle does.
