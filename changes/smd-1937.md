## Changelog

The entity-gate spike, measured on 201 blind-graded dogfood mentions the deterministic gate cannot judge: Verdict v1.4 behind the typed-decision tier does not beat the deterministic shape gate by the pre-registered margin (+10.1 points of balanced accuracy, 95% interval −0.7 to +21.0), types worse than the extractor (21–55% against 71%), and gives a stored confidence that barely trades precision for recall; the deterministic gate ships first, and nothing enters the extraction path (SMD-1937).

## FORK

A typed-decision gate after the entity extractor, measured on the cases a regex cannot judge — no margin over the deterministic gate; nothing enters the extraction path (SMD-1937)

**What changed.** Evals only:
- `evals/eval-jev-gate.ts` is rebuilt as SMD-1937's harness. SMD-2050's slice
  left it scoring one framing on numeric names against a weak positive label.
  It now scores the arms below on a graded set, against pre-registered baselines:
  - B0 = `NUMERIC_NAME_RE`;
  - B1 = SMD-1935's whole gate (numbers, the type vocabulary, an identifier's
    shape typed person or place);
  - the arms: v1, v2, claim, pertype, choice and the extractor's column;
  - a dev/test split by md5 of the thought, with the threshold, temperature and
    Platt refit taken from dev;
  - the paired-bootstrap margin, per-grader sensitivity, the typing confusion,
    and SMD-1925's keep-at-threshold table;
  - the drop-the-mechanism identity, a 1,000-shuffle permutation test, and the
    cost at real candidates-per-thought.
  `--dump-sample` writes the grading sample, which is brain text meant for
  outside the tree. `--cache` keeps the answers (probabilities and logits only),
  keyed by what was asked.
- `evals/fixtures/entity-gate-grades.json` holds the graded set, as ids and
  numbers only (check 9):
  - the pre-registered sample of 201 mentions whose names pass B0: every person,
    place and organization, and 50 each of tool, topic and project;
  - graded blind by two Claude graders from the name and a window alone, both
    graders' labels kept beside the adjudication;
  - kappa 0.82 on validity and 0.83 on the type.
- `bun eval-jev-gate.ts --self-check` is a fork-checks step and a package script.

**Why.** SMD-2050's first number (AUROC 0.509) used the obvious cases, which the
regex already gets right. SMD-1937's bar is a margin over the deterministic
gate on the ambiguous ones, before a second model enters a path SMD-1879
already strains.

**Pre-registered** in 793b1158, before the grades (770cf0be) and before any tier
call on them: the baselines, the sample, the split, the arms, and the margin
(≥ 10 points of balanced accuracy over B1 on test, with the interval above 0;
per-type typing ≥ 10 points over the extractor).

**Measured** on the dogfood brain at 053, with Verdict v1.4 on the host and the
harness in a container on the stack's network (the tables are in
`evals/README.md`):
- **The margin is not met.** v2 (validity in the prompt's own words) was chosen
  on dev.
  - v2 − B1 = +10.1 points, interval −0.7 to +21.0.
  - Against either grader alone it is +7.8 or +7.9, interval from −3.2.
- **The signal is weak but real.** v2's AUROC is 0.658, with permutation p = 0.005.
- **Typing is worse.** The extractor gets 71% of types right, the choice 55%,
  and the per-type binaries 21%, since they call nearly everything a tool.
- **Calibration.**
  - The served p leans valid (the binaries average 0.54–0.60, the choice 0.75)
    while 42% of the mentions are valid.
  - A temperature alone flattens every arm to a coin.
  - Platt brings the ECE to 0.03–0.12, but only v2 beats the base-rate
    constant's Brier score, by about 6%.
- **SMD-1925.** The extractor's column is two values with AUROC 0.487. v2's P
  keeps 60.5% precision and 60.5% recall at 0.4, and 87.5% precision with 18.4%
  recall at 0.5.
- **Cost, per thought.**
  - One binary a candidate: 0.87 s p50, 3.5 s p95, about a tenth of an
    extraction call.
  - Six binaries: 5.0 s p50, 17.6 s p95, as much as the call.
- **What the extractor mints.** 120 of the 201 graded mentions are invalid: 42 of
  50 topics and 37 of 50 tools. They are mostly code identifiers and generic
  words.

**What it settles.** SMD-1935's deterministic gate ships first, and Verdict v1.4
stays out of the extraction path, both as a gate and as a typer. SemIf
(SMD-2052) is the one untried lever on this harness. The other is the extractor
not minting code identifiers and generic words at all.

**Held.** The self-check covers:
- B0 and B1 on the shapes SMD-1935 names and on the ones they must keep;
- AUROC, balanced accuracy and the dev threshold;
- the temperature and Platt refits recovering a drawn slope and offset, and a
  temperature flattening a confident column the labels do not follow (its mutant,
  a clamped log loss, walks to 1/T = 50: the bug the first live run showed on
  the extractor's column);
- kappa, the bootstrap's determinism, and the permutation test both ways;
- the gate-off identity;
- the nine decisions per candidate and what each arm reads from them;
- the grades file's shape, and the committed file.

**Not taken.** No framing sweep past the pre-registered five: choosing among
more framings on 111 dev mentions would spend the test split's honesty. No
host-local latency: the dogfood worker runs in a container, so container to
host is the path it would pay.
