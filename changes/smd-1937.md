## Changelog

The entity-gate spike, measured on 201 blind-graded dogfood mentions the deterministic gate cannot judge: Verdict v1.4 behind the typed-decision tier does not beat the deterministic shape gate by the pre-registered margin (+10.1 points of balanced accuracy, 95% interval −1.0 to +21.0; under one grader's labels alone, −6.1), types worse than the extractor (21–53% against 71%), and gives a stored confidence that barely trades precision for recall; the deterministic gate ships first, and nothing enters the extraction path (SMD-1937).

## FORK

A typed-decision gate after the entity extractor, measured on the cases a regex cannot judge — no margin over the deterministic gate; nothing enters the extraction path (SMD-1937)

**What changed.** Evals only.
- `evals/eval-jev-gate.ts` is rebuilt as SMD-1937's harness. It replaces SMD-2050's
  single framing scored on numeric names against a weak positive label, and
  scores against pre-registered baselines:
  - **B0** = `NUMERIC_NAME_RE`.
  - **B1** = SMD-1935's whole gate: numbers, the type vocabulary, and an
    identifier's shape typed person or place.
  - **Five tier arms:** v1, v2, claim, pertype and choice.
  - **The extractor's column** is scored as a sixth arm.
- **Dev/test and the verdict.** A dev/test split by md5 of the thought. Per arm,
  a temperature, a Platt refit and a threshold are taken from dev. The arm is
  chosen on dev, and a paired bootstrap in fixture order gives the verdict.
- **Sensitivity.** The whole procedure is refit under each grader's labels, and
  the margin is recomputed without the windows that lack the name.
- **Also reported:** the typing confusion, SMD-1925's keep-at-threshold table, a
  1,000-shuffle permutation test, and the cost of whole thoughts' candidate
  lists.
- **Flags.** `--dump-sample` writes the grading sample, which is brain text, to
  outside the tree. `--cache` keeps the tier's answers (probabilities and logits
  only), keyed by the model's provenance and what was asked, and is written
  however the run ends.
- `evals/fixtures/entity-gate-grades.json` holds the pre-registered 201 mentions
  whose names pass B0, as ids and numbers only (check 9). It covers every person,
  place and organization, and 50 each of tool, topic and project. Two Claude
  graders labelled them blind, from the name and a window alone; both graders'
  labels are kept beside the adjudication. Their agreement is kappa 0.82 on
  validity, and 0.94 on the type where both call a mention valid.
- `bun eval-jev-gate.ts --self-check` runs as a fork-checks step and a package script.

**Why.** SMD-2050's first number (AUROC 0.509) used the obvious cases, which the
regex already gets right. SMD-1937's bar is a margin over the deterministic gate
on the ambiguous ones, before a second model enters a path SMD-1879 already
strains. It was pre-registered in 793b1158, before the grades (770cf0be) and
before any tier call on them:
- the baselines, the sample, the split and the arms;
- ≥ 10 points of balanced accuracy over B1 on test, with the interval above 0;
- per-type typing ≥ 10 points over the extractor.

**Held.** The self-check holds B0 and B1 on the shapes SMD-1935 names and the ones
they must keep. Beside them, each with a mutant killed:
- AUROC, balanced accuracy and the dev threshold with its tie;
- the refits: a drawn slope and offset recovered, a constant score giving the
  base rate, and a confident column the labels do not follow being flattened;
- kappa with unequal marginals;
- the bootstrap's pairing (nested errors never cross zero) and its percentiles;
- the permutation p, exact at 1/(n+1), with ties reaching;
- the gate-off identity;
- the pinned split and the window;
- each arm's reading, including the choice naming no type on an abstention,
  number or generic;
- every grades-file rule, and the committed file at 201 mentions, 111 of them dev.

Of the review's 88 mutants, 84 are killed. The four left are a NaN guard, the
loss's mean, and Platt's line search and start, each of which is killed only
alongside the other.

**Measured after.** On the dogfood brain at 053, with Verdict v1.4 on the host and
the harness in a container on the stack's network. The tables are in
`evals/README.md`.
- **The margin is not met.** v2, validity in the prompt's words, was chosen on
  dev by 0.637 to the choice's 0.635.
  - v2 − B1 = +10.1 points, interval −1.0 to +21.0.
  - Without the 4 test windows that lack the name, +10.7 (−0.6 to +21.8).
  - Under grader A's labels alone, v2 again, +7.9 (−3.3 to +19.0).
  - Under grader B's labels alone, the choice, −6.1 (−17.1 to +4.8).
- **The signal is weak but real.** v2's AUROC is 0.658; 4 of 1,000 shuffles
  reach it.
- **Typing is worse than the extractor's.** The extractor gets 71% of types
  right, always saying `project` 58%, the choice 53%, and the per-type binaries
  21%, since they call 23 of the 38 valid mentions a tool.
- **Calibration.**
  - The served p leans valid: the binaries average 0.54–0.60 and the choice
    0.75, while 42% of the mentions are valid.
  - A temperature alone flattens every arm toward a coin.
  - Platt brings the ECE to 0.03–0.12, but only v2 then beats a constant at
    dev's base rate (Brier skill 3.6%).
- **SMD-1925.** The extractor's column is 1.00 on 89 of the 90 test mentions,
  AUROC 0.487. v2's P gives 60.5% precision at 60.5% recall at 0.4, and 87.5%
  precision at 18.4% recall at 0.5.
- **Cost.** Over 40 whole thoughts, one binary a candidate costs 0.89 s p50, one
  request. Six a candidate cost 4.8 s p50 and 13.0 s p90, in 2–4 requests. The
  comparison is SMD-1879's 9-second call on a 3,000-token thought: about a
  tenth of it, and about half.
- **What the extractor mints.** 120 of the 201 graded mentions are invalid,
  including 42 of 50 topics and 37 of 50 tools. They are mostly code identifiers
  and generic words.
- **What it settles.** SMD-1935's deterministic gate ships first. Verdict v1.4
  stays out of the extraction path, as a gate and as a typer. SemIf (SMD-2052)
  is the one untried lever on this harness; the other is the extractor not
  minting such names at all.

**Not taken.**
- **No framing sweep past the pre-registered five.** Choosing among more framings
  on 111 dev mentions would spend the test split's honesty.
- **No bootstrap by thought.** The 90 test mentions sit in 60 thoughts, so
  resampling thoughts would only widen an interval that already fails.
- **No logits or temperature guard in the client.** That is server-portable's
  contract to change, and the live answers are sound: all 2,280 binaries.
- **No host-local latency.** The dogfood worker runs in a container, so container
  to host is the path it would pay.
