## Changelog

A tool call that outruns the runtime's 10 s idle timeout reaches the client: every SSE response carries a `: keepalive` comment frame every 5 s for as long as the tool runs, and a client that closes the connection first is logged by method and tool, never by content (SMD-1864).

## FORK

The tool-call stream is kept alive for as long as the tool runs, and a client that leaves is logged — a large capture no longer dies at ten seconds with no trace (SMD-1864)

**What changed.** `server-portable/index.ts` passes every response the MCP
transport returns through `withSseKeepalive`: when the body is
`text/event-stream`, it is piped through a `TransformStream` whose timer
enqueues a `: keepalive\n\n` comment frame every `SSE_KEEPALIVE_MS` (5 000) until
the transport closes the stream (`flush`, which also marks the request
settled) or the client leaves (the request's abort signal, or the next frame
finding the readable side closed — either stops the timer, so an abandoned
call leaks nothing); any other response is returned as it is. A line beginning
`:` is a comment in the WHATWG event-stream grammar, so every parser — the MCP
SDK's `eventsource-parser` included — drops it and no client sees an event.
The route also reads the request's JSON-RPC envelope through Hono's cached
request (the transport's own `req.json()` reads the same cache) for
`requestLabel` — the method and, for a tool call, the tool's name; a batch by
its first message; `?` for the unreadable; never the arguments — and registers
one abort listener: a client that closes the connection before the response is
complete logs `abandonedRequestLine`, "request abandoned by the client after
N s: tools/call capture_thought — …the call runs to its end on this side, so a
capture may still have landed". The Bun export gains no `idleTimeout`, and the
comment on it says why.

**Why.** Loading the board into the dogfood brain on 2026-09-21, 214 of 216
issues captured; the two largest failed deterministically at ~9.8 s with
`ECONNRESET` and nothing in the server's log, and raising `idleTimeout` on the
export fixed them. The ticket read that as Bun killing a slow handler. Measured
here first, on Bun 1.4.0, macOS and the `oven/bun:1.4.0-alpine` image alike: a
handler still pending at 12 s answers normally under the default, body read or
not, 2 B or 20 KiB, fresh or reused connection; a *streamed* response — a bare
`ReadableStream`, a `TransformStream`, Hono's `streamSSE`, the real transport
with a sleeping tool, headers flushed at 3 ms in every case — is closed at the
first of Bun's 4-second sweeps once silent for its 10 s, so after 8 to 12 s by
phase (streams opened at 0, 1, 2 and 3 s all closed at t = 12 s; one opened at
5 s closed at 16 s); `request.signal` aborts, the handler's next write throws
"Controller is already closed", Bun prints its own `warn: Bun.serve() timed
out a request after 10 seconds`; a comment frame every 5 s keeps the stream
open. `@hono/mcp` 0.3.2 answers a POST with `streamSSE` at once and writes the
result when the tool returns — the standalone GET stream has a 30 s ping, the
POST stream none — so the tool-call stream is exactly the shape the sweep
closes, and the fix is the stream's, not the number's. The sweep is why a
first stub at 11.5 s slipped under the cliff on a fresh server, and why the
suite's silence is 13 s.

**Held.** `test-server.ts` [17], four requests run at once (~13 s): a bare
Bun server's silent SSE stream is reset at a sweep between 8 and 12 s — the
premise, measured each run, and the drop-the-mechanism mutant in the same
breath; the same stream through `withSseKeepalive` reaches its event carrying
two comment frames; the real server, served with every field the export
declares, answers `search_thoughts` after a 13 s embedding from the suite's
slow stub, with the call's envelope and two frames; a client whose signal ends
the body read at 1.5 s is logged once, the line byte-equal to
`abandonedRequestLine` for `tools/call search_thoughts` at 1.5 s and free of
the query text; `requestLabel`'s four shapes; a non-stream response passes
through untouched. With the wrapper dropped from the route, the real-server
case fails — `ECONNRESET` at 11.9 s, no frames, and the sweep's kill logged as
a second abandoned request — and the rest hold. 201 assertions (188 before).
The export is now served as `Bun.serve({ ...worker, port: 0 })`, so what it
says about the runtime is what the suite measures.

**Measured after.** The suite's stub at 13 s answered in 13 005 ms with two
frames on the way; the bare control was reset at 11 918 ms (the 12 s sweep),
the wrapped control answered at 13 004 ms.

**Not taken.** *`idleTimeout: 240` or 255 on the export* (the ticket's first
item): a handler is never killed, so the number only moves the cliff a stream
falls off from 10 s to 255 s — a capture on a slow local model can pass that —
and lets a dead keep-alive socket linger 25× longer, which is what the timer is
for. *A per-request `server.timeout()`*: Bun-only, and needs the server
handle Hono does not pass. *Pinging through the transport*: its keepalive is
the standalone GET stream's alone, and that stream is 405 here (change 75).
*A log line on tool errors*: the transport streams the result, so the route
cannot see it; that is SMD-1849's per-request logging, and the abandoned line
is the one this defect needed.

**Follow-ups.** None filed. The partial write the memory recorded — a capture
the server finished after the client gave up, then a retry — is a duplicate
only for changed text: `upsert_thought` keys on the content fingerprint, so a
retry of the same thought is the existing row.

**Upstream status.** Not applicable: upstream serves from Deno Deploy, whose
limits are wall-clock, and its transport is the same SDK's.
