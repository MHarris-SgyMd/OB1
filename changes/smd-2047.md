## Changelog

A release fragment no longer names another fragment by its path, which the cut numbers and leaves pointing at nothing: the pending fragments name the ticket instead, and check 16 refuses such a path, as the release step does (SMD-2047).

## FORK

Fragments name another change by its ticket, not its fragment's path — the cut renumbers every fragment (SMD-2047)

**What changed.**
- **The rule** is in `scripts/fragments.ts`'s `fragmentProblems`, which check 16 and the release step both run. A fragment whose text holds a `changes/smd-NNNN.md` path is refused, and the message names the path. A path pinned to a commit (`<sha>:changes/smd-NNNN.md`) still resolves and passes, as SMD-2679's pointer to its mutant lists does.
- **Eight paths in five pending fragments** now name the ticket:
  - SMD-2295's record: two, to SMD-2287's;
  - SMD-2532's: one, to SMD-3101's;
  - SMD-2687's: two, to the fragments of SMD-2920, SMD-2923, SMD-2288 and SMD-2878;
  - SMD-2878's: one, to SMD-2890's;
  - SMD-3101's: two, to SMD-2874's, which v1.9.0 had already numbered.
- **`changes/README.md`** states the rule beside the version-contract paragraph, and lists it among what the checker validates.

**Why.** `scripts/assemble-release.ts` numbers each fragment at the cut and rewrites no mention of one, so a backticked path to a fragment points at nothing after the cut. Six of these paths would have died at v2.0.0's cut, and two already pointed at a fragment v1.9.0 had numbered. The board review of 2026-10-26 found them. The maintainer chose to name tickets now and hold the rule in the checker, rather than have the release step rewrite mentions.

**Held.**
- Check 16's probe: a fragment naming another fragment's path is refused.
- Its non-probe: a commit-pinned path, a ticket named instead of a path, and the letters pattern all pass.
- `check-fork-consistency` passes on the tree: no fragment holds an unpinned path.

**Not done.** Mentions of fragment paths outside `changes/` stay SMD-2047's Work: `db/README.md`, `docs/event-log-as-truth.md`, `evals/README.md` and two code comments. Its options are a rewrite at the cut, or a standing check over the tree's `.md` files.
