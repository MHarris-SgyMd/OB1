## Changelog

The session hook (1.10.0) reaches a forget's text on every brain this machine copies to (SMD-4411). A forget drops the thought's queued copies from every brain's queue: the first brain's own in a two-way fan-out, a brain's with no write key at the forget's run, and a queue a brain no longer in the config left on disk. A brain with no write key that has been copied to is held the forget, which lands once a key is set, and `--check` warns of copies waiting so. The first brain is queued the forget too, behind any copy it kept for having maybe landed, and a forget a brain answers as forgotten already is sent again by the thought's own `client_key`, which reaches a copy that landed there again. `bun session-capture.mjs --copy-forget <id> [--client-key <uuid>] [--brain <name>] [--from <name>]` copies a forget the hook never queued: its config unreadable at that run, which the hook's log now names with this command, or the hook not run. The first brain refuses a repeat as `FORGOTTEN`, so a repeat through the connector copies nothing. **Upgrading:** pull the hook's checkout; no settings change. If a brain is without its write key now and you forgot thoughts meanwhile on 1.9.0, its queue may hold copies of them that 1.9.0 neither dropped nor held a forget for. Before setting the key back, run `--copy-forget <id> --brain <name>` for each; `--check`'s warning says so.

## Record

The session hook's forget reaches every brain's queue, and a missed forget can be copied again (SMD-4411)

SMD-1723 PR 3's review passes 7 and 9 named three ways a forget's text stays on a brain this machine copies to:
- a forget the hook never queued, which the first brain then refuses as `FORGOTTEN`;
- a brain with no write key at the forget's run, which got neither the forget nor the drop, so a capture queued for it earlier landed once its key came back;
- the first brain's own queue in a two-way fan-out, never dropped.

**What changed** (`recipes/session-capture-hook/session-capture.mjs`, 1.10.0).
- **`queueForget`:** a forget's queueing, shared by the hook and the new command.
  - Each brain it covers first loses its queued and given-up copies of the thought (`dropCopiesOf`). It covers every configured brain, or the `--brain` one alone, and with every brain also the queues on disk of brains no longer in the config.
  - Then the forget goes in: queued where the brain has a write key, held (`held: true`) where it has none but its `writes/` exists.
  - The brain the forget was made on is queued it too where it has a write key, whatever its queue shows, since a drain racing the drop can write a kept copy back after the drop read the queue. Where it has none, it is held the forget only if its queue still carries a copy.
  - The `sent`/`sending` rule is unchanged: a copy that may have reached the brain stays, and the forget queued behind it reaches it.
- **`postWrite`:** a forget answered `FORGOTTEN` is sent again by the thought's own `client_key` (`alt`): a kept copy sent again ahead of it lands as a new thought under that key, which a forget by the brain's own id no longer reaches. `FORGOTTEN` then `NOT_FOUND` is done. A plain `NOT_FOUND` carries `notFound`.
- **`drainWrites`:** a held forget is re-dated at its first send, so a key set back after a week doesn't give it up at its first fault. A forget that lands removes the given-up copies of its thought from `writes/dead/`, where one sent again ahead of it and refused for good holds the text. Each outcome carries its file's name.
- **`fanOutPlan` and `fanOutHook`:** a forget with no target goes on to `queueForget`, and each queue it went into is drained, the first brain's included.
- **`--copy-forget <id>`:** builds the op the hook would have and queues it through `queueForget`. The op's `confirm` is the id, `detach_citations` is true, `--client-key` becomes its `alt`, and its `from` is `--copy-forget`. `--brain` narrows it to one brain, which may be keyless if it has been copied to. `--from` names the brain it was forgotten on. It drains each queue in-process and prints every outcome under the brain's name.
  - It says "nothing was forgotten" with what to try for a name that found nothing. It says when a held forget, a kept one, or one queued behind a running drain has not landed yet (all exit 1).
  - Exit 2 for an id or key that is no uuid, an unknown brain, a `--brain` with no write key never copied to, or a config with no brain to reach.
- **The hook's run whose config does not load:** it logs the forget it could not copy, with the `--copy-forget` (and `--client-key`) that copies it. An unreadable `key_file` or `write_key_file` is named by its setting and path.
- **`--check`:** for a brain with no write key whose queue holds copies, it warns how many wait and how many are forgets. It also names the `--copy-forget --brain` to run, before setting the key back, for a thought forgotten meanwhile on 1.9.0.
- The README's fan-out section and `metadata.json` (1.10.0) say so.

**Held.** The hook's suite [12]:
- **Two-way fan-out:** stable and the canary share one fake store, and stable's own thought is forgotten there already. A forget through stable drops stable's unsent capture and edit of the thought. It keeps the sent copy and queues itself behind it. The copy lands again under the thought's key, and the forget, answered `FORGOTTEN`, is sent again by that key and forgets it; no thought keeps the text.
- **A canary with no write key at the forget's run:**
  - Its unsent copy is dropped, two forgets are held behind a sent copy, and `--check` warns of three copies, two of them forgets. With the key back, the drain sends the sent copy, then both forgets.
  - A held forget back-dated eight days, with the canary away at the first drain, is re-dated and kept, while the sent copy ahead of it is given up. The next drain lands the forget and removes that copy from `dead/`.
  - A keyless brain never copied to gets no `writes/`.
- **`--copy-forget`:**
  - An upper-case id is lower-cased, confirmed and detaching, and forgets the canary's copy.
  - A repeat says nothing was forgotten, exit 1.
  - `--client-key` reaches a thought the canary knows by its own key alone. A queued edit of the thought is dropped first.
  - The receiving brain's own id, forgotten there already, with a sent copy ahead: the copy lands again and is forgotten by `--client-key`.
  - The canary away: queued, said so, exit 1, and the next drain sends it. A drain already running: said so.
  - With no write key, `--brain canary` holds the forget, exit 1, and it lands once the key is back.
  - `--brain` leaves other queues alone. `--from` spares the brain forgotten on a hold, which a keyless brain copied to otherwise gets.
  - Seven bad command lines exit 2 with nothing queued.
- **A hook run with an unreadable `write_key_file`:** it names the setting, the path, the lost forget and its `--copy-forget --client-key`.
- **Mutants:** the commit's first eleven are listed in its message. Each fails the case that holds it:
  - the first brain's queue not dropped;
  - a keyless brain's queue not dropped;
  - no hold;
  - a hold without `writes/`;
  - the plan skipping a forget with no target;
  - no `--check` warning;
  - the command without `--client-key`'s `alt`, or without the drop;
  - no "stays queued";
  - no uuid check;
  - a keyless `--brain` taken.

  Review pass 1 added thirteen more, each failing too:
  - the first brain not queued the forget;
  - `FORGOTTEN` not sent again by the key;
  - a held forget not re-dated, or not marked held;
  - `dead/` not cleaned;
  - "nothing was forgotten", or the running drain, unsaid;
  - `--brain` dropping everywhere;
  - `--from` ignored;
  - a stray queue not dropped;
  - a keyless `--brain` refused;
  - the lost forget unsaid;
  - the bare errno.

  The first set, re-anchored, still fails on the reworked code; its keyless-`--brain` mutant is gone, the rule having been reversed.

**Not here.**
- **The automatic route the ticket also named,** a repeated forget's `FORGOTTEN` read as "forgotten here; copy it": it needs the hook on `PostToolUseFailure`, which the fan-out doesn't install. The command is the explicit route.
- **A brain out of the config at the forget's run:** its queue on disk loses its copies, but it is held no forget. Once it is back, `--copy-forget --brain` sends it.
- **A held forget for a brain whose key is never set back:** it stays in `pending/`, and `--check` warns while the brain is in the config.
