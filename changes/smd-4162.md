## Changelog

`agenda` now leaves started tickets (In Progress, In Review: Linear's started type) out by default, as it leaves out claimed and blocked ones, and counts them in `leftOut.started`; `include_started` lists them. A started ticket under a live claim counts as claimed, so `include_claimed` lists it with its holder, and `holder: "mine"` still shows your own work, started or not. Each ticket says its Linear `status` and `statusType`, and the text shows the status beside the key. The answer gains `claimsLive`, how many live claims the brain records: 0 means no claim could leave anything out, as on a brain whose board is claimed through another, and the text says so (SMD-4162, migration 124).

## Record

The agenda leaves started tickets out unless asked, says each ticket's status, and counts the brain's live claims (SMD-4162)

On 2026-11-09 the canary's agenda named an In Progress ticket with another session's live claim as the top unstarted work: 123 takes every unsettled ticket, and the claim lived on stable, the brain this repo claims through. The maintainer chose to leave started tickets out by default.

**What changed.**
- **Migration 124:** `agenda` gains a ninth argument, `p_include_started` (default false), and 123's eight-argument function is dropped, so a positional call of eight reads as the default.
  - A ticket's candidate carries its head's `status` and `status_type`.
  - A started ticket no live claim covers is left out unless `p_include_started`; `left_out.started` counts it after the waiting, blocked and claimed filters. A started ticket under a live claim is the claim rule's alone: counted once, as claimed, and listed by `p_include_claimed` or under the holder (review pass 1: claimed work is started work here, so a started filter over it emptied `include_claimed`).
  - `claims_live` counts the live claims in `work_claims` on any ticket, in the scope or not; null when claims can't be read.
  - `query_log.filter`'s comment says an agenda row holds four include flags.
- **The `agenda` tool:** the `include_started` input, the store's reader (`statusType`, `leftOut.started`, `claimsLive`; an answer without the started count is refused), the MCP text (the status beside a ticket's key, the started count, a line when the brain records no live claim) and the regenerated REST client.
- **Docs:** db/README.md (124, the count, the map), server-portable/README.md's tool table, docs/priority.md, docs/agent-runner.md.
