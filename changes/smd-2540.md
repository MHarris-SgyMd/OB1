## Changelog

`evals/eval-stated-status.ts` prices how search should label a ticket a thought no longer tells right, on the dogfood brain against blind-graded labels: the reading it picks is `moved-session-settled` (the ticket's status when the session ended against now, no label when the writer's own move reached the brain within the hour), held out 29 of 30 right and 0.976 pooled; migration 092's `ticket_status_inputs` gives that reading its inputs in one call and `server-portable/stated-status.ts` ships it, held to the measured code, with no search change yet (SMD-2540).

## FORK

A ticket a thought no longer tells right, priced before it is labelled: the eval and its verdict (SMD-2540)

**What changed.** PR 1 of SMD-2540 is the measurement; the label is PR 2.
- `evals/stated-status.ts` — the pure readings: the tickets a thought names
  (077's key reading, plus list steps like `SMD-2287, 2286` and bare repeats
  like `2306 is in progress`), when it was written (stored, its supersedes
  chain's oldest row, or the session's end the hook's last line records), a
  ticket's status at a time, the status a text states after a mention, and the
  eight candidates' labels.
- `evals/eval-stated-status.ts` — read-only against a brain, no model: the
  census (labels per candidate, split by kind; tickets per labelled thought;
  pairs with no history), the samples, three pre-registered verdicts and a
  pooled precision over every graded label. `--self-check` holds the readings,
  the verdicts' arithmetic and the gold's provenance with no database, and runs
  in Fork Checks.
- `evals/fixtures/stated-status-labels.json` — 148 labels graded wrong, noise
  or useful, as ids and numbers only, in three rounds of two blind graders
  (κ 0.969, 1.000, 0.714), six disagreements to a third.

**Why.** 077 never demotes a thought that names an open ticket, so the session
summary `dd70dccc` ("2306 is in progress") is first for "what next" with
`prefer_current` on, after SMD-2306 went Done. The ticket asks for a label,
not a demotion. Which reading finds the moved tickets had to be measured: 351
of stable's 1,006 summaries were stored a day or more after their session
ended, so "when it was written" is not the row's `created_at`.

**What the measurement found.**
- The text pattern is wrong half the time (0.500–0.567): reported speech, and
  statuses that belong to a PR or a step.
- The first registration's pick, `moved-session`, passed its own sample (0/30)
  but not the pooled precision (0.897 then): a note written minutes before
  board-sync recorded its writer's own move. The registration read the sample
  alone; the second and third read both.
- Grading found an eval bug: the status history was read in audit `seq` order,
  and 046's backfill numbered older events after newer ones. It is read in the
  log's order through 060's `ob1_thought_events_in_order` now; PR 2's SQL must
  read it the same way.
- The second registration (read "then" an hour late) failed pooled (0.922).
  The third, chosen by the maintainer over stopping, qualifies:
  `moved-session-settled`, held out 29/30, 21 useful, pooled 82/84, 742
  labels on 409 thoughts (up to 12 tickets on one, 3 at p90). Its rule was
  chosen after a 53/54 post-hoc score, disclosed, so the held-out sample is the
  only independent evidence.
- The verdict is recorded in the gold and frozen (the samples that drew each
  item, the label sets that held it, each candidate's counts, as of round 3):
  the registrations are closed, so a re-run compares its snapshot with the
  record whole and reports drift, never re-decides; `--self-check` holds the
  recorded numbers and the record's invariants.

**PR 2a: the label's inputs and the shipped reading (migration 092).**
- `ticket_status_inputs(p_ids uuid[], p_issues text[])`, STABLE, one tagged
  row set: the hits with a lifecycle of their own (node_state's open IS NOT
  NULL, never labelled), each named ticket's status now (`ob1_ticket_head`, a
  known type), and one row per ticket with every status its rows held in 060's
  `ob1_thought_events_in_order` order (one row per ticket, so PostgREST's
  db-max-rows cuts no history). Each event's fields are read once and matched
  with `= ANY`: under a generic plan the first cut joined every event to every
  key (1,032 keys: 5.3 s against 148 ms). A NULL or empty list of tickets reads
  no ticket, of ids no hit. No grant; a role without `thought_sources` or
  `ob1_config` gets no label.
- 060's `ob1_thought_events_in_order` redefined to the same rows and order,
  its list an index probe whatever the plan: its old filter read the whole log
  when not inlined (held equal to 060's body on a copy of stable over ~2,600
  calls, and a replay of every event through the projector unchanged).
- `server-portable/stated-status.ts`: `moved-session-settled` as measured —
  the tickets named by key or list step, the session end else the stored
  time, the settle hour, the text line capped at three. The eval's self-check
  holds it equal to `evals/stated-status.ts` on hand cases and a seeded fuzz of
  2,000; six mutants of it die.
- `ticketStatusInputs` on both stores, best-effort as `supersededAmong`: a
  brain before 092 answers null and the search goes on.

**Not here.** The label on the reply (PR 2b: `search_thoughts` and `search`,
its `structuredContent` field, the e2e tests, a scratch restore of stable and
the paired cost per search). Two hazards inside the measured precision stay
stated: a list step can take a count for a ticket (`SMD-2306,
1006 summaries`), and a ticket's history spans the rows carrying its key now.
Whether a note of nothing but moved statuses should also be demoted stays a
measured follow-up.
