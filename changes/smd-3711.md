## Changelog

A deleted thought's or entity's priority statements now close (SMD-3711, migration 134). Deleting a thought or an entity, by `delete_thought`, a raw DELETE or extraction's pruning, closes every open statement on it with the reason `deleted`, so `priority_of` answers unknown for it and `priority_history` shows why each closed. Merging an entity restates the loser's statements on the survivor. A statement that takes its due time from a deleted item keeps its own level. The extraction grant group, whose pruning is the commonest entity delete, gains what closing takes. Any other role without the priority grants still deletes as before; `ob1_close_orphan_priorities()` closes what such a delete left, and runs once on upgrade for the items deleted before it.

## Record

A deleted thought's or entity's priority statements close — close reason `deleted`, by delete triggers on every delete path; a merged entity's restated on its survivor; a sweep for what a role without the priority grants left; migration 134 (SMD-3711)

**Why.** 116 keeps every priority statement, and its guard lets a row change only by closing. Deleting the item did nothing to its statements: they stayed open, `priority_current` kept the winner, and `priority_of` answered for an item the brain no longer held. SMD-3663's review pass 3 found it.

**What changed.** The maintainer chose (2026-11-12) triggers with a sweep, moving a merged entity's statements, and keeping a due reference's statement open. Migration 134:
- **A close reason, `deleted`:** `priority_assertions_closed` is replaced with the three reasons; `closed_by` stays for `replaced` alone.
- **AFTER DELETE row triggers on `thoughts` and `ob1_entities`** close every open statement on a deleted item as `deleted`, through `ob1_priority_close_items`, under the item's priority lock, and re-project it. A delete in a transaction above READ COMMITTED skips, as the priority writers refuse one there. They fire on `delete_thought`'s projector, a raw DELETE, a cascade and the extraction passes' pruning.
- **The extraction group** gains `SELECT, UPDATE` on `priority_assertions` and `SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE` on `priority_current`, granted to each role holding `DELETE` on `ob1_entities` already, so its pruning closes in the trigger. 116's guard lets it close a statement, never write or reopen one.
- **Any other role without the priority grants** (`ob1_priority_can_close()`) deletes as before: the trigger skips. **`ob1_close_orphan_priorities()`** closes every open statement on a thought or an entity the brain no longer holds. It runs once on upgrade, and after that when an operator calls it.
- **`merge_entities`** (016's, carried) restates each open statement on the loser on the survivor, by the same maker, with its level, times, reason, flag, tier and `asserted_at`, and closes the loser's, and the maker's earlier open one on the survivor, as replaced by it. Where the maker's latest word on the survivor is later, by when it was said (a statement's `asserted_at`, a restatement keeping its original's; a clear's time), it stands: a statement replaces the loser's, a clear clears it. A maker is matched as the writers match one. A restatement is the survivor's newest within its tier. A due reference naming the survivor is dropped from the copy. A role that could not restate merges as before. Its answer gains `priorities_moved`.
- **A due reference naming the deleted item** is left as it is: its statement keeps its own level, and the read finds no due time through it.

The server reads `deleted` (`store.ts`) and the history says it ("closed at …, when its item was deleted"). `docs/priority.md` decision 2, `server-portable/README.md` and `db/README.md` say it.

**Not changed.** A ticket is no row of its own and its head is rebuilt wholesale, so deleting a ticket's row closes that row's statements alone. Open items are dropped, not deleted. A reason's text stays: removing it is forgetting, SMD-1723's. A writer asserting on an item whose delete commits after the writer checked it leaves its statement open for the sweep: locking every deleted key would take a lock per row of a bulk delete. Filed as SMD-4241: an entity re-derived under a new id, a retype merge (056's), and due references to a merged entity.
