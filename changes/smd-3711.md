## Changelog

A deleted thought's or entity's priority statements now close (SMD-3711, migration 134). Deleting a thought or an entity, by `delete_thought`, a raw DELETE or extraction's pruning, closes every open statement on it with the reason `deleted`, so `priority_of` answers unknown for it and `priority_history` shows why each closed. Merging an entity restates the loser's statements on the survivor. A statement that takes its due time from a deleted item keeps its own level. A role without the priority grants still deletes as before; `ob1_close_orphan_priorities()` closes what such a delete left, and runs once on upgrade for the items deleted before it.

## Record

A deleted thought's or entity's priority statements close — close reason `deleted`, by delete triggers on every delete path; a merged entity's restated on its survivor; a sweep for what a role without the priority grants left; migration 134 (SMD-3711)

**Why.** 116 keeps every priority statement, and its guard lets a row change only by closing. Deleting the item did nothing to its statements: they stayed open, `priority_current` kept the winner, and `priority_of` answered for an item the brain no longer held. SMD-3663's review pass 3 found it.

**What changed.** The maintainer chose (2026-11-12) triggers with a sweep, moving a merged entity's statements, and keeping a due reference's statement open. Migration 134:
- **A close reason, `deleted`:** `priority_assertions_closed` is replaced with the three reasons; `closed_by` stays for `replaced` alone.
- **AFTER DELETE statement triggers on `thoughts` and `ob1_entities`** close every open statement on a deleted item as `deleted`, through `ob1_priority_close_items`, and re-project it. They fire on `delete_thought`'s projector, a raw DELETE, a cascade and the extraction passes' pruning.
- **A role without the priority grants** (`ob1_priority_can_close()`) deletes as before: the trigger skips. **`ob1_close_orphan_priorities()`** closes every open statement on a thought or an entity the brain no longer holds. It runs once on upgrade, and after that when an operator calls it.
- **`merge_entities`** (016's, carried) restates each open statement on the loser on the survivor, by the same maker, with its level, times, reason, flag, tier and `asserted_at`, and closes the loser's as replaced by it. Where the maker's own statement on the survivor is the later written, that one stands. A due reference naming the survivor is dropped from the copy. Its answer gains `priorities_moved`.
- **A due reference naming the deleted item** is left as it is: its statement keeps its own level, and the read finds no due time through it.

The server reads `deleted` (`store.ts`) and the history says it ("closed at …, when its item was deleted"). `docs/priority.md` decision 2, `server-portable/README.md` and `db/README.md` say it.

**Not changed.** A ticket is no row of its own and its head is rebuilt wholesale, so deleting a ticket's row closes that row's statements alone. Open items are dropped, not deleted. A reason's text stays: removing it is forgetting, SMD-1723's.
