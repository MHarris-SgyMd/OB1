## Changelog

Every read that shows a priority — `search`, `fetch`, `search_thoughts`, `search_thoughts_keyword`, `list_thoughts`, the agenda and `plan_context`'s board — marks an item sleep holds a pending stale finding on, and a level's source (an open item it is read from, a ticket it inherits from) alike: `unreviewed` (the finding's id, its newest statement's time, age in days, level and maker, its window; false for none, null when not checked), the items past the read's reach named in `unreviewedUnchecked`, and over MCP `…, medium (2) by "codex", stated 75 days ago, unreviewed` after its priority. The compact MCP values carry a mark only where one is present, and a failed read once, as `unreviewedRead: false`; `search` and `fetch` say what the mark means in `unreviewedNote`; `plan_context` gains `notes`, saying marks not read for its time. A mark, never a weight: it moves no order or rank (SMD-3671 PR 3, no migration).

## Record

The unreviewed mark — every read that shows a priority marks an item sleep holds a pending stale finding on, "stated N days ago, unreviewed", and moves no order; PR 3 (SMD-3671)

**Why.** Change 533's scan (migration 142) proposes a stale statement as a finding, and change 543 lets the operator list and decide it, but until then every read kept showing the stale level at full weight with nothing to say it was unreviewed (docs/priority.md decision 6). The maintainer chose (2026-11-18): the mark means a pending stale finding, so it follows the scan's own rules and lags it by up to six hours, with no migration; and every read that shows a priority carries it.

**What changed.**
- The store's `staleUnreviewedOn` reads the pending stale findings on a reply's items in one statement, by 142's partial index of the pending (both literals in its text), at most 1000 items. `normaliseStaleUnreviewed` reads each leniently: `statedAt` the newest `asserted_at` among its statements, so the age never overstates, and `days` the whole days from it to the database's now, read on the same row: the clock that stamped the statements, and the reader pure. Statements, or any one's time, not read leave both null: the item is marked without an age, since a finding stands on it.
- `PriorityOn`, the agenda's items and `plan_context`'s board tickets gain `unreviewed`: `{findingId, statedAt, days, level, maker, windowDays}` (the newest stale statement's level and maker, which need not set the level shown), `false` for none, `null` not checked; their `fromItem` and `inheritedFrom` carry their source's. Not checked is a failed read (a brain before 142, a role without SELECT), said once, or an item past the read's reach of 1000, named in the result's `unreviewedUnchecked`. A priority read's marks are read beside it, over every row's own thought and the tickets it asks, and a second read asks a level's sources only when there are any and the first read something; a ranked page that reuses its window's answers is marked too. The agenda reads its items and their sources in one read after it decided its list; `plan_context` reads its knowledge's marks in the knowledge's share of the deadline and its board's, for the 15 tickets it shows, in what is left, each out of time or failed a note in its `notes`. A failed read logs one line and leaves every priority as read.
- Over MCP a marked item reads `…, medium (2) by "codex", stated 75 days ago, unreviewed` (`1 day`, `today`, or no statement and no age) after its priority, a source's in brackets after the term naming it, in the `Priority` line, the compat titles and the agenda's and board's lines; a reply says once what the mark means, and once when a read failed; items past the reach are said as not checked. The compact value carries `unreviewed` only where a mark is present (no maker: a key's name rides the text), a failed read once as `unreviewedRead: false`; `search` and `fetch` carry the meaning, or the failure, in `unreviewedNote`. The descriptions, the server README and `docs/priority.md` decision 6 say it, its lag behind the scan (at most six hours) and that with sleep off nothing is marked. The REST client is regenerated.
