## Changelog

The brain now proposes a review of priorities that may have gone stale (SMD-3671 PR 1, migration 142). Sleep finds four kinds and records each as a pending finding. Nothing changes a priority until an operator decides. The kinds are: a level the brain's own keys stated more than 60 days ago on an item nobody has touched since; an item past its due time; the operator's level and Linear's two or more apart; and a level left on an item that has since settled. Accepting states the operator's own level, or clears the operator's own statement. Rejecting changes nothing. Both keep the same finding from coming back for the window. The operations to list and decide findings over REST and MCP come in PR 2. Until then, `review_priority_finding` is a SQL call.

## Record

Priority findings — sleep proposes a review of stale, overdue, disagreeing and settled statements, and an operator decides each; migration 142, PR 1 (SMD-3671)

**Why.** A priority stated once stood for ever. A level an agent stated in March and nobody touched since kept ranking at full weight. A due time could pass with the item still open, and the operator's level and Linear's could sit two levels apart. docs/priority.md decision 6 has sleep propose findings and accepting one make the operator's own statement. Decision 9 has sleep report the operator and Linear disagreeing.

**What changed.** The maintainer chose the following (2026-11-14).
- **`priority_findings`.**
  - Each finding has a kind, an item keyed by its pool (a ticket and its own rows are one item), the statements it is about, and evidence.
  - Its status is pending, accepted, rejected or moot. At most one pending finding stands per kind and item.
- **The four kinds.**
  - **stale:** a statement by the brain's own keys at level 1 or more, older than the window (60 days), not in force (its start and its resolved due time not still ahead), on an item with no activity within it. Activity (`ob1_priority_activity`) is a statement on its pool made or cleared; for a ticket, its head row written or a thought on its subject; for a thought, its row written (by anything but a background pass); for an open item, its last change; for an entity, a thought mentioning it; and an operator's decision on a finding about it. A source's statement never goes stale: board-sync's mirror of Linear is Linear's to keep (decision 9). Today both brains hold only those, 1,322 of them.
  - **overdue:** an unsettled item `effective_priority` reads as past its due time, including an open item past its own due time.
  - **disagree:** an operator's statement and a source's two or more levels apart.
  - **settled:** a statement by the brain's own keys left on a settled item. It replaces the ticket's "inherited level whose source closed", which cannot arise: `effective_priority` skips a settled source when it inherits.
- **The scan, `ob1_priority_review(window_days, now)`.**
  - It runs under one advisory lock. It writes what it newly finds, refreshes a pending finding's evidence, and marks moot one whose condition no longer holds.
  - A finding decided within its window is not raised again for that kind and item, so a re-confirmed level that still disagrees is not proposed the next night.
  - Sleep runs it at its first idle poll or period, then at most once every six hours, idle or not (`PRIORITY_REVIEW_EVERY_MS`). A role without what it takes is told once at start.
- **The decision, `review_priority_finding`.** Only an operator key may decide, by its agent id, as `set_priority_weights` checks.
  - Write scope alone: a read or capture key decides nothing (`NOT_WRITE_SCOPE`).
  - Accepting says the outcome: a level 0 to 5, and a due time if wanted, stated through `assert_priority`; or clear, which closes the operator's own statement through `clear_priority`. Clear with none answers `NOTHING_TO_CLEAR`: state 0 to outrank another maker's. The same level re-confirms it, keeping the operator's own statement's terms, but not an overdue finding's deadline. A settled finding takes clear or 0.
  - Rejecting changes nothing.
  - Both hold the same finding back for its window while its statements are those decided on.
- **Grants.** The priority group gains SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE on `priority_findings` (the scan prunes moot findings past the window), carried to the roles holding it already.

**Not changed.** No finding changes a priority. Nothing is posted to Linear: that is SMD-4304. The REST and MCP operations, `brain_info`'s count and preflight's row are PR 2. The reads' "stated N days ago, unreviewed" label is PR 3. A hand-run re-embed's writes are not marked background, so they read as activity.
