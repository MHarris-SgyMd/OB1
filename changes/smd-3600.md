## Changelog

Board-sync now releases a closed ticket's work claims: after each pass, every unreleased claim, live or lapsed, on a ticket the brain records as Done, Canceled or a Duplicate is ended, with the reason naming the status and the pass, so a holder that merged and never released no longer leaves `⚠ Claimed` on every read naming the ticket. Migration 117 adds the release, held from PUBLIC and granted by the new `closedclaims` group (SMD-3600).

## FORK

A closed ticket keeps no claim — board-sync releases every claim on a ticket the board closes (SMD-3600)

**What changed.**
- **Migration 117**, `release_closed_ticket_claims(p_run, p_dry_run, p_actor)`:
  - **Which tickets:** every ticket holding an unreleased claim whose head in `ob1_ticket_head` (068) has a settled status type (`node_settled_types()`: completed, canceled, duplicate) and is an importer's row (`metadata.source` is `linear`, which `capture_thought` and `update_thought`'s patch refuse to every key, `REFUSED_SOURCE_RESERVED`), so a key's own thought with an `issue` and a `status_type` releases nothing, even when it is the head. A write key can still edit the board's own rows (patch one's ticket keys, or retire an open head over an older closed board row: delete it, or point a `supersedes` at it) and so make a ticket read closed, as it can already mislabel `moved`; board-sync writes the board's status back when the ticket next moves.
  - **Which claims:** every unreleased claim on such a ticket, live or lapsed, the whole ticket's and every piece's, whenever it was made. A claim made on a ticket already closed is released at the next call too, by the maintainer's choice. A ticket that reopens brings none back.
  - **The end:** stamped from the call's envelope (board-sync's key, `ingested` on the stable brain), with the reason `<issue> closed: <status> (<status_type>); <run>`.
  - **`p_dry_run`** reads the same claims and writes nothing.
  - **115's concurrency rules:** READ COMMITTED alone, and each ticket's advisory lock taken in key order with the head read again under it, so a claim made on a ticket the call reached, while it waits for that ticket's lock, is released with the rest.
  - **Privileges:** EXECUTE is revoked from PUBLIC, the first core function so held, and granted by the new `closedclaims` group (EXECUTE, and SELECT and UPDATE on `work_claims`). The server never calls it. A plain `migrate.ts --grant` issues every group, this one included.
- **`db/sync-linear.ts`** makes the call after each pass, `--dry-run` and `--only` included and `--audit` not. It prints `claims on closed tickets: released N — …`, ten named, each with its holder, whether it had lapsed, and the ticket's status. A failed call fails the pass. A brain before 117, or a role without the group, is found once at start and said once.
- **The claim tools' words** (server-portable/core/claims.ts, render.ts, store `closedTicket`):
  - `claim_work` and `renew_claim` on a ticket the brain records as closed still claim or renew, and say board-sync will release the claim at its next pass — the end of the work if it merged, else the ticket closed under the holder (tell the user) — (`ticketClosed`: the status, else its type), read through 117's own test of a closed ticket, best-effort, a failed read logged;
  - a renewal, or `claim_work` with the token, of a claim board-sync ended answers `CLAIM_RELEASED` with `ticketClosed: true`: done if the holder's work merged (delete the token file and any marker), else the ticket closed under it (tell the user) — never a release to ask about claiming again. Only 117's own reason for the claim's own ticket (the lease's key, as 115 keyed it), by a releaser neither an operator nor an agent, counts;
  - `already_released` names who ended the claim and why;
  - `capture_thought`, and `update_thought`'s `metadata_patch`, refuse `source: "linear"` to every key (`REFUSED_SOURCE_RESERVED`, 400 over REST): the board's importers write it through the store directly.
- **The rule** (AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md, "Claim your work"):
  - a closed ticket (Done, Canceled or a Duplicate) keeps no claim, so work on one, such as its Verify or a follow-up, needs the ticket reopened or a ticket of its own;
  - board-sync releases a closed ticket's claims at its next pass; `release_claim` then answers `already_released`, and the hook's next renewal leaves an `.ended.json`. After your own work merged that is step 6 done for you: delete the files;
  - a piece's claim on a ticket still open is still the holder's to release, and a multi-PR ticket the board closes at its first PR takes the other pieces' claims with it: tell the user.
- **Docs:** db/README.md (117's paragraph, the board-sync section, the grants table, which groups a plain `--grant` issues), server-portable/README.md (Work claims), the tool descriptions (capture_thought's `source`, claim_work, renew_claim, release_claim), and the OpenAPI document and rest-client, regenerated.

**Why.** A claim ends when its holder releases it or its lease lapses (SMD-3597). An agent that merged and stopped without releasing left the claim unreleased. Since SMD-3599 every read naming the ticket says so: `⚠ Claimed: … lapsed at …, never released` on a Done ticket, for as long as the brain keeps the row. SMD-3599 left stale claims to this ticket.

**Not taken.**
- **A refusal for a claim on a closed ticket.** The maintainer chose a warning in the reply: no new refusal code, and the hook's revive path unchanged.
- **Keeping a role granted by a plain `--grant` out of `closedclaims`.** Documented instead: every group is what a plain `--grant` means.
- **Linear's close time from the census.** Releasing only claims made before Linear's `completedAt` would keep a claim made on a closed ticket. The maintainer chose the brain's own record and every claim instead.
- **A transition-only release**, on the pass that sees the move. A missed pass, or a claim left from before this change, would never be released.

**Upstream status.** Fork-only: work claims are the fork's (SMD-3596).
