## Changelog

Board-sync now releases a closed ticket's work claims: after each pass, every unreleased claim, live or lapsed, on a ticket the brain records as Done, Canceled or a Duplicate is ended, with the reason naming the status and the pass, so a holder that merged and never released no longer leaves `⚠ Claimed` on every read naming the ticket. Migration 117 adds the release, held from PUBLIC and granted by the new `closedclaims` group (SMD-3600).

## FORK

A closed ticket keeps no claim — board-sync releases every claim on a ticket the board closes (SMD-3600)

**What changed.**
- **Migration 117**, `release_closed_ticket_claims(p_run, p_dry_run, p_actor)`:
  - **Which tickets:** every ticket holding an unreleased claim whose head in `ob1_ticket_head` (068) has a settled status type (`node_settled_types()`: completed, canceled, duplicate) and is an importer's row (`metadata.source` is `linear`). No client can set `source`, so a key's own thought with an `issue` and a `status_type` releases nothing, even when it is the head.
  - **Which claims:** every unreleased claim on such a ticket, live or lapsed, the whole ticket's and every piece's, whenever it was made. A claim made on a ticket already closed is released at the next call too, by the maintainer's choice. A ticket that reopens brings none back.
  - **The end:** stamped from the call's envelope (board-sync's key, `ingested` on the stable brain), with the reason `<issue> closed: <status> (<status_type>); <run>`.
  - **`p_dry_run`** reads the same claims and writes nothing.
  - **115's concurrency rules:** READ COMMITTED alone, and each ticket's advisory lock taken in key order with the head read again under it, so a claim made while the call waits is released with the rest.
  - **Privileges:** EXECUTE is revoked from PUBLIC, the first core function so held, and granted by the new `closedclaims` group (EXECUTE, and SELECT and UPDATE on `work_claims`). The server never calls it, so a key's only way to end another key's claim stays an operator's forced release.
- **`db/sync-linear.ts`** makes the call after each pass, `--dry-run` and `--only` included and `--audit` not. It prints `claims on closed tickets: released N — …`, ten named, each with its holder, whether it had lapsed, and the ticket's status. A failed call fails the pass. A brain before 117, or a role without the group, is found once at start and said once.
- **The rule** (AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md, "Claim your work"):
  - a closed ticket keeps no claim, so work on one, such as its Verify or a follow-up, needs the ticket reopened or a ticket of its own;
  - board-sync releases a closed ticket's claims at its next pass, and `release_claim` then answers `already_released`;
  - a piece's claim on a ticket still open is still the holder's to release.
- **Docs:** db/README.md (117's paragraph, the board-sync section, the grants table) and server-portable/README.md (Work claims).

**Why.** A claim ends when its holder releases it or its lease lapses (SMD-3597). An agent that merged and stopped without releasing left the claim unreleased. Since SMD-3599 every read naming the ticket says so: `⚠ Claimed: … lapsed at …, never released` on a Done ticket, for as long as the brain keeps the row. SMD-3599 left stale claims to this ticket.

**Not taken.**
- **Linear's close time from the census.** Releasing only claims made before Linear's `completedAt` would keep a claim made on a closed ticket. The maintainer chose the brain's own record and every claim instead.
- **A transition-only release**, on the pass that sees the move. A missed pass, or a claim left from before this change, would never be released.
