## Changelog

Two maintainer scripts for CI-efficiency work. `scripts/ci-step-times.ts` prints a CI job's time and its steps' over a window of runs, with each one's median, p90, min and max. `scripts/suite-sections.ts` runs a suite and prints its numbered sections by wall time, slowest first (SMD-3305).

## FORK

Two maintainer scripts: a job's step times over a window of runs, a suite's sections by time (SMD-3305)

**What changed.**
- **`scripts/ci-step-times.ts --job <name> [--step <regex>] [--workflow] [--branch] [--days 7] [--runs <ids>] [--repo]`** reads runs through `gh`. For each run it prints the job's wall time and each matching step's, every step of a second or more when no pattern is given. Then, over the runs where the job succeeded, it prints each one's median, p90, min and max. A failed or cancelled job is listed, marked, and left out of the figures.
- **`scripts/suite-sections.ts [--top n] [--cwd dir] -- <command …>`** runs a suite and stamps each stdout line as it arrives. Each `[N]` section gets the time from its header to the next. It prints the tally, the sections slowest first and the total, and the command's stderr and exit code pass through.
- Both follow `scripts/`' conventions: a header docblock, a known-flags table that refuses an unknown flag with exit 2, and `--self-check` fixtures for the parsing and the figures. CI typechecks them with the rest of `scripts/`. Neither is a gate.

**Why.** Eight CI-efficiency tickets wait on a step's time over a week of main's runs: SMD-3170, 3193, 3194, 3195, 3198, 3201, 3202 and 3263. Each check so far was a /tmp loop over `gh run view`, and SMD-3201's hardcoded one job and four steps. Finding what to cut was likewise a /tmp script, which found test-rest [12]'s 38 of its 44 s for SMD-3263.

**Measured**, the Verify's own cases:
- `bun scripts/ci-step-times.ts --job "Portable server" --step "REST core's routes"` over main's last 7 days, 59 successful runs. The REST core step reads 57–58 s up to 8fe05eaa, 43–44 s from 31a74e8c (SMD-3201) and 16–17 s from 08fe0372 (SMD-3263), through 4594c369.
- `bun scripts/suite-sections.ts --top 4 --cwd server-portable -- bun test-rest.ts` names [12] at 10.0 s of the suite's 15.1 s.
- Both `--self-check`s pass, and `scripts/`' typecheck passes.

The same /tmp scripts gave two local tools that need no stack and stay out of the repo, in the maintainer's scratch kit: a suite mutant runner and a Linux CI-like suite runner.
