## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, re-embed now runs alone first for at most its share of a period — `OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE` (`--reembed-share`), 120 s by default, a live write's pause and its isolation of an outage not counted — then gives way to extraction and consolidation, and what it leaves begins the next period once they drain (SMD-4384). Under a steady inflow of thoughts with no vector its pool never drained, so it held the claim passes the whole period and nothing was extracted or consolidated. A model switch's backlog is shared the same way. `0` keeps the old rule, alone until its pool drains. No migration.

## Record

Re-embed under the sleep scheduler gives way at its share of a period — a steady inflow of thoughts with no vector no longer holds extraction and consolidation for good (SMD-4384)

**Why.** `db/sleep.ts` runs re-embed alone at a period's start, and the claim passes (extraction, consolidation) start only once it ends: its pool drained, or its provider not answering. Its follower pools every 15 s, so while thoughts with no vector arrive faster than that, one not yet pooled is always counted (`unpooled` at least 1) and `drained()` never holds. SMD-4254's review pass 1 measured it with the real scheduler at its shipped settings: on main, one vectorless thought every 4 s, extraction ran 0 of 64 thoughts in 240 s; on SMD-4254's branch, its crashing thought beside the same inflow, 0 of 80 in 300 s, where main's old set-down at the crash's outage had freed extraction by accident. Such an inflow comes from an egress policy keeping captures from a remote embedding provider, a provider too slow for captures while the vector mender queues them, or a bulk import of rows with no vector.

**Decided.** The maintainer's choices (2026-11-19), of the ticket's three options: a share of the period (not "drained enough", not re-embed beside the claim passes); by time, alone first, then stopping for the period (not a fraction of the period's time with the claim passes paused for it, not continuing beside them); applied to a model switch's backlog too (not exempting the backlog a period starts with), so consolidation may judge some pairs on the old model's vectors while a switch is re-embedded across periods; 120 s a period by default (SMD-4254's per-wait bound, about 400 vectors at 0.3 s each).

**What changed.**

- `db/sleep.ts`: `REEMBED_SHARE_S` (120, exported) and `--reembed-share SECONDS` (0–86,400; `SleepOptions.reembedShare`, `reembedShareMs` the tests' override). Re-embed's start in a period is taken on its clock (`reembedClock`, which a live write's pause stops); each poll while it runs, its time since less the period's isolation (`isolatedMs`) at or past the share — no wait in hand, no isolation in hand, the pool not drained — ends it after the thought in hand, said with what it leaves ("re-embed has had its share of the period — 120 s alone — N thought(s) left for the next period, which begins once the claim passes drain — extracting"), and the claim passes start. Nothing is set down (no `outage`, no row `aside`), nothing backed off, no suspect moved. `reembedLeft` is set, so a one-shot run exits 1, its pool left, as at a set-down; and `lastWhole` is cleared, so the next idle poll reads the pools whole — no poll between reads re-embed's claims (`workClaimed` skips it) — and the next period begins as soon as the claim passes drain, re-embed first again, where it waited up to the hourly rescan. The start says the share (`share:`), and the "re-embedding N thought(s)" line names it. 0 is no share, as before. SMD-4254's isolation keeps its own limits: the share never ends re-embed mid-isolation, nor counts its time, so the claim passes are held at most the share and the isolation's 240 s cap together.
- `db/coordinator.ts`: `OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE` among the sleep child's digits, passed as `--reembed-share` (unset, 120). `deploy/compose.yaml` passes it to the coordinator; `deploy/.env.example` documents it; `db/config.mjs`'s restart advice names it.
- `db/README.md` (the scheduler's usage, its settings and the re-embed paragraph) and `deploy/README.md` (the period's order and **Settings**) say the share, its default, what it leaves and when the next period begins.

**Contract.** A setting added (`OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE`, in `.env.example` and the coordinator's environment) and a flag added to a command the stack runs: a `minor`. Its default changes behaviour: a re-embed backlog longer than 120 s — a model switch's — is no longer re-embedded whole before extraction; `OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE=0` keeps the old order.
