## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, re-embed and the claim passes (extraction, consolidation) now take turns in a work period, each turn at most `OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE` (`--reembed-share`), 120 s by default: re-embed alone first, then, while it has work, the claim passes as long, waiting between thoughts in its turns; either side hands over sooner once it has caught up, after the followers' 15 s poll, a live write's pause and re-embed's isolation of an outage are not counted, and re-embed gives way only while the claim passes have work (SMD-4384). Under a steady inflow of thoughts with no vector re-embed's pool never drained, so it held the claim passes the whole period and nothing was extracted or consolidated. A model switch's backlog takes turns too. No migration. **Upgrading:** a re-embed backlog longer than 120 s — a model switch's, a brain's from before migration 021 — is no longer re-embedded whole before extraction while extraction or consolidation has work; consolidation may judge some neighbours on the old vectors meanwhile, and a one-shot `db/sleep.ts` run exits 1 with the backlog left. `OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE=0` (`--reembed-share 0`) keeps the old order.

## Record

Re-embed and the claim passes take turns in the sleep scheduler's periods — a steady inflow of thoughts with no vector no longer holds extraction for good, nor, once re-embed gives way, its vectors (SMD-4384)

**Why.** `db/sleep.ts` runs re-embed alone at a period's start, and the claim passes (extraction, consolidation) start only once it ends: its pool drained, or its provider not answering. Its follower pools every 15 s, so while thoughts with no vector arrive faster than that, one not yet pooled is always counted (`unpooled` at least 1) and `drained()` never holds. SMD-4254's review pass 1 measured it with the real scheduler at its shipped settings: on main, one vectorless thought every 4 s, extraction ran 0 of 64 thoughts in 240 s; on SMD-4254's branch, its crashing thought beside the same inflow, 0 of 80 in 300 s, where main's old set-down at the crash's outage had freed extraction by accident. Such an inflow comes from an egress policy keeping captures from a remote embedding provider, a provider too slow for captures while the vector mender queues them, or a bulk import of rows with no vector.

**Decided.** The maintainer's choices (2026-11-19), of the ticket's three options: a share of the period (not "drained enough", not re-embed beside the claim passes); by time, alone first, then stopping for the period (not a fraction of the period's time with the claim passes paused for it, not continuing beside them); applied to a model switch's backlog too (not exempting the backlog a period starts with), so consolidation may judge some pairs on the old model's vectors while a switch is re-embedded across turns; 120 s by default (SMD-4254's per-wait bound, about 400 vectors at 0.3 s each). Review pass 1 (of three: re-embed beside the claim passes after its share, the turns alternating, the one-way share documented): the turns alternate, each as long.
