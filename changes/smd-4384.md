## Changelog

Under the sleep scheduler, re-embed and the claim passes (extraction, consolidation and the subject sweep) now take turns in a work period, each turn at most `OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE` (`--reembed-share`), 120 s by default: re-embed alone first, then, while it has work, the claim passes as long, waiting between thoughts in its turns. Either side hands over sooner once it has caught up, after the followers' 15 s poll; a live write's pause and re-embed's isolation of an outage are not counted; and re-embed gives way only while the claim passes have work (SMD-4384). Under a steady inflow of thoughts with no vector re-embed's pool never drained, so it held the claim passes the whole period and nothing was extracted. A model switch's backlog takes turns too. No migration. **Upgrading:** a re-embed backlog longer than 120 s — a model switch's, a brain's from before migration 021 — is no longer re-embedded whole before extraction while extraction or consolidation has work; consolidation may judge some neighbours on the old vectors meanwhile, and a one-shot `db/sleep.ts` run whose claim passes drain within 15 s of re-embed giving way exits 1 with the backlog left. `OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE=0` (`--reembed-share 0`) keeps the old order.

## Record

Re-embed and the claim passes take turns in the sleep scheduler's periods — a steady inflow of thoughts with no vector no longer holds extraction for good, nor, once re-embed gives way, its vectors (SMD-4384)

**What changed.**

- `db/sleep.ts`: `REEMBED_SHARE_S` (120, exported) and `--reembed-share SECONDS` (0–86,400; `SleepOptions.reembedShare`, `reembedShareMs` the tests', range-checked). Each turn is counted on re-embed's clock (`reembedClock`, which a live write's pause stops).
  - **Re-embed's turn** (`reembedTurn`, at the period's start and mid-period) counts from its start, its probe inside it, less its isolation of an outage (`isolatedMs`; SMD-4254's count and 240 s cap now a turn's, as they were a period's). At a poll with no wait or isolation in hand and its pool not drained, it gives way after the thought in hand once the share is spent, or once caught up — nothing pending or in hand, only thoughts its follower has yet to pool — past `TURN_FLOOR` (the followers' own poll, 15 s), and only while a claim pass has work: a claim pending or in hand (`claimsWork`, an index read a pass) or thoughts not yet in extraction's pool (`claimsUnpooled`, a scan at most once a floor; on a brain with no extraction key yet no claim row shows them). Said: "re-embed has had its share — 120 s alone — N thought(s) left for its next turn, once the claim passes have had theirs or drained — extracting", or "re-embed has caught up — …". Nothing set down or backed off; the outage's suspects, should a wait have begun after the poll's reads, go behind the pool.
  - **The claim passes' turn** starts them (`startClaims`) or lets them go on (`resumeClaims`), counted from then (`claimsFrom`). Past the share, or caught up past the floor — none of this run's claims in hand and none pending since before the floor among the passes running (`claimsBehind`: 016's and 113's triggers pool each capture at once, so an empty pool never comes under an inflow) — with re-embed's pool not drained (read at most once a floor, once a share past a whole turn), they wait at a gate of their own (`turnGate`; `claimAdmit` asks the live write's gate, then it), between thoughts, windows and pairs as at a pause, holding no claim between thoughts; said ("the claim passes have had their turn — 120 s — with N thought(s) waiting for re-embed: they wait between thoughts while re-embed runs alone again", or "have caught up"), and re-embed's next turn begins in the same period — in a period whose start found re-embed not set aside (`reembedThisPeriod`): one taken up at a poll mid-period waits for the next, as before.
  - **A re-embed set down for an outage** in the period is tried again at the claim passes' whole turn's end, said, as a period's start would — not while SMD-4254's back-off sets such an outage down at once, and no other kind, which the next period or rescan tries as before.
  - **The period** ends as before, once the claim passes drain. Drained within a floor of re-embed giving way, re-embed's work is left (`shareLeft`): a one-shot run exits 1, as at a set-down, and the next idle poll reads the pools whole by a read of its own (`wholeNext`) — no poll between reads re-embed's claims — so the next period begins once the brain is quiet, re-embed first. The start says the turns (`share:`). 0 is no share and no turns.
- `db/coordinator.ts`: `OB1_SLEEP_REEMBED_SHARE` among the sleep child's digits, passed as `--reembed-share` (unset, 120); `deploy/compose.yaml` passes it to the coordinator; `deploy/.env.example` documents it; `db/config.mjs`'s restart advice names it.
- `db/README.md` and `deploy/README.md` say the turns, the default and when to change it, the log's lines, what a longer backlog costs (the pre-021 note, a model change), and when the next period begins.

**Why.** `db/sleep.ts` ran re-embed alone at a period's start; the claim passes started only once its pool drained or it was set down. Its follower pools every 15 s, so while thoughts with no vector arrive faster than that one not yet pooled is always counted (`unpooled` at least 1) and `drained()` never holds. SMD-4254's review pass 1 measured it with the real scheduler at the shipped settings: on main, a thought with no vector every 4 s, extraction ran 0 of 64 thoughts in 240 s; on SMD-4254's branch, its crashing thought beside the same inflow, 0 of 80 in 300 s. Such an inflow comes from an egress policy keeping captures from a remote embedding provider, a provider too slow for captures, or a bulk import of rows with no vector.

The maintainer's choices (2026-11-19): a share of the period by time (of the ticket's three options: not "drained enough", not re-embed beside the claim passes); applied to a model switch's backlog too; 120 s by default (SMD-4254's per-wait bound, about 400 vectors at 0.3 s each); the turns alternating (review pass 1); a side caught up handing over (review pass 2).
