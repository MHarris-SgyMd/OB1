## Changelog

`db/ci-parity.sh` runs `db/test-schema.ts`'s two widths in a lane of their own. They had run first in the lane of database-free suites and typechecks, and that lane had set a run's length; now test-live's longest shard does. A full run takes about 300 s on the maintainer's Mac, where it took about 370–430 (SMD-3715).

## FORK

ci-parity's test-schema widths in a lane of their own — ~370–430 → ~300 s (SMD-3715)

**What changed.**
- **The schema lane.** `schema_rows` runs test-schema at the default width, then at 768, each as CI's db-schema job runs it (CI runs the two at once). It's started, watched and stopped as every other lane is. Its block prints after the pair lane's, as "test-schema at its two widths, on PGlite". `free_rows` keeps every other database-free suite, self-check and typecheck.
- **Where the time went.** Since SMD-3688 started every lane at once, the database-free lane had set a run's length. In seventeen full runs from the two tickets before this one, the database-free lane took a median 424 s against a median run of 425 s, many of them beside another session's run. test-schema's two widths were 168 s of it (97 and 71), test-server 54, test-thoughts 34, test-connect 33, test-engines 25 and the ten typechecks about 26 together.
- **One new lane, not three.** Without test-schema the lane takes about 260 s, inside test-live's sleep shard (~292 s) and the pair lane (252–272 s, bar run four's 356). The two widths take about 120 s together, so a lane each, or the typechecks in another, would end no run sooner. Each lane more adds load to every lane's timing rows (SMD-3706, SMD-3712).
- **Docs:** ci-parity's header and comments. FORK.md's two paragraphs on what it runs stay true as written (test-schema is one of the database-free suites beside the database lane, its widths still one after the other), and FORK.md is within 100 bytes of its 64 KiB cap, so they're left as they are.
