## Changelog

`server-portable/test-preflight.ts` matches every age and next try it reads back from a heartbeat as a range that holds a late read, so a busy runner no longer fails a case though preflight is right (SMD-3569).

## FORK

test-preflight's heartbeat ages held to a range — a late read is not a failure (SMD-3569)

**What changed.**
- **Every seconds age** preflight reads back is matched as a range: `1\d s ago` for a row planted 10 s back, `2\d`, `3\d`, `4\d` and `6\d` likewise: 33 forms in 28 assertions, each range ten values, which hold 9 s of lateness. This is the convention SMD-2679 PR 4b-2 began with `3\d s ago`. An age only grows when the read lands late.
- **The next try** "in 80 s" is `(?:7[1-9]|80) s`, ten values too. A time still to come only shrinks.
- **A minute form** stays exact. `ago()` (brain-info.ts) rounds minutes over 30 s, and every form sits at least 10 s inside its rounding edge (the closest, "after 3 min", is a span of two plants, which lateness does not move). One sat on the edge:
  - the held scheduler's next try, `in_s` 240 from a stamp 30 s old, is 210 s, or 3.5 min, and a read half a second late said "3 min";
  - `in_s` is now 270, so the next try is 240 s, 4 min whole. A supervisor that forgot the stamp's age now prints 5 min and fails, where at 240 it printed 4 and passed.
- **A plant beside a threshold** keeps its place: the alive heartbeat "just inside three intervals" stays at 890 of 900, 10 s inside, past a late read's 9 s, so the pair with the stale one still brackets preflight's `ageS > 3 * every_s` closely. (Review pass 1 moved it to 880; pass 2 put it back, since at 880 a stale line moved to 885 would pass, by the rule's arithmetic.)
- **A comment beside the suite's regex helper** says why, and how to write the next one.

**Why.** `ago()` rounds to the second, so a read half a second late turns a planted 20 into 21. Under the load of SMD-3468's three background legs, the rest shard failed on one of these in 4 of 7 Linux rehearsals ("21 s ago", "ended 60 s ago", "11 s ago", "61 s ago"), and the first shard once ("stamped 20 s ago"). Earlier, SMD-3485's and SMD-3486's runs saw "79 s", "31 s ago" and "11 s ago", and CI failed SMD-2679 PR 4b-1 once on "30 s ago".

**Held.**
- **No exact seconds pin is left:** a scan of the file finds no `<digits> s ago` or `in <digits> s` outside a range. The one left, "within 5 s", is a configured limit, not a reading.
- **A late read,** simulated by planting a time further back:
  - 3 s late, the heartbeats, the sleep marks and the 20 s and 600 s intervals moved: main's file failed 30 cases; this file failed 1 (the held scheduler's 3.5 min) before its next try moved, and none after;
  - 8 s late, every plant moved (also the 10 s intervals, the lease expiries, the 8- and 30-day ages, `valid_until` and a JS timestamp; a run-it reviewer's mutant, review pass 1): 994 passed;
  - 10 s late, with the first set of plants: 33 failed, as each range holds 9 s at most.
- **Teeth:**
  - every seconds age printed a minute off (`ago()` + 60) fails 35 cases; 5 s or 10 s too small, 38; 10 s too big, 35;
  - the next try 10 s too small, 11 s too small or 1 s too big fails the held follower's case (10 s too small passed with `(?:7\d|80)`).
