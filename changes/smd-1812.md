## FORK

The page store — a page is a thought whose text is the render of its owned sections, a machine's regeneration parks on a human's section, the revisions reconstruct any prior state, a generated section records its lineage (SMD-1812)

**What changed.** Migration 063 adds the fork's document primitive, the store half of SMD-949. **A page is a thought.** `pages.id` is the page thought's id (a foreign key, cascade); the thought's content is `render_page()`'s text — `# title`, then each section by `(display_order, section_key)` with its `## heading` — written only through `update_thought` after every live change, so every render is an event in `thought_audit` (the page's own history, title included), stamped with the key that wrote it (050), searchable once the re-embed worker fills the vector 021 clears, and extracted like any thought. Its `derived_from` is the sorted union of the live sections' evidence, so 025's `trace_provenance` and `find_derivatives` read a page with no change to either; `supersedes` passes through and archives the superseded page. The structure is the store's: `page_sections` — each with an owner (`origin` `generated` is the machine's, `manual` or `locked` a human's), the live body, the machine's evidence and recipe, and the pending buffer a parked draft waits in with the evidence and recipe *it* was made from — and `page_section_revisions`, append-only by trigger (UPDATE and TRUNCATE refused for the owner too), one row per change to what the render reads and per ownership move. One door for a section's text, `write_page_section` (`ob1:page-regen-guard`): a generated write onto a human-owned section parks, the live text untouched (one that says what the live text says is `unchanged`); any other write updates in place — a manual write takes ownership — and snapshots a revision; `accept_page_section` promotes a parked draft and keeps the section the human's; `release_page_section` hands it back, `lock_page_section` locks it and `delete_page_section` removes it, each recorded; `render_page(page, at)` and `page_sections_as_of(page, at)` reconstruct any prior state. A generated section is a derived artifact under SMD-1729's rule: its `derivations` row (the sixth kind, `section` — 061's CHECK widened, `ob1_record_derivation` redefined on its own body plus the value) names the evidence at the fingerprints read and the generator's recipe (`deterministic` false unless declared, `declared: false` when none was sent), in the section's transaction, the same recipe held in the section's `generation_source`; a generated write without evidence is refused (a page is never its own), a parked draft parks its evidence's fingerprints and accept records those, a manual write that moves the body drops the row and empties the recipe, a deleted section's rows go by trigger, and preflight's `lineage` check counts sections carrying a recipe without one and warns on a page whose thought does not hold its render. Every writer locks the page thought, then the page, then the section — `delete_thought`'s order. Names differ from upstream's (`pages`, not `wiki_pages`), so a brain that applied `schemas/wiki-pages` by hand keeps its tables untouched; that directory retires (the README's row points here, `docs/vendored-disposition.md` records it, its community grant rows go), and a `pages` grant group (three tables) is what a role needs beside `capture`.

**Why.** The fork had no document: a synthesised runbook or an entity page was either kept outside the brain, losing the link to its evidence, or captured as one opaque thought its generator replaced whole on the next run — with whatever a human had edited. SMD-1812's "decide first": not SMD-1715's deliverable (one blob with a status machine — orthogonal, and compatible: a page has a thought id, so a deliverable facet can sit on it later), not one thought per section (003's fingerprint dedup collapses two sections with one text into one row, and every unaccepted draft would reach search, the extractor and the judge — the belief the guard exists to withhold). The decision is on the ticket.

**Held.** test-schema [58] (the suite 2043 → 2118): the Verify's two assertions first — a human-owned section byte-identical after a machine's regeneration (the draft parked with its own evidence and recipe, nothing else moved), and `render_page(page, at)` reconstructing five prior states byte for byte; the guard's matrix (created, updated, pending, locked, accepted, released, an identical regeneration moving nothing); the page thought's render, `derived_from`, 025's two readers, the log's events, the stamp from the session's envelope and not from `p_actor`, its place in the re-embed pool; the lineage row's inputs, fingerprints and recipe, dropped by a manual body and by a deleted section; fourteen refusals by name; 003's one text for a page (an empty page by its title line, a full one by its render); supersedes and the archive; append-only for the owner; the `pages` group (a granted role runs the store, may neither rewrite a revision nor delete a section, the identity needs no sequence grant; without the group the table is named); `delete_thought`'s cascade and the tombstone's render; a re-apply a no-op. test-live [31] (the suite 832 → 838): two sessions' first write of one section (one created, one updated), a human and a machine racing on one section (the human's text live either way; on a human-owned section the machine parks), two creates of one slug (one page, one refusal by name), the render the thought's content after every race. test-upgrade [20o] (the suite at 405): onto a populated brain at 062 — no row, event or lineage row moved, no seed, a page the day after, a re-apply a no-op, refused by name without 025, 032, 060 or 061. test-preflight (the suite at 534): a generated section without its row fails the start, the section named; the page gone through `delete_thought`, ok again. **Not taken.** A seed page (core ships no fixture row). Upstream's `deleted_at` (nothing set it, and the history could not see it; `delete_page_section` removes a section outright). Parking a heading or order a generated write proposed beside its body (the body is the draft; said in the column comment). A `type` key on the page thought (the extractor's vocabulary; `source: pages` marks it). Refusing writes to an archived page. MCP or REST tools for pages (the connectors' surface, SMD-949). Porting the three wiki writers (SMD-2143, unblocked by this). A foreign key from `derivations` to sections (061's polymorphic shape and drop trigger). A page-level revision table (the render's history is the thought's log). A per-section embedding.
